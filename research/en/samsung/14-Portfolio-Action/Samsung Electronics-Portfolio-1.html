Core call: size this for asymmetry, not as a core holding

Recommended position: initial 1.5%, cap 5%. This is not for lack of conviction, but because the volatility comes from the price, not the company: a single-quarter profit of RMB 8.9 trillion is unsustainable, and any quarter could deliver "profit halved + valuation unchanged." At a 5% position, the worst case (-60%) inflicts -3% damage on the portfolio, which is bearable.

No price stop-loss; only a logic-based stop-loss. Price stop-losses are the tool most likely to shake you out of cyclical stocks — after Samsung fell 40% in 2022, it rallied back, and everyone who stopped out missed it. A logic-based stop-loss (halve the position when contract prices turn down) reacts slowly, but it won't kill good positions by mistake.

Why 5%, not 10% or 15%

Position sizing is capped by the damage in the worst case, not by the return in the best case. For Samsung, the worst case is the bear scenario of KRW 110,000 (-60%): a 5% position implies -3% portfolio damage, 10% implies -6% and 15% implies -9%.

-3% is tolerable; -9% requires an explanation to holders. More importantly, Samsung's drawdowns tend to be synchronised with other holdings (memory, AI chips, semiconductor equipment); it is not a diversification tool but a risk amplifier. A 15% weight in a single name is equivalent to adding 15% leverage to semiconductors.

5% also means "permission to be wrong." If memory share stays at 33% and the price turns down in 2027, a 5% position will not cause serious damage; if HBM reaches 40% and foundry turns profitable, a 5% position can contribute 2%–3% to portfolio returns. This is a position with ordinary odds that will not knock you out of the game.

Position-building pace: three tranches, at least one month apart

Buy the first tranche now, 1.5%. This is not a call that we are at the bottom; it is an admission that we may be buying at the cycle top — paying 1.5% for not being absent, at a controllable cost.

The second tranche goes in below KRW 240,000, 1.5%. That level equates to 1.55x P/B, close to the median of Samsung's historical valuation range of 1.4-1.6x. If it never falls to that level, the market is more optimistic than I thought, so I won't add and won't chase.

The third tranche executes only when two conditions hold simultaneously: Q3 operating profit ≥ KRW 95 trillion (price increases still passing through) plus HBM4E certification secured (share can reach 40%). If either condition is missing, do not fill the position — this is the only mechanism preventing us from filling the position to the max at the cycle top.

Trim and stop-loss: trim on price, exit on thesis

Trim on price in two tiers: cut one-third at KRW 330,000 (PB 2.13x) and another half at KRW 400,000 (PB 2.6x). The logic is simple: the payoff disappears. At that price the optimistic scenario is already in the price, leaving only risk.

There is only one logical stop: if DRAM contract prices fall MoM for two consecutive months, cut half the position. The rule ignores the share price, the financials and management commentary; it looks only at TrendForce's monthly data. Its purpose is to prevent "telling a story during a decline" — the main way investors lose money in cyclicals is persuading themselves not to stop out on "long-term demand" while profits slide.

Why no price stop (say -20%)? Because cyclicals naturally swing more than 20%, and a price stop washes you out at the point of maximum panic. Samsung fell 40% in 2022 and recovered within a year; anyone who stopped out missed the entire rebound. A logical stop is slower, but it does not kill good positions by mistake.

Correlation: it is the same bet as your other holdings

Samsung's correlation with SK hynix and Micron exceeds 0.85 — same price cycle, same customers, same industry data. With TSMC and Nvidia, both part of the AI capex chain, it sits at 0.6–0.7, and with A-share semiconductors at around 0.5 on sentiment transmission. This is not a diversification tool but another expression of the same bet.

Position sizing must therefore be calculated on a combined basis: if the portfolio already holds Micron, SK hynix, Nvidia and TSMC, total exposure to memory and AI chips should not exceed 15%. Samsung's 5% must be counted within that 15%, not added on top.

FX is another dimension. Samsung is denominated in Korean won, and the won is weak in 2026 (KRW 1,500/USD). For a RMB investor, the FX exposure of won-denominated assets needs to be partly hedged against the duration of USD or RMB assets — but at a 5% position, FX is not the main issue and does not require a separate hedge.

Conclusion

Portfolio conclusion: initiate at 1.5%, cap at 5%, build in three tranches, trim on price strength, exit on thesis break. The name is highly correlated with our memory and AI chip holdings, so size exposure on a combined basis, capped at 15% in total.
Two rules: add nothing until the position exceeds 10%, and never panic-sell. The first guards against a cycle top; the second against being shaken out.
