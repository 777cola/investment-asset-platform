## Why 5%, not 10% or 15%

Position sizing is capped by the damage in the worst case, not by the return in the best case. Samsung's worst case is the bear scenario of KRW 110,000 (-60%); a 5% position implies -3% portfolio damage, 10% implies -6%, and 15% implies -9%.

-3% is tolerable; -9% requires an explanation to holders. More importantly, Samsung's declines tend to be synchronized with other holdings (memory, AI chips, semiconductor equipment); it is not a diversification tool but a risk amplifier. Giving 15% to a single name is equivalent to adding 15% leverage to semiconductors.

5% also means "permission to be wrong." If share stays at 33% and the price turns down in 2027, a 5% position will not cause serious damage; if HBM reaches 40% and foundry turns profitable, a 5% position can contribute 2%–3% to portfolio returns. This is a position with ordinary odds that will not knock you out of the game.

## Position-building pace: three tranches, at least one month apart

Buy the first tranche now, 1.5%. This is not a call that we are at the bottom; it is an admission that "we may be buying at the cycle top" — paying 1.5% for "not being absent," at a controllable cost.

The second tranche goes in below KRW 240,000, 1.5%. That level corresponds to 1.55x PB, close to the median of Samsung's historical valuation (1.4-1.6x). If it never falls to that level, the market is more optimistic than I thought, so I won't add and won't chase.

The third tranche executes only when two conditions hold simultaneously: Q3 operating profit ≥ KRW 95 trillion (price increases still passing through) + HBM4E certification secured (share can reach 40%). If either condition is missing, do not fill the position — this is the only mechanism preventing "filling the position to the max at the cycle top."

## Trim and stop-loss: trim on price, exit on thesis

There are two tiers for trimming on price: cut one-third at KRW 330,000 (PB 2.13x), and cut another half at KRW 400,000 (PB 2.6x). The reason is simple: the payoff disappears. At that price, the optimistic scenario is already priced in, and only risk remains.

There is only one logical stop: if DRAM contract prices fall month-on-month for two consecutive months, cut half the position. This rule ignores the share price, the financials and management commentary; it looks only at TrendForce's monthly data. Its purpose is to prevent "telling a story during a decline" — the main way investors lose money in cyclicals is persuading themselves not to stop out with "long-term demand" while profits slide.

Why no price stop (say -20%)? Because cyclicals naturally swing more than 20%, and a price stop washes you out at the point of maximum panic. Samsung fell 40% in 2022 and recovered within a year; anyone who stopped out missed the entire rebound. A logical stop is slower, but it does not kill good positions by mistake.

## Correlation: it is the same bet as your other holdings

Samsung's correlation with SK hynix and Micron exceeds 0.85 (same price cycle, same customers, same industry data), sits at 0.6–0.7 with TSMC and Nvidia (both part of the AI capex chain), and around 0.5 with A-share semiconductors (sentiment transmission). It is not a diversification tool but another expression of the same bet.

Position sizing must therefore be calculated on a combined basis: if the portfolio already holds Micron, SK hynix, Nvidia and TSMC, total exposure to memory and AI chips should not exceed 15%. Samsung's 5% must be counted within that 15%, not added separately.

Another dimension is FX. Samsung is denominated in Korean won, and the won is weak in 2026 (KRW 1,500/USD). For a RMB investor, the FX exposure of won-denominated assets needs to be partly hedged with the duration of USD or RMB assets — but for a 5% position, FX is not the main issue and does not require a separate hedge.
