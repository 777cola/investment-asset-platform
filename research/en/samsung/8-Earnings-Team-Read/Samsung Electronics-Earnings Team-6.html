In Samsung's earnings report, the most valuable line is not the KRW 89 trillion — Samsung Electronics

Release lead-in

Samsung earned KRW 89.49 trillion in operating profit in 2026Q2, overtaking Nvidia to become the world's most profitable tech company in a single quarter.But the most valuable line in this report is not that number — it is "HBM share 33%". The former was given by price; the latter was earned by capability. Price gets given back; capability stays.

First, look at that RMB 8.9 trillion: how "fake" is it?

Revenue KRW 171.5 trillion, operating profit KRW 89.49 trillion, net profit KRW 71.62 trillion — all three hit record highs. Operating margin 52.2%, gross margin 69.6% — a company that manufactures standard memory chips posting a gross margin two points above TSMC's.

How "fake" is this number? Break it down: memory revenue rose 471% year on year, but bit shipments grew only modestly. Almost all the growth came from pricing, with DRAM average selling prices up 44% quarter on quarter and NAND up 53%. In other words, the same fabs, the same engineers, the same equipment — only the price changed, and profit became 19 times last year's.

This profit is not sustainable, and it does not need to be — memory has always been this kind of business. The real question is: are you paying for it as if the profit were sustainable? A 7.5x PE says the market is not; it is applying a cyclical-stock discount.

Look again at that 33%: why it is worth more

HBM share rose from 21% to 33% in a single quarter. The significance of this number is not "12 points more sold" but "Samsung's HBM4 certification has cleared" — in 2024–2025 it was a year behind SK hynix on HBM3E, lost most of Nvidia's orders, and the market at one point believed it was out of AI memory.

Recovering 12 percentage points in one quarter shows two things: its engineering capability remains, and its error-correction speed is faster than expected. HBM4 is the first to ship in volume globally; this "first" is not a marketing line but the result of customer certification.

Why is this line of business more valuable? Because HBM gross margin is 15–20 percentage points higher than ordinary DRAM, and customer switching costs are high (more than 12 months of validation alongside the GPU). This profit does not vanish immediately when prices decline; it is the only asset Samsung can "carry with it" through this cycle.

The overlooked line: the phone business lost money

One inconspicuous number in the earnings report: the handset division posted revenue of KRW 33.2 trillion and an operating loss of KRW 0.7 trillion. Phone sales were not bad; the loss came from memory costs — Samsung raised its own prices and ate its own handset division's profit.

This is an intra-group transfer price, invisible in the consolidated statements, but it sends an important signal: memory price increases have begun to hurt end demand. When memory content in a flagship phone rises from 12% of bill-of-materials cost to over 20%, handset makers have only two choices — raise prices or cut specs. Samsung chose to absorb it; Apple and Xiaomi can only raise prices or cut specs.

For gauging where the cycle stands, this is more direct than any industry report: when upstream price increases start to squeeze downstream, the inflection point is near.

So, should you buy at KRW 275,000?

Three sentences. First, the company is fine: share is rising, HBM4 leads, foundry is turning around, and the balance sheet is net cash. Second, the price is not expensive but not cheap either: 7.5x PE is cheap, 1.8x PB is not cheap, and the gap between the two numbers is the definition of a "cycle top." Third, the denominator is at the top: a single-quarter profit of KRW 89 trillion is unsustainable, and once prices turn down in 2027, the PE will rise on its own.

So the answer is "buy, but buy it as a cyclical." Build one-third at the current price, add a second tranche below KRW 240,000; Q3 guidance and HBM4E certification are the two checkpoints. Total position no more than 5%; once DRAM contract prices turn down in 2027, cut half the position, regardless of profit or loss.

One last unpopular point: buying Samsung is not buying AI, it is buying the DRAM price cycle plus HBM share catch-up. If you cannot say where these two variables stand right now, you should not buy it, no matter how low the PE.

Conclusion

Published conclusion: good company, cheap PE, dangerous denominator. The RMB 8.9 trillion profit was given by price; the 33% share was earned by capability. The former will be given back, the latter will stay. The reason to buy should be the latter; position sizing should be designed around the former.
