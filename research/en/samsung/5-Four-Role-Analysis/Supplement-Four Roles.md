## How to read these five reports

The first four are run independently, with no cross-communication: commercial analysis covers business structure only, financial analysis the three statements only, industry research competition and cycles only, and risk assessment the downside only. The fifth is a synthesis, built specifically to resolve where they conflict.

The benefit is that it avoids "conclusion first, evidence second"; the drawback is duplication — the same number may appear four times, each time from a different angle. If you read only one, read the risk section; if you read only the conclusions, read the final section of the synthesis.

All four share a common data base: the 2026Q2 earnings report (disclosed 2026/07/30) — revenue KRW 171.5 trillion, operating profit KRW 89.49 trillion, net profit KRW 71.62 trillion, DS division KRW 89.2 trillion. Share data is from TrendForce (2026/09/07) and Counterpoint (2026/09/02). The divergence does not come from the data but from the weighting assigned to the same set of data.
