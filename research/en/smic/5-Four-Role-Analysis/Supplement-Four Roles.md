## Why four roles instead of a single report

The most common mistake in writing a report from start to finish is "conclusion first, evidence patched in later." Splitting the work into four roles, each answering only one question, forces contradictions to the surface—and this time it surfaced three: the sustainability of price increases, the depreciation peak, and the valuation metric.

The four reports run 700–900 words each, plus a 900–1,200-word summary, roughly 4,500 words in total, all contained in the 5 pages of this folder. This page serves only as navigation and adjudication.

## Ruling summary

The three disputes resolve as follows: price hikes can rescue revenue but not profit, so the risk view wins; depreciation will not peak before 2027, so the financial view wins; and valuation should rest on P/B rather than P/E or EV/EBITDA, so the financial and risk views win jointly. All three converge on a single sentence: a good company at the wrong price.
