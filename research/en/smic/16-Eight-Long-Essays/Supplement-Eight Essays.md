## Why write eight articles

A 40,000-word report gets read only for its conclusion. Split into eight pieces, each answering one question, and readers can read only the piece they care about. That is the formatting consideration.

Substance matters more: on SMIC, the bulls and the bears use entirely different frameworks. The bulls use "domestic substitution + AI demand"; the bears use "depreciation + valuation." The point of eight pieces is to lay out both frameworks so readers can judge for themselves which one holds up better.

## The shared foundation of all eight articles

All eight pieces rest on the same numbers: 2026Q1 revenue of USD 2.506 billion, gross margin of 20.1%, net profit of USD 197 million, depreciation of USD 1.088 billion and utilization of 93.1%; 2025 revenue of USD 9.327 billion, net profit of USD 685 million, depreciation of USD 3.81 billion and capex of USD 8.1 billion; and 2026 guidance of roughly USD 5 billion in depreciation and roughly USD 8.1 billion in capex.

Their conclusions must therefore agree—not through repetition, but because the same set of facts is being projected from different angles. If any one piece's conclusion conflicts with the other seven, its reasoning must be wrong.
