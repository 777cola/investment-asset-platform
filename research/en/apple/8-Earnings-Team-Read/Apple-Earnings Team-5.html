View synthesis: all four agree on one point — Apple

Earnings team analysis · Part Five
Research date 2026/08/03  ·  Price $303.42 (-1.78%)  ·  Market cap About USD 4.41 trillion  ·  PE(TTM，FY2026Q3) Approximately 34x
← Back to the hub

Core view

Read together, the four deep dives agree far more than they disagree. They agree that "the business isn't broken"; they disagree on "what multiple it deserves."

Three consensus views

First, pricing power remains. All four cited the same set of numbers: iPhone +21.7% while unit shipments grew only in the single digits, 20% share, 50.1% gross margin. Flat volume with rising price is the hardest evidence of pricing power, more reliable than any brand survey.

Second, growth is slowing. Services 16%→12%, Greater China below consensus, next-quarter gross margin guidance of 47%–48% — three marginal changes appearing at once. None of the four viewed this as a "one-off disturbance."

Third, 34x is too expensive. The four buy lines: Duan Yongping "wait for the price" (historically bought at 13x), Buffett 25x (USD 250), Munger "the odds are wrong," Li Lu below USD 260. The four lines fall in the USD 250–280 range, broadly overlapping this report's neutral valuation of USD 290.

Two disagreements

Disagreement one: is AI worth anything. Duan Yongping and Munger see AI as a "maintenance tool" that generates no standalone revenue and should not be valued separately; Li Lu sees it as an "entry option" worth 3–5x P/E. The ruling is to count it at 0 — because option pricing requires time and a pricing mechanism, and neither exists.

Disagreement two: is 12% services growth the floor or a midpoint. Buffett leans toward the floor (subscriptions and advertising still growing double digits); Munger leans toward a midpoint (take rate and search placements being structurally eroded). The ruling is a midpoint of 11%–12%, corresponding to a fair multiple of 29x rather than 33x.

Both rulings point the same way: lower. That is why all four target prices sit USD 50–70 below the sell side.

Reducing the disagreements to numbers: services growth of 12% versus 15%, a 3-point gap, worth 3x P/E; the AI option moving from 0 to 3x, worth 3x P/E. Together the two disagreements come to exactly 6x — 34x minus 6x is 28x, times FY2027 EPS of USD 10.0 gives USD 280. That is the answer once the four-way disagreement is quantified.

Synthesized action

Taken together, the four pieces point to one action: a 0-2% observation position, add to 5% below USD 260, trim to 0 above USD 380. No action in between.

This is not a bearish call; it is a refusal to pay for assumptions. Apple's business merits long-term ownership, but the cost of holding it (opportunity cost) peaks at 34x. Waiting is the cheapest move.

One more point belongs in the synthesis: the four disagree on buybacks but reach the same conclusion. Duan Yongping says buyback prices are too high, Buffett calls buybacks a plus, Munger says buybacks are destroying cash, and Li Lu says buybacks fund entry-point optionality. Four interpretations point to the same action: do not add above USD 300, because the company itself is buying stock above USD 300 with money that could have been saved for cheaper entry points.

Conclusion

Synthesis: three call it expensive, one likes the setup. Consensus: business 9 out of 10, price 2–4 out of 10. Entry point USD 260, tracking position 0%–2%, no chasing.
