Team Lead summary: three points of contention and the rulings — Apple

Four-role cross-review · disagreements and rulings

Core view

Read together, the four reports agree more than they disagree: business 9, moat 9, management 7, price 2–4. The disagreement centres on three points, each adjudicated below.

Divergence 1: is the services slowdown cyclical or structural?

The business view is that this is cyclical: the fee-rate adjustment forced by App Store regulation is one-off, and free AI features are a customer-acquisition cost rather than lost revenue. The financial view is that it is structural: 16%→12% marks two consecutive quarters of decline on a growing base, so a return to 15% requires a new category.

Verdict: treat it as structural, but keep reversal potential. Of the four major service revenue blocks (commissions, subscriptions, default search placement, advertising), commissions are being compressed by regulation and default search placement is threatened by AI search; together these two account for more than half of service revenue. Subscriptions and advertising are still growing at a double-digit rate, but the scale is insufficient. The service growth midpoint should therefore be cut from 15% to 11%–12%, and raised again only when the "AI subscription" new line emerges.

This verdict has a direct impact on valuation: cutting the service growth midpoint from 15% to 12% takes the corresponding fair multiple from 33x to 29x. That is where the neutral-case USD 290 comes from.

Disagreement 2: Is the gross margin decline cyclical or structural?

The industry view holds that the memory cycle will ease in 2H27 as all three suppliers expand capacity, allowing gross margin to recover to 48%–49%. The financial view holds that even with lower prices, Apple will remain under long-term pressure from "AI compute costs": on-device models require larger memory and more NPU area, a permanent step-up in BOM.

Verdict: both are right, on different time scales. Once memory prices fall in 2027, gross margin can recover to around 48%, but not to 50.1%, because the AI-driven memory upgrade (12GB→16GB) is a permanent cost step-up. The gross margin centre in 2027–2028 should therefore be 47.5%–48.5%, not 50%.

This verdict leaves the optimistic-scenario EPS at 11.3 but cuts the multiple from 35 to 33, for a net effect of lowering the target price from 395 to 373.

Disagreement 3: How much valuation should AI command

On both the commercial and risk views, AI is a maintenance tool: it makes the iPhone easier to sell but generates no standalone revenue, so it should not be valued separately. Li Lu's view treats it as an entry-point option — if a charging model materialises, it becomes a new profit pool and can carry option value of 3–5x P/E.

Ruling: assign 0x, wait for evidence. Option pricing requires volatility and a maturity date, and neither exists here: we do not know when charging will begin, how it will be priced, or what penetration will be. Folding option value into a target price under these conditions amounts to paying yourself a bonus.If fees are actually charged in 2027, buying at USD 320 then is still no loss — you earn certain money, not money from guessing right.

Combining the three rulings: fair value USD 280–290 against a current price of USD 303.42, 5%–8% above. Not absurdly expensive, but at no discount. All four roles agree: initiate a tracking position of 0%–2%, and wait for below USD 260 or for AI revenue to be reported separately.

Final point: if you must buy now, how should you do it?

If "waiting" is not acceptable, only one executable plan remains: open a tracking position at 1%–2% of the portfolio, set USD 260 as the add line and USD 380 as the trim line. Worst case, you lose 2%×29% = 0.6% of portfolio net value; best case, you gain 2%×23% = 0.46%. The payoff remains asymmetric, but the position is small enough to bear.

The benefit is not making money — it is staying in the game. Only with a position will you read every quarterly report closely, track services growth, track Greater China, and track gross margin guidance. The cost of researching a company is far below the cost of missing a mispricing.

Merge the four roles' watchlists into four items and put them on the calendar: ① quarterly gross margin guidance (47% is the psychological line; a break below 45% triggers a de-rating); ② services revenue growth (is 12% the floor or a midpoint — watch two quarters); ③ Greater China revenue (RMB 15 billion is the warning line); ④ average buyback price (continued large buybacks above USD 300 would mark down capital allocation). All four are quarterly observable readings; no need to wait for a narrative.

Conclusion

Bottom line: watch, wait for a pullback. All three disputed points argue for a lower valuation centre: services growth of 12% rather than 15%, gross margin of 48% rather than 50%, and the AI option valued at zero. Fair value cut to USD 280–290 from the sell-side's USD 335–360.

At USD 303.42 the stock sits 5%–8% above that range. The gap is too narrow to act on, but too narrow to turn bearish. Watch, 0%–2% position; add at USD 260, trim at USD 380.
