JD.com at 7x PE: so cheap it's hard to buy

📝 Conclusion: Watch and wait for food delivery losses to stop

Watch: wait for food delivery losses to stop. JD.com's 2026Q2 revenue was RMB 346.4 billion, down 2.9% YoY, yet operating profit swung from -RMB 0.9 billion last year to +RMB 4.5 billion; net profit attributable to shareholders was RMB 7.1 billion and non-GAAP RMB 8.9 billion — the profit inflection is real, but it came at the cost of a 24.8% cut in marketing spend and negative revenue growth. After two years of fighting in food delivery, its order volume share has fallen from a peak of 31% to 7%–10%, and new businesses still lost RMB 9.85 billion in the quarter. The current HKD 99.4 implies a market cap of about HKD 280 billion, 7.5x 2026 adjusted P/E, with cash and short-term investments of RMB 235.1 billion on the books — nine-tenths of market cap — at this price the company does not need to get better, it only needs to stop getting worse. But "not getting worse" requires management to first prove it will not fight another unwinnable war. Two buy triggers: a break below HKD 90, or Q3 confirming retail revenue turns positive and food delivery losses fall below RMB 8 billion.
