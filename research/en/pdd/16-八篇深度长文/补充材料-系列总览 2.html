2. Core views of each report

Part One · Pinduoduo's Business Miracle(Document temporarily unavailable)

Core thesis
How Pinduoduo covered in 5 years the ground Alibaba took 15 years to cover—social fission, the C2M model and the lower-tier market strategy.

Part Two · The Financial Code of the Efficiency King

Core thesis
The leap in ROE from -10% to 36% stems from the fixed-cost-dominated structure of the platform economy. A DuPont decomposition reveals a net margin of 22.6% as the core driver. ROE is expected to stabilize at 20-25%.

Part Three · Huang Zheng steps down, Chen Lei steps up

Core thesis
Management execution: A. Shareholder returns: C. Colin Huang is a strategic founder by nature, and Chen Lei has successfully proven a "preserve and refine" management style. Biggest problem: RMB 400 billion in cash with no buybacks and no dividends.

Part Four · Douyin E-commerce Arrives

Core thesis
Douyin's "passive consumption" model poses a fundamental challenge to Pinduoduo's "active price comparison." But the most likely outcome is coexistence—each serving different consumption scenarios. Not fatal.

Part Five · Temu Goes Global

Core thesis
The sexiest and most dangerous story. Full consignment + direct mail + subsidies in exchange for growth. The biggest risk is a change in the US de minimis exemption policy. The core question (whether it can be profitable) will take 2-3 years to answer.

Chapter 6 · How Duan Yongping views Pinduoduo

Core thesis
Duan Yongping's "Three Good" framework: good business 8/10, good management 8/10, good price 10/10. Overall score satisfactory. Key question: if the stock market closed for 5 years, would you be willing to hold?

Part Seven · How much is Pinduoduo worth

Core thesis
Three-scenario valuation: bull USD 209 (+154%) / base USD 146 (+78%) / bear USD 85 (+3%). Ex net cash, the market prices the operating business at less than 4x FCF—deeply undervalued.

Part Eight · Investment Decision Framework

Core thesis
Three core arguments: cheap enough, won't die, free Temu optionality. Recommended position 8-12% (build in the $75-85 range), with a complete sell discipline and tracking checklist.
