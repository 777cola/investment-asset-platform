🏢 Stage 1 · Duan Yongping's lens — business change ▼

Earnings Deep Dive · Stage One — Duan Yongping's Lens: How the Business Is Changing

Earnings report: Pinduoduo FY2025 Annual
Role: Duan Yongping (focusing on the essence of the business)

Core view

FY2025 results do not change my view of PDD's underlying business. It remains an extremely efficient platform, but the signals of slowing growth are becoming clearer.

Revenue mix and changes

Revenue RMB 431.9 billion (+9.7%), the slowest growth on record. But this reflects a broad e-commerce slowdown, not a Pinduoduo-specific problem. The issue is:When users are no longer growing rapidly, what does Pinduoduo rely on to keep making money?

The answer: serve the existing 800 million users well and get them to spend more.

The gross margin decline (60.9%→56.3%) looks bad, but it is mainly due to Temu's rising share. The domestic main site's gross margin remains healthy. Any company goes through this phase when building a new business—the key is whether Temu can grow into a second growth curve.

Key focus areas

  1. Management said it "will continue to invest in agricultural technology" — the right direction, as agriculture is Pinduoduo's differentiated advantage
  2. Management gave no clear Temu figures — that is somewhat unsettling
  3. Cash keeps piling up—the good news is the company has no shortage of money; the bad news is they don't know how to use it

Duan Yongping's conclusion

The good business has not deteriorated, but it has not improved either.The FY2025 results offer no new reason to buy or sell.

📈 Stage 1 · Munger's lens — competitive shifts ▼

Earnings Deep Dive · Stage One — Munger's Lens: Shifting Competition

Earnings report: Pinduoduo FY2025 Annual
Role: Munger (reading competitive change)

I. Hidden competitive signals in the financial reports

1. Change in selling expenses

SG&A RMB 133.4 billion, 30.9% of total revenue. Up slightly from 30.2% in FY2024.With user growth slowing, marketing spend rose instead—a sign that customer acquisition costs are climbingThis is a common problem for all mature e-commerce platforms.

2. Revenue growth slows

A growth rate of +9.7% means Pinduoduo is sliding from the "high-growth" band into the "low-growth" band. In the stock-market competition of e-commerce, market share is increasingly hard to win.

II. Assessment of the Competitive Landscape

Douyin e-commerce: a threat that cannot be ignored

Douyin e-commerce GMV broke through RMB 1 trillion in 2025; although its share still trails Pinduoduo,Growth far outpacing PinduoduoDouyin's "interest-based recommendation" model poses a fundamental challenge to Pinduoduo's "search-and-compare" model:

  • Users go to Pinduoduo because "I know what I want to buy, and I want to find the cheapest."
  • Users go to Douyin because "I don't know what I want to buy, but Douyin helps me find it"

The competition between these two models is essentiallyActive consumption vs passive consumptioncompetition.

Why did Alibaba's Taote fail?

Alibaba tried to take on Pinduoduo with Taote, pouring in over RMB 10 billion, but the results were limited. The reasons:
- Alibaba's DNA is brand e-commerce, not white-label e-commerce
- Pinduoduo's C2M network reflects eight years of accumulation and cannot be replicated in the short term

III. Munger-style inversion

Think in reverse: If Pinduoduo were losing the competitive battle, what would you see in its financials?

  • Revenue growth badly underperforms the industry (❌ Did not happen — Pinduoduo's growth remains above the industry average)
  • Margins keep sliding (⚠️ they are indeed sliding, but mainly for structural reasons)
  • User churn or declining engagement (❌ no evidence)
  • Management acknowledged competitive pressure (❌ Management stayed low-key but confident)

ConclusionCompetition is indeed intensifying, but there is no evidence that Pinduoduo is losing.

The analysis is based on public financial report data.

⚠️ Stage 1 · Li Lu lens — risk signals ▼

Earnings Deep Dive · Stage One — Li Lu Lens: Risk Signals

Earnings report: Pinduoduo FY2025 Annual
Role: Li Lu (hunting for risk signals)

I. Hidden signals in the notes

1. Equity incentives

SBC was RMB 7.94 billion, equal to 8.1% of net profit. That is a notable figure—not especially high, but nearly RMB 8 billion of equity incentives each year means shareholders' equity is continuously diluted (roughly 0.2% share count growth per year).

2. Investment income

Interest income RMB 25.58 billion, accounting for 21.4% of pre-tax profit.More than one-fifth of profit comes from passive interest income on cash. If rates fall, this profit will be squeezed—investors need to watch whether this is a structural contribution or a temporary one.

3. Tax rate changes

The effective tax rate rose from 15.3% to 18.2%. The higher tax rate eroded roughly RMB 2.8 billion of net profit.

III. Long-term risk scan

3.1 Industry risk

China's e-commerce industry faces a structural shift: from a growth market to a stock market. In a stock market, often only the top two players make money, while the third and beyond may fall into losses.

Pinduoduo currently ranks third by share (~15%), yet it is more profitable than both the leader (Alibaba) and the runner-up (JD.com). This shows that in a competition for existing market share,Efficiency matters more than scale。

3.2 Organizational risk

Huang Zheng's exit is a long-term risk. Chen Lei is an excellent executor, but Pinduoduo may need more than execution — it needs sustained strategic innovation. In five years, if management cannot offer a new growth narrative, the company could settle into a state of "maintenance rather than growth."

3.3 Systemic risk

As a Chinese ADR listed in the US, Pinduoduo always faces uncertainty in China-US relations. This is not something the financials can resolve, but it must be considered as a risk factor.

IV. Li Lu's assessment

No fraud or hidden "landmines" were found in the financial report.The risks are all public and predictable:

  1. Temu profitability uncertain (publicly disclosed)
  2. Domestic growth slows (already known to the market)
  3. Management succession (long-standing issue)
  4. Systemic risk of China concept stocks (non-diversifiable)

The most noteworthy signal is:Pinduoduo's success increasingly hinges on how long the domestic main site—its "cash machine"—can keep running., and when Temu, the "new machine", will take over.

Pinduoduo FY2025 Earnings Review: When the "Money Printer" No Longer Runs at Full Speed

Reading time: 8 minutes | Author: AutumnPants | 2026/07/05

Pinduoduo delivered a "neither good nor bad" earnings report.

The bear case: revenue growth slowed to a record low of 9.7%, and net profit fell for the first time (-13%). The growth story is over.

Put it this way, it is not bad: FCF remains as high as RMB 105.8 billion, net cash exceeds RMB 400 billion, and zero debt. The cash machine is still running steadily.

The market's reaction to this earnings report was muted—because expectations were already low enough. Pinduoduo now trades at a PE of under 10x; it is no longer a company that can sell a growth story.

01 Growth: the ceiling has indeed been reached

Pinduoduo FY2025 revenue was RMB 431.9 billion, up 9.7% year on year.

Compare with historical growth: +90% in 2023, +59% in 2024, +10% in 2025. This is not a management failure but a commercial law—when your annual active buyers exceed 800 million and account for the vast majority of Chinese internet users, demanding high-speed growth is unrealistic.

Pinduoduo has two response strategies:
1. Raise consumption frequency and average ticket size among existing users
2. Overseas expansion through Temu

The first strategy has a clear ceiling—price-sensitive users will not spend more just because Pinduoduo "gets better." The second strategy is fraught with uncertainty—whether Temu can make money overseas remains an open question.

02 Profit: first decline, but quality intact

Net profit RMB 97.8 billion, down 13% YoY. Gross margin fell from 60.9% to 56.3%.

Both numbers look weak. But the reasons behind them need to be understood:

The gross margin decline does not reflect weakening pricing power at Pinduoduo; it reflects the rising share of the low-margin Temu business.It is like shifting from selling software at a 70% gross margin to selling hardware at a 30% gross margin—the overall gross margin is bound to fall. Gross margin at the domestic main site remains healthy.

More critically, cash flow:

FCF RMB 105.8 billion, FCF/net profit = 1.08x.Every yuan of profit turned into cash. This is not "paper profit" manufactured by inflating receivables or stretching supplier payment terms—it is real money.

03 Valuation: cheap for a reason

PE 9x. The lowest among major global e-commerce platforms.

Why so cheap? Because the market is pricing it as "an e-commerce company with no growth." At this valuation, the expectation implied by the market is:
1. Domestic business no longer growing
2. Temu ultimately fails
3. Management remains indifferent to shareholder returns

These concerns are valid. But the problem is—What if these concerns prove wrong?

What if Temu succeeds? What if management starts large-scale buybacks? What if domestic revenue growth stabilizes at 5-10%?

The answer is simple: in these scenarios, the upside for the share price far exceeds the downside.

04 Risks: what you really need to worry about

What is most worrying is not that the PE is too low—it is that the market may be right.

The market says Pinduoduo's growth is over.

If the market is right (and the odds are not low), then Pinduoduo will forever be a low-growth company. Its share price may oscillate between $60 and $120 and never return to its past growth rate and valuation.

Such an investment will not lose much money (net cash provides a floor), but it will not make much either.

05 Conclusion

The core takeaway from Pinduoduo's FY2025 results: this is a company moving from "growth" to "maturity".

For investors, this is a classic value-investing setup:
- If the market is wrong about Temu and growth → rich returns
- If the market is right → no losses, but mediocre returns

It depends on your read of Temu and management.
