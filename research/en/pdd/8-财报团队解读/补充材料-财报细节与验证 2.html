3. FY2025 results: what changed, what did not

🔴 What changed

  • Revenue growth slowed from 59% to 9.7% — the growth story is over
  • Net profit falls for the first time (-13%) — the earnings inflection point may have passed
  • Gross margin fell from 60.9% to 56.3% — a structural issue (rising Temu share)
  • ROE fell from 36% to 24% — still an excellent level

🟢 What hasn't changed

  • FCF tops RMB 100 billion — the cash machine has not stopped
  • Net cash still growing (+29.9%) — balance sheet strengthening
  • Zero debt — no financial risk
  • Operating efficiency — still best in the industry
  • Management remains quietly confident — no admission of competitive pressure
