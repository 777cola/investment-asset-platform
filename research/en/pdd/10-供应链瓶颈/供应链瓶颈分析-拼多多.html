III. Second/third-layer bottlenecks (potential sources of alpha)

Bottleneck 1C2M flexible manufacturing capacity

Pinduoduo's core supply side consists of C2M factories able to switch production lines quickly and accept small-batch orders. As it keeps scaling the C2M model, capacity at such factories could become a scarce resource.

Characteristics:

  • Non-branded white-label factory, but with consistent quality
  • Capable of responding quickly to order changes (flexible production)
  • Capacity may fail to keep up after order surge

Beneficiaries: flexible manufacturing solutions (automated production line retrofits, MES systems, small-batch order management systems)

Bottleneck 2Temu cross-border logistics: the full chain

Temu uses a "fully managed" model—Chinese merchants deliver goods to Temu's domestic warehouses, and Temu handles cross-border logistics, customs clearance and last-mile delivery. This chain has several potential bottlenecks:

Key takeaways: Temu's core bottleneck is not on the supply side (Chinese factories have ample capacity), but onCross-border fulfillment side—In particular, the US may scrap the duty-free policy for small parcels under $800. This would fundamentally change Temu's cost structure.

Bottleneck 3The ceiling on user attention

The essence of e-commerce platform competition is the battle for users' time and attention. The problem Pinduoduo faces is:

  • Daily average user time spent ~30 minutes (far below Douyin's ~120 minutes)
  • In a zero-sum competition for existing users, "share of attention" is a scarce resource

What sets it apart: Pinduoduo can buy traffic with money, but it cannot buy the time users spend on the app. Without enough time, it cannot generate enough recommendations and conversions.

Key findings: Pinduoduo's biggest bottleneck is not in China (where the supply chain is extremely strong), but inCross-border segment。

Temu's fully managed model depends on the US de minimis exemption for small parcels. If that policy is scrapped, Temu's costs will rise sharply and the entire business model will need to be rebuilt.

V. Pinduoduo's Strategy for Addressing Bottlenecks

🏗️ Overseas warehouse construction

Temu begins building overseas warehouses, shifting from direct mail to a forward-stocking model

🌐 Localized fulfillment

Partner with overseas logistics providers to build localized delivery networks

🏭 Supply chain moves downstream

Deepen C2M further by bringing more factories into the direct-connection system

🌍 Diversification across multiple markets

Temu has also entered markets such as Europe and Southeast Asia, reducing its dependence on the US market alone
