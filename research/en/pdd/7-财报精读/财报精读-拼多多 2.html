TwoThe 3 most important changes this period

Change 1: Growth engine stalls — from +59% to +9.7%

FY2024 still had 59% revenue growth; by FY2025 only 9.7% remained. China's domestic e-commerce market is saturated, and Pinduoduo's user penetration has peaked.

Impact: The market has already completed its downward revision of Pinduoduo's growth expectations—the PE has fallen from over 30x in the past to 9x now, already pricing in a "zero growth" expectation.

Change 2: The "structural decline" in gross margin

Gross margin fell from 75.9% (2022) to 56.3% (2025), a drop of nearly 20 percentage points over four years. But this isStructuralRather thanDeteriorating operations—Because the low-margin Temu business accounts for a growing share, it "drags down" the overall gross margin.

Key distinction：
• Gross margin at the domestic main site remains very high (estimated ~70%+)
• Temu's gross margin is low (estimated ~30-40%, under the fully managed model)
• The decline in blended gross margin is a "mix effect," not a "loss of pricing power"

Change 3: Net profit falls for the first time, but FCF remains strong

Net profit fell from RMB 112.4 billion to RMB 97.8 billion (-13%), but FCF/net profit = 1.08x, indicating:

  • The profit decline is real (not an accounting adjustment)
  • But earnings quality remains high—every dollar of profit converted into cash collected.
  • The decline was mainly due to slowing revenue growth and investment in Temu.
