PDD: A value trap abandoned by the market, or a misunderstood cash machine?

A company with 30% ROE, annual FCF above RMB 100 billion, and cash on the books equal to half its market cap, trading below 10x PE. This is not a bank stock — it is Pinduoduo.

I. An absurd valuation

In July 2026, PDD's share price was $82.39.

Let us run a few quick numbers:

P/E = 8.98x.What does that mean? Coca-Cola trades at a PE of ~25x, McDonald's at ~30x, Amazon at ~40x. A company earning nearly RMB 100 billion a year is valued by the market at just 8 years of profit.

FCF yield = 18%.That means for every RMB 100 you invest, you get back RMB 18 in cash each year. In today's zero-rate world, that is close to free money.

Net cash/market cap = 50%.PDD holds RMB 416.9 billion in net cash on its balance sheet. After repaying debt with that cash, the remaining net cash equals 50% of its market cap.

You pay RMB 100 for Pinduoduo — RMB 50 buys its cash, and the other RMB 50 buys a business that earns RMB 100 billion a year. Few businesses are priced like that.

II. What is the market afraid of?

If everything is fine, why is the PE only 9x? The answer is:The market does not think everything is fine.

1. Growth is gone

Pinduoduo FY2025 revenue growth fell to 9.7%, a record low. Against 89.7% growth in 2023, a cliff-edge decline. Net profit also fell for the first time (-13%).

The concern is valid: why should a company with no growth command a high valuation?

2. Temu is a puzzle

Pinduoduo's overseas business Temu reached hundreds of billions in GMV in two years, but—No one knows whether it makes money.

Management does not disclose Temu's financials separately. Investors can only guess: is Temu running a "strategic loss" (burn cash first, profit later), or is it "losing money forever" (a fully managed model whose cost structure is structurally unprofitable)? No one can be sure.

3. Douyin enters the fray

Douyin e-commerce GMV exceeded RMB 1 trillion in 2025, expanding from "interest-based recommendations" into all categories. Douyin has what Pinduoduo wants most —User timeChinese users spend more than 2 hours a day on Douyin, but only 30 minutes on Pinduoduo.

If Douyin truly nails "low price plus content," what does Pinduoduo compete on?

III. Another angle: the undervalued hidden card

The market's negative view is justified. But it may alreadyOverpricedDone.

Card one: this is not a company that will die

In the most bearish case—Temu fails completely and domestic growth drops to zero—PDD still generates over RMB 100 billion in FCF annually, holds RMB 416.9 billion in cash, and carries zero debt.

It will not go bankrupt. It will not blow up. It will simply become a "slow-growth but steadily money-printing" machine.

In investing, "won't die" is itself a huge margin of safety.

Second trump card: efficiency is a permanent advantage

Pinduoduo serves 800 million users with 15,000 employees. Alibaba's headcount is 13 times Pinduoduo's; JD.com's is 30 times. This is not a management issue, it isGenetic issues— Pinduoduo has been an "efficiency machine" from day one.

Third card: Temu is a free call option

If Temu ultimately succeeds—even at just one-third the scale of Pinduoduo's domestic business—Pinduoduo's profit will double.

The market is currently pricing Temu atZeroThis means that if Temu shows any sign of success, the upside for the stock price is enormous.
