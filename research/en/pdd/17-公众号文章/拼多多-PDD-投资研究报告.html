III. Industry analysis
3.1 China's e-commerce market

China's e-commerce market has entered a stage of stock competition. In 2025, total online retail sales reached about RMB 15 trillion, growing 7-10%, with penetration at about 45%. The four-player landscape is broadly stable—Alibaba (~45%), JD.com (~20%), Pinduoduo (~15%) and Douyin E-commerce (~10%)—with Douyin the only competitor still gaining share.

3.2 Cross-border e-commerce market

The global cross-border e-commerce B2C market is about USD 500 billion, growing more than 20% a year. Temu's segment (the China-to-overseas fully managed model) is in a high-growth phase but faces the risk of adjustments to the US de minimis duty exemption.

VII. Growth drivers
7.1 Domestic: deeper penetration of the existing user base

User growth has peaked at 800 million, but ARPU still has room to rise. Pinduoduo is bringing in branded goods and lifting average order value through its "RMB 10 billion subsidy" program, while using AI recommendations to improve conversion efficiency. The growth model for its domestic business has shifted from user growth to growth in value per user.

7.2 Overseas: Temu's option value

Temu is the biggest growth variable today. It has entered 30+ countries within two years, with GMV surpassing USD 10 billion. If Temu can replicate the high-efficiency operations and profitability it has achieved domestically in overseas markets, it will open up growth space on par with the domestic market. The market currently prices Temu at close to zero—this is the largest source of expectation gap in investing in Pinduoduo today.

7.3 AI empowerment

Pinduoduo's distributed AI recommendation system is its core hidden asset. In AI-driven e-commerce competition, Pinduoduo has a stronger technology DNA than traditional e-commerce players (Alibaba/JD) and deeper supply-chain reach than content-driven e-commerce (Douyin).
