📝 3. Data definitions to note

  1. GMV vs revenue:Pinduoduo's platform GMV far exceeds recognized revenue (revenue consists mainly of advertising plus commissions), which is normal accounting treatment for platform-based e-commerce.
  2. Temu revenue recognition:Temu uses a full-managed model; the revenue recognition method (gross vs net) affects how results are presented
  3. Equity incentives:FY2025 SBC = CNY 7.937 billion, about 8% of net profit, within a reasonable range
  4. Investment gains and losses:Short-term investment income (interest income of RMB 25.58 billion) contributed a substantial share of profit; its sustainability warrants attention
  5. macrotrends methodology difference:Macrotrends' Cash On Hand includes cash and short-term investments, a presentation basis that differs from stockanalysis' line-item breakdown, causing a deviation of about 21% in the cash item. Core metrics such as net profit and revenue are highly consistent.
