III. Pinduoduo's position in the industry chain

Upstream advantage: C2M model

Pinduoduo's core innovation isC2M (Consumer-to-Manufacturer) model—By aggregating large volumes of orders, factories produce directly and sell directly to consumers, eliminating brand premiums and intermediary channel costs. This is the fundamental reason Pinduoduo can achieve "extreme low prices"—not by sacrificing quality, but by structurally reducing costs.

Midstream efficiency: lowest operating costs in the industry

Downstream coverage: penetration ceiling of 800 million users

Pinduoduo's user coverage has neared the ceiling of China's internet population, with growth shifting from "new users" to "purchase frequency among existing users + higher average order value."

Pinduoduo vs Douyin E-commerce

Douyin e-commerce is PinduoduoThe most direct and most dangerouscompetitors. Both are competing for the "low price" mindshare, but along different paths:

  • Pinduoduo:"I want to buy the cheap ones."— Proactive search, price sorting
  • Douyin:"It's cheap, buy it"— Recommendations drive discovery, impulse purchases

Douyin's advantage lies in user time spent far exceeding Pinduoduo's, and its recommendation algorithm may discover low-priced goods more efficiently than Pinduoduo's search model.

V. Industry-chain risks and social

Risk factors

  • Douyin e-commerce accelerates closed-loop buildout, eroding Pinduoduo's market share
  • When trade-down ends, low prices may no longer be the first choice
  • Factory capacity bottlenecks constrain the hit-product model
  • Temu cross-border logistics/customs clearance/compliance challenges

Opportunity areas

  • AI-enabled supply chain—demand forecasting, inventory matching
  • Agriculture Uptrend — Differentiated Depth in Agricultural Products
  • Cross-border e-commerce — Temu expands overseas to open up the global market
  • Deepening lower-tier markets — 400-500 million consumers in lower-tier cities

VI. Industry-level assessment

China's e-commerce has enteredThe era of competition for existing market share—Total growth fell from over 20% to below 10%. At this stage:

• WinnersIt is the platform with the highest efficiency and lowest operating costs

• LosersIt is a platform that relies on brand premium and a bloated cost structure

• WildcardContent e-commerce (Douyin) could be a game changer

At this stage, Pinduoduo is positioned as a defender, not an attacker.Its goal is to defend its position in low-price e-commerce while opening a second front through Temu. Domestic competitors are catching up, but Pinduoduo's efficiency barrier will be hard to breach in the near term.
