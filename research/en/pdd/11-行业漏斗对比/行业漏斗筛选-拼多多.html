Tier 3: Detailed analysis (300–500 words per company)

AlibabaBABA

Most complete ecosystem: e-commerce + cloud + payments + logistics

China's e-commerce veteran: Tmall/Taobao remains the largest e-commerce platform, but its share keeps declining. Its cloud business (Alibaba Cloud) ranks first in China. Valuation at PE~15x is reasonably low. Weak growth and a bloated organizational structure are the main challenges.

PinduoduoPDD

King of efficiency: ROE above 24%, FCF over RMB 100 billion

The king of low-price e-commerce, with the industry's best operating efficiency. Net cash/market cap ~50%, an extremely large margin of safety. Temu's overseas expansion opens a second growth curve. Valuation at PE~9x, extremely undervalued. The domestic ceiling and intensifying competition are the main risks.

JD.comJD

Logistics moat is very deep, but heavy-asset margins are thin

China's largest self-operated e-commerce company, with an inimitable logistics experience and high quality trust. Its asset-heavy model keeps margins thin and growth slow. Valuation at PE~12x is reasonable. Suited to stability-oriented investors.

MeituanMeituan

User stickiness is very high, but margins have long remained low

China's largest local life services platform, spanning food delivery, in-store services, travel and hotel booking, and on-demand delivery. User stickiness and purchase frequency are extremely high, but margins are thin, competition is intensifying (Douyin's in-store services), and social insurance costs are rising. Valuation at PE ~25x looks expensive.

Funnel conclusion

The best value investment in China's e-commerce sector:Pinduoduo

  1. Valuation (PE ~9x)is the lowest of the three and offers the largest margin of safety
  2. Operating efficiency (ROE ~24%)is the highest
  3. Growth potential (Temu overseas expansion)It is the most imaginative of the three.

Alibaba suits "recovery story" investors — if it can fix growth and efficiency, the current valuation is attractive. JD.com is a stable choice — low growth but steady, not suited to those chasing excess returns.
