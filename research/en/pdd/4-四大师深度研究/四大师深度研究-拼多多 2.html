The Buffett-style follow-up question: Will this moat still be there in 10 years? What could destroy it?

What could destroy it: ① a technological revolution (AI shopping assistants reshaping e-commerce); ② competitors subsidizing at even lower prices (unlimited capital); ③ regulation fundamentally changing the platform-economy business model.

  3. Douyin e-commerce's threat is underestimated— Interest-based recommendations are eroding search e-commerce's share
