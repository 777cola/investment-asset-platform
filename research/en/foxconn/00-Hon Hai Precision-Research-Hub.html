Revenue tops a trillion, profit up only 35%

📝 Conclusion: Watch and wait for the gross margin inflection point

Watch and wait for the gross margin inflection. Hon Hai's 2026Q3 revenue reached TWD 3.03 trillion, +47% YoY, with September alone topping TWD 1 trillion for the first time, making it the most direct beneficiary of the global AI rack ramp-up. Yet the same report showed gross margin of just 6.12% and net margin of 2.37% — revenue doubled, but profit grew only 35%, with the pricing gains retained upstream. At TWD 239.5 the stock trades at a market cap of about TWD 3.37 trillion and 12.6x 2026E P/E, which is not expensive in absolute terms; but the denominator is cycle-peak EPS, and free cash flow came under pressure in the first half, giving a downside/upside ratio of 33% down versus 17% up. The valuation ceiling for this business is set by gross margin, not revenue, so absent evidence of gross margin improvement, no amount of new revenue highs constitutes a buy reason. Two triggers for buying: the share price falls below TWD 180, or gross margin holds above 6.5% for two consecutive quarters.
