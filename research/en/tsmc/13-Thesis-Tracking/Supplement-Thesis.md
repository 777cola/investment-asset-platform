## 1. Point one: AI demand is still accelerating

This is the foundation of the thesis. The evidence chain has three layers: TSMC's own monthly revenue (RMB 514.806 billion in August, +53.3% YoY), customers' order behavior (HPC +20% QoQ, capacity booked through 2027), and downstream capex (the four major North American cloud providers sharply raising 2026 budgets). The three layers corroborate each other; this is not a single data point.

The falsification test is equally clear: monthly revenue turning negative on a sequential basis for two consecutive months. Note this is QoQ, not YoY. YoY is distorted by base effects; QoQ reflects the true change in demand. Historically, in October 2022, QoQ turned negative first while YoY was still growing, and the stock had already peaked.

## 2. Third takeaway: gross margin is the only weakening metric

Q2's 67.7% was a historical peak. The company's own Q3 guidance is 65%–67%, midpoint 66%. This is not a collapse; it is a pullback. There are two reasons, both "active choices": the 2nm ramp sacrifices near-term yield, and overseas fabs trade higher costs for supply-chain security.

The key question is whether it can return to 65% in 2027. If it can, pricing power remains, and 67.7% was merely cyclical fluctuation; if it cannot, or even falls below 60%, this is a structural step-down, and the valuation center must fall from 22x to 18x, corresponding to a share price near RMB 1,900. This is the single number to watch most closely in this thesis.

## III. Article 4: Conversion rate of capital expenditure

Capex of USD 60–64 billion in 2026 corresponds to revenue of roughly USD 130 billion (rough calculation), a capex/revenue ratio of nearly 50%. That ratio is high by historical standards—about 47% in 2022 and about 35% in 2019.

A high ratio is not a problem in itself, provided revenue keeps pace. The test: if 2027 revenue growth falls below 15% while capex remains above USD 60 billion, capacity has been overbuilt, and 2028 will bring falling utilization and gross margin compression.So the 2027 capex guidance matters more than 2026 revenue.

## IV. Falsification Conditions and Monitoring Calendar

Three falsification conditions; a review is triggered if any one is hit: first, quarterly gross margin falls below 60%; second, monthly revenue turns negative month-on-month for two consecutive months while capex is raised further over the same period; third, yields at the Arizona fab lag the Taiwan fabs materially (two or more generations behind), meaning overseas capacity cannot serve its "insurance" function.

Watch calendar: monthly revenue around the 10th of each month; the Q3 earnings call in mid-October (actual gross margin plus preliminary 2027 capex guidance); final 2027 capex in January 2027; whether 2nm's full-year share reaches double digits as a percentage in April 2027.

## V. The payoff structure of the thesis

Thesis holds (base case): 2027 EPS of RMB 125, 21x, RMB 2,625, +9%. Thesis disproved (bear case): EPS of RMB 95, 18x, RMB 1,710, -29%. Upside 9%, downside 29%, a 1:3 risk-reward.

This structure says one thing: the thesis not breaking does not mean you should add. The logic for holding is "stay at the table," not "double down." To add, wait for the price to price in the bear case — below RMB 2,000, at which point the risk-reward becomes better than 1:1.3.

Add a time dimension: this thesis is valid roughly through mid-2027. The reason is that three things land at once then — whether 2nm is fully loaded (determines whether gross margin can return to 65%), the 2027 capex decision (determines depreciation pressure), and the growth rate of AI capex (determines revenue). If by mid-2027 two of these three turn adverse, the thesis should be rewritten, not patched.
