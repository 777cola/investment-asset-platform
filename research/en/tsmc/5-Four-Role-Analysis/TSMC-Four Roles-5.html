Team Lead summary — TSMC

Points of divergence and adjudication across the four reports

Core view

Four independent reports agree on business quality — a top-tier score — but diverge on price. The commercial and industry perspectives argue for waiting for a pullback; the financial and risk perspectives argue for holding at the current price without adding. Verdict: hold; add only below RMB 2,000. The split reflects time horizon, not facts.

1. First point of contention: what is the calculated five-year return?

On Duan Yongping's view, the five-year annualised return is 2%–5% (neutral case, 15x exit multiple); on Buffett's, buying at 22.7x and holding long term earns "EPS growth + dividends," or 8%–10% annualised (assuming an 18–20x exit). The gap comes down to the exit multiple.

Verdict: A 15x exit is the more conservative assumption and better captures the long-term midpoint for an asset-heavy cyclical. TSMC traded at a P/E midpoint of roughly 18x over 2015–2025, and 22.7x sits at the top of that historical range. Duan Yongping's approach is therefore closer to reality; Buffett's optimism rests on "the market granting a long-term growth premium," a phenomenon that only emerged after 2020.

II. Second Point of Divergence: Gross Margin Decline — Risk or Opportunity

Through the Munger lens, the Q3 gross margin guidance pullback (66% midpoint, -1.7pct QoQ) is the first signal of a cyclical peak; through the financial lens, it is routine dilution from the 2nm ramp — spending money to buy the future.

Verdict: both are right, but on different time horizons. Near term (2–3 quarters) it is dilution, and management gave explicit reasons (2nm 3–4pct, overseas fabs 2–3pct). Medium term (2027–2028) hinges on whether gross margin recovers once the dilution passes: if gross margin returns above 65% with 2nm fully loaded, pricing power remains intact; if not, the shift is structural.

One layer deeper: the gross margin decline is not linear. 2nm dilution peaks from H2 2026 to H1 2027 (the ramp period), while overseas fab dilution intensifies year by year as Arizona Fab 2, Kumamoto Fab 2 and Dresden come online in succession. With the two overlapping, 2027 marks the gross margin trough, after which full 2nm loading and depreciation absorption could drive a 2–3 percentage point recovery. So the "below 60%" falsification threshold has a decent probability of being hit in some quarter of 2027—we need to decide in advance how to act then.

III. Third point of disagreement: should geopolitical risk be discounted

The risk lens says apply a discount; the business lens says don't abandon a good company over an unpredictable risk. This is the oldest disagreement in value investing.

Verdict: no discount, but cap the position. A discount cannot be quantified — you don't know how much to apply — whereas a position can be. Holding TSMC within a 15% single-position cap is more honest than stuffing a 10% geopolitical discount into the valuation model.

IV. Consensus view: the best AI picks-and-shovels play

The four reports agree on one fact: August 2026 revenue was RMB 514.806 billion, up 53.3% YoY — a fourth consecutive record month, with no sign that AI demand is slowing. Q2 net profit was RMB 706.56 billion, up 77.4% YoY, with EPS of RMB 27.25 — the best quarter in history.

The disagreement is only over what this good news is worth. The market's answer is 27.9x TTM and 22.7x forward — not cheap, but not crazy either. This is exactly the typical scenario for a "Hold" rating: no reason to sell, and no reason to chase.

V. Execution checklist

1. Existing positions: no action; review gross margin and capex quarterly.

2. No position: set two entry points — first tranche at RMB 2,000 (19x 2026), scaled in; second tranche at RMB 1,800 (17x).

3. The only trigger for trimming: 2027 capex raised again, plus monthly revenue negative MoM for two consecutive months.

4. Triggers for a review: quarterly gross margin below 60%, or Arizona fab yield significantly lagging Taiwan fabs.

One addition: if the Q3 earnings call gives preliminary 2027 capex guidance above USD 65 billion while Q3 actual gross margin comes in below 65% (the low end of guidance), that combination of accelerating investment and falling returns is a signal to trim, not to add. Conversely, if 2027 capex holds at USD 60 billion and gross margin holds at 66%, the 2nm ramp is going more smoothly than expected; maintain the existing position and consider adding modestly near RMB 2,100. Both branches must be decided on the day of the earnings call, not deferred to quarter-end.

Conclusion

Verdict: Hold — all five tests passed, margin of safety barely adequate. The four differ on time horizon, not on facts. Execution replaces forecasting with position size plus price levels: add in tranches below RMB 2,000; revisit the thesis if gross margin falls below 60%.
