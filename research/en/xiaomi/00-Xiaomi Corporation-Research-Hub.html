Title: Profit down 43%, share price halved: where did Xiaomi's money go?

📝 Conclusion: Watch and wait for handset gross margin to bottom out

Watch and wait for handset gross margin to bottom. Xiaomi's Q2 2026 revenue was RMB 108.9 billion, down 6.1% YoY, and adjusted net profit was RMB 6.219 billion, down 42.6% YoY. Vehicle deliveries reached 104,200 units, up 28.2% YoY, which looks like success; but average selling price per vehicle fell 9.6%, gross margin dropped from 26.4% to 19.2%, and operating loss widened to RMB 2.6 billion—the car business has gone from an "option" to a heavy-asset business, so the market has naturally switched valuation methods. Combined with a handset gross margin of 8.5%, the share price halving from 59.45 to 29.02 reflects a double hit to both EPS and the multiple. At 28x 2026E, it is not cheap; wait for gross margin to recover or for HKD 24 or below.
