Core view

Break the buy thesis into six testable assumptions and check them one by one: two have already broken (gross margin recovery, valuation safety), one is pending verification (memory cycle peaking), and three are in progress (auto scale, ecosystem monetization, buyback support).

This is not a "position tracker" but a "watchlist" — because there is no position now. Its purpose: to know the moment an assumption turns.

Two already broken: gross margin recovery and valuation safety

Assumption ①: The original projection was gross margin of around 9% for handsets in 2026Q1, with memory price increases slowing in Q2 and gross margin recovering to 10%. Actual: 8.5%, down 3.0pct YoY and still falling QoQ. This assumption has broken, meaning the timeline for "waiting for gross margin to bottom" must be pushed back by at least one quarter.

Assumption ⑥: The original projection was that after a 51% share price decline, 19.6x TTM offered a margin of safety. Actual: the TTM denominator includes two good quarters uncontaminated by memory, and on 2026 net profit of RMB 24.6 billion, the multiple is 28x. This assumption has also broken — a 51% decline still does not make it cheap.

Both assumptions breaking at once points to one thing: what the market is cutting is not the share price but earnings expectations. The share price has run ahead of expectations, but expectations are still heading lower.

The only one still standing: automotive gross margin 19.2%

This is the most critical support among the six. Although 19.2% is down 7.2pct from 26.4% last year, it remains above the industry average (most new EV makers are below 15%), showing that Xiaomi EV's BOM control and manufacturing efficiency are real.

Its fragility lies in this: this gross margin is propped up by "scale of 10.4 million units in a single quarter." If Pengcheng cuts prices again after launch to chase volume and gross margin falls below 18%, this support breaks too. The observation window is Q4 2026 to Q1 2027.

Another angle: gross profit per vehicle of RMB 4.4 million, operating loss per vehicle of RMB 2.5 million. As long as gross margin holds at 19%, annual sales above 60.0 million units would break even. So "holding 19%" is a more important precondition than "sales growth."

Three ongoing: scale, monetization, buybacks

Scale: Q2 annualized 41.7万辆, reaching 50万辆 requires 12.5万辆 in a single quarter. Pengcheng is key — if it replicates SU7's ramp curve, that arrives in H1 2027. This is the most achievable of the six.

Monetization: Ecosystem synergy (people, car, home) could in theory lift internet services revenue, but Q2 smartphone × AIoT revenue fell 11.3%, and services revenue was not disclosed separately. This cannot be verified for now; it is a "narrative" and should not be given valuation credit.

Buyback: The HKD 20 billion in Q1 was the largest on record, equal to 2.7% of market cap. Its role is to floor the stock, not to lift it. If Q3 steps up to above HKD 30 billion, it can be upgraded to a signal that "management sees severe undervaluation."

Falsification conditions: when to abandon the thesis entirely

Three red lines: breach any one and Xiaomi comes off the watchlist:

  • Handset gross margin falls below 7%: memory cost pressure has exceeded what the business can absorb, and the handset segment goes from thin profit to loss.
  • Auto gross margin falls below 15%: per-vehicle gross profit drops to just RMB 3.4万元, which cannot cover the RMB 6.9万元 fixed cost, and losses widen to 40亿元 per quarter.
  • Quarterly auto deliveries fall sequentially for two consecutive quarters: the price-for-volume strategy has failed and the scale story is over.

None of the three is a falling share price; all three are a deteriorating business. You can add to a falling share price; you can only walk away from a deteriorating business. That distinction is the core of discipline.

Watch calendar: what to look for over the next six months

October 2026: Q3 memory contract price negotiations conclude, setting Q4 handset costs; Pengcheng monthly delivery data.

November 2026: Q3 results (key) — watch three things: handset gross margin, auto gross margin, quarterly auto loss. This report is the first test of the "Under Review" rating.

December 2026–January 2027: Double 11 and Chinese New Year sales, to verify whether IoT stops declining; a full quarter of Pengcheng deliveries.

March 2027: Full-year results and 2027 guidance, setting the timetable for "auto turnaround." If two of the three Q3 metrics improve, the rating can be upgraded from "Under Review"; if none improve, consider removing it from the list.

Conclusion

Hypothesis tracking conclusion: two of the six have broken, one holds but is fragile, three are in progress. The "handset gross margin has bottomed" hypothesis has broken, so the rating is "Watch" not "Buy"; the "valuation is safe" hypothesis has broken, so the stock cannot be bought at the current price. Only one thing matters over the next six months: the handset gross margin in the November Q3 results. Back above 10% and the hypothesis is rebuilt; below 7% and it is abandoned outright.
