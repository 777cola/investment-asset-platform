Core view

Eight pieces read, distilled onto one page: what to buy (assets plus options, not growth), at what price (HKD 63.55, roughly net cash value, a reasonable-to-discounted valuation), on what signal (advertising within -10%, or cloud at RMB 10 billion with profits turning positive), and how much position (0–6%). A decision framework earns its keep not by predicting but by ensuring no one improvises on the spot.

Of the seven items, three are "Yes", three are "No" and one is "To be determined", corresponding to a rating of "Watch". If "Yes" is ≥4 items and "No" is ≤1 item, the rating can be upgraded to "Buy".
Question one: what to buy—assets, or growth

This question determines the valuation method.If you buy growth, you must value AI cloud's RMB 50 billion of 2027 revenue at 15–20x PS, implying a market cap well above the current level—but only if the growth rate holds.If you buy assets, you must calculate net cash, the discount and liquidation value.

This report takes the latter approach, for two reasons: the growth piece (AI cloud) carries only a 20%–30% gross margin and generates no profit, while the asset piece (RMB 276.6 billion) is real and verifiable. Buying "unprofitable high growth" is inferior to buying "assets that certainly exist but trade at a discount."This is not a bearish call on AI; it is an acknowledgment that AI cloud is not yet profit.

Second question: what price — is HKD 63.55 expensive?

SOTP: net cash and investments at a RMB 160 billion discount (about HKD 66.5/share) + core business at 15x 2027 net profit of RMB 9–11 billion (about RMB 135–165 billion, HKD 46–57/share) + Robotaxi option (HKD 0–10 billion, HKD 0–3.4/share).

Summing the three gives a neutral range of HKD 112–127? Wrong — the "discount" must be subtracted here. The market applies a 30%–50% holding-company discount to assets of this kind (the cash sits in someone else's hands, pays no dividend and cannot be controlled), so RMB 160 billion counts for only RMB 100–120 billion. That is how the neutral-case HKD 85 is calculated: the assets are not worthless, you just cannot get at them.

At HKD 63.55, the stock therefore sits below the value "if management were willing to return the money" (about HKD 110) and above the value "if the cash is burned" (about HKD 45). The range in between is the price range for "watch".

Question three: what signal — two hard indicators

Signal one: online marketing YoY decline narrows to within -10%. This metric is reported every quarter, cannot be faked and cannot be dressed up by narrative. Narrowing to -10% means total revenue can turn positive and the valuation shifts from a "recession discount" back to a "normal internet" multiple.

Signal two: AI cloud quarterly revenue exceeds RMB 10 billion and the cloud segment's operating profit turns positive. Note this is the "cloud segment", not overall adjusted profit—the overall figure mixes in advertising and interest income, obscuring cloud's true profitability. A positive cloud segment profit means this capital-intensive business can fund itself.

If either signal materialises, build a 3% position; if both materialise, add to 5%–6%. Any other reason—cheap, fallen too far, the great AI era, a China ADR rebound—does not justify buying. This is discipline, not judgment.

Question four: when to sell

Sell criteria matter more than buy criteria — they guard against holding too long. Three rules:

  • Thesis-invalidation sell: any one of advertising -30%, gross margin below 30%, capex back above RMB 10 billion, or still no return-of-capital action by mid-2027 triggers a full exit.
  • Valuation sell: stock back above HKD 110 (close to the full value of "management repaying capital") — halve the position; above HKD 130 (JPMorgan's target price) — exit entirely, because by then the "discount repair" is complete and what remains is a bet on AI winning.
  • Time-based sell: three consecutive quarters with no signal — clear the position and stop tracking. Opportunity cost is also a cost.

No price stop-loss. A stock at 0.52x PB with a price stop-loss means selling assets to the market at a discount. A stop-loss should stop "the thesis being wrong," not "the quote falling."

Finally: where does this framework most easily break down?

To be honest: this framework is most likely to fail on "Signal One." A narrowing decline in advertising could simply reflect a base effect — last year's base was low, so this year's YoY figure naturally looks good even as the absolute amount keeps falling. So require confirmation for two consecutive quarters, and look at the absolute amount at the same time.

The second failure point is "dividends." A token dividend (say HKD 0.1 per share) may be read by the market as "starting to return money," but its practical significance is zero. Set the standard in stone: only an annual dividend yield above 2%, or a buyback mandate expanded to more than 5% of market cap, counts.

A framework's value is not in making you right, but in making you lose less when you are wrong. The 0–2% observation position is exactly this "lose less" design.

Conclusion

Decision framework, finalised: buy the assets, not the growth; HKD 63.55 is the watch price; two hard signals; 0–6% position; four falsification-based exits; no price stop-loss. Eight long pieces later, the conclusion is unchanged from the first: Baidu is worth this price, but not a higher one; wait for it to prove it can earn more, or that it will return the money to shareholders.
