📝 Conclusion: Watch and wait for the inflection point

Watch, wait for the inflection point. Baidu's 2026Q3 was a "structure shifting, total unchanged" report: total revenue RMB 30.807 billion, -1.1% YoY; AI cloud infrastructure RMB 8.2 billion, +94%; online marketing RMB 11.9 billion, -22% — the two exactly offset. Net profit attributable to shareholders of RMB 1.395 billion looks like a turnaround, but is actually a high-base effect from last year's RMB 16.190 billion impairment; non-GAAP net profit RMB 2.395 billion, -36% YoY. The stock at HKD 63.55, -60.4% from its 52-week high, market cap about HKD 168 billion, against cash and investments of RMB 276.6 billion and a P/B of 0.52x — the market is pricing the core business at close to zero. Cheap is real, and so is the shrinking business. Wait for one of two signals: the YoY decline in advertising narrowing to within -10%, or AI cloud quarterly revenue exceeding RMB 10 billion with operating profit turning positive.
