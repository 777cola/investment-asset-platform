Core view

Four independent perspectives, one conclusion — do not buy — each for entirely different reasons. That divergence is itself the signal: when four completely different analytical approaches point to the same action, that action is usually sound. What follows sets out three substantive disagreements and how we resolve them, then the final course of action.

Divergence one: is the advertising decline structural or cyclical?

Both the commercial and industry perspectives call it "structural"; the financial perspective pushes back: if it were structural, how did the general business still reach RMB 25 billion, up 1% YoY?

Verdict: structural. That 1% growth was carried by AI (RMB 13.2 billion, +37%); strip out AI and the traditional business was shrinking. The cleaner evidence: AI-native marketing came in at RMB 2.4 billion, down 15% YoY—even advertising in its new form is falling, which points not to advertisers' budget cycles but to ad slots themselves disappearing. AI search returns answers, users stop clicking links, and click inventory evaporates. The mechanism has nothing to do with macro and everything to do with user habits—and is therefore irreversible.

Disagreement 2: How long can AI cloud growth of +94% last?

The industry view is the most bearish: cloud is a scale business, and both Alibaba Cloud and Volcano Engine are an order of magnitude larger than Baidu. Once a price war starts, the 94% margin will likely fall below 40%. The financial view is relatively optimistic: GPU cloud +191%, +43% QoQ, reflects demand-side scrambling for capacity, not price cuts to buy volume.

Verdict: it can hold above 50% through the first half of 2027; after that, watch the price war. In 2026, industry-wide capex rises and HBM and advanced nodes stay tight, so compute remains in short supply. Baidu's Kunlun chips secure part of its supply, so it will not fall behind near term. But the cloud moat is scale and power costs, not technology—which is why the base case gives AI cloud only RMB 50 billion of revenue in 2027 (about 40% annualized growth).In other words, we do not dispute its current high growth; we just will not give it a long-term multiple.

Divergence 3: Can net cash translate into shareholder returns?

The financial view frames this as the biggest highlight: RMB 276.6 billion in cash and investments against a market cap of RMB 153 billion. If the board nods, buybacks plus dividends could halve the discount. The risk view cautions that Baidu has never paid a dividend, that buybacks have stayed "small-scale and routine", and that cumulative buybacks since 2021 have consistently remained below 5% of market cap.

Verdict: uncertain, but it is the only catalyst that can materialize within 12 months. Hong Kong Economic Journal reported on 2026/11/15 that the company "expects to announce its first dividend before year-end". If it lands, the nature changes — from "a transformation story stock" to "a value stock starting to return cash". I put the probability at 50%: the motive exists (share price at a five-year low, PB 0.52), but the temptation to keep the cash on the balance sheet for AI is greater. So this constitutes a reason to watch, not a reason to buy.

What they agree on: all four perspectives converge on three points

Only three points of disagreement and four of consensus — all the more reason to set them down. First, the assets are real. None of the four perspectives questions the RMB 276.6 billion in cash and investments, and none flags solvency risk. Second, the business is shrinking. None treats the advertising decline as temporary, and none believes AI cloud can close the gross-margin gap. Third, this is not a buying point. All four vote "watch."

The fourth consensus is implicit: if you must buy, you are buying "assets + optionality," not "growth." The position must therefore be small, the horizon long and the triggers hard. Any heavy position justified by "the great AI era" mistakes the option for the main course.

Disposition: Watch, two triggers

Rating: "Watch, wait for the inflection"; share price HKD 63.55. Trigger one: the YoY decline in online marketing narrows to within -10%, confirmed over two consecutive quarters. Trigger two: quarterly revenue from AI cloud infrastructure exceeds RMB 10 billion and the cloud segment turns an operating profit. On either trigger alone, build a 3% position; on both together, add to 5%–6%.

Falsification conditions (exit on trigger, no price stop-loss): the advertising decline widens beyond -30%; or quarterly capex returns above RMB 10 billion while cloud revenue growth falls below 40%; or no dividend/buyback step-up is announced by mid-2027. Watch position cap 2%; whether to hold it depends on the portfolio's cash ratio, and passing on it is no loss.

Conclusion

TeamLead final ruling: Watch, wait for the inflection point, rating unchanged. All four perspectives agree that the asset is cheap and the business is shrinking; they diverge only on timing and magnitude, which does not change the action. At HKD 63.55, a HKD 168 billion market cap and 0.52x PB, this is a watch price, not a buy price. The only legitimate reason to buy is "advertising stops falling" or "cloud profit turns positive", not "it has fallen too much".
