Core view

The most common mistake buyers of Baidu make is mistaking "cheap" for "safe". The real risk is not another 30% drop in the share price, but three slower-moving things: an accelerating decline in advertising, cash being slowly eaten away by capex, and no shareholder returns for five or ten years. The first two affect intrinsic value; the third determines whether you can wait for that value to be realized. Together, these three are the reason a 0.52x PB exists.

Risk one: advertising is not declining at a steady pace — it is accelerating

The most dangerous thing about the -22% figure is not its magnitude but the shape of the trend. Search advertising decline has a self-reinforcing mechanism: advertisers cut spending → ad slot quality falls → user experience deteriorates → more users shift to AI products → fewer ad slots. Once this loop starts spinning, the decline goes from -22% to -30%, rather than gradually converging to -10%.

The signal to watch is simple: online marketing year-over-year in the quarterly earnings. If Q4 is -25% or worse, the loop is accelerating and the base case needs reassessment. What makes this risk so damaging is that it is compounding—losing 25% a year leaves advertising revenue at just 42% after three years, and AI cloud cannot fill that gap.

Risk 2: Cash is not a safety cushion; it may be fuel

RMB 276.6 billion in cash and investments looks like an iron floor, but it is not frozen. Capital expenditure this quarter was RMB 7.3 billion, operating cash flow RMB 2.8 billion, a net outflow of RMB 4.5 billion. If the cloud business keeps expanding and capex returns above RMB 10 billion, RMB 30 billion of cash burns in a single year—gone in nine years. This is certainly not the base case, but it shows that "net cash" is a dynamic number, not a static asset.

In Li Lu's framework, this situation calls for valuing "residual value" rather than "book value": if the cash is put into compute assets returning 5%, then RMB 1 of cash is worth only RMB 0.5. That is why net cash in the SOTP is discounted to RMB 160 billion (rather than RMB 276.6 billion).

Risk 3: Governance structure determines how long "cheap" can last

Li Yanhong controls more than half the voting power through Class B super-voting rights while holding less than 20% of the equity. The upside of this structure is strategic stability—over two decades, Baidu's direction has not swung back and forth under capital-market pressure; the downside is that minority shareholders have no way to push for any change in capital allocation. If they want a dividend, they can only wait for the board to come around on its own.

The track record is not good: a secondary listing in Hong Kong raised capital in 2021, followed by five years without a single dividend; buybacks are perpetually "small-scale and routine," with USD 373 million during the year amounting to less than 2% of the USD 21.5 billion market cap. This approach works fine in a growth phase; in a decline phase it is the main drag on valuation. Governance risk is not "the company will do bad things," it is "the company will not do what is best for shareholders."

Risk 4: The structural discount on China ADRs will not disappear on its own

There is another layer of risk unrelated to company operations: the overall discount on China concept stocks. The Hang Seng Tech Index has fallen more than 25% in 2026, Tencent and SMIC down more than 30%, Alibaba, Meituan and Xiaomi down more than 20%. In this environment, no matter how good a stock's fundamentals are, it will be systematically discounted. Of Baidu's -60.4% decline, part is the company's own problems, and part is the whole sector's problems.

This layer of risk cannot be used as a reason to buy ("China concept stocks will rebound" is an unverifiable bet), but it must be used to set a position cap. With sector beta this high, a single-stock position of 5% is already the ceiling.

Conclusion

Risk perspective conclusion: This is an investment with a floor on the downside, conditions on the upside, and a time cost in between. The downside floor rests on RMB 276.6 billion in cash; the upside is conditional, requiring either advertising to stop declining or cloud profit to turn positive; time cost is the biggest enemy—the governance structure means the discount could persist for three to five years. Disposition recommendation: keep an observation position of no more than 2%; the falsification conditions are advertising decline widening beyond -30% or capex returning above RMB 10 billion in a single quarter—exit on trigger.
