## Notes

This module is the summary page for the four-role parallel analysis. The full text of the four independent perspectives is in pages; this page lists only the points of divergence and the verdicts. Basis for the verdicts: the segment data from the 2026Q3 financial report, management remarks at the 15 November 2026 earnings call, and the 18 November 2026 JPMorgan research report (target price HKD 130; AI cloud passed the return test, advertising did not).

## Divergence one: is the advertising decline structural or cyclical?

Both the commercial and industry perspectives judge it "structural," while the financial perspective raises a counterargument: if it is structural, why can the general business still do RMB 25 billion, +1% YoY?

Verdict: structural. The +1% in the general business is pulled up by the AI business (RMB 13.2 billion, +37%); strip out AI and the traditional business is in negative growth. The more direct evidence is structural: online marketing RMB 11.9 billion, -22%, AI-native marketing RMB 2.4 billion, -15% — even "advertising in new forms" is falling, which shows this is not a matter of advertisers' budget cycles but that ad slots themselves are disappearing. AI search gives answers, users do not click links, and click inventory is gone. This mechanism has nothing to do with macro.

## Disagreement two: how long can AI cloud's +94% growth hold

Most bearish from an industry perspective: cloud is a scale business, and both Alibaba Cloud and Volcano Engine operate at an order of magnitude larger scale than Baidu; once a price war starts, Baidu's 94% will fall below 40%. Relatively optimistic from a financial perspective: GPU cloud +191%, +43% QoQ, indicating demand is still chasing capacity rather than volume being bought with price cuts.

Verdict: it can stay above 50% before the first half of 2027, after which the price war decides. The reason is that China's AI compute remains in short supply (industry-wide capex rises in 2026, HBM and advanced process nodes are tight), and Baidu's Kunlun chips secure part of its supply, so it will not fall behind in the near term. But the moat in cloud is scale and electricity prices, not technology—once Alibaba Cloud and Volcano cut prices, Baidu's RMB 8.2 billion/quarter growth rate will be hard to defend. This is also why the neutral scenario assigns AI cloud only RMB 50 billion of revenue in 2027 (implying roughly 40% annualized growth).

## Divergence 3: Can net cash be converted into shareholder returns?

The financial perspective sees this as the biggest highlight: RMB 276.6 billion in cash and investments against a market cap of RMB 153 billion — if the board nods, buybacks plus dividends could halve the discount. The risk perspective cautions: Baidu has never paid a dividend in its history, buybacks have remained "small-scale and routine," and cumulative buybacks since 2021 have consistently stayed below 5% of market cap.

Verdict: uncertain, but this is the only catalyst that can materialize within 12 months. Hong Kong Economic Journal reported on 2026/11/15 that the company "expects to announce its first dividend before year-end"; if it lands, the nature changes — from "a transformation story stock" to "a value stock starting to return cash." I put the probability at 50%: management has the motive (share price at a five-year low, PB 0.52), but the temptation to keep cash on the balance sheet for AI investment is stronger. This is uncertain, so it does not constitute a reason to buy — only a reason to watch.

## The unanimous conclusion: nobody wants to buy now

Four perspectives, four independent conclusions: the business view says "the business is being replaced, not worth paying a premium for"; the financial view says "assets are cheap but with conditions"; the industry view says "growth is unsustainable"; the risk view says "cash is the only safety cushion, and it could also become fuel." Four arguments, one action: don't buy, but watch.

What to watch — the four perspectives together produce one checklist: advertising year-on-year (monthly third-party data + quarterly earnings), AI cloud segment operating profit (not overall adjusted profit), the first dividend announcement, and Apollo Go's per-ride cost (safety-driver-to-vehicle ratio). If any one of the four moves in a favorable direction, the valuation shifts one notch from "asset discount" toward "value re-rating."

Positioning: observation position 0–2% (optional), add to 5% when trigger conditions are met, cap at 6%. No stop-loss price; instead set falsification conditions — if the advertising decline widens beyond -30%, or capex returns to above RMB 10 billion in a single quarter, exit outright.
