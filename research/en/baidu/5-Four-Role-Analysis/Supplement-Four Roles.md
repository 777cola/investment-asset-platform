# Baidu（9888.HK）— Four Roles Supplement

## Notes

This module summarises the four-role parallel analysis. Full text of the four independent perspectives sits in pages; this page lists only points of divergence and verdicts. Verdicts rest on segment data from the 2026Q3 financial report, management remarks at the 15 November 2026 earnings call, and the 18 November 2026 JPMorgan research report (target price HKD 130; AI cloud passed the return test, advertising did not).

## Divergence one: is the advertising decline structural or cyclical?

Both the commercial and industry perspectives call it "structural"; the financial perspective offers a counterargument: if it were structural, how could the general business still deliver RMB 25 billion, up 1% YoY?

Verdict: structural. That 1% growth in the general business is the AI business (RMB 13.2 billion, +37%) pulling it up; strip out AI and the traditional business is shrinking. The more direct evidence is structural: online marketing RMB 11.9 billion, -22%, AI-native marketing RMB 2.4 billion, -15% — even "advertising in new forms" is falling, which shows this is not a matter of advertisers' budget cycles but of ad slots themselves disappearing. AI search gives answers, users do not click links, and click inventory is gone. This mechanism has nothing to do with the macro environment.

## Disagreement two: how long can AI cloud's +94% growth hold

The industry view is the most bearish: cloud is a scale business, and both Alibaba Cloud and Volcano Engine operate at a scale an order of magnitude larger than Baidu. Once a price war starts, Baidu's 94% will fall below 40%. The financial view is relatively optimistic: GPU cloud +191%, +43% QoQ, indicating demand is still chasing capacity rather than volume bought through price cuts.

Verdict: it can hold above 50% until the first half of 2027; after that, watch the price war.China's AI compute remains in short supply (industry-wide capex rises in 2026, HBM and advanced process nodes are tight), and Baidu's Kunlun chips secure part of its supply, so it will not fall behind in the near term. But the moat in cloud is scale and electricity prices, not technology—once Alibaba Cloud and Volcano push prices down, Baidu's RMB 8.2 billion/quarter will struggle to defend its growth rate. This is also why the neutral scenario assigns AI cloud only RMB 50 billion of revenue in 2027 (implying roughly 40% annualized growth).

## Divergence 3: Can net cash be converted into shareholder returns?

The bull case centres on this as the single biggest catalyst: RMB 276.6 billion of cash and investments against a RMB 153 billion market cap — with board approval, buybacks plus dividends could halve the discount. The bear case cautions that Baidu has never paid a dividend, that buybacks have remained "small-scale and routine," and that cumulative buybacks since 2021 have consistently stayed below 5% of market cap.

Verdict: uncertain, but the only catalyst deliverable within 12 months. Hong Kong Economic Journal reported on 2026/11/15 that the company "expects to announce its first dividend before year-end." If it happens, the nature of the story changes — from a "story-driven turnaround stock" to a "value stock starting to return cash." I put the probability at 50%: management has the motive (share price at a five-year low, PB 0.52), but the temptation to keep cash on the balance sheet for AI investment is stronger. Uncertain, so not a reason to buy — only a reason to watch.

## The unanimous conclusion: nobody wants to buy now

Four independent conclusions: business — "the business is being replaced, not worth paying a premium for"; financials — "assets are cheap but conditional"; industry — "growth is unsustainable"; risk — "cash is the only safety cushion, and it could also become fuel." Four views, one action: don't buy, but watch.

What to watch — together the four perspectives produce a checklist: advertising YoY (monthly third-party data plus quarterly results), AI cloud segment operating profit (not overall adjusted profit), the first dividend announcement, and Apollo Go's per-ride cost (safety-monitor-to-vehicle ratio). If any one of the four moves favourably, the valuation shifts one notch from "asset discount" toward "value re-rating."

Positioning: observation position 0–2% (optional); add to 5% if trigger conditions are met; cap at 6%. No stop-loss price; instead set falsification conditions — if the advertising decline widens beyond -30%, or capex returns above RMB 10 billion in a single quarter, exit outright.
