Financial analysis (Buffett's perspective) — Baidu

Core view

The most misread part of this report is the "return to profit." Net profit attributable to shareholders was RMB 1.395 billion, versus a loss of RMB 11.232 billion a year earlier, which looks like a sharp improvement; but last year's RMB 11.2 billion loss included an RMB 16.190 billion impairment on long-lived assets.Strip out the base effect, and non-GAAP net profit was RMB 2.395 billion, down 36% YoY, with an adjusted operating margin of 5.6%. The real picture: profit is shrinking, there is simply no more impairment noise.

Income statement: the return to profit is an accounting phenomenon, not an operating improvement

Net profit attributable to shareholders of RMB 1.395 billion looks like a "turnaround to profit" on its own, but viewed across two years it reflects a "low base." The RMB 11.232 billion loss in Q3 2025 stemmed mainly from a RMB 16.190 billion impairment of long-lived assets—a one-off absent this year, which is why the year-on-year comparison looks sharply better.

The real operating profit metrics are: operating profit of RMB 630 million, a 2.0% margin; adjusted operating profit of RMB 1.7 billion, a 5.6% margin; non-GAAP net profit of RMB 2.395 billion, down 36% year on year.All three point to the same thing: the core business is shrinking, and not slowly. For a company with annual revenue on the order of RMB 120 billion, a single-quarter operating margin of 2% is not healthy by the standards of any industry.

Gross margin and expenses: savings subsidised capex

Gross margin 35.4% (rough calculation), down 15 points from around 50% three years ago. The cause is not a failed price increase but a changed revenue mix: low-margin cloud rose from single digits to 27% (82/308.07), while high-margin advertising fell from over 70% to 39%. Once the mix shifts, gross margin cannot go back—unless scale effects in cloud can lift gross margin above 40%, which is not visible for now.

The expense side pulled off a neat reallocation: selling and administrative expenses RMB 5.5 billion, down 17% year on year; R&D expenses RMB 4.8 billion, down 7% year on year. Together the two saved about RMB 1.5 billion, exactly offsetting the pressure from a 9% rise in cost of sales. But there is a floor—cutting R&D by 7% is already damaging long-term competitiveness, and cutting further would be killing the goose that lays the golden eggs. So this reallocation room can only last a year or two.

Cash flow and assets: the only hard asset, and the only hope

Operating cash flow was RMB 2.8 billion, positive, showing the business still generates cash rather than surviving on financing. Capex was RMB 7.3 billion, down sharply from RMB 11.4 billion in the second quarter, and free cash flow was negative RMB 4.5 billion (rough calculation). Cash and investments totaled RMB 276.6 billion, 1.8x the market cap of RMB 153 billion.

This is the kind of balance sheet Buffett would like: net cash near twice the market cap, PB 0.52, no debt-servicing pressure whatsoever. He would also certainly point out the problem: over RMB 200 billion of assets producing only a 1.9% ROE (annualized single-quarter net profit ÷ implied net assets of RMB 294.2 billion), a sign of extremely low capital allocation efficiency. Parking the money in wealth management products would earn a higher yield than that.

Return: a company with RMB 120 billion revenue cannot earn RMB 6 billion

Annualize the single-quarter figures: revenue of about RMB 123.2 billion, net profit attributable to shareholders of about RMB 5.58 billion, net margin 4.5%. That earnings power against a market cap of HKD 168 billion gives a PE of 19.7x — not cheap, only "cheap-looking." What is genuinely cheap is the 0.52 PB, because net assets are stuffed with over RMB 200 billion of cash and investments, not operating assets.

So the correct financial framing is: on earnings power, Baidu is not cheap; on assets, Baidu is very cheap. Both statements hold at once, depending on whether you are buying "profit" or "assets." Profit buyers should wait for AI cloud profit to turn positive; asset buyers should wait for management to start returning cash. Both paths need a catalyst — that is the financial rationale for "watch."

Conclusion

Financial perspective conclusion: Balance sheet grade A, income statement grade C. Net cash at 1.8x market cap and PB of 0.52 form a hard floor; but ROE of 1.9%, operating margin of 2.0% and FCF of -RMB 4.5 billion show operating quality below grade B. In Buffett's framework, this is a cigar butt — cheap assets, deteriorating business. You can take one puff, but you must judge precisely when to throw it away. The trigger to buy is not a lower valuation, but an inflection point in the income statement.
