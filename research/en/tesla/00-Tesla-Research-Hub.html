📝 Conclusion: Watch, don't chase the rally

Watch, don't chase. Tesla 2026Q2 turned 480,000 deliveries into USD 28.2 billion in revenue, its fastest growth in three years; but net profit fell 5% and blended gross margin dropped to 16.9%, showing the growth was bought. The current USD 374 corresponds to a market cap of about USD 1.48 trillion, of which the auto business is worth USD 450 billion at most, with the rest resting entirely on options on Robotaxi and Optimus. A good company is not the same as a good price — wait until below USD 300, or until FSD revenue is disclosed as a separate line in the financials, before discussing a purchase.
