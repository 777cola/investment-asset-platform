Tracking conclusion

The Hold rating rests on five testable assumptions: cloud growth is sustainable, cloud margins are sustainable, AI applications will narrow losses, e-commerce monetisation rate will stop deteriorating, and capex will decelerate. Only the first two have data support, and that from a single quarter; the remaining three are entirely unverified.

This is not a bearish call but a case of insufficient evidence, which is why the tracking table matters more than the conclusion: each assumption carries an explicit verification metric and falsification line, and the November earnings report will test three of them at once.

Hypotheses 1 and 2: the cloud story, half supported by the evidence

Cloud is the whole investment case this round (48% of the neutral valuation), and it needs two things to hold at once: growth does not falter and profit does not collapse. Growth has data (RMB 48.44 billion, +45%) and margin has one quarter of data (11.6%, +133%), but both rest on a single quarter of sample.

Verification is simple: FY2027Q2 (released in November) — watch two numbers: is growth ≥40%, and is EBITA margin ≥12%? If both are met, the assumption holds, the cloud multiple rises from 6x PS to 8x and the target price goes from HKD 129 to above HKD 150; if either misses, the assumption is void, the multiple reverts to 4x and the target price falls below HKD 95.

Falsification line: quarterly growth <30%, or EBITA margin <9%. Once either number appears, cloud reverts from a "growth asset" to a "heavy asset" and the entire SOTP must be recalculated.

Assumption 3: AI applications will narrow losses — currently zero evidence

This is the most fragile assumption of all, and the current evidence points the other way: a RMB 13.86 billion loss, a -415% loss ratio and a 330% YoY widening. Management offered no path to narrowing losses, no timetable and no target loss ratio.

What would count as "starting to narrow losses"? Three signals: 1) a sequential decline in the quarterly loss, even just 5%; 2) the loss ratio narrowing from -415% to within -300%; 3) revenue growth rising from +16% to above +30%, evidence that commercialisation is scaling. Two of the three must appear before this segment can be valued at 0x rather than -3x.

Falsification line: the quarterly loss exceeds RMB 16 billion, or the loss ratio widens further to -500%. That would signal commercialisation is failing outright to keep pace with investment, and the multiple should move from 3x to 5x, cutting the target price by a further HKD 12. If by the end of fiscal 2027 (March 2027) the loss still shows no sequential narrowing, this assumption should be judged "permanently invalid" and treated as a pure expense in valuation.

Assumptions 4 and 5: e-commerce and capex, two pieces of counter-evidence

E-commerce monetisation rate: Customer management revenue fell 7% as reported and rose 1% like-for-like; neither basis points to a recovery. We validate this assumption on two consecutive quarters of like-for-like growth, with growth above 3% (meaningful only if it keeps pace with inflation). Falsification line: two consecutive quarters of like-for-like decline, or China e-commerce revenue decline widening beyond -10%. That would signal material thinning of the core business moat and require the e-commerce 9x EBITA multiple to de-rate to 7x.

Capex: This is the only assumption where current evidence contradicts the thesis. We assume capex decelerates; actual capex was RMB 67.68 billion, +75%, and management gave no capex ceiling for FY2027. Validation metric: a quarter-on-quarter decline in quarterly capex, or an explicit annual ceiling from management on the earnings call.

Falsification line: capex rising quarter-on-quarter for three consecutive quarters, with the free cash flow gap exceeding -RMB 50 billion. That would make the "asset-heavy transformation" irreversible, shift the valuation framework entirely toward an infrastructure stock, move the multiple centre down 20% and take the target price from 129 to below 105.

Watch calendar: what to watch over the next 12 months

October–November 2026 (FY2027Q2 results): the year's single most important checkpoint, testing three hypotheses — cloud growth and margins, QoQ losses in AI applications, and like-for-like customer management growth. January–February 2027 (FY2027Q3 results): watch for capex peaking and narrowing instant retail losses. March 2027 (FY2027 annual report): watch full-year free cash flow, a possible second round of financing, and the scale of depreciation.

Beyond earnings, three unscheduled events warrant attention: 1) expiry of the 90-day lock-up on placed shares (late November) — concentrated selling by placees would create overhang; 2) changes to Nvidia export controls, which directly affect the substitution value of Pingtouge; 3) Alibaba Cloud's quarterly share data (Omdia, every quarter) — if 38.1% falls below 35%, the cloud pricing logic changes.

The point of writing down the calendar: do not change your judgment because the share price moves; change it because the data arrives. The next data point lands in November; the moves in the two months in between are noise.

Conclusion

Five assumptions: two partly confirmed, three blank (one reversed). Tracking discipline: the November earnings report provides a one-time verification; downgrade if it falls short of expectations, and do not change the call because of a share-price rebound. The buy trigger is unchanged: HKD 95 or below, or cloud margin at 12% or above for two consecutive quarters with AI losses narrowing quarter on quarter.
