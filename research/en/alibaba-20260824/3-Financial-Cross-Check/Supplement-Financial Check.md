# Alibaba（09988）— Financial Check Supplement

- Research date：2026/08/24
- Price：112.5 HKD
- Market cap：Approximately HKD 2.30 trillion (approximately 20.46 billion shares after placement)

## Notes

Verification method: company announcements/press releases are primary sources; sell-side commentary and financial media are secondary sources; self-calculated values are derived sources. Deviation = |A−B| / A. PE basis note: GAAP net profit attributable to shareholders includes impairments and one-off fines, while Non-GAAP excludes share-based compensation; the two must not be mixed. This report uses SOTP for valuation and does not use PE to set target prices.

## Three items with zero deviation: revenue figures can be used with confidence

Revenue, cloud revenue and cloud EBITA — the gaps between company announcements and sell-side commentary all fall within 0.05%, a rounding-level difference. This alignment says two things: first, the new segment definitions were already aligned at the time of release, with no sell-side adjustments; second, the cloud figures are the most closely watched item this quarter and have been checked repeatedly, giving them the highest credibility.

This also explains why the core conclusions of this report can rest on cloud: RMB 48.44 billion, +45%, EBITA RMB 5.63 billion, +133% — four numbers corroborated across three sources. By contrast, the RMB 13.86 billion loss at AI Labs and Applications has only the company announcement as its source; sell-side commentary mostly just repeats the -415% loss margin without independent modeling — this is a single-source number and should be used more conservatively.

## Deviation one: 11.6% or 12%—rounding, but it affects the valuation anchor

The company announcement states "EBITA margin improved to 12%", but 56.28 ÷ 484.37 gives 11.62%. A 0.4 percentage point gap looks like rounding, but this line is the valuation anchor. Pricing cloud at 12% implies a steady-state margin assumption 3% higher than 11.6%; if cloud revenue annualizes to RMB 193.7 billion and the margin differs by 0.4pp, that corresponds to an EBITA difference of RMB 770 million, or roughly RMB 11.5 billion of market cap at 15x EBITA and about HKD 0.6 per share. It does not change the conclusion, but the two cannot be used interchangeably in a research report.

Approach: use 11.6% throughout the tables and body text (it can be recomputed), and note "announcement states 12%" only when quoting the announcement verbatim.

## Deviation 2: customer management -7% vs +1% reflects a definitional difference, not fraud

This is the most misread set of numbers this quarter. The segment table shows customer management revenue of RMB 82.55 billion, down 7% YoY; another section of the same announcement says "customer management revenue grew 1% YoY on a like-for-like basis." The gap comes from the 2025 business reclassification — some technical services previously booked under customer management, along with commission revenue tied to instant retail, were reclassified, scrambling the base.

My approach: state both in the body, treating -7% as the "reported basis" and +1% as the "true operating basis." In my judgment, weight -7% more heavily: if like-for-like growth really were +1%, the company would describe it as "growth" rather than "decline," and management's commentary on e-commerce at the earnings call was cautious. The safe read: customer management revenue is near zero growth, and neither basis supports the claim that "e-commerce has already recovered."

## Deviation 3 and 4: the benchmark for placement discount and dilution ratio

Two discount figures are circulating: 8.4% and 3.6%. The 8.4% uses the August 21 close of HKD 123.0 as the base (112.70 ÷ 123.0 − 1 = -8.37%); the 3.6% uses the placement "reference price" as the base, and the reference price is typically the volume-weighted average price over the trading days before pricing, not the closing price. I use 8.4% because it reflects the actual price gap borne by existing shareholders—the August 24 close of HKD 112.5 has already fallen below HKD 112.70.

The dilution ratio works the same way: 710 million shares ÷ 19.89 billion shares before the placement = 3.57%, ÷ 20.46 billion shares after the placement = 3.47%. The announcement's statement that the shares represent "approximately 3.57% of the total number of issued shares after completion" reflects the flawed intuition of putting the new share capital in the denominator; the rigorous calculation is 3.47%. This report uses 3.47% throughout, because the valuation uses the post-placement share count.

## The only thing that doesn't line up: PE, so only a range is given

The 31x in the working papers is back-solved: HKD 2.30 trillion market cap ÷ 0.92 ÷ RMB 72.54 billion TTM net profit attributable to shareholders = 29.2x, a 5.8% gap. The difference may come from the exchange rate (0.92 is the August 2026 HKD/CNY rate; using 0.88 gives 30.5x), or from using the ADR price for conversion. This metric itself deserves a question mark: attributable net profit was depressed by RMB 4.46 billion of goodwill impairment, about RMB 4.3 billion of EU fine provisions, and a 57.4% effective tax rate — the TTM figure of RMB 72.54 billion is not "normal earning power."

Treatment: do not give a single-point PE; write "about 29–31x, weak reference value." If a forward multiple is truly needed, the sell-side Non-GAAP basis is more meaningful: Soochow Securities forecasts FY2027 Non-GAAP net profit of RMB 105.81 billion, implying 20.0x — note this is Non-GAAP, excluding share-based compensation and impairments, and is not comparable to the GAAP 29x.
