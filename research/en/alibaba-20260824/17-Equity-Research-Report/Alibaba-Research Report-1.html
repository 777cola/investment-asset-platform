Investment summary

Rating: Watch (wait for pullback). Target price neutral HKD 129, bear case HKD 88, bull case HKD 171. Current price HKD 112.5 (2026/08/24 close, -8.54%).

Fiscal 2027 Q1 was a mixed report card. Revenue came in at RMB 268.95 billion, +9% and the fastest in nearly four years, with AI cloud and compute at RMB 48.44 billion, +45%, and EBITA margin at 11.6%, the best on record. Net profit attributable to shareholders, however, fell 76% to RMB 10.54 billion, and free cash flow was -RMB 44.67 billion, plugged by an HKD 80 billion placement.

We value Alibaba in three parts: e-commerce (annualized EBITA RMB 159 billion × 9), cloud (annualized revenue RMB 193.7 billion × 6) and AI applications (annualized loss RMB 55.4 billion × -3), totaling RMB 2.42 trillion, or HKD 129 per share. The current price implies a 13% discount, a 1:1.5 payoff, not enough. Buy conditions: ≤HKD 95, or cloud margin ≥12% for two consecutive quarters with AI application losses narrowing quarter on quarter.

Chapter 1 Company Overview

Alibaba Group (09988.HK / BABA.US) was founded in 1999, listed on the NYSE in 2014 and completed a secondary listing in Hong Kong in 2019. The stock closed at HKD 112.5 on 24 August 2026, implying a total market cap of about HKD 2.30 trillion on about 20.46 billion shares following the placement.

From Q1 of fiscal 2027, the company reports under four segments: Alibaba E-commerce Group (Taobao Tmall, instant retail, international e-commerce), AI Cloud and Computing Services (Alibaba Cloud plus T-Head), AI Lab and Applications (Qwen models, Qwen App/office) and All Others (local services, digital media and entertainment, etc.).

On 23 August 2026 the company announced a placement of 710 million new shares at HKD 112.70 per share, raising HKD 80 billion (net about HKD 79.7 billion), with 100% of proceeds earmarked for full-stack AI and AI infrastructure. Settlement is expected on 26 August.

Chapter 2 Industry Analysis

Two battlegrounds. E-commerce: China's online retail penetration has peaked and the industry has become a zero-sum game split three ways between Taobao Tmall, Pinduoduo and Douyin E-commerce, with price wars now routine. AI cloud: an incremental market where the whole industry is scrambling for capacity in 2026; Alibaba Cloud leads with a 38.1% share (Omdia).

By cycle, e-commerce sits at the tail of maturity (zero customer management growth is the evidence), instant retail in the middle of a subsidy war (a 5:4:1 split among the three), and AI compute on the upswing of the capex cycle (Alibaba RMB 67.68 billion in the quarter, Tencent RMB 52.8 billion in Q2, +176%, with the four major North American cloud players raising in tandem). The upside of the upswing flatters revenue; the downside is depreciation that arrives later.

Chapter 3 Business model

Three revenue streams, three economic models. Commission (customer management RMB 82.55 billion, gross margin 70%+, zero inventory and zero fulfillment) is the best business, but it has lost pricing power (+1% like-for-like). Selling compute (RMB 48.44 billion, 11.6% EBITA margin) is a build-first-then-lease heavy business with strong scale effects, dependent on utilization and unit price. Selling fulfillment (instant retail RMB 53.3 billion, gross margin 10%–15%) is a defensive battle, sustaining share through subsidies.

The fourth block, AI applications (RMB 3.34 billion revenue, RMB 13.86 billion loss), is not a business but an entry-point investment: if AI assistants become the next-generation shopping entry point, e-commerce players without an entry point will be demoted to suppliers. That is why Alibaba is willing to burn RMB 55.4 billion a year, and why it has given no timetable for narrowing the loss.

Chapter 4 Financial Analysis

Revenue is the healthiest line and profit the ugliest, and both trace to the same driver. Cloud +45% (RMB 48.44 billion) and instant retail +45% (RMB 53.3 billion) supplied nearly all the incremental revenue, while China e-commerce -8% (RMB 110.9 billion) and customer management -7% (RMB 82.55 billion) dragged.

Decomposing the -76% net profit: AI applications lost roughly RMB 10.6 billion more, all other segments swung from profit to a RMB 4.0 billion loss, goodwill impairment of RMB 4.46 billion, an EU fine provision of about RMB 4.3 billion, investment income down RMB 8.38 billion, and an effective tax rate rising from 17.6% to 57.4% added about RMB 6.0 billion in tax. AI was the only operational deterioration.

Cash flow: operating cash flow RMB 22.95 billion (+11%), capex RMB 67.68 billion (+75%), free cash flow -RMB 44.67 billion. Narrow-basis net cash RMB 35.1 billion (RMB 179.3 billion a year earlier). Net placement proceeds of RMB 73.3 billion cover only 1.6 quarters.

Chapter 5 Management

Dual-core leadership: Joseph Tsai (Chairman, born 1964, holds about 1.43%) oversees capital and external affairs, while Eddie Wu (CEO, holds about 0.29%, annual compensation of about RMB 495 million including equity incentives) oversees strategy and technology; Jiang Fan (CEO of the e-commerce business group, age 40) runs e-commerce and Toby Xu (CFO) runs finance, having become a partner in June 2026.

Capital allocation track record: buybacks and cancellation (2024–2025, positive); focus on the core (2023–2025, positive); RMB 380 billion AI infrastructure (from February 2025, about RMB 190 billion invested to date, pending verification); HKD 80 billion placement (August 2026, negative). Weighted score (execution 40%, capital allocation 40%, communication 20%): 6.2.

Chapter 6 Growth Drivers

Driver 1: AI compute demand. AI-related product revenue reached RMB 12.376 billion, growing triple digits for a 12th consecutive quarter, while AI-related product ARR has surpassed RMB 49.5 billion. China AI cloud share stands at 38.1%, underpinned by a full stack (chips + cloud + models).Driver 2: Cost reduction from in-house chips. Zhenwu M890 serves 650+ external customers, displacing high-priced external procurement and lifting cloud gross margin and supply flexibility.Driver 3: Instant retail and overseas expansion. Instant retail +45%; AliExpress recorded operating profit this quarter; international e-commerce RMB 27.76 billion, -1% (near flat).

The three drivers differ in certainty: cloud highest (good order visibility), chips medium (dependent on controls), instant retail lowest (dependent on retention after subsidies end).

Chapter 7 Competitive Landscape

E-commerce: Taobao Tmall (share defended, monetisation rate declining); Pinduoduo (low-price, market cap of about RMB 839.9 billion); Douyin E-commerce (content-driven, with Douyin local-services GTV above RMB 800 billion in 2025, +60%); JD.com (1P plus instant retail, 7% food-delivery share). Instant retail: Meituan 52%, Taobao Shangou 41%, JD.com 7%. AI cloud: Alibaba Cloud ranks first at 38.1%, with Volcano Engine, Tencent Cloud and Huawei Cloud catching up.

The most dangerous rival is ByteDance, which weakens both the transaction and the compute ends at once and runs a lower traffic cost. The second most dangerous rival is Alibaba itself: if the RMB 67.68 billion per quarter of capex mistimes the cycle, heavy assets become a burden.

Chapter 8 Risk factors

①Capex return risk: RMB 190 billion already invested, static return 11.8%; if compute prices decline in 2027–2028, margin could fall below 9% (HKD -19 per share). ②E-commerce monetisation risk: customer management flat; two consecutive quarters of negative growth would imply HKD -15 per share. ③AI application loss risk: loss ratio -415% with no path to narrowing losses (HKD -12 per share). ④Secondary placement risk: another share issuance would permanently lower the valuation. ⑤Regulatory and geopolitical risk: EU fines of about RMB 4.3 billion already provisioned; export controls and ADR policy persist long term.

Extreme scenario (all five occur simultaneously): RMB 1.45 trillion, HKD 71 per share, probability below 10%.

Chapter 9 Earnings Forecast

We adopt the sell-side consensus direction but not its optimistic slope. We forecast FY2027 non-GAAP net profit of RMB 105–110 billion (flat), on key assumptions of 40% annualised cloud revenue growth at an 11.5% EBITA margin, flat e-commerce EBITA, RMB 55–60 billion of losses in AI applications and RMB 250–270 billion of capex. The key variable for FY2028 is depreciation: amortised over 5 years, incremental annual depreciation of RMB 50–54 billion would compress the operating margin by a further 3–4 percentage points.

Soochow Securities is more optimistic, forecasting RMB 105.81 billion in FY2027, RMB 141.87 billion in FY2028 (+34%) and RMB 185.37 billion in FY2029 (+31%). The disagreement centres on whether cloud margins can keep rising. We take the midpoint and express it in valuation via SOTP rather than P/E, avoiding over-reliance on a single profit figure.

Chapter 10 Valuation

We use sum-of-the-parts (SOTP) because capital intensity and growth differ by more than threefold across the four businesses, which makes a consolidated P/E misleading. Neutral case: e-commerce EBITA of RMB 159.0 billion × 9 = RMB 1.43 trillion; cloud revenue of RMB 193.7 billion × 6 = RMB 1.16 trillion; AI applications loss of RMB 55.4 billion × -3 = -RMB 0.17 trillion; total RMB 2.42 trillion, HKD 129.

Cross-check: 20x FY2027 Non-GAAP net profit (RMB 105.8 billion) gives RMB 2.12 trillion = HKD 113, broadly in line with the current price — the market is therefore pricing Alibaba on a consolidated P/E basis, whereas SOTP implies a sum-of-the-parts framework; the 13% gap between the two is where the market disagrees on AI and cloud.

Comparables: Tencent trades at about RMB 3.49 trillion and Pinduoduo at about RMB 839.9 billion; Alibaba at HKD 2.30 trillion (about RMB 2.12 trillion) sits in between, consistent with its e-commerce plus cloud asset mix.

Chapter 11 Investment Recommendations

Rating: Watch (wait for pullback). Neutral target price HKD 129 (+15%), bear case HKD 88 (-22%), bull case HKD 171 (+52%). Odds of 1:1.5 require a 60% win rate; actual is only around 33% — negative expected value, no position.

Two entry conditions, either one suffices: ① share price ≤HKD 95 (odds then invert to 5:1, expected value +7.2%); ② cloud segment EBITA margin ≥12% for two consecutive quarters, growth ≥40%, and AI application quarterly loss narrows QoQ (win rate then rises above 60%).

Position sizing: 3% at HKD 95, build to 6% at HKD 88, build to 8% once evidence appears, 10% cap on dual trigger. Falsification lines: cloud growth <30%, cloud EBITA margin <9%, capex expanding for three consecutive quarters with FCF gap >-RMB 50 billion — any one triggers a full exit, regardless of cost.

Conclusion

Investment view: Watch, wait for a pullback. Target price HKD 129 base case, HKD 88 bear case, HKD 171 bull case; no position at the current HKD 112.5. Buy trigger: ≤HKD 95, or cloud margin ≥12% for two consecutive quarters with AI application losses narrowing QoQ. This is a "good asset + fair price + insufficient evidence" report; the conclusion is to wait, not to avoid.
