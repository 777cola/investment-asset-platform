## Chapter 1 Company Overview

Alibaba Group (09988.HK / BABA.US) was founded in 1999, listed on the NYSE in 2014 and completed a secondary listing in Hong Kong in 2019. It closed at HKD 112.5 on 24 August 2026, with a total market cap of about HKD 2.30 trillion (about 20.46 billion shares after the placement).

From Q1 of fiscal 2027, the company has reorganised into four segments: Alibaba E-commerce Group (Taobao Tmall, instant retail, international e-commerce), AI Cloud and Computing Services (Alibaba Cloud plus T-Head), AI Lab and Applications (Qwen models, Qwen App/office), and All Others (local services, digital media and entertainment, etc.).

On 23 August 2026 the company announced a placement of 710 million new shares at HKD 112.70 per share, raising HKD 80 billion (net about HKD 79.7 billion), with 100% of proceeds to be invested in full-stack AI and AI infrastructure. Settlement is expected on 26 August.

## Chapter 2 Industry Analysis

Two battlegrounds. E-commerce: China's online retail penetration has peaked and the industry has entered a zero-sum game, split three ways among Taobao Tmall, Pinduoduo and Douyin E-commerce, with price wars now routine. AI cloud: an incremental market, with the whole industry scrambling for capacity in 2026; Alibaba Cloud leads with a 38.1% share (Omdia).

On the cycle, e-commerce sits at the tail of maturity (zero customer management growth is the evidence), instant retail in the middle of a subsidy war (a 5:4:1 split among the three), and AI compute on the upswing of the capex cycle (Alibaba RMB 67.68 billion in the quarter, Tencent RMB 52.8 billion in Q2, +176%, with the four major North American cloud players raising in tandem). The upside of the upswing is flattering revenue; the downside is depreciation that arrives later.

## Chapter 3 Business model

Three revenue streams, three economic models. Commission (customer management RMB 82.55 billion, gross margin 70%+, zero inventory and zero fulfillment) is the best business, but it has lost pricing power (+1% on a like-for-like basis). Selling compute (RMB 48.44 billion, 11.6% EBITA margin) is a build-first-then-lease heavy business with strong scale effects, dependent on utilization and unit price. Selling fulfillment (instant retail RMB 53.3 billion, gross margin 10%–15%) is a defensive battle, sustaining share through subsidies.

The fourth block, AI applications (RMB 3.34 billion revenue, RMB 13.86 billion loss), is not a business but an entry-point investment: if AI assistants become the next-generation shopping entry point, e-commerce players without an entry point will be demoted to suppliers. That is why Alibaba is willing to burn RMB 55.4 billion a year, and why it has given no timetable for narrowing the loss.

## Chapter 4 Financial Analysis

The revenue line is the healthiest and the profit line the ugliest, and both stem from the same cause. Cloud +45% (RMB 48.44 billion) and instant retail +45% (RMB 53.3 billion) contributed nearly all the incremental revenue; China e-commerce -8% (RMB 110.9 billion) and customer management -7% (RMB 82.55 billion) were the drags.

Unpacking the -76% net profit: AI applications lost roughly RMB 10.6 billion more, all other segments swung from profit to a RMB 4.0 billion loss, goodwill impairment of RMB 4.46 billion, EU fine provision of about RMB 4.3 billion, investment income down RMB 8.38 billion, and the effective tax rate rising from 17.6% to 57.4% added about RMB 6.0 billion in tax. The only operational deterioration was AI.

Cash flow: operating cash flow RMB 22.95 billion (+11%), capex RMB 67.68 billion (+75%), free cash flow -RMB 44.67 billion. Narrow-basis net cash RMB 35.1 billion (RMB 179.3 billion a year earlier). The RMB 73.3 billion net placement proceeds cover only 1.6 quarters.

## Chapter 6 Growth Drivers

Driver 1: AI compute demand. AI-related product revenue reached RMB 12.376 billion, growing triple digits for 12 consecutive quarters; AI-related product ARR has exceeded RMB 49.5 billion. China AI cloud share stands at 38.1%, with a full stack (chips + cloud + models).Driver 2: Cost reduction from in-house chips. Zhenwu M890 serves 650+ external customers, replacing high-priced external procurement and lifting cloud gross margin and supply flexibility.Driver 3: Instant retail and overseas expansion. Instant retail +45%; AliExpress recorded operating profit this quarter; international e-commerce RMB 27.76 billion, -1% (near flat).

The three drivers differ in certainty: cloud highest (good order visibility), chips medium (dependent on controls), instant retail lowest (dependent on retention after subsidies end).

## Chapter 7 Competitive Landscape

E-commerce: Taobao Tmall (share defended, monetization rate declining), Pinduoduo (low prices, market cap of about RMB 839.9 billion), Douyin E-commerce (content-driven, Douyin local-services GTV above RMB 800 billion in 2025, +60%), JD.com (1P plus instant retail, 7% food-delivery share). Instant retail: Meituan 52%, Taobao Shangou 41%, JD.com 7%. AI cloud: Alibaba Cloud ranks first at 38.1%, with Volcano Engine, Tencent Cloud and Huawei Cloud catching up.

The most dangerous rival is ByteDance: it weakens both the transaction and the compute ends at once, and its traffic costs are lower. The second most dangerous rival is Alibaba itself: if the RMB 67.68 billion per quarter of capex mistimes the cycle, heavy assets become a burden.

## Chapter 8 Risk factors

①Capex return risk: RMB 190 billion already invested, static return 11.8%; if compute prices decline in 2027–2028, margin could fall below 9% (HKD -19 per share). ②E-commerce monetization risk: customer management flat; if it posts negative growth for two consecutive quarters (HKD -15 per share). ③AI application loss risk: loss ratio -415% with no path to narrowing losses (HKD -12 per share). ④Secondary placement risk: another share issuance would permanently lower the valuation. ⑤Regulatory and geopolitical risk: EU fines of about RMB 4.3 billion already provisioned; export controls and ADR policy persist long term.

Extreme scenario (all five occur simultaneously): RMB 1.45 trillion, HKD 71 per share, probability below 10%.

## Chapter 9 Earnings Forecast

We adopt the sell-side consensus direction but not its optimistic slope. FY2027 Non-GAAP net profit is forecast at RMB 105–110 billion (flat), on key assumptions: cloud revenue growing 40% annualized with an 11.5% EBITA margin; e-commerce EBITA flat; AI applications losing RMB 55–60 billion; capex of RMB 250–270 billion. The key variable for FY2028 is depreciation: amortized over 5 years, incremental annual depreciation of RMB 50–54 billion would compress the operating margin by a further 3–4 percentage points.

Soochow Securities is more optimistic: FY2027 RMB 105.81 billion, FY2028 RMB 141.87 billion (+34%), FY2029 RMB 185.37 billion (+31%). The disagreement centers on whether cloud margins can keep rising — we take the midpoint and express it in valuation via SOTP rather than PE, avoiding over-reliance on a single profit figure.

## Chapter 10 Valuation

We use sum-of-the-parts (SOTP) because capital intensity and growth across the four businesses differ by more than threefold, making a consolidated PE misleading. Neutral case: e-commerce EBITA of RMB 159.0 billion × 9 = RMB 1.43 trillion; cloud revenue of RMB 193.7 billion × 6 = RMB 1.16 trillion; AI applications loss of RMB 55.4 billion × -3 = -RMB 0.17 trillion; total RMB 2.42 trillion, HKD 129.

Cross-check: at 20x FY2027 Non-GAAP net profit (RMB 105.8 billion) we get RMB 2.12 trillion = HKD 113, broadly in line with the current price — this shows the market is currently using a consolidated PE framework, while SOTP gives a sum-of-the-parts framework; the 13% gap between them is the market's pricing disagreement over AI and cloud.

Comparables: Tencent trades at about RMB 3.49 trillion and Pinduoduo at about RMB 839.9 billion; Alibaba at HKD 2.30 trillion (about RMB 2.12 trillion) sits in between, consistent with its e-commerce plus cloud asset mix.

## Chapter 11 Investment Recommendations

Rating: Watch (wait for pullback). Neutral target price HKD 129 (+15%), bear case HKD 88 (-22%), bull case HKD 171 (+52%). Odds 1:1.5 require a 60% win rate; actual is only around 33% — negative expected value, no position.

Two entry conditions, either one suffices: ① share price ≤HKD 95 (odds then invert to 5:1, expected value +7.2%); ② cloud segment EBITA margin ≥12% for two consecutive quarters, growth ≥40%, and AI application quarterly loss narrows quarter-on-quarter (win rate then rises above 60%).

Position sizing: 3% at HKD 95, build to 6% at HKD 88, build to 8% once evidence appears, 10% cap on dual trigger. Falsification lines: cloud growth -RMB 50 billion — any one triggers a full exit, regardless of cost.
