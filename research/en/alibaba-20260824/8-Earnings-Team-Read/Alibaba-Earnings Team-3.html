Earnings Deep Dive (Munger) — Alibaba

Core view

Munger's method for reading financial reports is to find "the most dangerous number." This quarter it is not -76%, but -415% — the loss ratio of AI labs and applications. A loss of RMB 4.15 for every RMB 1 of revenue; in internet history, only the most frenzied subsidy wars have produced this.

The most dangerous number: -415%

AI Labs and Applications: revenue RMB 3.34 billion, +16% YoY; loss RMB 13.86 billion, loss margin -415%, loss widened 330% YoY. In plain terms: for every RMB 1 of revenue this segment brings in, it spends RMB 5.15. Management's explanation: "investment in AI capability building + rising inference costs for the Qwen App."

Munger would say: the level of the loss margin is not the issue; the issue is whether the loss margin is converging. If the loss margin was -100% in the year-ago period and is -415% this year, the business model is deteriorating, not improving; if it was -500% a year ago and -415% now, it is improving. Public disclosure gives only "loss widened 330%" and omits last year's loss margin — that in itself is a signal: the company is disclosing selectively.

Compare with peers: Tencent attaches AI capabilities to existing monetized scenarios (advertising, gaming, payments), so marginal cost is low; ByteDance's Doubao trades traffic for users, but Volcano Engine itself is profitable. Alibaba is the only company that carves out "model applications" as a standalone segment, makes it self-funding, and then publicly reports a 415% loss — the candor deserves credit, but the numbers themselves look ugly.

The second risk: zero growth in customer management

Customer management revenue was RMB 82.55 billion, down -7% as reported and up +1% on a like-for-like basis. This is a more alarming number than the -415%, because it comes from the core business. Customer management is Alibaba's most profitable revenue (extremely high gross margin, almost no capital intensity). Its stall in growth means merchants' ad budgets are flowing elsewhere — to Douyin's content feeds and Pinduoduo's low-price marketplace.

Munger would compare it with history: customer management was still growing mid-single digits in 2023–2024, began slowing in 2025, and hit zero growth in 2026. Three years completed a full downcycle — this is not a "quarterly fluctuation" but a structural change.

More troublesome is its relationship with instant retail: instant retail was RMB 53.3 billion, +45%. The revenue looks good, but it was bought with subsidies, and its gross margin is far below customer management's. "High-margin revenue stalling, low-margin revenue surging" is the standard recipe for margin compression. Group adjusted EBITA fell -30% year on year — the result of that recipe.

Third risk: all other swings from profit to loss

The "all other" line swung from a profit of RMB 690 million in the year-ago period to a loss of RMB 3.34 billion, a gap of RMB 4 billion. This line holds scattered businesses such as local services, digital media and entertainment, and Sun Art Retail.Munger never trusted "other": a catch-all drawer usually means someone does not want you to see what is in it.

A RMB 4 billion deterioration is not large, but the direction is bad: as group profit declines, non-core businesses are not shrinking but widening their losses. This shows "focus" is only half done — core businesses are focused, but peripheral businesses have not been cleanly cut.

String the three danger numbers together and a clear picture emerges:zero growth in the base business (customer management), huge losses in the new business (AI applications), widening losses in peripheral businesses (all other).The only thing improving is cloud. That is why the market gives HKD 112.5 — it is pricing "one good cloud plus a mediocre group."

Conclusion

Munger-style conclusion: the problem with this company is that it makes money while leaking money, and the leaks (AI applications, all other segments) are absent from management's core narrative. He would demand two things before buying: the loss ratio in AI applications starts to converge, and non-core businesses stop widening losses. Neither is present, so he would not buy.
