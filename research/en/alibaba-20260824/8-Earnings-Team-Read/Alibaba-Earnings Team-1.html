Earnings Deep Dive (Duan Yongping) — Alibaba

Ask about the business first, then look at the numbers
Research date 2026/08/24  ·  Price HK$112.50 (-8.54%)  ·  Market cap Approximately HKD 2.30 trillion (approximately 20.46 billion shares post-placement)  ·  PE (TTM, back-solved) ~31x (earnings distorted by AI investment; limited read-across)

Core view

Duan Yongping reads financial reports in the reverse order to most: first, whether the business is still there; then, whether the numbers are any good. This quarter's answer: the business is there, but its nature has changed—from "collecting rent" to "investing first, collecting rent later."

First, ask whether this business still exists.

Three pieces of evidence show the business is still there. First, the e-commerce group posted revenue of RMB 205.86 billion and EBITA of RMB 39.75 billion, a 19.3% margin — a business earning a 19% operating margin while consuming almost no capital is down to two or three players in Chinese internet. Second, 88VIP members number about 64 million, growing double digits YoY — direct evidence that users are still willing to pay an annual fee to stay. Third, AliExpress posted operating profit this quarter — international operations turned from burning cash to making money, something that has not happened in the past five years.

But the nature has indeed changed. Alibaba's cash flow structure used to be "collect money from merchants, spend almost nothing"; now two large pockets are draining cash: instant retail subsidies and AI infrastructure capex. Quarterly capex was RMB 67.68 billion, three times operating cash flow of RMB 22.95 billion. That means it is no longer a business that generates cash while sitting still, but one that must immediately reinvest what it earns.

Now look at "whether the money is well spent"

Duan Yongping's test for spending is simple: will this money earn more next year? Cloud passes: RMB 48.44 billion revenue, +45%, EBITA RMB 5.63 billion, +133%, evidence that the past two years of investment are starting to pay off and returns are rising (margin 7.2%→11.6%). This is what he wants to see.

AI applications fail the test: RMB 3.34 billion revenue, RMB 13.86 billion loss, RMB 4.15 lost for every RMB 1 of revenue, and the company has not said when the loss will narrow. In his language, this is not investment; it is "spending before figuring out how to make money."

He would therefore likely value the two separately: cloud as a growth asset, AI applications as an expense. That is also the approach behind this report's SOTP — cloud at 6x PS (RMB 1.16 trillion), AI applications at 3x losses as a negative value (-RMB 0.17 trillion).

Finally, look at "what management is doing"

The most glaring issue this quarter is not the -76% but the placement. Two days after the earnings call, on the evening of August 23, the company announced an HKD 80 billion placement at HKD 112.70 per share, an 8.4% discount to the August 21 close of HKD 123.0. Duan Yongping has always been clear on such matters: raising capital is not wrong; the wrong lies in the timing and the reason — if it is "we have money but want to share the risk with others," that is a capital allocation problem; if it is "we have no money and must raise," that is an operating problem. This time it looks more like the former: the balance sheet still holds RMB 474.505 billion in cash and current investments.

He would note one detail: on August 24, Joseph Tsai and Wu Yongming together bought HKD 120 million worth of shares, 0.15% of the placement size. That ratio shows management wanted to send a signal but was unwilling (or unable) to bet a larger amount. His comment would probably be: "The gesture is fine; the sincerity is so-so."

Conclusion

Duan Yongping's verdict: the business is still there, but it has become one where you have to keep pouring in money just to stay ahead; cloud has proven that the investment pays off, AI applications have not. He will wait—until AI applications can speak for themselves, or until the price is cheap enough that they don't need to. At HKD 112.5, neither condition is met.
