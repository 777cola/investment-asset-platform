Industry Research (Munger's Lens) — Kuaishou

Who makes money in this industry, who pays, and how the landscape will settle
Research date 2026/10/12  ·  Price HK$29.26 (-2.79%)  ·  Market cap About HKD 126.7 billion  ·  P/E (TTM) About 10.5x

Core view

China's short-video industry has shifted from "grabbing incremental users" to "dividing up the existing base." The 2026 landscape: Douyin dominates alone, WeChat Channels holds a firm second on the back of social relationships, Kuaishou sits third, and Hongguo and Xiaohongshu carve out specific use cases from the flanks. The rule of a stock market: the leader's share does not fall; the runner-up's share is the first to be eaten.

Industry growth: from 30% to single digits in three years

Consensus puts China's overall internet advertising market at around 6% growth in 2026. Short-video advertising, the largest segment within it, has seen growth slow from over 40% in 2021 to single digits. Kuaishou's Q2 advertising grew 4.4%, below the 6.3% consensus and below the market — the industry is not starving it out; it simply cannot capture its own share.

Live-streaming tipping is even more direct: the entire sector is shrinking. Regulatory restrictions on tipping by minors, the economic downturn squeezing discretionary spending and short-video platforms channeling traffic toward e-commerce — these three factors compound, and Kuaishou's -13.5% in live streaming is not its problem alone but the direction of the entire track.

Competitive structure: pressure from four fronts, each eroding a different layer of the model

Douyin takes the budget: for the same advertiser, Douyin delivers higher ROI, more expensive audiences and better conversion, so brand budgets naturally flow to it first. WeChat Channels takes the relationships: WeChat's social graph means older users do not need to scroll — they see the content in group chats. Hongguo takes the time: free short dramas capture time-killing with a completely different content format, and their revenue share is more aggressive than Kuaishou's. Xiaohongshu takes the seeding: the decision entry point for high-spend female users, with ad unit prices several times Kuaishou's.

What these four fronts share: none attacks Kuaishou's core audience head-on; they fragment that audience's time from four directions. That is harder to defend than a frontal attack, because you do not know where the enemy comes from.

AI video: model layer still contested, application layer yet to consolidate

In the 2026 video-generation race, ByteDance's Seedance, Kuaishou's Kling, Google's Veo and Runway are all competing, alongside Alibaba's Tongyi Wanxiang in China. Kling consistently ranks in the top tier in third-party evaluations, though it is not No. 1.

The economics of this track rest on "compute in exchange for revenue": every generation incurs inference cost, and at scale gross margin hinges on in-house chips and inference optimisation. That is the mirror image of advertising, where marginal cost tends toward zero. The endgame for video generation is therefore most likely "oligopoly plus low gross margin" — not a business that can support a 36x PS.

Cycle position: three cycles all trending down

The advertising cycle is rolling over on weak macro consumption and advertiser budget cuts; the content cycle is rolling over as Hongguo captures the short-drama dividend; and the technology cycle is turning up on exploding AI video demand — but that is someone else's battlefield. Of the three cycles, Kuaishou can play in only one, and even there it ranks second.

Reading cycles is about timing: the advertising cycle should recover in 2027 as the macro improves, and how much Kuaishou captures then hinges on whether it gets the product right now. What it is doing now is shifting R&D resources to another battlefield.

Conclusion

Industry view: the industry has not collapsed; Kuaishou's relative position is. In a mature market, the number three player faces slow share loss, margins capped by competition and a valuation that drifts toward utility-like levels. Absent first place in AI video, that fate will not change.
