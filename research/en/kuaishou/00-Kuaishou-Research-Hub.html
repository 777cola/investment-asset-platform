📝 Conclusion: Avoid

Avoid. Kuaishou's 2026Q2 revenue was RMB 35.535 billion, up only 1.4% YoY, with advertising +4.4% and livestreaming -13.5%; only Kling grew +200% — but Kling generated just RMB 850 million in the quarter, 2.4% of total revenue, not enough to support a HKD 126.7 billion market-cap story. The profit side sends a more direct signal: gross margin -4.1pct, R&D +34.7%, adjusted net profit -30.3%; AI investment is eating the core business's cash flow. This is not "the AI story is compelling" — it is "the core business is decelerating while AI is still burning cash," both happening at once. 10.5x PE and RMB 24.27 billion in net cash look cheap, but the denominator is declining; the neutral scenario is 9% below the current price. Conditions to revisit: advertising revenue growth returns above 8%, and Kling ARR exceeds USD 1 billion with a verifiable path to narrowing losses.
