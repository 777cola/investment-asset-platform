# Kuaishou（01024）— Quality Screen Supplement

- Research date：2026/10/12
- Price：29.26 HKD
- Market cap：About HKD 126.7 billion

## Notes

The seven hard metrics follow the AI Berkshire negative-screen criteria: revenue growth, gross margin, net margin, earnings trend, expense ratio, net cash and shareholder return/share count. Figures are taken from Kuaishou's 2026Q2 results announcement: total revenue RMB 35.535 billion, gross margin 51.6%, adjusted net profit RMB 3.913 billion, reported net profit RMB 3.152 billion, R&D RMB 4.6 billion, cash and cash equivalents RMB 24.27 billion, 4.33 billion shares, and Q2 buyback of HKD 880 million/19.60 million shares. 2025Q2 livestreaming revenue of RMB 10.06 billion and DAU of 408.7 million are back-solved from YoY growth rates; revenue per DAU is arithmetic (206/4.12, 87/4.12), not a disclosed figure. Annualised buyback of HKD 3.5 billion = 8.8 × 4.

## Of the three criteria met, the quality differs

Gross margin 51.6%: clears the bar, but not comfortably. The 40% threshold is calibrated for manufacturing and retail; for an advertising platform, 51.6% is only mid-range, and the direction is down. A 4.1pct decline means RMB 4.1 less earned per RMB 100 of revenue, or RMB 1.46 billion in a single quarter. Management attributes this to higher revenue-sharing costs — in plain terms, the platform gave up more money to retain creators and streamers.

Net cash of RMB 24.27 billion is the hardest of these seven items. That is 20.9% of market cap, or about HKD 6.1 per share, with no near-term debt-servicing pressure. Note, however, that this is an aggregate "cash-like assets" measure (cash and equivalents, time deposits, wealth-management products), not free cash available in full for dividends. Shareholder returns of HKD 880 million per quarter — about HKD 3.5 billion annualized, or 2.8% of market cap — are adequate, but were bought in at HKD 45.

## Of the four that failed, only one is a real problem

An R&D expense ratio of 12.9% (+3.2pct) fails, but that is a choice, not deterioration: of the RMB 4.6 billion, roughly RMB 1.2 billion of the increase went largely into AI training. A company raising investment while its core business stalls can be criticized for misjudgment, but that is a strategic disagreement, not poor financial quality.

A net margin of 11.0% fails; it is the arithmetic result of the preceding items, not a standalone problem. Revenue +1.4% is the real disease: advertising +4.4% (below the consensus of 6.3%), livestreaming -13.5%, and only Kling +200%—while Kling is just 2.4% of revenue. Strip out Kling and the remaining businesses are negative YoY in aggregate. A platform with 400 million DAU whose revenue stalls shows it cannot sell more advertising and cannot retain tippers.

The profit trend of -30.3% follows close behind. These two items only make sense together: revenue flat, profit down 30%, meaning operating leverage is working in reverse. Over the past three years the market's valuation logic for Kuaishou was "margin repair"; this hard metric failing means that logic has been disproven.

## One hidden indicator beyond the seven

Advertising revenue per DAU per quarter: 206 ÷ 4.12 = RMB 50.0; livestreaming revenue per DAU per quarter: 87 ÷ 4.12 = RMB 21.1, versus about RMB 24.6 in the same period last year (backed out from DAU of 408.7 million). Ad revenue per user is rising, livestreaming is falling—hence total revenue still edges up while profit falls: advertising carries low marginal cost, livestreaming requires revenue sharing.

The structural shift is not bad in itself; the problem is that it is too small. Advertising adds RMB 2 per DAU per year, not enough to offset livestreaming's RMB 3.5 per DAU decline.

## Three conditions that will improve together

Two of the four unmet criteria — net margin and profit trend — share a single root cause: both are squeezed between gross margin and R&D expense ratio. Sensitivity: every 1pct of gross margin recovery adds RMB 355 million to quarterly profit, and every 1pct reduction in the R&D expense ratio adds the same RMB 355 million. Recover 3pct across the two and quarterly adjusted net profit climbs from RMB 3.9 billion back above RMB 5 billion, clearing six of the seven criteria.

The question, then, is not whether a recovery is hard, but whether the company wants one. The AI spending was its own choice; the revenue-share concession was its own giveaway. Neither is an external shock. Any recovery therefore has to be management-timed, not cycle-timed.
