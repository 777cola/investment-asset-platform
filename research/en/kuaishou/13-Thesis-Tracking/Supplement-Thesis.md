## Two have been falsified, with completely different implications

The claim that livestreaming has bottomed is genuinely falsified. In 2025 the market widely expected livestreaming to "narrow its decline and stabilize in 2026"; the actual result was -13.5%, two consecutive years of negative growth. The industry is shrinking (regulation, trade-down consumption, traffic shifting toward e-commerce). This is not an execution problem specific to Kuaishou; it is the end of the runway for the sector. This assumption should be deleted from the thesis permanently, not given "another two quarters."

The claim that advertising holds at 4% is "half-falsified." The 4.4% figure has not yet turned negative, but it is below the consensus of 6.3% and below the industry overall. More troubling is the guidance: management itself says e-commerce marketing and commissions will come under pressure in the second half. "Holding at 4%" has been downgraded from a base-case assumption to an optimistic one.

## Two lines are wobbling; the key is Q1 2027

Kling as a second curve comes down to one number. Q2 RMB 850 million, up 31% QoQ, RMB 3.4 billion annualized; hold 25% QoQ for three quarters and 2027Q2 alone reaches RMB 1.7 billion, RMB 6.8 billion annualized — only then does it qualify as a "second curve." The watch point is not the growth rate but whether the QoQ slope holds.

AI spending peaking: no evidence yet. R&D expense ratio rose from 9.7% to 12.9%, capex fell from a high to RMB 5.9 billion — two contradictory signals: headcount investment rising, compute investment falling. The reasonable read is "training costs front-loaded, inference costs back-loaded," meaning next year stays high. If the R&D expense ratio is still above 13% in 2027, this thesis is falsified.

## How the assumptions were written down: start by stating "where I am wrong"

A proper thesis table requires every assumption to be falsifiable by a specific number. "Kuaishou will get better" is not an assumption; "online marketing returns to 8% year on year" is. The value of this discipline lies not in being right, but in knowing immediately when you are wrong.

In hindsight, the market's thesis on Kuaishou at the end of 2025 read like this: livestreaming stabilizes, advertising grows double digits, Kling opens a second curve. Two of the three no longer hold today; the third is still being tested. That is why the valuation is now 10x rather than 20x — the market is not de-rating on sentiment; it is striking out assumptions one by one.

## Falsification conditions: when to abandon the thesis outright

Four hard falsification conditions; any two triggered together removes the name from the watchlist entirely: ① Online marketing revenue turns negative year on year; ② Kling quarterly revenue stops growing sequentially (two consecutive quarters); ③ Cash-like assets fall below RMB 19 billion (under 17% of market cap); ④ R&D expense ratio exceeds 15% while revenue growth is below 3%.

The most sensitive of the four is the first. Advertising is 58% of this company; if it turns negative, the entire valuation framework has to be redone.

## Watch calendar: what to look for over the next four quarters

November 2026 (Q3 report): advertising growth, R&D expense ratio, management's full-year guidance. March 2027 (annual report): Kling's full-year revenue and whether standalone P&L is disclosed, capex plans, whether buybacks continue. May 2027 (Q1 report): whether advertising returns to 6%, whether the decline in livestreaming narrows. August 2027 (interim report): whether Kling's annualized revenue exceeds RMB 6 billion, whether the adjusted net margin stops falling.

Track only two numbers at each checkpoint: advertising year-on-year, Kling quarter-on-quarter. Everything else is noise.
