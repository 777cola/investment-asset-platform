Meituan Q2: a RMB 2.69 billion swing back to profit, but not profit won back — Meituan

Core view

Meituan reported Q2 2026 results on the evening of August 28: revenue of RMB 104.64 billion, up 14.4% YoY; operating profit of RMB 2.69 billion, swinging to positive from a loss; and core local commerce operating profit of RMB 5.67 billion, up 52.3% YoY. Against consensus for a RMB 850 million loss, the print came in well ahead.

First, the good news: three areas improved simultaneously

First, revenue. RMB 104.64 billion, +14.4%, the fastest growth since Q2 2025. By segment: delivery services RMB 26.78 billion, +13.1%, back to positive growth; new initiatives RMB 33.11 billion, +25%.

Second, core business profit. Core local commerce operating profit RMB 5.67 billion, +52.3% YoY, margin 7.9%, up 2.2pp YoY. This segment is Meituan's sole source of profit, so the group only turns profitable once it does.

Third, narrower losses in new initiatives. Loss of RMB 1.74 billion, with the loss ratio narrowing from 7.1% to 5.3%. Xiaoxiang Supermarket covers 68 cities (39 at end-2025), Kuailehou has 40 stores, and Keeta has reached profitability in Hong Kong and Saudi Arabia.

One easily overlooked figure: net operating cash inflow of RMB 9.7 billion in Q2, 4.5x net profit. For full-year 2025 the figure was -RMB 13.815 billion. A cash flow turnaround is harder to fake than a profit turnaround.

Squeezed out of cost savings, to put it less politely: RMB 2.69 billion.

Margin 2.6%. For full-year 2024, that figure was 13.4% — in other words, it has recovered to only one-fifth of the level two years ago.

Where did the savings come from? Cost of sales ratio down 0.6 percentage points (about RMB 630 million), marketing expense ratio down 0.6 percentage points (about RMB 630 million). No price increases, no higher commission rates, only "spending less."

And there are limits to spending less. Management stated three things plainly at the earnings call: per-order delivery cost in Q3 will be higher than in Q2 (summer marketing, high-temperature rider subsidies, nationwide contributions to the new work-injury insurance from July 1); in-store business will step up investment in Q3 and Q4, with operating margin below Q2; industry subsidy levels remain above 2024 and will take several quarters to normalize.

In other words, RMB 2.69 billion is very likely one of the peaks for the next four quarters. That is not a bearish call; it is what the company itself said.

The most critical line: merchant services grew only 4.0%

One line in the revenue deserves attention: merchant services revenue of RMB 39.60 billion, up just 4.0% YoY. This segment—commissions plus advertising—is the thermometer for the platform's bargaining power over merchants.

4.0% means merchants are not growing, merely being retained. Two reasons: Douyin is poaching chain brands and influencer shops with low commissions, and merchants are themselves cutting costs, with ad budgets cut harder than commissions.

Food delivery's moat is fulfilment density; in-store's moat is merchant relationships. The first still holds; the second is loosening. And in-store is Meituan's fattest profit pool—a bigger worry than delivery share falling from 65% to 51%.

How to value it, how to buy

HKD 77.5, market cap HKD 478.56 billion (6.175 billion shares). Cash and short-term investments of RMB 168.3 billion; net cash of about RMB 120 billion after interest-bearing debt and restricted funds.

Loss-making in 2026, so no P/E; on 2027 normalised profit of RMB 25 billion the stock trades at 17.6x, or 12.8x ex-net cash; on RMB 29 billion, 15.2x and 11x. The "about 15x, about 12x ex-net cash" in the working notes is exactly this range.

Three scenarios: bear case HKD 60 (-23%), base case HKD 110 (+42%), bull case HKD 150 (+94%), a risk-reward of 1:1.8.

Conclusion: Buy, building a small position in tranches. Start at 2%, add to 4%–5% at HKD 65, and cut the position if Q3 core local commerce operating profit falls below RMB 2 billion. This is a position to be validated by quarterly results, not one to be held on conviction.

Three sentences for readers

If you remember only three sentences, remember these.

First: The RMB 2.69 billion is real, but the 2.6% margin shows it was saved, not earned. Saved profit has a shelf life, and Q3 is when it expires.

Second: The share drop from 65% to 51% is real, but what was lost was low-price orders. By value it still holds over 60%, which underpins its defence of 10% revenue growth.

Third: HKD 77.5 is a fair price, not a cheap one. That is 15–18x 2027 normalized profit and 11–13x ex-cash; for cheap, wait for HKD 65.

Conclusion

One-line summary: Meituan has won back profit, but not yet pricing power. RMB 2.69 billion proves it can make money as subsidies recede; a 2.6% margin proves that money is still hard-won. Buying the stock is a bet on recovery, not on greatness.
