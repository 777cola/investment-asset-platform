## Why this order

Most research reports open with "Company Overview" and close with "Investment Recommendation." This series inverts that: first show you where the market is wrong, then explain what the business actually is, and only then put a price on it.

The most common way to lose money in investing is not "getting the numbers wrong" but "misjudging the object"—you think you are buying a growth stock when it is really a cyclical, or a platform when it is really a retailer. Get the perception wrong and every valuation that follows is wrong too.
