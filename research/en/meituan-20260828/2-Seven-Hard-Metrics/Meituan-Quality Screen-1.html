Core thesis

Only two and a half of the seven hard metrics pass: gross margin 33.5%, interest-bearing debt at net cash, revenue growth +10.1%; the rest — ROE, net margin, operating cash flow, free cash flow — all fail. But this is not the picture of an inferior company; it is the picture of a company that has just come out of a war. In 2024 its operating profit was RMB 45.1 billion and net profit RMB 35.8 billion; a year later these became -RMB 17 billion and -RMB 23.355 billion, with revenue down only 8 points. The purpose of negative screening is not to find perfect companies but to exclude structurally poor businesses — Meituan fails because of reversible war damage, not because the business itself does not make money. The conclusion: keep it in the pool, wait for the income statement to repair itself, and do not use the seven failures as an excuse to avoid it.

Which lines fail: all four on the profit side, one on the cash side

ROE, net margin, operating cash flow, free cash flow — all four fail, and for the same reason: the 2025 subsidy war punched through the income statement. 2025 revenue was RMB 364.85 billion, up 8.1% year on year; operating loss was RMB 17 billion and net loss RMB 23.355 billion; operating cash flow was -RMB 13.815 billion (versus +RMB 57.147 billion in 2024). A company that earned RMB 35.8 billion a year earlier posting negative ROE and negative cash flow a year later — that change cannot reflect a change in business quality, only the cost of fighting a war.

Notably, the direction of cash flow has already turned: net operating cash inflow in Q2 2026 was RMB 9.7 billion, 4.5x quarterly net profit of RMB 2.155 billion. Meituan's money is collected before it is paid out (users pay first, merchants settle later, riders are paid weekly), so as long as order volume holds, operating cash flow naturally leads profit. The "operating cash flow fails" criterion therefore no longer holds in practice as of Q2 2026 — it is only the annual measure that has yet to flip.

Why the gross margin only rates a "barely passing" grade

A 33.5% gross margin is not high for an internet platform: Pinduoduo at 57.3%, Alibaba e-commerce around 40%, Meituan at just 33.5%, because it books rider costs in cost of sales—it does not sell traffic; it sells "getting the meal into your hands," and more than 60% of the cost of that is hard cost.

"Barely" has two reasons. First, the trend: the cost-of-sales ratio fell from 67.1% in 2025Q2 to 66.5%, down only 0.6 percentage points, driven by grocery retail improvement and operating leverage, not structural price increases. Second, the floor: if subsidies restart and low-margin self-operated categories (Xiaoxiang, Kuailehou) keep rising as a share, gross margin will fall further. Full-year 2025 is the example—revenue grew 8.1%, and gross margin was dragged down enough to push Core Local Commerce straight into a loss.

But is it fatal: reversible, but it takes time

There is only one criterion for judging whether this is fatal: are these failing metrics structural or cyclical? A structural example is JD.com's first-party retail (gross margin is inherently 8%–15% and will never clear the 30% line); a cyclical example is Meituan today. The difference: Meituan's core local commerce operating margin was 20.9% in 2024 and its net margin 10.6% (358 ÷ 3,375) — it once cleared both lines with ease.

The cost is time. It still posted a net loss of RMB 4.67 billion in the first half of 2026, and will most likely lose RMB 5–7 billion for the full year (rough math: H1 -RMB 4.67 billion; Q3 weakens sequentially on rider subsidies and heavier in-store investment; Q4 is the peak investment quarter). That is, annual ROE, net margin and free cash flow will not turn positive until 2027 at the earliest. Failing these metrics will not be fatal, but it leaves the valuation without an anchor until 2027 — which is precisely why this can only be a small position.

What remains after weeding out the weak: a verifiable turnaround candidate

The seven hard metrics serve to screen out 90% of companies. Meituan stays here not because of all seven, but because of three: revenue is still growing (+10.1%), the balance sheet is still net cash (RMB 168.3 billion in cash and short-term investments), and gross margin has not collapsed (33.5%). These three ensure it will not die before the war ends—a point more important than many realize; in the 2025 battle, the players who faltered first were actually the smaller-share ones.

So it enters the "watchlist" differently from others: not by waiting for it to get cheap, but by waiting for it to become a company that "passes five of seven." The trigger is specific—if in the first half of 2027 there is "core local commerce operating margin back above 12% + annual operating cash flow turning positive + net profit turning positive," at least four of the seven will flip automatically. The real use of negative screening on Meituan is to treat it as a health check sheet, measured once a quarter.

One more point: why not fall back on PB or PS

Loss-making companies are usually valued on PB or PS, and neither suits Meituan. PB fails because most of its core assets (rider network, merchant relationships, dispatch algorithm) sit off the balance sheet, so book net assets do not reflect the business. PS fails because revenue mixes in low-margin, high-turnover self-operated merchandise sales, which inflates revenue and depresses PS, making the stock look "cheap" when it is not.

The only workable method is "normalized PE + net cash", which is also the approach used in the working papers. The cost is that it rests on an assumption rather than a fact — hence a margin of safety of only 6, not 8.

Conclusion

Two and a half of seven pass (gross margin, interest-bearing debt, revenue growth); four fail, concentrated in profit and cash flow. Not fatal, because the same asset base earned RMB 35.8 billion in 2024 — the problem is the price war, not the business structure. But it cannot be read as good news either: the company will still post a full-year loss, and the four annual metrics will not turn positive until 2027 at the earliest.

How to use it: put it on the "to be repaired" list and measure it with this table every quarter; only when five or more of the seven pass and the price is still below 15x normalized profit is it time to build a heavy position. For now it is a small position to test the thesis, not a pass on quality.
