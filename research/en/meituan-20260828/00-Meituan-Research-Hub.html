RMB 2.69 billion turned positive, but this is not money won back

📝 Conclusion: Buy, build in small tranches

Buy; build a small position in tranches. The stock closed at HKD 77.5 on 2026/08/28, implying a market cap of HKD 478.6 billion. The Q2 report released that evening marked a turning point: revenue of RMB 104.64 billion, +14.4%; operating profit swung back into positive territory at RMB 2.69 billion; core local commerce operating profit of RMB 5.67 billion, +52.3% YoY; and new business losses narrowed to RMB 1.74 billion. That said, H1 still showed a net loss of RMB 4.67 billion, and management explicitly guided to higher costs in Q3 and heavier investment in in-store. On valuation, 2027 normalized profit implies about 15x, or about 12x after net cash, for a payoff ratio of 1 : 1.8. Buy, but only a small position — if Q3 core local commerce operating profit falls below RMB 2 billion, this investment is wrong.
