📝 Conclusion: Avoid

Avoid. Palantir's 2026Q2 scorecard is one rarely seen in software: revenue of USD 1.935 billion, up 93% YoY, a 47% GAAP operating margin, a 63% free cash flow margin, and full-year guidance raised to USD 8.15 billion. The business is real, and so is the growth. The problem is the price. A USD 456 billion market cap on 74x TTM price-to-sales has already discounted six years of 30%-plus annual growth with no missteps. Across three scenarios, the bull case offers just 3% upside and the base case sits 37% lower. This is not an investment judgment; it is a bet that the market will keep paying a premium for perfection. The conditions for revisiting are clear: price-to-sales back below 30x (about USD 80), or order TCV growth back above revenue growth with non-US revenue accelerating. Until both appear, we stay out.
