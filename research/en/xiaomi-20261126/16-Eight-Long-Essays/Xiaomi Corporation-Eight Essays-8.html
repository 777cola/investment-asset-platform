Decision framework: what price to buy at, what signal to add on, what conditions to exit on — Xiaomi Corporation

Turn the judgment into three numbers: 23, 20, 32

Core view

The decision framework must turn a "view" into "action." For Xiaomi, only three numbers are needed: HKD 23 (start building the position), HKD 20 (add to 5%), HKD 32 (exit entirely). Add one non-price signal: smartphone gross margin rising quarter on quarter for two consecutive quarters. These four conditions form a complete buy-and-sell discipline.

Why HKD 23

HKD 23 corresponds to 21.8x 2027 neutral net profit of RMB 25 billion, a market cap of HKD 592 billion. This price assumes "memory prices fall back in H2 2027 + annual vehicle deliveries of 480,000 units," both neutral assumptions, leaving room for "getting half the assumptions wrong."

Working backwards: if 2027 net profit is only RMB 18 billion (bear case), HKD 23 implies 30x — still expensive; if RMB 33 billion (bull case), it implies 16x — very cheap. So HKD 23 is not "cheap," it is "symmetric odds" — 36% upside, 13% downside (to neutral), worth betting on.

Why not the current HKD 26.60? Because the current price implies 25x 2027 neutral earnings, with 36% upside and 54% downside (to bear case) — odds of 1:1.5, asymmetric to the downside. Waiting for another 13% decline flips the odds.

Why add to the position at HKD 20

HKD 20 corresponds to 18.9x 2027 neutral net profit and 26.3x pessimistic net profit, with a market cap of HKD 514.8 billion. Even after discounting the neutral case, it is still 19x — not expensive for a manufacturer with a net margin of about 5%. That is what a margin of safety means — not "I think it's cheap," but "even if I'm wrong, I don't lose too much."

Adding to the position requires one more condition: two consecutive quarters of operating profit in the auto business. If the price reaches 20 but autos swing to a loss, the pessimistic scenario is playing out — then you should not add; you should wait. Price and fundamentals both in place is the sufficient condition for adding.

Single-position cap at 6%. The reason is excessive profit volatility — net profit fell 68.4% year on year in Q3 2026, and at that level of volatility, a 6% position can already contribute 4 points of NAV swing. Above 6%, you are using the portfolio to bet on a cycle.

Why clear the position at HKD 32

HKD 32 corresponds to 30x 2027 neutral net profit and 23x optimistic net profit. At this level, even if the optimistic scenario materializes, the annualized return is only in the single digits.

The more important reason is "the boundary of the valuation method." Xiaomi's historical range is 15-40x: 40x was the 2025 peak of the dual narrative of "ecosystem platform + auto growth," and 15x was the 2022 low of the "smartphone downturn." 32x has already entered the zone that "needs a new story to support it," and the 2026 story is "auto revenue -19%."

If the share price rebounds above HKD 32 without fundamental improvement, it means the market is once again paying for narrative. At that point the right move is to trim, not add — because you are not earning the company's money, you are earning the next buyer's money.

Timeline and "what would change my mind"

January 2027: first full month of Pengcheng deliveries (watch whether it exceeds 15,000 units). March 2027: annual report + Q4 (watch whether autos are profitable for two consecutive quarters and whether inventory is written down). May 2027: Q1 (watch for the first signal of a sequential rebound in handset gross margin). The monthly delivery announcement on the 1st and the mid-month memory contract price are the two high-frequency tracking points.

What would make me upgrade from "Watch" to "Buy"? Two of three conditions: ① handset gross margin rebounds sequentially for two consecutive quarters; ② autos post operating profit for two consecutive quarters with sequential delivery growth; ③ Pengcheng monthly deliveries reach 20,000 units. Note that all three are "facts that have already happened," not "expectations" — that is the core of the discipline.

What would make me downgrade from "Watch" to "Avoid"? Two conditions simultaneously: ① handset gross margin falls below 8% with no sign of recovery; ② autos swing back to a loss. Or one condition: Lei Jun steps down for health reasons with no clear succession plan. The former is a business problem, the latter a governance problem — both warrant an exit.

There is also an easily overlooked "soft risk": narrative risk. The market gave Xiaomi 40x in 2025 because the "auto growth + ecosystem platform" stories held simultaneously; in 2026 both stories weakened and the multiple fell to 32x. If auto growth returns to single digits in 2027 and the ecosystem adds no new categories, the market may assign only 15–18x — this "narrative depreciation" requires no deterioration in fundamentals, only the absence of a new story.

Conclusion

Decision framework: build the position at HKD 23, add at HKD 20, exit at HKD 32, cap any single name at 6%, and validate with four fundamental signals. The advantage of this framework is that it requires no forecasting—no need to guess when memory peaks, no need to guess how much Pengcheng can sell, only to act when price or facts appear.
