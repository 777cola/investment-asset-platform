Decision framework: what price to buy at, what signal to add on, what conditions to exit on — Xiaomi Corporation

Turn the judgment into three numbers: 23, 20, 32

Core view

The decision framework has to convert a "view" into "action." For Xiaomi, three numbers suffice: HKD 23 (start building the position), HKD 20 (add to 5%), HKD 32 (exit entirely). Add one non-price signal: smartphone gross margin up QoQ for two consecutive quarters. Together these four conditions form a complete buy-and-sell discipline.

Why HKD 23

HKD 23 implies 21.8x 2027 neutral net profit of RMB 25 billion, or a market cap of HKD 592 billion. The price embeds two neutral assumptions — memory prices falling back in H2 2027 and annual vehicle deliveries of 480,000 units — leaving room to get half of them wrong.

Working backwards, 2027 net profit of just RMB 18 billion (bear case) would put HKD 23 at 30x — still expensive; RMB 33 billion (bull case) would put it at 16x — very cheap. HKD 23 is therefore not "cheap" but "symmetric odds": 36% upside versus 13% downside (to neutral), a bet worth taking.

Why not the current HKD 26.60? That price implies 25x 2027 neutral earnings, with 36% upside and 54% downside (to bear case) — odds of 1:1.5, skewed to the downside. A further 13% decline flips the odds.

Why add to the position at HKD 20

HKD 20 implies 18.9x 2027 neutral net profit and 26.3x pessimistic net profit, for a market cap of HKD 514.8 billion. Even on the neutral case it is still 19x — not expensive for a manufacturer with a net margin of about 5%. That is what a margin of safety means — not "I think it's cheap," but "even if I'm wrong, I don't lose too much."

Adding to the position requires one more condition: two consecutive quarters of operating profit in the auto business. If the price reaches 20 but autos swing to a loss, the pessimistic scenario is playing out — then you should not add; you should wait. Price and fundamentals both in place is the sufficient condition for adding.

Single-position cap at 6%. The reason is excessive profit volatility — net profit fell 68.4% YoY in Q3 2026, and at that level of volatility, a 6% position can already contribute 4 points of NAV swing. Above 6%, you are using the portfolio to bet on a cycle.

Why clear the position at HKD 32

HKD 32 equates to 30x 2027 neutral net profit and 23x optimistic net profit. At this level, even if the optimistic scenario plays out, the annualised return is only in the single digits.

More important is the boundary of the valuation method. Xiaomi's historical range is 15-40x: 40x marked the 2025 peak of the dual narrative of "ecosystem platform + auto growth," while 15x marked the 2022 trough of the "smartphone downturn." 32x has already entered the zone that needs a new story to support it, and the 2026 story is "auto revenue -19%."

If the share price rebounds above HKD 32 without fundamental improvement, the market is once again paying for narrative. At that point the right move is to trim, not add — because you are not earning the company's money, you are earning the next buyer's money.

Timeline and "what would change my mind"

January 2027: first full month of Pengcheng deliveries (watch for a print above 15,000 units). March 2027: annual report plus Q4 (watch for two consecutive quarters of auto profitability and any inventory write-down). May 2027: Q1 (watch for the first sign of a sequential rebound in handset gross margin). The monthly delivery announcement on the 1st and the mid-month memory contract price are the two high-frequency tracking points.

What would move me from "Watch" to "Buy"? Two of three conditions: ① handset gross margin rebounds sequentially for two consecutive quarters; ② autos post operating profit for two consecutive quarters alongside sequential delivery growth; ③ Pengcheng monthly deliveries reach 20,000 units. All three are facts that have already happened, not expectations — that is the core of the discipline.

What would move me from "Watch" to "Avoid"? Two conditions simultaneously: ① handset gross margin falls below 8% with no sign of recovery; ② autos swing back to a loss. Or one condition alone: Lei Jun steps down for health reasons with no clear succession plan. The former is a business problem, the latter a governance problem — both warrant an exit.

There is also an easily overlooked soft risk: narrative risk. The market paid 40x for Xiaomi in 2025 because the "auto growth + ecosystem platform" stories held at the same time; in 2026 both weakened and the multiple de-rated to 32x. If auto growth returns to single digits in 2027 and the ecosystem adds no new categories, the market may assign only 15–18x — this narrative depreciation requires no deterioration in fundamentals, only the absence of a new story.

Conclusion

Decision framework: build the position at HKD 23, add at HKD 20, exit at HKD 32, cap any single name at 6%, and validate with four fundamental signals. Its advantage is that it requires no forecasting—no need to call the memory peak, no need to guess how much Pengcheng can sell, only to act when price or facts appear.
