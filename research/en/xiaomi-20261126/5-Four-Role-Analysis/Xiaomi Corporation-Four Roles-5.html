Team lead summary: disagreements and rulings across the four perspectives — Xiaomi Corporation

Consensus view: the cycle is exogenous and valuations are rich. Key debate: where is the ceiling for autos?

Core view

Four roles ran the same set of financials and converged on three points while splitting on a fourth. Agreement: good company, bad cycle, not cheap. Disagreement: is the auto business "a long slope just past the break-even line" or "a peak within a price war." That split does not affect today's decision, only the position cap after purchase.

Consensus point one: the profit collapse is exogenous rather than a loss of operational control

All four agree. Revenue fell just 5.2% and came in slightly above consensus, while deliveries were broadly flat and share was not lost, pointing to no operational problem. Of the RMB 8.39 billion profit decline, RMB 4.24 billion came from gross profit (memory price increases), RMB 2.19 billion from expenses (deliberate investment in R&D and stores) and RMB 1.97 billion from swings in investment items. None of these three items reflects "goods that cannot be sold."

Consensus point two: the business cannot earn excess profit

Buffett would point to gross margin (20.2%, and falling), Munger to industry structure (homogeneous, no pricing power), Duan Yongping to the nature of the business (the more it sells, the harder costs are tested) and Li Lu to industry position (an integrator holding pricing power in no single link). Four paths, one destination: this is a good company, not a good business.

Consensus point three: 32x is not a buy price

All four carry target prices below the current level. Buffett is the harshest at HKD 10.3–12.9, Li Lu the mildest at a HKD 21 midpoint, while Duan Yongping and Munger give no specific number but both say "wait." Weighted, the midpoint sits at HKD 20–23, 13%–25% below the current price. That does not mean the stock must fall, only that this price leaves no room for error.

Point of contention: is autos a long runway or a peak

Duan Yongping and Li Lu are positive: 10.88 vehicles delivered, the segment is turning profitable, 7.0 locked orders for the Pengcheng and overseas expansion in 2027 — a curve still climbing. Chinese automakers' globalisation has only just begun, and Xiaomi has a shot at leading the second tier.

Buffett and Munger are cautious: the first profit may come from expense normalisation rather than scale effects; the 20.0–30.0 price band is the bloodiest battlefield; average selling price per vehicle fell 19% in a year, showing brand strength is not yet enough to support pricing.Munger's line would be: "One quarter of profit to prove a business — too early."

Verdict: size positions around "the bad case isn't fatal", not "the good case is sexy"

The Team Lead's ruling translates the disagreement into a position cap. Under the long-slope view (Duan/Li), 2027 net profit of RMB 33 billion at 26x implies HKD 36; under the peak view (Ba/Munger), net profit of RMB 18 billion at 16x implies HKD 12. That is a threefold spread between the two outcomes, which makes this an asymmetric payoff: the upside requires three things to hold at once, the downside only one.

Hence no Buy rating — a Watch instead. Watch position cap 3%, pending one of two signals: the share price below HKD 23 (payoff becomes symmetric), or handset gross margin up QoQ for two consecutive quarters (evidence of a cyclical bottom). If both signals appear, add to 5%; if one appears, hold at 3%; if neither appears and the share price rebounds above HKD 32, cut to 0.

Conclusion

Verdict: Watch, with a watchlist position cap of 3% and a buy trigger price of HKD 23. All four perspectives — exogenous cycle, middling business, expensive price — point the same way and underpin the "do not buy" case; the one divergence, the auto ceiling, does not change the current decision, only the position cap. We call it Watch rather than Avoid because product strength, execution and balance sheet are all intact — what is broken is the cycle and the price. Wait for memory to peak, or wait for the price to come down.
