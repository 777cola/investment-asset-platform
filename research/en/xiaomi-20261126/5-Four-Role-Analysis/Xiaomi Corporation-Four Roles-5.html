Team Lead summary: disagreements and rulings across the four perspectives — Xiaomi Corporation

Consensus: the cycle is exogenous and valuations are rich. Disagreement: where is the ceiling for autos?

Core view

Four roles ran the same set of financials and reached three points of agreement and one disagreement. Agreement: good company, bad cycle, not cheap. Disagreement: is the auto business "a long slope just past the break-even line" or "a peak within a price war." This disagreement does not affect the decision today, only the position cap after purchase.

Consensus point one: the profit collapse is exogenous, not a loss of operational control

All four agree on this. Revenue fell only 5.2% and came in slightly above the consensus, while deliveries were broadly flat and share was not lost, showing no operational problem. Of the RMB 8.39 billion drop in profit, RMB 4.24 billion came from gross profit (memory price increases), RMB 2.19 billion from expenses (deliberate investment in R&D and stores), and RMB 1.97 billion from swings in investment items. None of these three items is a case of "goods that cannot be sold."

Consensus point two: this business cannot earn excess profit

Buffett points to gross margin (20.2%, and falling), Munger to industry structure (homogeneous, no pricing power), Duan Yongping to the nature of the business (the more it sells, the harder costs are tested), and Li Lu to industry position (an integrator that holds pricing power in no single link). Four paths lead to the same place: this is a good company, not a good business.

Consensus point three: 32x is not a buy price

All four have target prices below the current price: Buffett is the harshest (HKD 10.3–12.9), Li Lu the mildest (HKD 21 midpoint), while Duan Yongping and Munger give no specific number but both say "wait." Weighted, the midpoint sits at HKD 20–23, 13%–25% below the current price. This does not mean the stock must fall, but that this price leaves no room for error.

Point of contention: is autos a long runway or a peak

Duan Yongping and Li Lu lean positive: 10.88 vehicles delivered, segment turning profitable, 7.0 locked orders for the Pengcheng, overseas expansion in 2027 — a curve still climbing; Chinese automakers' globalization has only just begun, and Xiaomi has a shot at leading the second tier.

Buffett and Munger lean cautious: the first profit may come from expense normalization rather than scale effects; the 20.0–30.0 price band is the bloodiest battlefield; average selling price per vehicle fell 19% in a year, showing brand strength is not yet enough to support pricing.Munger's line would be: "One quarter of profit to prove a business — too early."

Verdict: size positions on "the bad case isn't fatal," not on "the good case is sexy"

The Team Lead's ruling quantifies the disagreement into a position cap. If the auto business is a long slope (Duan/Li's view), 2027 net profit of RMB 33 billion at 26x gives HKD 36; if it is a peak (Ba/Munger's view), net profit of RMB 18 billion at 16x gives HKD 12. The two outcomes differ threefold, making this an "asymmetric payoff" name — the upside requires three things to hold simultaneously, while the downside needs only one.

Hence: no Buy rating; a Watch instead. Watch position cap 3%, awaiting one of two signals — the share price below HKD 23 (payoff becomes symmetric), or handset gross margin rising quarter-on-quarter for two consecutive quarters (evidence of a cyclical bottom). If both signals appear, add to 5%; if one appears, hold at 3%; if neither appears and the share price rebounds above HKD 32, cut to 0.

Conclusion

Verdict: Watch, with a watchlist position cap of 3%, buy trigger price HKD 23. The consensus across the four perspectives (exogenous cycle, middling business, expensive price) forms the case for "do not buy"; the divergence (the auto ceiling) does not affect the current decision, only the position cap. The call is "Watch" rather than "Avoid" because the company's product strength, execution and balance sheet are all intact — what is broken is the cycle and the price. Wait for memory to peak, or wait for the price to come down.
