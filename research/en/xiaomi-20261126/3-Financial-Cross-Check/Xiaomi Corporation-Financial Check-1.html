Core view

We cross-checked the key 2026Q3 figures across two sources — company filings versus media flashes, consensus and our derived values. Seven of the eight items deviate by less than 0.2%. The one large deviation is the automotive segment's operating profit: we derive RMB 0.5 billion, the sell-side derives RMB 1.2 billion, a 140% gap.

The gap reflects definition, not arithmetic. The sell-side calculates "automotive vehicles" standalone, whereas the company's segment includes AI innovation businesses and shared-cost allocation. We take only the direction (turning positive), not the amount, and avoid citing any absolute automotive segment profit figure throughout this report. Revenue, deliveries and gross margin show zero deviation and can be used directly.

The over-50% deviation: what exactly is the auto segment's profit

Start with the facts. The announcement states only that "the Smart EV and AI innovation business segment achieved operating profit for the first time in a single quarter" and discloses no absolute figure. The market has therefore been running its own numbers: some sell-side analysts assume a 26% gross margin and RMB 4.8 billion of expenses, deriving RMB 1.2 billion; I assume a 21.5% gross margin and RMB 4.5 billion of expenses, deriving about RMB 500 million. The two estimates differ by more than a factor of two.

Three factors explain the gap. First, scope of the denominator: the company's segment includes AI innovation businesses (chips, robotics and so on), with revenue of about RMB 300-500 million and meaningful expenses, which sell-side analysts often strip out. Second, expense allocation: the company gives no rules for allocating shared procurement between autos and phones, cloud services and store costs. Third, one-off items: 2026Q2 included one-off provisions related to stores and capacity (the market broadly reads it this way), which normalized in Q3; this item may have contributed more to the "swing to positive on a sequential basis" than gross margin improvement did.

The approach is to cite no absolute figures.The text uses only the directional statement "first single-quarter operating profit"; the m07 breakdown explicitly states that "profit came from expenses normalizing, not from gross margin expansion," and sets out what it would mean if that judgment is wrong—if the auto gross margin really is above 25%, that is better news and does not affect the "Watch" conclusion.

Revenue, deliveries and total share capital all came in without deviation

Revenue of RMB 107.24 billion came in 0.8% ahead of the RMB 106.4 billion consensus — the only "beat" this quarter, and it was a beat against a downwardly revised estimate. Sell-side analysts cut Q3 revenue forecasts from RMB 112 billion to RMB 106.4 billion over the past three months, so the "beat" really means "didn't fall as much."

Deliveries of 108,800 units match the sum of the monthly announcements — the hardest item to get wrong, given the company discloses it on the 1st of every month. Total shares of 25.741 billion versus 25.736 billion on HKEXnews, a 0.02% difference stemming from the timing of buyback cancellations. We use the announcement figure, as market cap calculation requires the latest share count.

Back-solving the 2025Q3 base: three figures that cross-check

The announcement discloses only YoY growth rates, not absolute prior-year comparatives, so the base must be derived: revenue 1,072.39÷0.948 = RMB 113.12 billion; net profit 38.76÷0.316 = RMB 12.27 billion; adjusted net profit 48.84÷0.432 = RMB 11.31 billion.

The three figures cross-check: 2025Q3 net margin 122.7÷1,131.2 = 10.85%, adjusted net margin 10.0%, consistent with the basis disclosed in the 2025 third-quarter report (consensus at the time was "RMB 11.3 billion profit in the quarter, 10% net margin"). The derived figures match the public record and can be used.

Gross profit of RMB 25.90 billion is calculated as 1,131.2×22.9%; the error depends on the decimal places of the gross margin — at 22.85%, gross profit differs by RMB 60 million, which does not affect the conclusion that gross profit fell by RMB 4.24 billion.

Final adopted basis
  • Revenue, gross margin, net profit and adjusted net profit: as reported by the company, with no adjustments.
  • Vehicle deliveries: 108,800 units, as reported.
  • Vehicle segment revenue: RMB 22.9 billion, derived from the -19% YoY change; labelled "approximately" throughout.
  • Vehicle segment operating profit: direction only ("turned positive"), with no amount disclosed.
  • Segment revenue split (smartphone x AIoT approximately RMB 83.6 billion, others approximately RMB 0.7 billion): derived as "total revenue minus vehicle segment", labelled "derived".
  • Valuation: market cap HKD 685 billion, RMB conversion 0.92, P/E on an annualised basis, all noted in m04 and m17.

Conclusion

Eight checks, seven at zero deviation or within 0.1%, are safe to use. The one large deviation — automotive segment operating profit — is now identified as a definitional issue rather than a data error. All conclusions in this report rest on just three zero-deviation figures: revenue of RMB 107.24 billion, gross margin of 20.2%, and adjusted net profit of RMB 4.88 billion. These three drive the 32x P/E calculation and the "Watch" rating.
