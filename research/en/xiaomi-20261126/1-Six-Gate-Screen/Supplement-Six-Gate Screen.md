## The first three hurdles: a 20% gross margin business that survives on efficiency

Start with the shape of this business. 2026Q3 revenue was RMB 107.239 billion, gross profit RMB 21.66 billion (at a 20.2% gross margin), net profit RMB 3.876 billion, and adjusted net profit RMB 4.884 billion. **Sell RMB 100 of goods, get RMB 20 of gross profit, and RMB 4.6 lands in adjusted net profit.** For consumer electronics that is not bad—the global handset industry averaged a gross margin of around 15% in 2026Q3—but it is far from a "good business."

Circle of competence scores 8. All four segments are understandable: phones sell hardware, IoT sells an ecosystem, autos sell vehicles, and internet services sell advertising and game revenue sharing. The hard part is not understanding them but pinning down the long-run margin of the auto segment—the company discloses only deliveries and segment gross margin in its quarterly reports, not per-vehicle cost or an expense breakdown.

Moat scores 7. Three things are real: first, the brand—models priced above RMB 3,000 have held steady at over 30% of domestic mix; second, the human-car-home ecosystem, where phones, car units and home devices share one account and one system, raising switching costs; third, channel efficiency, with Xiaomi Home's inventory turnover and sales per square meter leading the industry. But the moat has only one test—can it raise prices? 2026Q3 auto revenue fell 19% year on year on broadly flat deliveries, implying average selling price per vehicle dropped nearly 20%; on phones, when memory prices rose, the company chose to absorb the cost rather than pass it on through higher prices. **Absorbing it is a capability; being unable to raise prices is fate.**

## Why the second gate fails: not low gross margin, but gross margin heading lower

Last year the read was "mid-range gross margin, thin net margin." This year that call has to change. The 2026Q3 consolidated gross margin was 20.2%, down from 22.9% a year earlier, a drop of 2.7 percentage points, taking gross profit from RMB 25.90 billion to RMB 21.66 billion, a decline of RMB 4.24 billion. Net profit over the same period fell from RMB 12.27 billion to RMB 3.876 billion, a decline of RMB 8.39 billion—**the RMB 4.24 billion of lost gross profit is only half of it; the other half comes from fixed costs and a reversal in investment income.**

Fixed costs are easy to understand: R&D runs above RMB 9 billion a quarter (RMB 9.2 billion in Q2, +18.9% year on year), and none of the three lines—XRING chips, autonomous driving and AI—can be paused; the fixed costs of auto stores and production capacity do not disappear just because a quarter's sales are lower. Investment income swung from a positive contribution last year to a negative one this quarter—net profit of RMB 3.876 billion was RMB 1.008 billion below adjusted net profit of RMB 4.884 billion, implying that investment items were a drag rather than a subsidy this quarter (inferred).

So the 4 points on the second gate is not a complaint that gross margin is thin, but that there is no buffer when gross margin falls. A company with a 45% gross margin that loses 3 points might see net profit fall only 10%; a company with a 20% gross margin that loses 3 points can see net profit halve. **That is the leverage effect of a "thin business," and the third quarter of 2026 taught everyone a lesson.**

## Stage four: Lei Jun has not changed; the industry has

Management scores 8; the two points deducted are not about capability. Lei Jun announced the car in 2021, first deliveries in March 2024, and by 2026 quarterly deliveries reached 108,800 units (10.88 万辆) with the segment turning profitable—five years to cover a path others take ten, execution that ranks top-tier even by global manufacturing standards. Premiumization of phones follows the same playbook: from being mocked in 2021 for "benchmarking Apple" to models above RMB 3,000 accounting for 30% in 2026, ground out one product generation at a time.

A bonus point for one remark. On the earnings call, Lu Weibing said "this is the toughest quarter of my career"—executives at Chinese hardware companies rarely admit difficulty in public; most would frame a -68% net profit decline as "proactive adjustment." **Admitting difficulty means next quarter's guidance carries more credibility.** He also gave two directions: the extended-range SUV "Pengcheng" locked more than 70,000 orders (7.0 万辆) in its first month, and entry into Europe in 2027.

The deduction is for capital-allocation transparency. The HKD 20 billion buyback announced early in the year (about 2.9% of market cap) has been executed well, but the company has not paid a single cash dividend since listing in 2018; how much has been invested in the car business cumulatively and what the per-vehicle cost structure looks like are given only as direction on calls, not as numbers. **This does not affect "whether he can be trusted"; it affects "whether he can be sized up."**

## Fifth and sixth hurdles: the 32x denominator needs two quarters before it can be verified

HKD 26.60, 25.741 billion shares, market cap HKD 685 billion, about RMB 630 billion. On Q3 adjusted net profit of RMB 4.884 billion annualized to RMB 19.5 billion, that is 32x; on full-year 2026 adjusted net profit of about RMB 20 billion, 31.6x. For Xiaomi's own history this multiple sits below the midpoint — at the 2025 peak the market gave it over 40x — **but "it was more expensive before" is not a reason to buy.**

Margin of safety scores 3, because the three-scenario odds are unattractive: bear case HKD 12.2 (-54%), base case HKD 23.2 (-13%), bull case HKD 36.2 (+36%); weighted 25/50/25, expected value HKD 23.7, 11% below the current price. In other words, **buying now means paying full price for a good outcome with less than even odds.**

On discipline the verdict is "questionable" rather than "fail", because the premises are verifiable: ① handset gross margin recovers sequentially; ② the auto segment posts two consecutive quarters of operating profit; ③ Pengcheng monthly deliveries climb above 20,000 units. All three numbers will surface in the March 2027 annual report and Q1 operating data; the cost of waiting is six months. **For a company in transition whose earnings are still being cut, six months is not long.**

## Veto items and items pending verification

- **Veto one: margin of safety.**32x annualized P/E against a quarter with net profit down 68% YoY and gross margin still sliding leaves no cushion.

- **Veto two: earnings visibility.**Memory contract prices rose 60%–70% in early 2026, and institutions expect the shortage to persist into 2027; that means the trough in handset gross margin is not this quarter.

- **To verify one:**whether Q4 handset gross margin can recover more than 1 percentage point QoQ (memory spot prices have already loosened ahead of contract prices).

- **To verify two:**whether the auto segment can post operating profit for a second consecutive quarter, and without one-off expense reversals driving the profit.

- **To verify three:**the conversion rate on Pengcheng's 70,000 locked orders — locked orders typically convert to deliveries at 70%, and whether monthly deliveries can reach 20,000 units (2.0 million shares) decides whether 2027 auto revenue is RMB 90 billion or RMB 120 billion.
