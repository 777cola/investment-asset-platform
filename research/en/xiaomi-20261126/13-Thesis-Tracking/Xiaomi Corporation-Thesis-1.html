Thesis — Xiaomi Corporation

Core thesis

Framing the Xiaomi bull case as five verifiable assumptions gives: one already broken (rising internet services share), one partly validated (quarterly profit at the auto unit) and three still to be validated (smartphone gross margin recovery, Pengcheng volume ramp, Europe rollout). That distribution makes the thesis not "proven" but "pending verification."

The most critical is smartphone gross margin. It determines whether 2027 net profit is RMB 18 billion or RMB 28 billion, and whether a 32x valuation is expensive or reasonable. The verification window is the annual report in March 2027.

The most critical point: handset gross margin

If you track only one number, track handset gross margin: it is the denominator of the entire thesis. Of the 20.2% blended gross margin in 2026Q3, handsets were the main drag. If margin recovers above 10% in 2027, net profit can reach RMB 26–28 billion and 32x becomes 23x on 2027 numbers; if it keeps sliding to 6%, net profit is only RMB 18 billion and 32x becomes 35x. Same share price, two fates.

Verification must be specific: watch two things. First, monthly memory contract prices (TrendForce basis); second, handset gross margin in the quarterly reports. Contract prices move first and the financials follow with a 1–2 quarter lag — so once contract prices roll over you can turn optimistic without waiting for the reports.

The evidence so far points to "loosening but no turn": memory spot prices have edged down since October 2026, but contract prices remain elevated, and institutions are sharply divided on 2027 (continued shortage vs. a peak in the first half). This assumption is now 50/50.

Autos: Partly validated, but the evidence is not yet solid

The first-ever quarterly operating profit is the first piece of evidence for this thesis, but its quality is questionable: the profit may reflect expense normalisation (the one-off accrual in Q2 did not repeat) rather than scale effects. Two figures matter in verifying scale effects: whether per-vehicle gross profit rises with deliveries, and whether per-vehicle operating expenses fall with deliveries.

The company discloses neither, leaving only indirect indicators: if 2026Q4 deliveries grow QoQ and segment profit improves at the same time, scale effects are the most likely explanation; if deliveries grow but profit does not improve, expense normalisation is. That judgment cannot be made until March 2027.

The risk sits with Pengcheng. Extended-range SUVs typically carry higher per-vehicle gross profit than battery-electric vehicles (smaller battery, higher pricing); if Pengcheng scales up, automotive segment gross margin could rise from 21% to 24% in 2027. This is the core assumption of the bull case and the most vulnerable point of the bear case.

One already broken: internet services share

The assumption that "internet services' share will rise to 12%" dates from 2024; two years on it is still around 10%. Hardware revenue simply grew faster than services — autos contributed several tens of billions in revenue with no matching growth in services — so the denominator expanded while the numerator did not, and the share naturally failed to rise. That miss shows the "ecosystem monetisation" story has progressed more slowly at Xiaomi than expected.

Does that warrant a valuation cut? Yes. Internet services are Xiaomi's only high-gross-margin business (above 70%); without share gains, the ceiling on group gross margin cannot rise. This is the fundamental reason the "ecosystem platform" valuation method fails.

Falsification conditions: what would invalidate this thesis entirely
  • Handset gross margin below 8% for three consecutive quarters: would confirm that higher memory prices reflect a structural shortage rather than a cycle, and that Xiaomi's cost advantage has been permanently eroded.
  • Auto segment back in loss for two consecutive quarters: would confirm that Q3 profitability was an illusion created by expense normalisation, and that autos remain a cash-burning business.
  • Pengcheng monthly deliveries stuck below 10,000 units: would confirm that the 70,000 locked orders were a marketing number and that product competitiveness is insufficient to support the RMB 200,000–300,000 price band.
  • Europe plan pushed back to 2028 and beyond: would confirm the overseas path is unworkable, leaving no outlet from the domestic price war.
  • Lei Jun departs for health or other reasons with no clear succession plan: the governance discount would materialise immediately.

If any one of the above holds, downgrade from "Watch" to "Avoid"; if two hold simultaneously, exit the position.

Watch calendar
  • Early January 2027: first full-month delivery data for Pengcheng (monthly announcement); watch for a print above 15,000 units.
  • Mid-March 2027: 2026 annual report and Q4 data; check whether the auto business is profitable for a second consecutive quarter and whether inventory is impaired.
  • Late May 2027: 2027Q1 results; look for the first sign of a quarter-on-quarter rebound in handset gross margin.
  • 1st of each month: delivery announcement; mid-month each month: TrendForce memory contract prices.
  • Ongoing: European certification and first-store progress; conversion of Pengcheng locked orders into firm orders.

Conclusion

Thesis status: one broken, one partly validated, three pending. That distribution argues for watching, not buying. The key validation point is handset gross margin: it decides whether 2027 net profit lands at RMB 18 billion or RMB 28 billion, and whether 32x is expensive or reasonable. Any purchase before the annual report in March 2027 is a bet on the memory cycle, not on Xiaomi.
