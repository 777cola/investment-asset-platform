Core thesis

Writing the bull case for Xiaomi as five verifiable assumptions yields: one already broken (rising internet services share), one partly validated (quarterly profit at the auto unit), three still to be validated (smartphone gross margin recovery, Pengcheng volume ramp, Europe rollout). That distribution shows the thesis is not "proven" but "pending verification."

The most critical is smartphone gross margin. It determines whether 2027 net profit is RMB 18 billion or RMB 28 billion, and whether a 32x valuation is expensive or reasonable. The verification window is the annual report in March 2027.

The most critical point: handset gross margin

If you can track only one number, track handset gross margin. It is the denominator of the entire thesis: of the 20.2% blended gross margin in 2026Q3, the decline in handsets was the main driver; if margin recovers above 10% in 2027, net profit can reach RMB 26–28 billion, and 32x becomes 23x (on 2027 numbers); if it keeps sliding to 6%, net profit is only RMB 18 billion, and 32x becomes 35x. Same share price, two fates.

The verification method must be specific: watch two things. First, monthly memory contract prices (TrendForce basis); second, the company's handset gross margin in its quarterly reports. Contract prices move first, the financials follow, with a 1–2 quarter lag — so once contract prices roll over you can start turning optimistic; no need to wait for the reports.

The evidence so far is "loosening but no turn": memory spot prices have edged down since October 2026, but contract prices remain elevated, and institutions are sharply divided on 2027 (continued shortage vs. a peak in the first half). This assumption is now 50/50.

Autos: Partly validated, but the evidence is not yet solid

"First-ever quarterly operating profit" is the first piece of evidence for this thesis, but its quality is questionable: the profit may stem from expense normalization (the one-off accrual in Q2 did not repeat) rather than scale effects. To verify scale effects, two figures matter: whether per-vehicle gross profit rises with deliveries, and whether per-vehicle operating expenses fall with deliveries.

The company does not disclose either figure, so only indirect indicators are available: if 2026Q4 deliveries grow quarter-on-quarter and segment profit improves at the same time, it is most likely scale effects; if deliveries grow but profit does not improve, it is expense normalization. This judgment cannot be made until March 2027.

The risk lies with Pengcheng. Extended-range SUVs typically carry higher per-vehicle gross profit than battery-electric vehicles (smaller battery, higher pricing); if Pengcheng scales up, the automotive segment gross margin could rise from 21% to 24% in 2027. This is the core assumption of the bull case and the most vulnerable point of the bear case.

One already broken: internet services share

The assumption that "the internet services share will rise to 12%" was written in 2024; two years on it is still around 10%. The reason is that hardware revenue grew faster than services (autos contributed several tens of billions in revenue, with no corresponding growth in services); the denominator grew while the numerator did not, so the share naturally failed to rise. The failure of this assumption shows that the "ecosystem monetization" story has progressed more slowly at Xiaomi than expected.

Should the valuation be cut on this basis? Yes. Internet services are Xiaomi's only high-gross-margin business (above 70%); if its share does not rise, the ceiling on overall gross margin cannot rise. This is the fundamental reason the "ecosystem platform" valuation method fails.

Falsification conditions: what would invalidate this thesis entirely
  • Handset gross margin below 8% for three consecutive quarters: confirms memory price increases are a structural shortage, not a cycle, and Xiaomi's cost advantage has been permanently eroded.
  • Auto segment back to a loss for two consecutive quarters: confirms Q3 profitability was an illusion of expense normalization; autos remain a cash-burning business.
  • Pengcheng monthly deliveries stuck below 10,000 units: confirms the 70,000 locked orders were a marketing number, and product competitiveness is insufficient to support the RMB 200,000–300,000 price band.
  • Europe plan pushed back to 2028 and beyond: confirms the overseas path is unworkable, leaving no outlet from the domestic price war.
  • Lei Jun departs for health or other reasons with no clear succession plan: the governance discount would materialize immediately.

If any one of the above holds, downgrade from "Watch" to "Avoid"; if two hold simultaneously, exit the position.

Watch calendar
  • Early January 2027: Pengcheng's first full-month delivery data (monthly announcement); watch whether it exceeds 15,000 units.
  • Mid-March 2027: 2026 annual report + Q4 data; verify whether the auto business is profitable for a second consecutive quarter and whether inventory is impaired.
  • Late May 2027: 2027Q1 results; verify the first signal of a quarter-on-quarter rebound in handset gross margin.
  • 1st of each month: delivery announcement; mid-month each month: TrendForce memory contract prices.
  • Ongoing: European certification and first-store progress; conversion ratio of Pengcheng locked orders to firm orders.

Conclusion

Thesis status: one broken, one partly validated, three pending. This distribution does not support buying; it supports watching. The most critical validation point is handset gross margin—it determines whether 2027 net profit is RMB 18 billion or RMB 28 billion, and whether 32x is expensive or reasonable.Before the annual report in March 2027, any purchase is a bet on the memory cycle, not a bet on Xiaomi.
