📝 Conclusion: Buy, accumulate in tranches

Buy, scale in. Alphabet 2026Q3 revenue USD 127.58 billion, +25% YoY; operating profit USD 40.58 billion, operating margin 32%; Google Cloud revenue USD 30.71 billion, +103% YoY, operating margin 36.2% — spending USD 200 billion of capex in a single year while holding margins at 32%, the only one of the four to do so. The stock at USD 279.84 sits 30.5% below its 52-week high of USD 402.62; the market is selling the -USD 9.07 billion of free cash flow, USD 125.1 billion of long-term debt and the paused buyback. But at 21x EV/EBIT against 25% revenue growth, 36% cloud margins and a USD 56.1 billion backlog, this price buys only Search and Cloud — Gemini, Waymo and external TPU sales come essentially free. The worst antitrust outcome (breakup) is already off the table. Action: half position initially, fill out below USD 240; the only sell trigger is cloud growth falling below 30% without capex coming down.
