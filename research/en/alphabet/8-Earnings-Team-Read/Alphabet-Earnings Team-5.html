Core view

Four readers of the same earnings report converge on one point: all see cloud +103% and the 36.2% margin as the most valuable information in the report, since it is the only evidence that the RMB 200 billion capex "bought the right things." They diverge most on time horizon: Duan Yongping takes three years, Buffett five, Munger two, Li Lu ten. Different horizons produce completely different verdicts on the same FCF of -RMB 9.07 billion.

Consensus point: cloud is the whole answer to these results

All four paused on cloud, each for a different reason: Duan Yongping saw scale effects (operating profit +209%, faster than revenue at +103%), Buffett saw a good business with margins climbing (23.7% → 36.2%), Munger saw "the only evidence for judging whether the RMB 200 billion was spent right or wrong," and Li Lu saw "real AI demand." The conclusion is unanimous: if cloud breaks, the entire investment thesis breaks.

That also means risk monitoring needs to watch only one place: cloud revenue growth and cloud operating margin. Every other metric—net profit, EPS, search growth, YouTube—is secondary. This matters because it reduces complex tracking to a single variable.

The debate: does the RMB 9.07 billion negative FCF reflect investment or value destruction?

Buffett and Munger read it as a negative signal; Duan Yongping and Li Lu see it as normal for an investment phase. The disagreement is not about how they read the numbers but about how much they trust management's judgment. Buffett has seen too many cases of shareholders' money being bet on an uncertain return; Munger remembers telecom in 2000; Duan Yongping has run companies and knows expansion requires spending; Li Lu looks at the infrastructure landscape ten years out.

My verdict leans toward "investment", with one condition attached: if cloud growth cannot hold 50% and operating margin falls below 28% in 2027, this FCF is bleeding, not investment. In other words, the answer is not in the financials; it is in the 2027 financials. All that can be done now is to control position size.

Synthesis conclusion: three actions
  • Buy: At 21x EV/EBIT you are buying 25% growth and 36% cloud margins at a reasonable price. Open an initial position at half the target size.
  • Wait: Wait for Q1 and Q2 2027 cloud growth and depreciation data. Add in full below USD 240; stop adding above USD 350.
  • Watch: Track only four numbers: cloud growth, cloud margin, depreciation as a share of revenue, and FCF. Ignore everything else.

In one sentence: this report proves both that the business is getting better and that it is getting heavier. The former decides whether to buy; the latter decides how much.

Conclusion

Bottom line from the four masters: buy in tranches, half position initially. The cloud story is the entire thesis; free cash flow is the risk gauge. Three moves: buy half, wait two quarters, track only four numbers.
