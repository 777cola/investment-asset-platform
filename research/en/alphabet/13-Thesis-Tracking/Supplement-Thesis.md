## Hypothesis 1: Cloud growth — green, but the most easily misread

Q3 cloud revenue came in at RMB 30.71 billion, up 103% YoY — seemingly unstoppable. But the comparable base was RMB 15.16 billion, so half the growth came off a low base. Sustaining 100% growth every quarter through 2027 would require Q3 2027 cloud revenue of RMB 120 billion — unrealistic. The real assumption, then, is not "103% persists" but "still above 50% after deceleration."

The evidence sits in backlog: RMB 56.1 billion, slightly more than 55% of which is confirmed within 24 months. That covers only 13% of quarterly cloud revenue, implying growth rests mainly on immediate demand — high elasticity, low predictability. The assumption is therefore "green," but not "solidly green."

## Assumption 2: depreciation — negative, as the only bad thing that is "certain to happen"

RMB 200 billion of capex depreciated over six years is RMB 33.3 billion a year; by 2027 total depreciation doubles to over RMB 50 billion, equal to 10 percentage points of annualised revenue of RMB 500 billion. Absent gross margin improvement, operating margin falls from 32% to 22% — the most bearish calculation. Reality should be somewhat better, with the scale effect from doubling cloud revenue absorbing part of it.

Base case 30%, bear case 26%, bull case 32%. The key question is not "whether it falls" but "how far it falls" — a fall to 30% is a normal asset-heavy transformation; a fall to 26% means the valuation method has to change. The checkpoint is the depreciation detail in the 10-Q, not the language in the press release.

## Assumption 3: Free cash flow — in the red, but the nature of the red flag needs to be clear

Q3 came in at -RMB 9.07 billion, the second consecutive negative quarter, with TTM at just RMB 19.7 billion. That assumption is now clearly falsified. Two kinds of red flags are worth separating: one is "the business doesn't make money," the other is "all the money it makes is being invested back out." Alphabet is the latter—operating cash flow was positive at RMB 42.29 billion; capex of RMB 51.36 billion simply exceeded it.

But a red flag is a red flag, and an explanation doesn't turn it green. It means anyone buying now must accept at least 4–6 quarters without positive free cash flow. If that horizon exceeds your holding patience, this stock isn't for you—that's not a problem with the company, it's a problem of fit.

## Assumptions four to six: search, regulation, option pricing

Search (Yellow). Q3 +12% versus consensus +14%, a 2-percentage-point miss. This is not a collapse; it is the first observable signal that AI answers are starting to affect clicks. The threshold is set at —beyond that it becomes a business-model problem, not a valuation problem.

Regulation (Green). DOJ remedies exclude a breakup, the EU has only imposed fines, and appeals are underway. The worst structural risk (AdX divestiture) affects 3% of market cap. This can be downgraded from "core risk" to "background noise."

Option pricing (Green). Search at 15x EBIT is worth RMB 2.2 trillion and cloud at 25x 2027 EBIT is worth RMB 1 trillion—RMB 3.2 trillion combined, close to the RMB 3.42 trillion market cap. Gemini, Waymo and external TPU sales are genuinely not priced in—this is the source of the margin of safety, and also the source of risk if Waymo keeps losing RMB 8 billion a year.

## Falsification conditions and monitoring calendar

Three falsification conditions; halve the position if any one triggers:

- Cloud revenue growth below 30% for two consecutive quarters with no cut to capex guidance — the compute being bought cannot be sold.

- Operating margin below 26% for two consecutive quarters — depreciation has swallowed the operating leverage.

- Search and other revenue growth below 8% for two consecutive quarters — AI diversion has moved from "affecting growth" to "eroding the existing base".

Watch calendar: February 2027 Q4 results (full-year capex landing point, 2027 guidance and cloud growth); April 2027 Q1 results (first full disclosure of depreciation detail); July 2027 Q2 results (whether FCF turns positive); DOJ appeal ruling during 2027; check each quarter whether buybacks resume. Make one decision per quarter, complete it within 48 hours, and do not watch the market the rest of the time.
