📝 Conclusion: Watch and wait for a pullback

Watch, wait for a pullback. Pop Mart's first-half revenue was RMB 17.17 billion, up 23.8% year on year, and net profit was RMB 5.04 billion, up 10.1%, both below consensus; overseas revenue fell 11.1% for the first time and the Labubu family fell 7.5%, evidence that growth has peaked, not just a valuation reset. HKD 156.2 corresponds to a market cap of about HKD 209 billion and 14.2x TTM PE; the price has already reverted to consumer-stock levels, but profit still has room for downward revision. Wait for overseas revenue to stop falling in the third-quarter report, or for the stock to fall below HKD 120.
