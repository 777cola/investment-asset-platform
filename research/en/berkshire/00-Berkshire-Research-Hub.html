📝 Conclusion: Watch and wait for a pullback

Watch; wait for a pullback. Berkshire's 2026Q3 scorecard was steady but unspectacular: operating profit of USD 12.705 billion, down 5.8% YoY, with underwriting profit -43%, and only hedging from BNSF and manufacturing kept it from looking worse. What really supports the stock is USD 377.4 billion in cash and a structure that bets on no single narrative — when the AI trade collapsed, it outperformed the S&P by 19.2 percentage points in a month, with the stock hitting an all-time high. The problem is that this defensive premium is already priced in: 1.66x book value, about 25x annualized operating profit, 6% upside, 18% downside. The entry point should be below 1.4x book (about USD 500), or wait for a genuine market selloff. Triggers: ① the stock falls back below USD 500; ② a single acquisition of USD 30 billion or more; ③ underwriting profit returns to USD 2 billion/quarter.
