Core view

Start with anomalies when reading financial statements. Here the anomaly sits not in the income statement but in the juxtaposition of two lines: "RMB 9.6 billion in buybacks" and "1.66x book value."

Accounting quality: clean, but three footnotes to check

Clean overall. No restructuring charges, no goodwill impairment this quarter, no investment gains folded into operating profit.Berkshire's accounting conservatism ranks in the top 5% of the S&P 500.

Three items to check in the footnotes: ① Catastrophe loss reserves — how much of the decline in underwriting profit this quarter came from major catastrophes versus auto insurance; quarterly reports usually address this only in MD&A; ② BHE's wildfire litigation reserves; ③ The composition of unrealized investment gains — which are equities and which are bond revaluations.

None of the three changes the conclusion, but they will change the judgment on whether next quarter will be worse.

Think like a short seller: where is the attack surface?

The bear case rests on three points: ① The underwriting cycle. The combined ratio has moved from about 85% to 91.4%, with further deterioration to come; once the auto insurance price war begins, it could reach 96% in 2027. ② Cash drag. RMB 377.4 billion sits in assets yielding 4.5%; if tech stocks keep rising, this opportunity cost will be cited again and again. ③ Succession. Abel has not been tested by a crisis, and the market will express its doubts through a "discount".

All three hold, but none is enough to halve the stock. They can take it down 20% — and 20% is exactly the move from 1.66x back to 1.35x.

So my call is simple: the bears are right, but only 20% right. This is not a stock to short; it is a stock to wait on.

Conclusion

The accounting is fine, the business is fine — the problem is the price. Buying back RMB 9.6 billion at 1.66 times book is one of the few "un-Munger-like" decisions I have seen in my sixty years.

Either they know something I don't (quite likely), or the bar has been lowered (also possible). Either way, I won't buy at this price.
