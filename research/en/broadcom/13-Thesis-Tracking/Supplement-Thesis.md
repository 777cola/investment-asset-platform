## Notes

The six assumptions were selected because each can be verified from public data within a single quarter and each bears directly on valuation. Assumptions excluded on those grounds include "ASICs will replace GPUs" (a ten-year trend, not verifiable quarterly) and "Hock Tan will keep making acquisitions" (unpredictable). Customer deployment data are drawn from the company's account of customer plans; customers have not publicly confirmed them. The RMB 70 billion EBITDA figure is a rough calculation (Non-GAAP operating profit plus estimated depreciation and amortisation).

## Three things verified: the business fundamentals are sound

Three things now stand validated: software revenue, net debt ratio and customer capex. Software revenue came in at RMB 8.75 billion, up 30% YoY, showing that the VMware subscription conversion has not driven customers away; net debt/EBITDA is 0.85x, with USD 59.6 billion of debt no burden against USD 70 billion of EBITDA; and customer deployment plans are being revised upward, with Anthropic rising from 1GW to 5GW and OpenAI targeting 1.3GW.

What these three validations mean: Broadcom is not a company betting on the future; its current cash flow and customer relationships are already enough to support an 18x valuation. Even if AI revenue falls short, software plus non-AI semiconductors is a USD 52 billion business that can hold the floor.

## Two observations: gross margin and AI revenue

Gross margin is the line to watch most closely. Q3 came in at 75.0% and Q4 guidance is 73%, down from 78% four quarters ago — a one-way trend. The assumption is "no lower than 71%", on the logic that XPU hardware gross margin is low but will not fall below 55%, while software gross margin of 90% lifts the blended figure.

The AI revenue line of RMB 115 billion is harder to verify because it depends on customers. Current evidence is Q4 guidance of RMB 21.7 billion (RMB 86.8 billion annualized) plus customer deployment plans.Getting from RMB 86.8 billion to RMB 115 billion requires +32% — not unachievable, but it needs both the Anthropic and OpenAI projects to ramp at the same time.

The two lines are linked: the more AI revenue meets expectations, the more likely gross margin misses. They are not independent — it is a trade-off.

## One item fails: financing exposure

This is the only "failing" assumption. The company provides credit support for AI customers' procurement, with scale, tenor and recourse terms all undisclosed; BofA estimates up to USD 370 billion in senior debt could accumulate by mid-2029.

Why call it an "assumption" rather than a "risk"? A risk can be priced; an assumption can only be verified. It cannot be verified now, so this item in the thesis is blank. A blank assumption means the entire thesis has a hole.

How to handle it: make this a "veto item" — until it is disclosed, position size stays at no more than 5% however cheap the price; if disclosure confirms limited recourse and a quantifiable exposure, the cap can be raised to 8%.

## Falsification conditions: when this thesis is invalidated

In an investment thesis the assumptions matter less than the falsification conditions. Broadcom has four:

- FY27 AI revenue guidance cut by more than 15% (from RMB 115 billion to below RMB 98 billion), signalling customers are cutting orders;

- Non-GAAP gross margin below 70% for two consecutive quarters, signalling a loss of pricing power rather than a mix issue;

- The first default on the financing platform, or guarantees returning to the balance sheet, signalling the worst case has begun to materialise;

- A single large customer (Google or Meta) publicly confirms it has awarded its next-generation project to a second supplier.

If any one of these occurs, the thesis is void and valuation is no longer discussed.All four can be verified through public information; no guesswork is required.

Conversely, if none of the four occurs by 2027, the thesis upgrades from "watch" to "buy" — not because the stock has risen, but because time has proven the risk does not exist. That is how a watchlist works: trade time for certainty, not price. A watchlist is not a list of "waiting to buy" — it is a list of "waiting to judge."
