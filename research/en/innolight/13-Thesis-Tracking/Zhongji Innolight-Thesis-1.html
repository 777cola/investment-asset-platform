Core thesis

No position yet, so this is not a "holding thesis" but a "watchlist thesis" — first spell out the conditions under which to buy, then the conditions under which to abandon it permanently. Of the five assumptions, 1.6T demand sustainability holds and the 3.2T technology reserve holds; two remain unverified — gross margin holding at 42% and net cash ratio recovery; customer concentration has been partly falsified (75.98% → 81.9%). The most critical is the net cash ratio: if the Q3 report comes back above 50%, the thesis stands; below 30%, the thesis is void.

The thesis in one sentence: what you are buying is "generational-gap rent plus cash-flow repair," not the "AI narrative."

To buy below RMB 700, the thesis must read like this: "Zhongji Innolight has a 2–3 quarter technology lead in the 1.6T generation, supporting gross margins above 42%; the cash flow tightness in the first half of 2026 is a one-off from inventory build and will correct in the Q3 report; 3.2T takes over in 2027, with revenue still growing 25%."

Miss any one of the three and the thesis is incomplete. Especially the second — if cash flow does not recover, the RMB 19.8 billion of inventory is unsellable, and the 42% gross margin is fake.

Verify item by item: which is most fragile

Assumption 1 is the most solid. Demand-side evidence is hard: cloud capex is up 86% YoY, 1.6T begins volume ramp in 2026, and industry bodies put 2026 demand at 8.6–20 million units. The risk to this assumption is not demand — it is whether demand is already priced in. It is.

Assumption 2 is the most fragile. The 46.59% gross margin rests on three supports: 1.6T supply shortage, silicon-photonics cost reduction, and scale effects. The first two are eroding: Eoptolink's 1.6T volume ramp and falling silicon-photonics barriers. The 42% neutral case implies a 4.6 percentage point gross margin decline — a move history shows can happen in a single quarter (2025Q2 gross margin 41.49%, +4.79pct QoQ).

Assumption 3 is the most critical. The evidence chain for the inventory build: long-term payables up RMB 4.16 billion (materials locked in for over a year), prepayments of RMB 950 million, inventory of RMB 19.826 billion. There is only one disproof: if these materials do not convert to revenue in 2026H2, inventory keeps piling up and cash flow will not recover. The Q3 report is the sole arbiter.

Assumption 5 is already partly falsified. Customer concentration rose from 75.98% in 2025 to 81.9% in 2026Q1 — the opposite direction from "diversifying risk." This does not affect its ability to make money, but it affects the PE multiple it deserves — a company with a single customer at 25.1% should carry a valuation discount.

Falsification conditions: three red lines; abandon if any one is triggered

Red line 1: Q3 net cash ratio below 30%. This would mean materials build-up has not converted into revenue, and the RMB 19.8 billion inventory is starting to pile up. Once this breaks, the entire "one-off cash flow squeeze" thesis collapses.

Red line 2: 1.6T ASP down more than 8% QoQ. This would signal a price war, making the 46% gross margin unsustainable; the 42% neutral assumption would need to be cut to 38%, and the neutral target price from RMB 772 to about RMB 690.

Red line 3: Top five customers exceed 85% of revenue, or a single customer cuts orders by 20%. The former signals deepening dependence; the latter signals a demand inflection. Either would invalidate the 2027 revenue forecast.

One soft red line to add: if Nvidia's CPO switch port share exceeds 20% in 2027, the long-term valuation ceiling drops from 30x to 22x — this does not affect performance over the next year or two, but it will affect the exit price.

Watch calendar: what to watch and when

Map out the future information nodes so you don't get swept away by day-to-day noise.

  • Late October 2026: Q3 results — net cash ratio, inventory QoQ, average selling price per unit; these three numbers settle it in one go
  • End-October to early November 2026: capex guidance from Microsoft, Amazon, Meta and Google Q3 results, which sets 2027 demand
  • Mid-November 2026: Nvidia earnings and CPO switch shipment disclosures, which set the substitution pace
  • January 2027: annual earnings pre-announcement, verifying whether full-year 2026 net profit of RMB 31 billion (rough estimate) is delivered
  • March–April 2027: annual report and Q1 report — whether 3.2T is sampling/mass producing, and whether customer concentration improves

Before late October, any purchase is a bet on the Q3 report. Win the bet and you make 20%; lose it and you lose 30% — the odds aren't worth it.

Conclusion

Conclusion: the thesis holds, the price does not. Of the five assumptions, three support "good company", one supports "cash flow will recover", and one is partly disproven. The buy condition is "below RMB 700 + Q3 net cash ratio ≥50%"; the abandon condition is "net cash ratio <30% or 1.6T average price down 8% QoQ". Stay flat and watch until late October; no anticipatory positioning.
