① Cognitive reset: this is not an AI stock, it is an arms dealer — Zhongji Innolight

Put the 53x PE back into the framework of a cyclical stock

Core view

Start by stripping off the label. Zhongji Innolight is not an "AI concept stock" but an arms dealer in the speed arms race — customers fight the war, it sells the bullets. That is the arms dealer's fate: excess profit in wartime, a return to thin margins in peacetime. In June the market applied a growth-stock framework and gave it a static P/E of 143x (RMB 1.54 trillion / 2025 net profit of RMB 10.797 billion); at 53x today, the framework is still growth stock. The cognitive reset: first measure it with the cyclical stock ruler, then decide whether to use the growth stock ruler.

Tag 1: AI stocks — mistaking a customer's story for your own

In the first half of 2026, every company with any AI exposure rallied. But the degree of that exposure varies widely: NVIDIA sells the heart of compute, with gross margins above 70%; TSMC sells manufacturing capability, with gross margins of 58%; Zhongji Innolight sells interconnect, with gross margins of 46.59%. A 25-percentage-point spread in gross margins shows these three occupy completely different positions in the chain.

More critical is the revenue source. 94.8% of Zhongji's revenue comes from overseas, and 81.9% from five customers. Its earnings growth is not handed to it directly by "AI demand" but by "those five customers' procurement budgets for this year." Mistaking a customer's story for one's own is the most common cognitive error of this cycle.

Tag 2: Growth stocks — mistaking a cyclical peak for a trend

On June 22, 2026 the stock traded at RMB 1,382.33, a market cap of RMB 1.54 trillion. Against 2025 net profit of RMB 10.797 billion that is a static P/E of 143x; on the TTM visible at the time it was still above 100x. The price implied a five-year net profit CAGR above 40%.

The problem: this company's net profit has never grown at a high rate for five consecutive years. Net profit fell about 18% YoY in 2019, and gross margin dropped to 32.99% in 2023. Its historical shape is "steps plus deep pits," not a "straight line." Pricing steps with a straight-line framework produced a 33% decline from June to September.

Correct label: cyclical growth stock, valuation centre 20–25x

On any classification it is a "cyclical growth stock": long-term growth (speed rises one generation every two years), short-term cyclicality (cloud capex runs in 3–4 year cycles). Valuation for such companies typically centres on 20–25x, reaching 40x at cycle peaks and 10x at troughs.

On that framework, the current 53.3x TTM and 39.9x H1 annualised sit at the upper edge of "peak prosperity but already beginning to roll over." 2027 neutral net profit of RMB 35 billion × 22x = RMB 770 billion market cap = RMB 654/share; × 26x = RMB 772. That is why below RMB 700 is a "negotiable" level, and RMB 925 is not.

The final line on the cognitive reset: It is not "the next Nvidia," it is "this generation's Delta Electronics." Do what a manufacturer should do, earn what a manufacturer should earn, and give it the valuation a manufacturer deserves.

Conclusion

Conclusion: strip off the AI label and value the stock on a cyclical-growth yardstick. A 46.59% gross margin is a cyclical-peak reading, yet 53x P/E is growth-stock pricing — the two are contradictory. A 20–25x valuation centre implies RMB 654–772 in 2027, the price anchor; RMB 925 sits 20% above it.
