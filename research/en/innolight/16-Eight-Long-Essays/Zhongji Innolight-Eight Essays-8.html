Core view

Execution comes last. A ten-item checklist answers "can I buy," three prices answer "at what price," and two conditions answer "act early or wait." The checklist currently passes 5 items, partially passes 2 and fails 3 (cash flow, customer concentration, valuation). The three prices are RMB 772 (neutral), RMB 700 (first tier) and RMB 600 (margin of safety); the two conditions are a Q3 net cash ratio of ≥50% and no decline in the 1.6T average price.

Checklist reading: three items fail, and they happen to be the three most expensive ones

Six of ten criteria passed, two are partial and three failed. The three failures are cash flow (13.2%), customer concentration (81.9%) and valuation (neutral, 17% below the current price). These are not issues that can wait; they bear directly on the buy decision.

Cash flow and customer concentration are structural and cannot be fixed in the short term; valuation is the only one that will correct on its own — a lower share price would see criterion 9 pass. The decision logic is therefore simple: wait for criterion 9 to pass, while watching whether criterion 5 repairs itself.

Three prices: 772, 700, 600

RMB 772 = 2027 neutral net profit of RMB 35bn × 26x ÷ 1.178bn shares. That is fair value, not a buy point: buying at fair value earns you the company's growth, not the re-rating.

RMB 700 = about 23x 2027 P/E. This is the first buy point, offering a 10% downside buffer against 60% upside (bull case RMB 1,131). RMB 600 = about 20x, the margin-of-safety price, with the bear case (RMB 407) already half priced in.

The three levels are for scaling in: build 3% at RMB 700, add to 4% at RMB 650, add to 5% at RMB 600, and up to the 6% cap below RMB 550. Don't "buy a little to test the waters" at RMB 900 — the cost of testing is a 30% drawdown.

Valuation and strategy: two conditions under which it pays not to wait for the price

Condition one: Q3 net cash ratio ≥50%. That would show the RMB 19.8 billion of inventory is converting into revenue and the cash-flow hole is one-off. If the share price is also below RMB 800, build one-third of the position early.

Condition two: 1.6T ASP flat or down less than 3% QoQ. That would show the price war has not started and the 46% gross margin can hold for at least two more quarters. Only when both conditions are met is this "fundamental confirmation"; if only one is met, keep waiting on price.

The reverse conditions must also be hard-coded: net cash ratio <30%, ASP down more than 8% QoQ, customer concentration >85% — any one triggers cancellation of the buy plan and a downgrade from "Watch" to "Avoid", regardless of how far the share price falls.

Conclusion

Conclusion: the checklist passes 5 items, partially passes 2 and fails 3; three prices, two conditions. The action now is to wait: for the Q3 report to validate cash flow, and for the share price to fall below RMB 772. Build 3% at RMB 700, add to 5% at RMB 600, cap at 6%; if the reverse conditions trigger, abandon it regardless of price. This turns "observe, wait for a pullback" into executable action.
