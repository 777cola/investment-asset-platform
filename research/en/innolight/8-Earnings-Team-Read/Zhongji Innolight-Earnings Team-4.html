Core view

Li Lu reads financial statements for "industry position." This report gives two coordinates: on the revenue side, 94.8% comes from overseas, with the US accounting for more than 60%; on the cost side, the largest supplier accounts for 38.3% of procurement. One end abroad, the other end also abroad, and the processing link in between takes a 46% gross margin—the most typical and also the most fragile position in the global division of labor.

Revenue: five customers, one currency

Overseas revenue of RMB 39.615 billion accounted for 94.8% of the total (86.4% a year earlier); the top five customers represented 81.9%, with the largest at 25.1%.This is no longer simply a "high export share" — the company is effectively a dedicated workshop for North American cloud providers.

Li Lu would place this within the arc of civilizational progress: compute infrastructure is being built out globally in a concentrated wave, optical interconnect is the nervous system, and the direction of demand is certain.But "certain demand" resting on "five customers" becomes "certain dependence."Capacity in Thailand, Taiwan and Mexico (planned) hedges geopolitical risk — the right move, but it does not change the nature of the customer structure.

Cost side: largest supplier 38.3%

In Q1 2026, the largest supplier accounted for 38.3% of total procurement.Optical chips (EML/CW) and electrical chips (DSP) are the largest cost items, and electrical chips above 200G are sourced mainly overseas.Prepayments of RMB 950 million and long-term payables of RMB 5.06 billion both serve to lock in these materials.

What this structure implies:the company has no pricing power on the customer side (five customers) and none on the supply side (one supplier at 38.3%).It earns a processing margin in between on the strength of scale and delivery certainty, and its 46% gross margin marks the peak of that processing margin.

Li Lu's verdict would be:an excellent manufacturer, but its profit rests on "no one can replace it for now," not "no one can ever replace it."The real long-term rent collectors are the upstream optical chip makers and the downstream cloud providers. In this chain, position matters more than the company.

Conclusion

Li Lu-style conclusion: position determines the ceiling. With 94.8% of revenue from overseas and 38.3% sourced from a single supplier, the company has no bargaining power at either end of the chain; excess profit can only come from a generational technology gap. Worth researching, but not a long-term hold at 53x P/E; if you must invest, wait for a cyclical trough and for share to keep concentrating.
