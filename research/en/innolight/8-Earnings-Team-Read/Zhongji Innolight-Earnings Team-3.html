Core view

Munger reads financial reports looking for "things that don't add up." This interim report has three: a 46.59% gross margin, 10 points above the three-year average; management expenses up 164% YoY; inventory up 56.3% while revenue rose 182.5%. All three can be explained, but each is a reminder: these are the statements of a cyclical peak.

Gross margin: 10 points above the three-year average

32.99% in 2023, 33.80% in 2024, 42.04% in 2025 and 46.4% in 2026H1. Against the 2023–2025 three-year average of 36.3%, the current level sits 10 percentage points higher. Munger's question never changes: who is handing you those 10 points?

The answer lies in volume and average price: 1.6T supply is short, leaving customers no choice but to accept an average price of RMB 2,178. That shortage is temporary, because capital flows to high-margin areas — the whole industry is adding capacity in 2026. Zhongji is building RMB 3.7 billion of works itself, and Eoptolink and TFC are expanding too. The day supply catches up, those 10 points go back.

Management fee +164%; inventory +56.3%

Management expenses rose from RMB 291 million to RMB 767 million, up 164% — faster than the 182.5% revenue growth? No, slower. So they are not out of control, just the normal cost of capacity expansion. Munger would let this one pass.

He would not let inventory pass: inventory rose 56.3% against 182.5% revenue growth — inventory growth far below revenue growth, which looks like a good thing — but the absolute figure reached RMB 19.826 billion, equal to 160 days of cost of goods sold. Stockpiling 160 days' worth means the company expects stronger demand next year; it also means that if that call is wrong, the RMB 19.8 billion has to be worked down or written down within two quarters.

Munger's summary would be: "There is no sign of fraud in this report — it simply captures a photograph of the cycle top. The photograph is beautiful, but you cannot live in a photograph as if it were a house."

Conclusion

Munger-style conclusion: honest financials, dangerous position. Gross margin sits 10 points above the three-year average, alongside RMB 19.8 billion of inventory and RMB 15 billion of receivables — together signalling a moment of "strongest demand, tightest supply." That is a moment to sell into, not to buy.
