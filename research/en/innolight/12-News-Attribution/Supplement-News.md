## Three selloffs, two causes

Break the three-month decline into its parts and it amounts to three plunges. The first came on July 30, when the H shares broke issue at HKD 980: on the same day, for the same company, Hong Kong investors would pay less than A-share investors. The A shares fell as much as 16% intraday, wiping out nearly RMB 180 billion in market value, before recovering late in the session. The second came on August 24, when the stock closed at RMB 870.22 on the first trading day after the interim results, down 7.72%, touching an intraday low of RMB 850 and briefly dropping below RMB 1 trillion in market value. The third was the slow bleed from late August to early September, until a Goldman Sachs report on September 7 triggered a 10% rally.

The two plunges had different causes. July 30 was about valuation disagreement: the A shares priced the company at RMB 1.5 trillion, while H shares would pay only HKD 980 per share (about HKD 1.05 trillion in market value). August 24 was about fundamental signals: net profit of RMB 13.651 billion and operating cash flow of RMB 1.8 billion — the market was scrutinizing the cash flow statement seriously for the first time.

## Three real signals: cash flow, broken IPO, 3.2T

Signal one: cash flow. Net cash ratio 13.2%, versus 80% a year earlier and 101% for full-year 2025, with inventory of RMB 19.826 billion and receivables of RMB 15.001 billion. This is not an accounting issue but a question of cycle position — the company converted cash into materials at peak demand. The -7.72% move on August 24 was the market pricing this, and it priced it correctly.

Signal two: H-share debut below offer price. The offer price of HKD 980 was set at a 3% discount to the HKD 1,010 upper end of the bookbuilding range, yet the stock still broke issue on day one, closing near HKD 950. International long-only money will not pay for a HKD 1.5 trillion valuation — it is looking not at 241% growth in 2026 but at sustainability in 2027.

Signal three: 3.2T pipeline. On September 12 the company disclosed that 3.2T NPO and 6.4T NPO are fully developed, and that 2.4T adopts a silicon photonics + lithium niobate hybrid solution compatible with OCS. The share price did not react to this news, but it is the key evidence of whether the company can carry the baton in 2027 — far more important than the share-price reaction.

## Four noise items: don't get carried away by them

Noise one: overtaking Moutai. Market cap reached RMB 1.5 trillion on 18 June and the share price overtook Kweichow Moutai — a sentiment indicator, not a fundamental one. Historically, every "overtaking Moutai" narrative has mostly proved a top marker.

Noise two: Stock Connect. H shares were included in Stock Connect on 27 August, which changes liquidity only, not the capacity of a single factory.

Noise three: brokers cutting each other's target prices. In the same week of September, Goldman Sachs put it at RMB 2,645, Nomura at RMB 1,325 and BOCOM International at RMB 1,488 — the high and the low differ by a factor of two. This is not an analysis-quality problem but an assumption-quality problem: whoever's 2027 net profit assumption is closer to reality is right. And the market that day answered the highest one with +10% — that is sentiment.

Noise four: a big name entering the top ten shareholders. The interim report shows Zhang Jianping newly entering as the ninth-largest shareholder with 5.9348 million shares (0.53%). A star retail investor's position is an outcome, not a cause, and a 0.53% stake has no effect on a RMB 1 trillion market cap.

## Attribution: the de-rating reflects valuation, not fundamentals

Down 33% in three months even as earnings over the same period were the best in history. Valuation cross-check on three scenarios: RMB 1,416.88 on 22 June implies roughly 48x P/E on 2027 neutral net profit of RMB 35 billion; RMB 925.24 on 18 September implies about 31x. The entire decline is valuation-driven; earnings expectations are even being revised up (Goldman Sachs lifted its 2026 and 2027 net profit forecasts to 25% and 42% above consensus).

This attribution determines the trade. If earnings are falling, wait for the earnings inflection; if valuation is falling, wait for valuation to bottom out. RMB 925 implies 31x 2027 and 35x 2026, still rich for a cyclical growth stock — so wait, do not bottom-fish.
