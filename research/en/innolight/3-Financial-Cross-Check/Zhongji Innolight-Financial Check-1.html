The only deviation above 1%: 2025 net margin

30.28% versus 28.24% is a 7.2% gap — not a decimal-point issue but two different bases. RMB 10.797 billion is net profit attributable to shareholders; RMB 38.240 billion is operating revenue; dividing one by the other necessarily gives 28.24%. The platform shows 30.28%, implying the numerator uses total net profit (including minority interests) — yet Zhongji Innolight's minority interest is very small, so a 7-percentage-point gap looks more like the platform conflating pre- and post-non-recurring figures, or including other businesses.

The fix is simple: for any ratio, recalculate using the numerator and denominator from the same table; never cite a third-party pre-computed ratio. The same applies to the 2026H1 net margin: 136.51/417.78 = 32.68%, versus 32.7% in the working papers — consistent after rounding.

One item differs in scope: which date's total share count to use

The half-year report cover shows 1,169,734,641 shares (including 54.50 million H shares) as of June 30. The H shares listed on July 30 and the 8.175 million-share over-allotment was completed on August 31, lifting total share capital to about 1.178 billion shares. Valuation must use the latest 1.178 billion shares; otherwise market cap is understated by RMB 7 billion.

Cross-check: RMB 925.236 × 1.178 billion shares = RMB 10,899 billion, consistent with the working paper's "about RMB 1.09 trillion". Using 1.1697 billion shares by mistake gives RMB 10,822 billion — a 0.7% difference that is still within an acceptable range but would feed through to the target price.

Self-consistency check: TTM vs. single-quarter breakdown

Three figures must reconcile; if they do not, one has been copied wrong. TTM net profit = full-year 2025 107.97 − 2025H1 39.95 + 2026H1 136.51 = RMB 20.453 billion, matching the RMB 20.45 billion in the working papers; EPS = 204.53/11.78 = RMB 17.36, and 53.3x P/E = 925.24/17.36, so it closes.

The quarterly breakdown also closes: Q1 revenue RMB 19.496 billion (disclosed in the Q1 report, +192.12%), H1 RMB 41.778 billion, Q2 = RMB 22.282 billion, up 14.3% QoQ; Q1 net profit RMB 5.735 billion (+262.28%), Q2 = RMB 7.916 billion, up 38.0% QoQ.Q2 profit grew far faster than revenue QoQ, showing that the 1.6T structural upgrade only truly reached gross margin in Q2.

Final adopted basis

Conventions: Revenue and profit are consolidated attributable-to-parent figures; all ratios self-calculated; total shares 1.178 billion; market cap RMB 1.09 trillion; P/E 53.3x (TTM); the full-year 2026 net profit of RMB 31 billion is a rough estimate (H1 already delivered RMB 13.651 billion, assuming H2 slightly better than H1), used only for forward P/E and not as a forecast.

One point needs to be clear: customer concentration of 81.9% and largest customer at 25.1% are on the 2026Q1 disclosure basis; the interim report did not update these figures. This report uses the Q1 basis throughout and flags it, does not use it for quarter-on-quarter comparison, and cites it only as evidence of structural risk.

Conclusion

Conclusion: the data is usable, subject to two definitional caveats. Net margin is always calculated in-house (28.24% for 2025) and total share count is always 1.178 billion shares. On that basis, six of the eight items show zero deviation between the interim report and public reports, and TTM, EPS, P/E and market cap are internally consistent. These figures can support the judgments that follow without any discount.
