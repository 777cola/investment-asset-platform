📝 Conclusion: Watch and wait for a pullback

Watch; wait for a pullback. Zhongji Innolight's 1H revenue reached RMB 41.78 billion, up 182.5% YoY, with net profit of RMB 13.65 billion, up 241.7% YoY — the 1.6T ramp is translating into real earnings. But the net cash ratio is only 13%, inventory plus receivables total RMB 34.8 billion, and the top five customers account for 81.9%: a highly cyclical, highly concentrated business that runs on funded working capital. RMB 925.24 implies a market cap of RMB 1.09 trillion and 53.3x P/E (TTM). The best company in the industry, but not the best price; wait for below RMB 700, or for the third-quarter net cash ratio to return above 50%.
