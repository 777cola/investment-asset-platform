Core view

Current price RMB 925.24, 53.3x TTM PE, recommended position 0%. The reason is not that the company is bad, but that the risk-reward is wrong: the neutral case of RMB 772 is 17% below the current price, the bear case of RMB 407 is 56% below, and the bull case of RMB 1,131 is only 22% above. Entry is split into three tiers: RMB 750–800 to probe with 1%, RMB 700 for the first tranche of 3%, RMB 600 to add up to 5%, with a 6% cap per stock. Stop-loss is driven by thesis conditions rather than price — exit unconditionally if the net cash ratio breaks below 30% or customers cut orders.

Why 0%: the risk-reward structure does not hold

The premise for building a position is "limited downside, ample upside." Now it is the reverse: 56% downside (bear case RMB 407), 22% upside (bull case RMB 1,131). To turn this into positive expected value, the probability of the bull case must rise above 45%—tantamount to betting that cloud capex beats expectations for a fourth consecutive year.

Another angle: buying at RMB 925 requires 2027 net profit of RMB 35 billion and a 31x PE just to break even. That is a "everything must go right" price, not a "room for error" price. The portfolio-management principle: never pay an "everything must go right" price for any single name.

Why still set three tiers: because it is worth tracking

0% does not mean not watching. This company is the purest "speed upgrade" play in the AI compute chain, with 23% global share and a 2–3 quarter head start in 1.6T; a company like this deserves a permanent place on the watchlist.

The purpose of the three tiers is "behavioral discipline." Buying 1% at RMB 750–800 is not about making money; it is about forcing myself to look at its data every day—only with a position will I seriously read the Q3 report. Buy 3% at RMB 700, because that is the break-even point under the neutral scenario. Buy 5% at RMB 600, because half of the bear case is already priced in there. Each tier corresponds to a calculable reason, not "buy because it fell."

Stop-loss: use thesis-based stop-loss, not price-based stop-loss

Using price stops on deep-cyclical stocks gets you shaken out — a 30% drawdown in optical modules can happen in a single quarter (they fell 33% from June 22 to September 18). So write the stop conditions in fundamental terms:

  • Net cash flow to net profit ratio below 30% for two consecutive quarters: inventory cannot be sold, inventory writedowns loom, exit unconditionally
  • 1.6T average selling price down more than 8% quarter on quarter: price war confirmed, gross margin assumptions invalid, halve the position
  • Top five customers exceed 85% of revenue, or a single customer cuts orders by 20%: demand or mix deteriorating, exit
  • CPO ports exceed 20% of new interconnect additions: the long-term thesis is being displaced, liquidate and do not re-enter

Keep only one price stop as a backstop: 35% below the entry cost. This will most likely never trigger, because the fundamental stops will fire first.

Correlation: it shares the same risk factor as the AI hardware in the portfolio

This matters more than the position size.Zhongji Innolight's earnings are the third link in the transmission chain of "North American cloud capex → Nvidia GPU shipments → optical module orders," and are highly correlated with Nvidia, TSMC, SK hynix and WUS Printed Circuit. On August 24, 2026, the two leading optical module names fell 7%+ in unison (Eoptolink −7.52%), evidence of same-factor, same-direction moves.

So size positions by risk factor, not by number of stocks.If the portfolio already holds AI compute hardware (GPU, HBM, PCB, servers), the optical module allocation must come from the same pool; we suggest total "AI capex beta" not exceed 15%, with Zhongji as a single name not exceed 6%.

Hedging approach: to retain long-term optical module exposure without single-stock risk, wait for a pullback and split the allocation half in Zhongji, half in Eoptolink—the two overlap on customers and technology roadmaps, but trade at different valuations, smoothing single-stock valuation swings.

Conclusion

Conclusion: 0% position, three price tiers, thesis-based stop-loss. Do not buy at the current price (risk/reward −56%/+22%); probe with 1% at RMB 750–800, 3% at RMB 700, 5% at RMB 600, with a 6% cap per stock. The stop-loss tracks three fundamental indicators — net cash ratio, average selling price per unit, and customer concentration — with a price floor of −35%. At the same time, count its allocation within "AI capex beta", measured jointly with Nvidia, HBM and PCB, capped at 15% in total.
