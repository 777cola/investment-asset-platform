Core thesis

We screened six global optical module and optical component names and kept two. Zhongji Innolight wins on scale — RMB 41.778 billion in the half-year, roughly 23% global share — and on its 1.6T first-mover position; Eoptolink wins on gross margin (48.44%) and valuation (about 40x annualised H1 vs Zhongji's 53.3x TTM). T&S Communications has the highest gross margin but is an upstream component maker, small in scale and trading at 126x; Accelink's 25.53% gross margin and 8.67% net margin leave it with neither scale nor profitability; Coherent and Lumentum sit upstream — better businesses in this chain, but not better stocks.

Step 1 screening: 20 companies down to 6

The screen applies three hard criteria: 1) makes 800G/1.6T datacom optical modules (not telecom, not PON); 2) overseas revenue above 30%, proof it can enter North American cloud vendors' supply chains; 3) market cap above RMB 50 billion, sufficient liquidity. Three filters leave only four A-share names: Zhongji Innolight, Eoptolink, T&S Communications and Accelink; overseas, Coherent and Lumentum are added for reference.

Screened out: telecom-focused players, where demand growth is slower; optical component makers that do not move into modules, all small except T&S; silicon-photonics startups, unlisted or unprofitable; and second-tier module makers absent from 1.6T customer lists.In optical modules, a supplier not on North American cloud vendors' lists is simply not in the game.

Step two elimination: why the four fell out

Accelink: Revenue RMB 6.61 billion, up 26.1%, the slowest of the four; gross margin 25.53%, net margin 8.67%. It covers the full product line (telecom + datacom) and carries no 1.6T premium. Not a bad company, but not at the core of this cycle's windfall.

T&S Communications: Gross margin 60.87%, the highest of the six; it makes optical engines and passive components, a better position than module makers. But 2026H1 revenue was only RMB 2.828 billion, up 15.2% — upstream component growth cannot keep pace with module volume ramp, and a market cap of RMB 303.7 billion implies 126x annualized H1 P/E. Good business, too expensive, too small.

Coherent, Lumentum: They control EML optical chips, the link in this chain with the strongest pricing power. But they also compete head-on with Chinese vendors in modules, and their share has been overtaken by Eoptolink over the past two years (Eoptolink rose to global No. 2 in 2025). Buying them means buying a mix of "upstream stronghold + module retreat"; better to buy pure upstream names directly.

Final pick: Zhongji vs. Eoptolink — what decides the choice

The gap between the two is narrower than expected. Zhongji's revenue is twice Eoptolink's and it ranks first globally by share; but Eoptolink's 48.44% gross margin is 2pp higher, its 36.0% net margin is 3.3pp higher, its 21.5% net cash ratio beats Zhongji's 13.2%, and its valuation (40.3x H1 annualised) is below Zhongji's (53.3x TTM).

So why still pick Zhongji as the research subject? Three reasons. ① Scale determines supply-chain position: 18.99 million units shipped in the half-year gives it a notch more bargaining power on chip procurement and capacity priority; ② deeper in-house silicon photonics: 1.6T silicon photonics, 3.2T NPO and 6.4T NPO are already developed, and 2.4T silicon photonics + lithium niobate can iterate toward 3.2T — a fuller technology roadmap than its rival's; ③ a more "NVIDIA" customer structure: top customer at 25.1%, directly tied to GPU shipment cadence.

But to be honest: on "current odds" alone, Eoptolink is better — higher gross margin, better cash flow, lower valuation, and though growth is slower, the base is smaller. Zhongji's advantage is "leader certainty," and the price is a valuation premium. The difference between these two stocks is not who is better, but who is more expensive.

Why it, and why not buy now

Why this one: it has the strongest delivery capability, the deepest technology reserves and the closest customer relationships in this 1.6T cycle. Half-year revenue of RMB 41.78 billion, a 46.59% gross margin and a 37.62% half-year ROE are not luck.

Why not buy now is equally clear: a 53.3x P/E (TTM) and a 39.9x annualised 1H P/E have already priced in the 2027 handoff to 3.2T. Our base-case target price of RMB 772 implies 17% downside from the current price; the bear case of RMB 407 implies 56% downside. Paying 53x P/E at a mid-to-late stage of the cycle, with 81.9% customer concentration and a 13% net cash ratio, does not offer a viable risk-reward.

Conclusion

Conclusion: two of the six pass the screen. Zhongji is the leader; Eoptolink offers the better price. The other four each carry hard flaws: Accelink's gross margin is too thin, TFC is too expensive and too small, and Coherent and Lumentum sit in a mixed state of "strong upstream, weak modules." On execution: put Zhongji on the watchlist and talk below RMB 700; if the goal is simply to earn this cycle's money, Eoptolink's odds near 40x annualized H1 P/E are better than Zhongji's.
