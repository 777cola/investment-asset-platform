Core view

Eight key figures were cross-checked against two sources: revenue 24,397, net profit 6,451, tirzepatide 16.075 billion and guidance of 91–92 billion — all four sources agree exactly and can be used directly. Two deviations exceed 1%, both in the "base" and the "consensus" rather than in the company's own disclosures. The first is the 2025Q3 revenue base: the draft shows 17,552, while the company's financial report and Securities Star both show 17,601, a 0.28% difference; I have revised the YoY figure to +38.6%. The second is Foundayo's consensus estimate of 229 million against an actual 184 million — a forecast variance rather than a data error, and one that must be presented separately.

How to explain the two deviations exceeding 1%

The first issue is the revenue base. The draft put 2025Q3 revenue at USD 17.552 billion, whereas both company disclosures and Stockstar show USD 17.601 billion. The USD 49 million gap looks trivial, but it shifts YoY growth from +38.6% to +39.0% — a difference that crosses a whole number. I switched to USD 17.601 billion, wrote YoY growth as +38.6%, and made it consistent across all tables. Such errors usually stem from mistaking "some slice of the first three quarters of 2025" for a single quarter, or from rounding when back-solving from YoY growth. Any base figure back-solved from YoY growth should not be treated as a company-disclosed value.

The second issue is not a deviation but an expectations gap. Foundayo sold USD 184 million against a consensus of USD 229 million, a USD 45 million difference (-20%). Many reports write "below consensus by USD 25 million" or "USD 50 million" — definitions differ: some use LSEG consensus, some Visible Alpha, some count only the US market. I do not use this difference for inference; I use it only to compare against the absolute scale of oral Wegovy (USD 811 million vs USD 184 million), and that comparison is unaffected by consensus definitions.

Three different scopes, and none of them is wrong

Volume and price basis. Company-wide, volume rose 53% and realized price fell 15%; in the US alone, volume rose 41% and price fell 7%; overseas, volume rose 76% and price fell 29%. All three sets are correct — they cover different scopes. Pick just one for a report and you reach the opposite conclusion: US only, and the price cuts look mild; overseas only, and it reads as a price collapse. My approach is to give both, and state plainly that overseas -29% is what the US will look like.

The P/E year. The draft says "about 40x 2027 estimates," but on 2027 neutral EPS of USD 42, the current price is only 33.6x; 40x corresponds to the 2026 guidance of USD 35.50 (39.8x). That sentence mixes two years. This report standardizes on three side-by-side figures: TTM 48.2x, 2026 guidance 39.8x, 2027 neutral 33.6x. The conclusion is unchanged — none of the three is cheap — but readers deserve to know which year's multiple they are looking at.

Non-GAAP EPS includes a one-time item. The USD 35.00–36.00 range already deducts USD 4.57 of acquired IPR&D. Add it back and the range is USD 39.57–40.57. Which to use for valuation? I use the deducted figure, because M&A is routine for this company, not an isolated event — three consecutive years of buying pipeline means it should not be treated as one-time.

The finally adopted basis

Revenue and profit are taken on a GAAP basis (USD 24,397mn / 6,451mn); EPS is non-GAAP (Q3 7.48, full year 35.00–36.00); gross margin is non-GAAP at 84.4%, noting that the GAAP figure would be 3–4pp lower with acquisition IPR&D and amortisation charged to cost; market cap is 1,411.95 × 941mn shares = USD 1.33trn, with no adjustment for net debt; valuation multiples show the TTM figure of USD 29.32 alongside 2026/2027 forecast EPS, without a single P/E. Every derived figure is labelled "rough calculation" or "back-solved" in the text; no derived figure is presented as disclosed.

One cross-check did not make it into the table: Q3 diluted EPS of USD 7.22, up 16% YoY, is consistent with net profit of USD 6.451bn, up 16% YoY, implying no change in share count and no large preferred dividend, so the transmission chain through the income statement holds. That the small items tie out is better proof that the financials have not been stitched together than any single number.

Conclusion

Conclusion: eight data points usable, two corrections applied throughout. We revise the revenue base to RMB 17.601 billion, +38.6% YoY, and split the P/E into three tiers: 48.2 TTM, 39.8 2026 and 33.6 2027. Neither change alters any investment judgment, but both make the numbers more honest. One more item for the next checklist: any base figure back-solved from a YoY rate must be traced to the company's original disclosure before it counts.
