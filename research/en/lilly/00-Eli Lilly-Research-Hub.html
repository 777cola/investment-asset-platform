📝 Conclusion: Watch and wait for a pullback

Watch; wait for a pullback. Eli Lilly's Q3 2026 was an almost flawless report: revenue of USD 24.397 billion, +39% YoY; tirzepatide USD 16.075 billion in the quarter, 65.9% of revenue; full-year guidance raised for the third time this year to USD 91–92 billion. All four of the first gates (circle of competence, good business, moat, management) passed with high marks, and an 84.4% gross margin is the pinnacle of the business model. The fifth gate, however, vetoes it outright: the current USD 1,411.95 implies a market cap of about USD 1.33 trillion, 48x TTM and about 40x 2027 expectations, against a base-case target price of USD 1,344, 5% below the current price. Today's 4.28% gain — with an FDA eye-disease warning added, yet the stock hitting a new high — reads as overheated sentiment rather than an entry point. Wait for a drop below USD 1,200, or for Foundayo to sell past USD 500 million in a quarter and prove the oral drug can carry the baton, before discussing a purchase.
