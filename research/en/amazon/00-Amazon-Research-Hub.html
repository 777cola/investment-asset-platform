RMB 77.2 billion Q1: betting on 2028

📝 Conclusion: Buy; build the position in tranches

Buy; build the position in tranches. Amazon's 2026Q3 income statement was its best in a decade: operating profit of USD 28.08 billion, up 61% YoY, and a record 13.9% operating margin. AWS grew 39.3%, its sixth consecutive quarter of acceleration, with a backlog of USD 578 billion and 2027–2028 capacity already locked in by customers. In retail, North America margin improved from 4.3% to 7.7% — a structural improvement, not a one-off. The cost: quarterly capex of USD 77.26 billion, TTM free cash flow of -USD 42.4 billion, and long-term debt up from USD 65.6 billion to USD 173.1 billion. The current USD 259.30 implies a USD 2.80 trillion market cap and 20.8x TTM, just 8.7% below the USD 284.02 high — the market is not giving it a discount; its earnings are simply outrunning its share price. Build one-third of the position first, then decide on filling it out once Q1 2027 capex and depreciation data are out.
