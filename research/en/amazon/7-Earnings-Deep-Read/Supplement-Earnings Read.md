## Layer one: revenue mix — services revenue becomes the sole source of growth for the first time

Net sales were RMB 201.94 billion, with product sales of RMB 75.29 billion up only 1.7% and service sales of RMB 126.65 billion up 19.4%. **This is a structural shift: Amazon's growth has decoupled from "selling goods."** The same holds for the first nine months: products +8.7%, services +21.1%, with services at 61.6% of total revenue.

The segment breakdown makes it clearer. Online stores rose just 2% to RMB 68.47 billion (versus nearly 10% growth in the year-ago period), while third-party seller services grew 9% to RMB 46.49 billion—seller goods now account for 61% of global paid units, meaning Amazon increasingly resembles a "platform collecting rent" rather than a "store earning the spread." Advertising grew 12% to RMB 19.64 billion and subscriptions grew 11% to RMB 14.01 billion, the two highest-margin businesses. **In one sentence: first-party is decelerating, while platform, advertising and cloud are accelerating, and revenue quality is improving.**

One markdown to note: management attributes advertising's mere 11%–12% growth to Prime Day shifting from Q3 to Q2 (an impact of about 850 basis points), yet paid units still grew 12% over the same period. The explanation is credible but insufficient to account for everything; the Q4 guidance of "returning to high-teens" is a promise that must be verified.

## Layer two: gross profit and expenses — three expense lines in conflict

Gross profit: revenue of RMB 201.94 billion less cost of revenue of RMB 94.57 billion gives gross profit of RMB 107.37 billion and a gross margin of 53.2% (52.4% for the first three quarters), up 2.4 percentage points from 50.8% a year earlier. **The margin improvement did not come from price increases; it came from the revenue mix shifting from goods to services.**

The three expense lines moved in completely different directions: **fulfillment expenses** of RMB 29.997 billion, +8.4%, below the 12% volume growth — scale effects at work; **technology and infrastructure** of RMB 34.814 billion, +20.2% (+23.3% for the first three quarters), reflecting AWS depreciation plus technical headcount, the fastest-growing line; **sales and marketing** of RMB 11.649 billion, -0.3%, and **general and administrative** of RMB 2.701 billion, -6.1% — the organizational contraction after cutting 30,000 jobs shows up directly in these two lines.

Taken together, the operating expense ratio fell from 90.3% to 86.1%, and the operating margin was 13.9%. The quality of this improvement is good: **it was not bought by cutting long-term investment** (technology and infrastructure is still surging), but came from fulfillment efficiency and organizational slimming.

## Layer three: profit — operating profit is real, net profit is not

Operating profit was RMB 28.076 billion, up 61.2% year on year, with an operating margin of 13.9% — the highest quarter in the company's history. AWS contributed 17.773 billion (63.3%), North America 8.723 billion (31.1%) and International 1.580 billion (5.6%). **The margin gap across the three segments shows that this business earns almost all its profit from "renting out assets", not from "selling goods".**

Net profit, however, was 20.685 billion, down 2.4% year on year, and EPS was USD 1.89, down 3.1%. The cause is not weaker operations but the USD 9.5 billion pre-tax gain on Anthropic in the year-earlier period, which raised the base. Other income this quarter was in fact -703 million. **In other words: operating profit rose 10.85 billion year on year while net profit fell 502 million — the entire gap comes from non-operating items.**

The first three quarters need more careful reading: other income totaled 68.359 billion, 46% of pre-tax profit, and net profit rose 101% year on year. **If you describe this company as "net profit doubled", you are talking about Anthropic's valuation, not Amazon's business.**

## Layer four: cash flow and balance sheet — where the money went, and the answer is a single line

Operating cash flow TTM RMB 168.3 billion, +28.7% YoY, healthy. Purchases of property and equipment RMB 77.26 billion in the quarter, +120% YoY, roughly RMB 165 billion cumulative over the first three quarters (on quarterly run-rate), full-year guidance RMB 220 billion. **Free cash flow TTM negative RMB 42.4 billion, versus negative RMB 7.6 billion at the second-quarter report — meaning the third quarter alone burned about RMB 34.8 billion.**

Two balance-sheet shifts: long-term debt from RMB 65.6 billion to RMB 173.1 billion, up RMB 107.5 billion in a year; quarterly interest expense from RMB 538 million to RMB 1.691 billion. **The company is financing its AI buildout with debt — the marker of a transition from cash-rich to capital-intensive.** The good news is interest coverage still stands at 16.6x; the bad news is that multiple is falling fast.

An easily missed signal: the sales and marketing and G&A expense lines are declining, while technology and infrastructure grows +20%. **The company is cutting 30,000 corporate roles while installing one AI accelerator every 14 seconds** — that is its current capital allocation stance: swap labor cost for compute capital, betting the latter earns a higher return.

## A frequently overlooked accounting detail: the sign on other income

"Other income (net)" was -USD 703 million this quarter, versus +USD 10.186 billion in the same period last year. The same line item swung from a large positive contribution to a small negative drag, a difference of USD 10.89 billion—**this alone explains the entire -2.4% year-on-year decline in net profit, and has nothing to do with operations.**

When reading quarterly reports, the sign change on this line deserves to be flagged separately: it is outside operating control, yet it can turn "profit growth" into "profit decline." Likewise, the cumulative +RMB 68.359 billion over the first three quarters lifted net profit to +101% year on year. **One line item, two directions, two narratives—readers of financial statements must decide for themselves which one to believe.**
