Key features of the governance structure:

  • Partnership system:The Alibaba Partnership is the core of the company's governance. Jack Ma and Joe Tsai are "permanent partners"; other partners are elected annually. It holds the power to nominate a majority of the board. This is the "power insurance" Jack Ma left behind—ensuring the founding team's control of the company is not lost to equity dilution.
  • Dual-class share structure:Although Alibaba abandoned its Hong Kong listing in 2014 (because HKEX would not accept dual-class shares), by the time it returned for a secondary listing in 2019 it had adopted a weighted voting rights structure. Jack Ma, Joe Tsai and SoftBank held shares with higher voting power. Dual-class shares ensure the founding team retains strategic control even when diluted.
  • The significance of the 2023 handover:The Cai/Wu replacement of Daniel Zhang is not a routine executive rotation but a "founder restoration." Although Daniel Zhang is an Alibaba heavyweight (creator of Double 11, who led Alibaba from PC to mobile), he was always a professional manager. Facing extreme competition from Pinduoduo and a major shift in the technology paradigm (AI), Jack Ma chose to bring the founding team back — a "big bet" decision logic.
