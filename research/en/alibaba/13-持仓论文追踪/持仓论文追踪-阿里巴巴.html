I. Investment Thesis (Rationale for Buy)

Core thesis:Alibaba is trading at a "liquidation price" (PB 0.22x), yet its core e-commerce business is still generating positive cash flow, and Alibaba Cloud has second-curve potential in the AI era. If market sentiment returns to rationality, the room for valuation repair is enormous.


1. Deep Value / Net-Net— PB just 0.22x; the market is pricing this company, which still generates ~RMB 800 billion in annual revenue, at liquidation value. Net cash is about USD 56 billion (partly being burned), but even after deducting that, the asset side—e-commerce, cloud, logistics and other businesses—is being handed over almost for free.

2. Alibaba Cloud's second growth curve— In the era of large AI models, demand for cloud services is recovering. Alibaba Cloud is China's largest public cloud provider (~34% market share), and its Qwen large model is already competitive. In FY2025, Alibaba Cloud's revenue growth accelerated to ~5-6% year on year, with AI-related revenue growing even faster.

3. E-commerce moat intact— Taobao Tmall Group still holds ~45% e-commerce market share (by GMV), with annual GMV above RMB 7 trillion. Although Pinduoduo and Douyin are eroding its share, its absolute scale remains first in the industry, and its user mindshare ("everything-store Taobao") is irreplaceable.

4. Improved shareholder returns— FY2025 marked the first resumption of dividends (about USD 2.5 billion per year) alongside sustained large-scale buybacks (FY2025 buybacks of about USD 12 billion). Although the FY2026 buyback scale is sharply reduced to about USD 4 billion, dividends plus buybacks still total about USD 6.5 billion per year, corresponding to a dividend yield of about 2.5%.

5. Valuation-recovery optionality— PE 15x vs global e-commerce average of ~25x (Amazon ~40x, MercadoLibre ~50x). Even excluding any AI cloud premium, a mere re-rating to the industry average implies ~67% upside.

V. Contrarian View and Weaknesses in the Thesis (Honest Self-Questioning Required)

1. "Value trap" risk:Alibaba may not be "deep value" but a "value trap." The 15x PE may reflect falling profits: 15x is the PE on past profits, and the PE on future profits could be far above 15x. If earnings keep shrinking, the PE will look increasingly "expensive."

2. The trap in the PB logic:A PB of 0.22x looks cheap, but book assets are heavy with goodwill, intangibles, and a portfolio of investments that may be overstated (Ant Group's valuation, for one, has shrunk sharply). Actual liquidation value could fall well below book value.

3. Pinduoduo and Douyin are not a cyclical problem:Alibaba's e-commerce share loss may be structural (irreversible) rather than cyclical (recoverable). Pinduoduo and Douyin serve consumers' new demands for "low prices" and "content", and Alibaba has been unable to respond effectively.

4. Alibaba is no longer the "best" Chinese internet company:In AI large models, ByteDance (Doubao), Baidu (Ernie) and Tencent (Hunyuan) each commit heavy resources. In e-commerce, Pinduoduo and Douyin grow far faster than Alibaba. On its core tracks, Alibaba is turning from leader into chaser.

5. Management issues:Joseph Tsai and Eddie Wu are Alibaba veterans, but neither had prior successful experience running the whole company as CEO. Judging by the results of reforms since FY2025, business restructuring has been slow and shareholder-return policy insufficiently consistent.

📋 VI. Current conclusion

🟡 Simulation thesis: unverified, requires ongoing monitoring
Alibaba is a classic "deep value trap" — extremely cheap with a margin of safety, but profitability and market share are deteriorating structurally. Whether the buy thesis holds depends on two key inflection points:

① Can FCF turn positive in FY2027?
② Can Alibaba Cloud accelerate growth in the AI era?

If both answers are "yes," the current price of $98.40 will deliver significant returns within 2-3 years. If both are "no," Alibaba will prove to be a classic "value trap."
