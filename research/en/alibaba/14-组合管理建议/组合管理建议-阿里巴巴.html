Core thesis:Tencent simultaneously meets all three criteria: "good business" (high ROE plus a wide moat), "good management" (stable and shareholder-friendly) and "good price" (a PE of ~15x, a historical low). Should the Alibaba position be larger or smaller than the Tencent position?

Small, and it should be much smaller.Recommended allocation:Tencent : Alibaba = 2:1 ~ 3:1。

Reason:
① Earnings quality: Tencent's operating margin is ~30%, Alibaba's only 4.9% — Alibaba needs 2-3 years before it can return to Tencent's profitability level
② FCF: Tencent FCF RMB 230.5 billion, Alibaba FCF negative — Alibaba is burning cash, Tencent is printing it
③ Certainty: Tencent's WeChat ecosystem moat remains intact, while Alibaba's e-commerce barrier is being eroded by Pinduoduo and Douyin
4) Shareholder returns: Tencent's RMB 100 billion buyback lifts intrinsic value per share, while Alibaba's buyback plunges 91%
⑤ Alibaba's only advantage is its extremely low PB of ~0.22x, but a low PB alone does not justify investment—it must have fundamental recovery as a catalyst

Bottom line:The rationale for holding a larger Alibaba position than Tencent does not hold. Unless Alibaba's SG&A returns to a normal level (from 27% down to<18%）且FCF转正，否则阿里只能作为"小仓位困境反转对赌"。

Final recommendation:

① Tencent is the cornerstone of the portfolio— Highest certainty among the three names; recommend the largest position (12-15%) and holding for the long term.
② Pinduoduo is an odds engine— Deeply undervalued (PE ~9x) plus high growth (revenue +50%+) plus a net cash safety cushion offers potential for excess returns. Trading lower certainty for a high payoff.
③ Alibaba is a small-position distressed betThe extreme undervaluation of PB~0.22 justifies a small position, but FCF and SG&A must be monitored strictly. If no improvement appears within 12 months, exit decisively.
④ Together, the three account for no more than 40%— Chinese internet has high macro correlation, so risk must be diversified with non-Chinese assets (US tech, A-share consumer, US Treasuries).
⑤ Do not give Alibaba a large position just because it "looks cheap"— A low PB does not equal a good investment, especially when profitability and FCF are both deteriorating. Adding to the position after fundamentals improve is far safer than "bottom-fishing" into a decline.
