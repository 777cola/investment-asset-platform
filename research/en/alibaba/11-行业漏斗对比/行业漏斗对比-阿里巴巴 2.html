Alibaba Comprehensive Assessment

Alibaba's industry positioning: leading in scale but lagging in efficiency

  1. E-commerce #2 (GMV #1 but lowest growth):Tmall + Taobao remains the largest platform, but under pressure from Pinduoduo + Douyin on two fronts, growth was only +2.7%
  2. Cloud #1 (leads on share but growth is being chased):Alibaba Cloud still leads with a 34% share ahead of Huawei Cloud's 19%, but Huawei Cloud's growth of over 20% far outpaces Alibaba Cloud's ~3%
  3. Industry-wide #4 (negative FCF is the key flaw):FY2026 free cash flow turns negative due to massive capex (RMB 126.1 billion), the biggest negative in the investment framework
  4. Valuation margin of safety (PB 0.22x):A price-to-book ratio of just 0.22x means the market values the company's assets at less than one-fifth of book value — deeply undervalued
  5. AI + cloud is the biggest call option:The Qwen large model plus Alibaba Cloud's AI platform is the most likely catalyst over the next three years

Alibaba's core risks

  • Growth stalls:Revenue growth of just 2.7% is among the lowest of China's internet giants
  • FCF turns negative:FY2026 free cash flow was negative RMB 49.8 billion as AI/cloud infrastructure investment drove a surge in capex
  • Share loss:E-commerce GMV share fell from a peak of 70% to ~45%, and cloud market share dropped from 40%+ to 34%
  • Organizational efficiency:SG&A surged 47.7% to RMB 278.1 billion in FY2026, with the operating margin plunging from 14% to 4.9%
  • Competitive pressure:Frontline: Pinduoduo + Douyin; flanks: JD.com + Meituan — fighting on multiple fronts
