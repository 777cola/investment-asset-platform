6.2 Data issues identified ⚠️

1. PB 0.22x appears to be a data citation error

The reported PB of 0.22x is severely inconsistent with the calculated value of 1.53x. The root cause may be that the USD 224.58 billion market cap was mistakenly recorded in CNY units during data entry (CNY 224.58 billion / CNY 1,060.9 billion = 0.212 ≈ 0.22).Recommend revising to ~1.5x.

2. EV/EBITDA must state the calculation basis

The report's 8-10x is based on FY2025 EBITDA (RMB 183.4 billion). Using FY2026 EBITDA (RMB 97.3 billion), it is 16.1x. When using it, the fiscal year must be clearly stated.

3. The anomalous features of RMB 87.5 billion in interest income

FY2026 interest income of RMB 87.5 billion (+321% YoY) is the key to net profit not declining, but it also severely masks the deterioration in operations. When interest rates fall, this "safety cushion" will thin rapidly. Alibaba should be assessed on "operating profit excluding interest income".

6.4 Implications for investors
  1. FY2026 is not a "normal year":A one-off SG&A hit, a peak in CapEx, and abnormally high interest income — three factors distort the reported results. But the distortion cuts both ways: the surface looks better than reality (interest income propping up the bottom line) and worse than reality (record-high SG&A) at the same time.
  2. Focus on the marginal changes in FY2027:Operating margin (whether it can return to 8-10%), FCF (whether it can turn positive) and SG&A/revenue (whether it can fall below 25%) are the three most important metrics.
  3. The PB data has been revised:Alibaba's actual PB is ~1.5x, not the 0.22x previously recorded. This means the "below book value" call was a data error.
  4. Web scraping is not feasible:Macrotrends and Yahoo Finance have fully blocked automated data collection; future data gathering will depend on manual collection or paid data terminals.
