Alibaba's position in the e-commerce industry chain

Full-chain integrated e-commerce infrastructure provider— Covers the full chain of "transactions + logistics + finance + marketing + cloud".

✅ Core strengths

  • Highest product variety (Taobao's billion-level SKUs + Tmall's full brand coverage)
  • Mature transaction trust system (Alipay escrow)
  • Most complete merchant ecosystem (Alimama + Business Advisor + 1688)
  • Broad logistics network coverage (Cainiao)
  • Strongest AI capabilities (Qwen powers search/recommendation/customer service)

⚠️ Risks and weaknesses

  • Share continuously eroded by Pinduoduo/Douyin (~45% → estimated 38-42% by 2028)
  • Live commerce far behind Douyin (RMB 500 billion vs RMB 1.5 trillion)
  • Instant retail footprint is fragmented, squeezed by Meituan and JD.com
  • Content transition slower than Douyin/Xiaohongshu
  • Price war compresses take rate

Key trends and Alibaba's response (2026-2028)

📉 Stock competition intensifies

E-commerce GMV growth overall is ~6-7%, with incremental growth bottoming out. Alibaba is pivoting to profit improvement rather than share expansion.

🤖 AI reshapes e-commerce

Qwen powers AI search (Taobao Wenwen), AI customer service and AI virtual try-on—its differentiation rests on being the only integrated "large model + e-commerce + cloud" platform.

🌏 The second growth curve: going global

AliExpress + Lazada + Trendyol revenue growth ~30%+, but hit by TEMU. Responding with the Choice full-managed model.

⚡ The rise of instant retail

Meituan Instashopping ~RMB 300 billion GMV. Alibaba has assembled a near-field e-commerce matrix of Ele.me + Freshippo + Taoxianda, but lags on share.

Alibaba's position in the cloud computing value chain

China's largest public cloud platform and large-model enabler— Shouldering the mission of leapfrogging from e-commerce technology services to AI infrastructure for society at large.

✅ Core strengths

  • First-mover advantage: China laid out plans earliest (2009) and has the deepest technical accumulation
  • Broadest product matrix: 200+ products spanning compute/storage/network/database/AI
  • Most mature developer ecosystem: million-strong community, rich ISV ecosystem
  • Tongyi large model: open-source strategy builds developer ecosystem, global downloads >100 million
  • Internal refinement → practical output: validated in extreme scenarios at Double 11, Ant Group and Cainiao
  • Open-source contributions: widely adopted projects including RocketMQ, Dubbo and Nacos

⚠️ Challenges and risks

  • Growth slowing sharply (FY2026 ~3% vs industry ~10-12%)
  • The government and enterprise market is being heavily eroded by Huawei Cloud and Tianyi Cloud
  • Organizational turmoil: spin-off terminated, repeated management reshuffles
  • AIGC competition: Baidu ERNIE, Huawei Ascend and Tencent Hunyuan race to catch up
  • Price war compresses margins (multiple price cuts in 2024)
  • Overseas expansion constrained: the US market is all but inaccessible
  • Consumer-facing large models lag far behind DeepSeek and Doubao in influence

Part Three: Industry Chain Synergies and Investment Perspective

Synergies between the two major industrial chains

  • E-commerce → Cloud computing:Taobao and Tmall Group is Alibaba Cloud's largest "testing ground" — the 11.11 peak validates its technology, while AI search and recommendation scenarios hone the Tongyi models
  • Cloud computing → e-commerce:Qwen empowers e-commerce (AI search, virtual try-on, intelligent customer service, merchant operations assistant)
  • Data flywheel:Massive e-commerce transaction data → improved Tongyi model capabilities → better AI products → more cloud customers
  • International synergy:AliExpress/Lazada overseas expansion drives demand for Alibaba Cloud's overseas nodes, creating a bundled "e-commerce + cloud" go-global model

Overall assessment

Alibaba spans two industry chains, e-commerce and cloud, and leads China in each, but is being caught up in both.

E-commerce share fell from a peak of ~65% to ~45% (2025E) and is expected to decline further to ~38-42% by 2028, but profit resilience is above expectations. Cloud computing share fell from ~40%+ to ~29% and is expected to stabilize at ~25-28% by 2028, forming a duopoly with Huawei Cloud.

The synergy between the two major industrial chains in the AI era is Alibaba's most distinctive competitive moat:Tongyi LLM connects e-commerce scenarios with cloud infrastructure, forming an "application-model-computing power" closed loop. If AI inference demand scales up, Alibaba Cloud's growth could return to double digits, driving a re-rating of the overall valuation.
