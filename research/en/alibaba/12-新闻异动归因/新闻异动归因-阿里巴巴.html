🔍 One-line attribution

Alibaba's current share price of $98.40 (down 49% from its 52-week high of $192.67) reflects the market'sFY2026 earnings shock (operating margin 14.14%→4.90%), sharply scaled-back buyback (RMB 86.7 billion→7.6 billion), competitive pressure from Pinduoduo, and the systemic discount on China ADRsmultiple layers of pessimistic pricing. The single largest shock came with the release of the FY2026 annual report in May 2026 — SG&A surged 47.7%, operating profit collapsed, and the stock, after a sharp one-day selloff, kept probing lows in the $92-$105 range.

📅 Timeline of key events, 2025–2026

📌 FY2026 earnings shock (2026/5/13) — core event

2026/05/13
FY2026 annual report released: operating margin plunges to 4.90%, shares crater
Source: BABA official IR | Weight:🔴 High
Alibaba released its FY2026 annual report for the year ended March 2026. Revenue was RMB 1.02 trillion (+2.74%), but operating profit plunged from RMB 140.9 billion in FY2025 to RMB 50.2 billion (-64.4%), with the operating margin collapsing from 14.14% to 4.90%. SG&A expenses surged from RMB 188.3 billion to RMB 278.1 billion (+47.7%), mainly due to the consolidation of Sun Art Retail and organizational expansion. Free cash flow turned negative for the first time (-RMB 49.9 billion). The stock fell from ~$115 to a range of ~$92-$105 within 1-2 weeks after the earnings release and has not recovered since.

📌 Sharp cut in buybacks (FY2026)

FY2026
Buybacks plunged from RMB 86.7 billion in FY2025 to RMB 7.6 billion (-91%)
Source: BABA IR | Weight:🔴 High
Alibaba repurchased about RMB 86.7 billion (roughly USD 12 billion) in FY2025, with single-quarter buybacks in Q1-Q2 2025 each exceeding RMB 20 billion. But in FY2026 it repurchased only RMB 7.6 billion for the full year, a 91% decline. Management gave no clear explanation on the earnings call, and the market widely interpreted it as cash flow pressure (FCF turned negative) and capital expenditure (RMB 126.1 billion) taking priority over buybacks. Buybacks had been the stock's most core support mechanism; after the sharp reduction, the share price lost a key support.

📌 Organizational restructuring (2023–2024)

2023/03
"1+6+N" spin-off reform launched
Source: official announcement | Weight:🟡 Medium
Zhang Yong announced the split of Alibaba Group into 1 holding company + 6 business groups + N subsidiaries, with each business able to raise capital and list independently. The market was bullish in the short term (the stock briefly rebounded to ~$110), but subsequent IPO progress was slow and market sentiment cooled.
2024
Strategic shift: from spin-off to integration, back to three segments
Source: official announcement | Weight:🟡 Medium
After Cai Chongxin and Wu Yongming took over, they shifted direction: halting the Cainiao IPO, withdrawing the Hema IPO, and consolidating into three core segments: "e-commerce + cloud computing + AI." This 180-degree reversal of the spin-off strategy reflects management's correction of an overly complex organizational structure, but it adds near-term uncertainty.

📌 PDD Holdings' market cap overtakes Alibaba (end of 2023)

2023/11/29
Pinduoduo's market cap surpasses Alibaba's for the first time
Source: public market data | Weight:🟡 Medium
Pinduoduo's market cap briefly overtook Alibaba's intraday, making it the most valuable e-commerce company in China. Jack Ma responded in an internal post: "Alibaba will change, Alibaba will transform." The event symbolizes a major shift in the e-commerce landscape — Pinduoduo's "low-price strategy + Temu globalization" model commands a higher market premium. To this day, Pinduoduo trades at a PE of ~9x (cheap but slowing growth) versus Alibaba at ~15x (cloud/AI premium).

📌 Jack Ma developments (2023–2026)

2023/06
Jack Ma returns to China and holds internal meeting: "Alibaba will change"
Source: media report | Weight:🟡 Mid
After returning to China from an overseas trip, Jack Ma held an internal meeting at Alibaba Cloud headquarters and set out three directions: return to Taobao, return to users, return to the internet. Market sentiment got a short-term lift (the stock price rebounded slightly), but the long-term downtrend was not reversed. From 2024 to 2026, Ma's public appearances remained rare, and the market had limited confidence in his influence.

China ADR Regulation and Antitrust Aftermath

2021–2026
Antitrust penalty of RMB 18.2 billion (2021) + PCAOB audit standoff
Source: Reuters, official filings | Weight:🟢 Sustained
In April 2021, Alibaba was fined RMB 18.2 billion for "choose one of two" practices (the largest antitrust penalty in history). Four years on, the impact has largely been absorbed, but changes in the regulatory framework (the platform economy shifting from "strong regulation" to "normalized regulation") still intermittently affect investor sentiment. The PCAOB audit working papers issue has repeatedly resurfaced amid China-US tensions; risk eased after an agreement was reached in late 2022, but it has not been fully eliminated in 2025-2026.

📌 Alibaba Cloud price cuts + AI large models (2024–2026)

2024–2026
Alibaba Cloud cuts prices repeatedly; Qwen continues to iterate
Source: Alibaba Cloud official | Weight:🔵 China – Watch
In 2024, Alibaba Cloud launched the cloud computing price war (cutting prices of core products by 20-55%), with price cuts continuing through 2025-2026. Tongyi Qianwen released the Qwen2.5/Qwen3 series, maintaining competitiveness in the open-source large model space. Alibaba Cloud's AI narrative is one of the few positive premium factors in the current valuation, but the pressure of the price-cutting strategy on cloud business margins still warrants attention.

📌 Free cash flow turns negative + capex expansion

FY2026
FCF turned negative for the first time (-RMB 49.9 billion), CapEx rose to RMB 126.1 billion
Source: BABA IR | Weight:🔴 High
Alibaba's FY2026 capital expenditure surged to RMB 126.1 billion from RMB 86 billion in FY2025 (+46.6%), driven mainly by AI infrastructure investment and the consolidation of Sun Art Retail. Free cash flow swung from +RMB 77.5 billion to -RMB 49.9 billion. This is the first negative FCF since its 2014 listing, and the market is highly sensitive to this signal—it means the company must borrow or draw down cash to fund investment, constraining its capacity for buybacks and dividends.

🏷️ Nature assessment

Current view:Value events + ongoing pricing adjustments + structural change

Three key judgments

1️⃣ The FY2026 results are a milestone event for the "valuation reset"
Alibaba has shifted from a "profit improvement + large-scale buyback" narrative to "margin pressure + constrained buybacks." The market's ~26x PE for Alibaba in 2025 rested on two premises: continued profit improvement and buyback support. Both premises were broken simultaneously in FY2026.

2️⃣ The core issue is the fair valuation of the "new Alibaba"
What PE should Alibaba trade at without large-scale buybacks? If FY2027 operating margin recovers to 8-10% and e-commerce growth improves, a fair PE of 12-15x implies $80-110. If margins keep declining, below $80 is not out of the question.

3️⃣ Alibaba Cloud AI is the last positive story
If Alibaba Cloud can hold its position as China's number one public cloud and break through in monetizing AI large models, a re-rating of the cloud business could offset downside in the e-commerce valuation. But the cloud business's current price-cutting strategy is squeezing margins.
