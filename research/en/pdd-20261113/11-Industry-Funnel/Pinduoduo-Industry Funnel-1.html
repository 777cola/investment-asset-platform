Core call: not the cheapest, but the cheapest per dollar of profit

Six candidates entered the funnel. Douyin E-commerce and SHEIN are unlisted and drop out outright; Amazon, at 21.5x P/E, falls outside our scope; JD (2% net margin, revenue −2.9%) and Alibaba (operating profit −57%, with half its valuation in cloud) each fail one criterion.

Pinduoduo is the final pick: 8.2x TTM, 3.4x ex-cash and a 57.3% gross margin — the only one of the three to clear all three tests.

Step 1 Initial screen: three hard criteria halve the list

The funnel applies three coarse screens: understandable, positive free cash flow, and a valuation that does not pay for things we do not understand. Three of the six are cut on the spot: Douyin E-commerce and SHEIN are unlisted and uninvestable; Amazon, at a USD 2.88 trillion market cap and 21.5x P/E, sits outside the range we would discuss at this price.

Three Chinese e-commerce names remain: all cheap, all profitable, all understandable.

Step 2 Eliminate JD.com: good company, bad business

JD.com 2026Q2 revenue was RMB 346.4 billion, down 2.9% YoY, with net profit attributable to shareholders of RMB 7.1 billion, a 2.0% net margin and a P/E (TTM) of 17x. The problem isn't the valuation, it's the model: the 1P business must carry hundreds of thousands of staff and thousands of warehouses, earns a 2% spread, and no amount of scale can conjure high gross margins.

Its moat is real — fulfillment speed and authentic-goods mindshare — but it has not translated into excess profit. A company at 17x P/E with negative growth and a 2% net margin does not sit at the end of this funnel.

Step 3: Strip out Alibaba — half its valuation isn't e-commerce

Alibaba's June 2026 quarter revenue came in at RMB 268.95 billion, +9%, but operating profit was RMB 15.16 billion, −57% YoY, and net profit fell 75% YoY. The company is paying for AI cloud and instant retail: cloud revenue growth of 45% looks good, but profit is bleeding.

The bigger problem is the valuation structure. Within the 13.4x P/E, how much is e-commerce, how much is cloud, and how much is Ant and investments? To buy Alibaba, you have to understand cloud, instant retail, overseas and a pile of associates all at once. That is not something this funnel can screen for.

Step 4 final pick Pinduoduo: three conditions met simultaneously

Condition one: cheap. 8.2x TTM, 3.4x ex-cash. It is the only one of the three Chinese e-commerce names where "low valuation + net cash" both hold. Alibaba trades at 13.4x and JD at 17x, and neither carries net cash at 60% of market cap.

Condition two: high gross margin. 57.3% versus Alibaba's 38% and JD's 9%. On the same dollar of revenue it keeps 57 cents; the others keep a dime. That gap is not efficiency, it is the model.

Condition three: downside is floored. RMB 456.4 billion in cash and short-term investments, 57% of market cap. In a bear case (RMB 70 billion net profit, 3x), the stock is at USD 66, down only 17%. In a market full of 50% drawdowns, 17% downside is scarce.

Why this one, not "the cheaper one"

Some will argue JD is cheaper on 0.19x P/S. But cheapness depends on the denominator. JD's 0.19x P/S rests on a 2% net margin; Pinduoduo's 1.87x P/S rests on a 24% net margin. On price per dollar of profit, Pinduoduo is more than three times cheaper.

That is the point of the funnel: not to find the stock that has fallen the most, but to find the lowest price/earning power. On this yardstick Pinduoduo has no rival among the three Chinese e-commerce companies.

Screening limits: what the funnel misses

It misses three categories: fast-growing loss-makers (JD.com then, Pinduoduo itself in 2018), unlisted names (Douyin, SHEIN) and "expensive but excellent" companies (Amazon). The screen is defensive: it hunts for "opportunities that won't lose you big money," not "opportunities that can make you ten times your money."

For Pinduoduo, that means it is not a ten-bagger; it is the kind of name that does not fall much and, once re-rated, can deliver 60%. A name that comes out of a defensive screen should be held in a defensive position size — 3%, not 30%.

Conclusion

Conclusion: Pinduoduo is the final pick of the six, as the only name that simultaneously clears all three conditions — high gross margin, low valuation and net cash. The other two each fall short on one.

JD lacks gross margin (9%); Alibaba lacks valuation clarity (half of its 13.4x is cloud). Pinduoduo trades at 8.2x, 3.4x ex-cash, with a 57.3% gross margin — the only one of the three that passes all three.

But "why it" only answers "which to buy", not "whether to buy". The answer to whether to buy comes from the six gates and valuation: buy, 3% position. This is not a perfect target; it is a target with favourable odds.
