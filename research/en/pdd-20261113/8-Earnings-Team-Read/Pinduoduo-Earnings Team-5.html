Core view

Read together, the four close readings share one big commonality: none of them changed their view because profit fell 13%. All four treat the decline as the product of deliberate investment and institutional costs, not operational deterioration.

The disagreement is over whether this is good or bad. Duan Yongping sees ecosystem spending as a duty; Buffett sees money spent without return as a capital allocation failure; Munger sees a heavier model as warranting a lower multiple; Li Lu sees it as buying admission, which has to be paid for.

Compress the four views into one sentence: the profit decline was bought, and the question is what was bought.

Three synthesized conclusions

1. Business quality intact, growth gone. Gross margin is up, cash flow is strong and debt is zero — none of the three has deteriorated. Advertising +3.5% against net profit −13% marks the shift from growth stock to cash cow, and the valuation method must change accordingly.

2. Regulatory costs are entering the financials, and they are irreversible. The EU's fixed cost of EUR 5 per category, proposed US rules and the EUR 200 million fine are not one-off disturbances but a new cost baseline. Munger and Li Lu agree completely on this point.

3. Cash is the only certainty, and also the thing most uncertain to be obtained. RMB 456.4 billion provides a valuation floor, but zero dividend and zero buyback mean that floor can only be realised through the share price.

Synthetic operational implications

Buy, but size the position at just 3%, phased over three to four quarters.The case is not "bullish"; it is 3.6:1 upside/(downside) with a cash cushion beneath it.

Do not buy it for the net cash.Cash sits on the financial statements, not in your pocket: its role is to cap downside, not to generate returns. Returns can only come from a re-rating, and the triggers — buybacks or a recovery in advertising growth — have not materialised yet.

What to watch next

Q3 results (late November): transaction services growth is the first read. A drop to single digits would signal that Temu's unit economics have broken down; holding above 10% would show localization is working.

Annual report (March 2027): capital return policy. Even a USD 5 billion buyback authorization would mark the start of a re-rating.

Conclusion

Synthesis: this is a chance to buy a cash cow at a 70% discount, not to bottom-fish a growth stock.

The four read the financials differently but agree on valuation: 3.4x ex-cash P/E is cheap. They also agree that whether the return can be captured is uncertain.

So the answer is buy, but buy small.
