Core view

Duan Yongping does not read a financial report from start to finish; he looks for just three numbers: gross margin, growth of the core business, and where the cash went. Three lines, and the report is read.

The three lines of this report: gross margin 57.3% (good), advertising +3.5% (bad), cash RMB 456.4 billion (a problem).

First line Gross margin: the business is not broken

57.3%, up 1.4 percentage points year on year. This line tells you the platform's bargaining power over merchants remains intact—if it had started buying revenue with subsidies, gross margin would fall; the fact that it held means the rent-collection capacity is still there.

But note that this 1.4 percentage points is structural: Temu's commission share rose, and commission gross margin is higher than advertising. So it is not "the core platform got stronger," it is "overseas share got higher." The same line of numbers, two readings, opposite conclusions.

Second row, advertising growth: this is the most critical row

RMB 57.6 billion, +3.5%. Advertising revenue in domestic e-commerce is essentially merchants' marketing budgets; whether merchants are willing to spend depends on whether they can still make money on the platform. 3.5% means merchants' willingness to spend has saturated.

If this line turns negative, it marks a change in the nature of the business. Not "slower growth" but "merchants pulling back." Duan Yongping would treat it as a signal for the stop-doing list: when you can't understand it, don't act.

Third line Cash: the money was neither spent nor repaid

RMB 456.4 billion, up RMB 34.1 billion in six months. Over the same period the company spent on its "RMB 100 billion support" program and invested in Temu localization. Add the two together and cash still rose—which shows the intensity of spending is far below the narrative, and that it has the capacity to buy back stock but chooses not to.

His judgment will be blunt: this is not an earnings problem, it is a people problem. However pretty the earnings, if the people do not repay, all you can do is wait on the share price.

Conclusion

Duan Yongping-style conclusion: financials score 7 out of 10. The business is not broken (gross margin 57.3%), growth has stalled (advertising +3.5%), and there is nowhere to put the money (cash of RMB 456.4 billion).

Whether to buy does not depend on this report; it depends on whether you believe management will one day buy back stock.
