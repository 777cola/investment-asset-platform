## Why do this

Half of the Pinduoduo figures circulating in the media have been reprocessed, with GAAP and non-GAAP mixed together, point-in-time and period-end figures conflated, and ADS counts confused with ordinary share counts. Stack those three conflations and the same company ends up with two P/E ratios, 8x and 12x. Pin down the accounting basis first; only then argue about whether the stock is cheap.

## Four items deviate by more than 1%; explain each one

Net profit: 4.9% gap. GAAP net profit attributable to shareholders is RMB 27.18 billion; Non-GAAP (excluding equity incentives) is RMB 28.49 billion. Broker summaries often cite "net profit of RMB 28.5 billion" without qualification, leading readers to assume GAAP. Throughout this report, all YoY figures use GAAP and all "earning power" figures use Non-GAAP, with each labelled as such.

Cash: 9.5% gap. RMB 456.4 billion is cash and short-term investments at end-June 2026; RMB 416.9 billion is the reading near end-2025 (another research note's basis also excludes long-term deposits). Cash rose RMB 34.1 billion in a single quarter — that growth rate matters more than the absolute number.

EPS: 4.1% gap. Backing out EPS from a 8.2x P/E gives USD 9.63; dividing reported net profit by ADS gives USD 9.25. The gap stems from differing TTM window conventions (whether 2025Q2 is included). This report uses USD 9.25 and states P/E only as "about 8.2x".

Temu Europe share: 20pp gap. Investment banks estimate 33%; third-party research says above 40%. This number determines the damage from EU tariffs, yet the true figure is unobtainable. The approach is to give no precise value: the main text says "around four tenths", and the sensitivity analysis uses 33% and 45% as the two endpoints.

## The finally adopted basis

Revenue, profit and expenses: 2026Q2 results, with GAAP and Non-GAAP presented separately. Cash: RMB 456.4 billion as of 6/30/2026. Market cap: USD 78.97 × 1.423 billion ADSs = USD 112.4 billion, or RMB 798.0 billion at 7.1. The exchange rate is shown separately and excluded from profit calculations. The three-scenario valuation is computed in RMB and converted to USD at 7.1, avoiding a double conversion from the USD basis.

## Reverse check: are the historical figures back-solved from growth rates reliable?

Some historical data in this report is back-solved from YoY growth rates (2025Q2 revenue of RMB 104.04 billion, advertising of RMB 55.6 billion, transaction services of RMB 48.4 billion). The method can be validated against a known answer: full-year 2025 revenue of RMB 431.8 billion, +10% YoY, back-solves to RMB 392.5 billion for 2024, versus RMB 393.8 billion disclosed in the company's 2024 annual report — a 0.33% difference.

That 0.33% error confirms back-solving is usable, but also that rounding eats the decimals. Wherever figures are back-solved, this report flags them in the table notes and does not treat them as disclosed values. Any figure entering the valuation model uses disclosed values or research consensus.

## The part that has not been verified

Pinduoduo does not disclose segment data for Temu, so GMV, unit economics and fulfilment costs all rest on external estimates that may carry errors of up to 30%. Duoduo Maicai is likewise not reported separately. We assign these two businesses only a qualitative weighting in valuation; neither enters the model. An honest caveat: the main site and the consolidated statements are the only things you can know with precision. On Temu, the market is guessing just as you are.

## There are three numbers I chose not to write.

Three figures are not disclosed: Temu's GMV, Temu's segment gross profit and Duoduo Maicai's loss. Every version in circulation is an outside estimate, with errors of up to 30%. Putting a falsely precise number in a report is more dangerous than writing "unknown"—downstream analysis will cite it as fact.
