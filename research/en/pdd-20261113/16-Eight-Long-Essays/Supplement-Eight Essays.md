## Misreading 1: Treating it as a growth stock

2026Q2 revenue +8%, advertising +3.5%, and the market's verdict is that growth has stalled. True — but the framework is wrong.A company with 57% gross margin, 24% net margin and zero interest-bearing debt: slow growth is not a flaw, it is a phase.

When Apple's revenue went negative in 2016, the stock traded at 10x earnings and then rose tenfold over the next decade — because it began buying back stock. Pinduoduo sits almost exactly at that stage now; what it lacks is the buyback.

## The rent collector: the platform captures the most stable slice of the value chain

A product with a factory price of RMB 10 retails for RMB 40. The factory earns RMB 0.5 to 1.5, the courier RMB 1.5, and the platform RMB 8 (RMB 2 commission plus RMB 6 amortised advertising), with the remainder going to traffic and channels. The platform captures the thickest and most stable slice of the value chain.

This distribution is no accident. The platform controls two things: demand (800 million users) and the rules — how traffic is allocated, how prices are set, when cash is settled. Whoever writes the rules takes the excess profit.

## Layer one, low-price mindset: a real moat, but a "negative brand"

In consumers' minds, the equation "Pinduoduo = cheap" already holds. Taobao and JD.com spent three years and tens of billions in subsidies and still could not change it. This is a genuine moat—sustained not by advertising but by habit.

That said, it is a "negative brand": users take no pride in buying on Pinduoduo, only satisfaction at saving money. This caps average order value and rules out any move upmarket to attack the branded segment.
