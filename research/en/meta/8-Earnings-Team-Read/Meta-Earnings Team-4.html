Earnings Deep Dive (Li Lu) — Meta

Reading a company's place in civilization through its income statement

Core view

When Li Lu reads financial reports, he looks at whether a company's position in the network of human cooperation has changed. This report's answer: the position has not changed, but the company is paying a deposit on the next position, and the deposit is USD 140 billion a year.

Location: the daily routine of 3.64 billion people

Daily active users of 3.64 billion, up 3% year on year, looks like only modest growth. But the base is already close to half of global internet users, and still growing 3% at this scale shows it has not been displaced by any new platform. For comparison: Snap has 495 million DAUs, Pinterest 661 million MAUs, Reddit about 130 million DAUs — together less than a third of Meta.

How much are advertisers willing to pay for this position? Unit price rose 14%. That is the pricing of the position.

Transformation: from connecting people to doing work on their behalf

Muse's significance lies not in revenue but in relationships. For the past two decades Meta sold person-to-person connection; now it wants to sell person-to-task connection. If it succeeds, it transforms from a social company into an operating-system-level gateway. If it fails, it remains a social company, just USD 50 billion poorer.

Early signals in the financials: 10 million accounts within 40 days of launch, DAU at 25%, usage in shopping scenarios above 25%, average order value above USD 200, and Stripe temporarily raising its credit limit after transaction volume came in above the consensus of expectations. These are evidence that demand genuinely exists, not evidence that revenue has already been booked.

Capital: where the money comes from determines who gets the returns

Operating cash flow for the first nine months of 2026 was RMB 97 billion, capex RMB 83.06 billion, and what was left was not enough to cover dividends and taxes. Hence the USD 95 billion financing framework on October 26.

Li Lu would focus on this: fund with your own cash and the returns accrue entirely to existing shareholders; fund with equity and the returns must be split between old and new shareholders. Meta chose the latter, which shows it is itself unsure whether returns can cover its cost of capital; otherwise it should have issued debt, where interest is tax-deductible and there is no dilution.

Endgame: three possibilities

First, the agent succeeds, Meta becomes the personal computing gateway, and with three legs — advertising, subscriptions and transaction take rates — the valuation re-rates above 30x. Second, the agent is mediocre but the advertising base holds firm, and the company becomes a mature business with high investment and mid-single-digit growth, valued at 15 to 18x. Third, the gateway is captured by OpenAI or Google, Meta degrades into a content supplier, valued at 10 to 12x.

I assign probabilities of 25%, 50% and 25% to these three scenarios. The expected value roughly equals the current price — that is the mathematical basis for watching.

Conclusion

Close-read conclusion: position solid, transition unproven, ownership structure has shifted. He would give this company high long-term attention and a very low current position. The best entry point is the moment the transition is proven and the market has not yet reacted, not now, when the transition has just begun and the market has already paid a deposit on it.
