TeamLead summary: divergences and rulings across four parallel tracks — Meta

Three points of contention, one conclusion

Core view

All four tracks are now complete. Business and industry score the name a "good business"; financials flag a "bad entry point"; risk says "don't buy at the current price." The combined verdict: maintain "Watch, wait for a pullback," target price USD 620 (neutral), trigger price USD 500. The three genuine disagreements are set out below.

Disagreement one: is the RMB 140 billion capex "investment" or "cost"?

The commercial team frames this as investment: ad unit pricing up 14% is evidence of return, and AI-driven recommendations are indeed lifting conversion. The finance team frames it as cost: investment needs a calculable rate of return, and none can be calculated today — only depreciation is visible.

Verdict: lean toward the finance view, but give the commercial view half the credit. The ad-side return is real (volume and price both up for two consecutive quarters), but its scale cannot match the scale of the outlay: an extra USD 70 billion over a year yields roughly USD 40 billion in incremental ad revenue (a rough calculation assuming half of the 26% growth comes from AI), leaving net return close to zero after depreciation and operating costs. For now, then, this spending counts only as "necessary capex to maintain competitiveness," not as "value-creating investment."

Divergence two: how much is Muse worth

The commercial side assigns option value, believing a tenfold improvement in cost efficiency is achievable by 2027; the risk side assigns zero, given no auditable revenue.

Verdict: optimistic scenario, not base case. Muse's data looks good—10 million downloads in 40 days, 25% daily active users, average order value of USD 200 in shopping scenarios. But unit economics are negative: monthly cost per user is about USD 37, subscriptions top out at USD 100, with the mainstream tier at USD 20. Until cost efficiency improves by an order of magnitude, more users means more losses. In valuation terms, this business counts only as an option, not as cash flow.

Disagreement 3: Is the placement "ammunition" or an "alarm"

The industry role reads this as normal capital activity: all four major cloud players are raising capital — Microsoft issued USD 25 billion of debt on October 29, SpaceX raised USD 40 billion, Anthropic is filing for an IPO, and the financing window remains open. The risk role reads it as an alarm: a company with negative free cash flow, whose stock is -22% from its high, choosing to issue equity signals that it judges both debt and cash flow to be unsustainable.

Verdict: both are right, but timing determines the nature.If the 2027 expense guidance confirms that investment has peaked, this equity issuance is stockpiling grain in advance — a plus. If 2027 requires further escalation, it is the first shovel in filling the hole — a minus. It is therefore a watch item, not a conclusion item.

Final verdict and execution conditions

Rating: Watch, wait for pullback. Target price: neutral USD 620 (28 USD 2027 EPS × 22x), bear case 410, bull case 860. After dilution from the new share issuance, neutral is about USD 580.

Three hard execution conditions: ① Buy trigger — share price ≤500 USD (below 19x diluted), or 2027 expense growth guidance below revenue growth; ② Add condition — free cash flow turns positive and advertising growth ≥15%; ③ Abandon condition — advertising growth below 10% for two consecutive quarters, or capex guidance raised by another 20% or more.

One-line conclusion: Good company, bad timing, no discount on the price. Wait.

Three facts all four people agree on

Views diverge widely, yet all four agree on three facts: ① the advertising business is in its best state in history, with both volume and price rising and share expanding; ② cash flow turned negative for the first time, and not because of operating problems; ③ management treats shareholder money as a tool for pursuing a personal mission, and the system offers no check on this.

Put the three facts together and the conclusion is neither "buy" nor "sell" but "wait." What we are waiting for is price, not news. News arrives daily—Muse launch, model releases, fines, share issuance—and none of it changes those three facts. Only two numbers can: the 2027 expense growth rate, and the share price.

Conclusion

TeamLead verdict: watch, wait for a pullback.The four roles split on whether the spending is investment or cost, but all agree the current price offers no margin of safety. On execution, USD 500 is the price trigger and 2027 expense guidance the fundamental one; act only when either is met.
