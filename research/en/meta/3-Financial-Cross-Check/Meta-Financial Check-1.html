Cross-check: the core numbers hold up, with one definitional trap

I cross-checked seven key 2026Q3 figures against two independent sources: revenue, advertising, operating profit, net profit, operating cash flow and free cash flow all deviate by less than 1%, confirming the financial data in the working papers is accurate. The only item requiring care is capex — the company discloses two figures, RMB 34.91 billion (including finance lease principal) and RMB 33.94 billion (purchase of property and equipment), a 2.9% gap; mixing them would throw the free cash flow recalculation off by nearly USD 1 billion.

The only deviation above 1%: the two measures of capex

RMB 34.91 billion is "capital expenditure including finance lease principal repayments"; RMB 33.94 billion is "purchase of property and equipment" in the cash flow statement. The USD 970 million gap is precisely the finance lease principal component. This is not a calculation error by anyone; it reflects a difference in the boundaries of the two concepts.

Why it matters: free cash flow is 327.9 − 339.4 − 9.7 = -21.2. Using only 339.4 gives -11.5 billion and the erroneous conclusion that the company is "close to break-even"; using only 349.1 gives -21.2 but leaves the deduction unexplained. Both numbers are correct, but only by putting all three together does the account close. This is also why every reference to capital expenditure in this report is labelled "including finance lease principal."

The 11.6% market-cap gap reflects timing, not an error

Closed at USD 697.52 on 21 October, implying a USD 1.78 trillion market cap, and at USD 606.73 on 2 November, implying USD 1.575 trillion. The 11.6% gap is entirely price-driven, not a change in share count. The 2.596 billion shares stay unchanged until the offering closes — the USD 37.5 billion issuance priced on 26 October, with settlement typically T+2 to T+5, and the new shares will only be counted at the end of the fourth quarter or in early 2027.

This report therefore uses 2.596 billion shares and USD 1.575 trillion throughout, and calculates dilution separately when discussing the offering.Do not assume others are wrong when they write "USD 1.7 trillion market cap" — that reflects the price before 21 October.

One more figure that is easy to misread: +264% net profit

Net profit came in at RMB 9.86 billion, up 264% YoY, and the media duly reported a "surge." The number carries no interpretive value: in Q3 2025 Meta booked roughly USD 18.95 billion of income tax following a change in US tax law — an effective tax rate of 87% — which crushed net profit to USD 2.71 billion. The base was artificially depressed, and with the tax rate back at 24% this year, growth naturally runs at triple digits.

The adjusted basis is what matters. Excluding the USD 9.89 billion litigation settlement, EPS was USD 7.40 on a 17% effective tax rate; GAAP diluted EPS was USD 3.83. The two differ by nearly a factor of two, and which one to use depends on the question being answered — 7.40 to judge operating quality, 3.83 to judge what shareholders actually received this year. This report uses GAAP in the income statement and adjusted figures when discussing the core business, flagging which is which each time either appears.

How should we treat the three items that cannot be cross-checked against two sources?

① 2027 total expense guidance: management referred only to a "significant increase" without quantifying it, leaving the market to speculate. Approach: cite the qualitative statement alone, give no forecast figure, and flag it as an "author's assumption" in the scenario assumptions.

② Muse revenue: not broken out in the Q3 results; media reports cover only downloads and account counts (10 million in the 40 days since launch, daily active users about 25%). Approach: no revenue estimate; treat as "option value" only.

③ Reality Labs' USD 15 billion loss target: an oral commitment from Zuckerberg on the earnings call only, with no written guidance. Approach: quote his words and note "management commentary, not formal company guidance"; reconciliation possible only in Q1 2027.

The three numbers not adopted

① 26.27x P/E: calculated on the October 21 close and now stale; this report uses the 21x based on November 2 throughout. ② "AI brings in X billion in incremental ad revenue": the company said only "meaningful increment" without breaking it out, so any specific figure is a third-party estimate. ③ Sell-side 2027 EPS consensus: estimates range from USD 26 to USD 31, too wide a dispersion, so this report uses its own three-scenario assumptions and labels them "author's estimate" in the valuation section.

Conclusion

Final basis: GAAP income-statement data, capex including finance-lease principal, and 2.596 billion shares. Six of the seven checks deviate by less than 1%, so the underlying workpaper data can be used with confidence. Readers should note the two capex definitions and the timing gap in market cap — the two points most likely to be conflated when the note is circulated, which would in turn cause free cash flow of -RMB 2.12 billion to be misread as -RMB 1.15 billion.
