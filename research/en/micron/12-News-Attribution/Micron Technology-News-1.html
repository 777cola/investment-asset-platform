Core view

Four of the seven Micron-related developments over the past three months (July–September) are genuine signals, ranked by impact on fundamentals: TrendForce's share data (CXMT 9.5%), Counterpoint's HBM share (Micron 18%, down 3pct QoQ), SK hynix's CEO's 2027 supply warning, and Wall Street's change of methodology. The CEO's share sale, Burry's short and the war of target prices are noise.

The stock action confirms it: up about 14% cumulatively since September, +5% on 22 September to USD 1,096.16 (the day Burry announced he was adding to his short), down more than 2% on 23 September and closing at USD 1,082.28 on 25 September, 10.8% below the 52-week high of USD 1,213.56. The move shows the market is pricing "how much longer the cycle lasts", not "who is short".

The 226% gain was not driven by any single news item

The +226% year-to-date move reflects a process of "continuous re-rating" rather than any single news item. It breaks into three phases: the first was the March FY26Q2 results (revenue RMB 23.86 billion, gross margin 74.4%), which confirmed the pricing upcycle; the second was the June FY26Q3 results (revenue RMB 41.46 billion, gross margin 84.9%), which confirmed the earnings elasticity; the third is September's "methodology switch", as sell-side analysts began valuing the stock on 2029 earnings.

The driver is the same across all three phases: earnings expectations were repeatedly revised upward, and the share price was revised upward even faster. Mid-September P/E (2027E) was 5.77x; it is now 6.5x. The rising multiple shows this rally is no longer purely earnings-driven and now carries an element of "valuation expansion" — which is where caution is warranted.

Attribution 2: Share data has been the single most important information flow of the past three months

Put the two share datasets together and the story is clear. TrendForce: Micron's DRAM share is 23.3%, revenue up 67% QoQ (company basis), share broadly stable; but CXMT rose from 7.6% to 9.5% with revenue up 99.3% QoQ, growing at twice Micron's rate — it is taking the incremental volume in commodity DRAM.

Counterpoint: Micron's HBM share is 18%, down 3pct QoQ; Samsung jumped from 21% to 33%, SK hynix fell from 58% to 50%. Samsung grabbed 12 points in a single quarter, the most substantive change in this round of HBM competition. Micron's capability is not in question (12-layer HBM4 ramping twice as fast as the prior generation, monthly capacity doubling to 100,000 wafers, or 10.0, by year-end); the problem is that it is the defender in this share battle, not the attacker.

Together, the two datasets imply for valuation: Micron is holding on "total volume" and retreating at the "highest-end incremental" segment. If HBM accounts for 35% of DRAM wafers in 2027 and Micron holds only a 15% HBM share, its product-mix upgrade will lag the industry average.

Which are noise: CEO share sales, Burry's short, conflicting price targets

The CEO sold 40,000 shares for USD 38.76 million under a 10b5-1 plan adopted on January 30, 2026 — compliant and predictable. The sale equals 4.6% of his 870,000-share holding and 0.0035% of the company's 1.13 billion shares.The message is that insiders see USD 970 as a fair price, not that insiders are bearish on the company.

Burry's short rests on the thesis that "this DRAM tightness comes from a supply-side effect as capacity is diverted to HBM, not from structural demand." The logic holds, but he gave no timing trigger. On the day he announced the short (9/22), Micron's stock rose 5% —the market voted with its feet that he was early.Shorting a company on 6.5x P/E with USD 30.2 billion of net cash and USD 28.2 billion of quarterly earnings power requires not a thesis but a timing catalyst.

Target prices clash: Citi 1,300, Wells Fargo 1,400, BofA 1,550, UBS 1,625 — a 25% spread. This is not "analysts being unprofessional" but "different years in the denominator": UBS applies 8x to 2029, Citi a more conservative multiple to 2027.The dispersion in target prices is itself evidence that a cyclical stock cannot be priced off a single multiple.

One overlooked signal: the "accounting change" of September 16

Most read the "Wall Street is starting to change its valuation method" narrative as an explanation of the share-price move. In fact it is a change in valuation methodology: from "5–8x current-year profit" to "8–9x 2028–2029 profit", on the grounds that SCA long-term contracts lengthen revenue visibility.

If the switch holds, Micron's valuation centre shifts permanently higher. But it carries one precondition: long-term contracts must have been honoured through a downcycle. There is no evidence of that today, so the "methodology change" is currently just sell-side wishful thinking rather than market consensus — the 6.5x 2027E multiple is the proof; had the market really switched methods, it would be 9x now.

Conclusion

Conclusion: four of the seven events are noise; two are real signals. The real signals are share (Samsung taking HBM, CXMT taking commodity DRAM) and valuation method (the sell side trying to reclassify the stock from cyclical to contract). The noise is the CEO's share sale, Burry's short, the target-price fight and the USD 250 billion investment, which won't affect profit for three years. The next catalyst is already set: the September 30 earnings report. Consensus is revenue of RMB 50.8–51.4 billion and EPS of USD 31.5–31.75; hitting guidance is not a positive — only above RMB 51.4 billion counts.
