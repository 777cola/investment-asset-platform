📝 Conclusion: Hold, do not add

Hold, do not add. Micron FY26Q3 revenue USD 41.46 billion, gross margin 84.9%, net income USD 28.24 billion — the purest beneficiary of this memory cycle; HBM4 has ramped, 16 take-or-pay long-term agreements lock through 2030, and the business model is genuinely improving. But USD 1,082 corresponds to 6.5x 2027 expected earnings — that multiple is not the market making a mistake, it is the market knowing the denominator is at a peak. Existing holders keep holding, waiting for the September 30 earnings report to confirm FY27Q1 guidance; new positions wait for one of two things: the stock back below USD 800, or SCA coverage clearly above half.
