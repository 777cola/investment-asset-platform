Core view

Six verifiable assumptions underpin the Micron holding case: three hold (the 2027 supply-demand gap, no dilution from net cash, a manageable capex cadence), two are at a tipping point (gross margin not peaking in 2027, rising SCA coverage) and one is deteriorating (HBM share holding at 18%).

Gross margin matters most. Q4 guidance of 86%, up only 1pct QoQ, marks the first time this cycle that it "won't go higher." Should gross margin fall sequentially in any quarter of 2027, the stock falls first even if the other five assumptions all hold—because that gross margin is what supports the 6.5x denominator.

Three assumptions that hold: they guarantee "no death"

Supply-demand gap: this is arithmetic, not a forecast. Wafer supply rises 12% in 2027, half of it absorbed by HBM; ordinary DRAM bit growth of 15% against 22% demand leaves a 7-percentage-point gap; finished-goods inventory at Samsung and SK hynix covers less than 10 days. Absent a sudden brake on AI capex, a shortage in the first half of 2027 is highly likely.

Capex cadence: USD 27 billion in FY26 and over USD 40 billion in FY27 looks aggressive, but the ramp is gradual — USD 7.1 billion in Q3, about USD 10 billion in Q4, and roughly USD 11.5 billion per quarter in FY27. Management is not betting everything at once, which is right. The risk to watch is another upward revision in 2027 — that would signal a loss of control.

Balance sheet: USD 30.2 billion in cash, 1.13 billion shares unchanged for five years and a dividend raised only 30% together mean that even if prices collapse in 2027, no equity issuance or asset sales will be needed. This is the most reassuring part for holders.

Two critical assumptions determine how much it is worth

Gross margin: Q4 guidance of 86%, up 1pct QoQ, marks the first point in the cycle where it "can't go higher." That does not mean it will fall—hold above 84% and the 2027 EPS assumption of USD 166 stands. The threshold: one more quarter of gains and it is safe; a QoQ decline is the alarm.

SCA coverage: 16 contracts, USD 100 billion over five years, averaging USD 20 billion a year—15%–20% of FY26 revenue of USD 129 billion. Management has said "long-term contracts correspond to minimum volumes at minimum prices," implying actual revenue will be higher. But "actually higher" cannot be used for valuation—only disclosed coverage can. If this assumption holds, the valuation can re-rate from 6.5x to 8–9x.

One assumption is deteriorating: the variable to watch most closely this cycle

HBM share: 2026Q2 Micron 18%, -3pct QoQ; Samsung 21%→33%, SK hynix 58%→50%. Samsung was first globally to mass-produce HBM4 in February 2026, and in June Jensen Huang confirmed all three had passed Nvidia certification—Micron's "HBM4 certification dividend" lasted only one quarter.

Why does this matter? HBM is the narrative core of this round of valuation expansion: HBM unit prices are 5–8x those of DDR5 of the same capacity, so whoever holds higher share has the better product mix. If Micron's share falls from 18% to 15%, its ASP uplift will lag the industry and the "structural support" for gross margin weakens.

Counter-evidence deserves fair recording too: Micron's monthly HBM capacity will double to 100,000 wafers by year-end (40,000–50,000 wafers at end-2025), and the 12-layer HBM4 ramp is twice as fast as the prior generation. With capacity doubling plus a yield lead, share may not keep falling.So the status is "deteriorating," not "disproven."

Falsification conditions: three lines; triggering any one forces a re-rating
  • Contract prices turn negative MoM for two consecutive months: the first hard signal of a cycle reversal. The share price has historically peaked 3–6 months ahead of it, but the decline accelerates once the reversal is confirmed
  • Gross margin guidance falls below 80%: pricing has begun to erode profit, invalidating the 2027 EPS assumption of USD 166 and immediately taking 6.5x to 9–10x on the revised-down E
  • HBM share falls below 15% while Samsung's share exceeds 40%: the HBM competitive landscape has settled, Micron is relegated to third supplier and the product-mix upgrade story is over

The reverse confirmation conditions are equally clear: disclosure that SCA coverage exceeds 30%, or HBM accounting for more than 50% of FY27 capex details. If either materialises, the valuation centre can be raised.

Conclusion

Conclusion: three of six assumptions hold, two are borderline and one has deteriorated. The holding thesis has not been falsified, but neither has it been strengthened. The key things to watch are gross margin (Q4 guidance +1pct QoQ, now borderline) and HBM share (18%, -3pct QoQ, already deteriorated). Action: hold without adding; cut if gross margin guidance falls below 80% or contract prices turn negative QoQ for two consecutive months; reassess adding if SCA coverage disclosure exceeds 30%.
