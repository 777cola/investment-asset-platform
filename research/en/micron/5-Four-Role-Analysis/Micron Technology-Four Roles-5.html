Core view

Read together, the four reports agree more than they disagree: the company is sound, the balance sheet is safe, industry momentum can run another 4–6 quarters, and prices sit in the second half of the cycle. The real disagreements are three: how much of the cycle remains, how much valuation premium the SCA long-term contract deserves, and where the line falls between "hold" and "trim". Each is adjudicated below, followed by a unified action conclusion.

The conclusions of the four independent reports were cross-checked by the Team Lead; disagreements were not forced into consensus but adjudicated under the principle of "verifiability first."
Consensus one: on company-level quality, the four reports do not disagree

Business analysis scores the business 7; financial analysis passes cash-flow quality; industry research acknowledges the three-oligopoly structure; risk assessment concedes the company will not die. All four agree on "this is a good company": lowest bit cost of the three, HBM4 12-layer in mass production and designed into the full Vera Rubin lineup, net cash of RMB 30.2 billion, 16 long-term contracts locked through 2030, and USD 250 billion of US capacity backed by USD 6.165 billion in subsidies.

This means any "avoid"-level conclusion is wrong — this is not a company to hide from. All disagreement centers on "price" and "timing," not on "the asset." That is why the final rating is "Hold, do not add," not "Sell" or "Buy."

Disagreement one: how much of the cycle remains — verdict: fundamentals run to the first half of 2027, share prices move earlier

The industry research evidence is hard: 2027 wafer supply +12%, half of it for HBM; commodity DRAM bits +15% vs demand +22%; inventory below 10 days; meaningful supply growth only in 2028. This data supports "shortage persists through end-2027."

The risk assessment evidence is also hard: contract price growth has narrowed from +90%–95% YoY to +10%–15% QoQ, HBM share -3pct QoQ, Samsung grabbing 12 points in a single quarter. Historically, share prices peak 3–6 months before contract prices turn negative.

Verdict: the two are not contradictory; they differ in time scale. Fundamentals (prices, revenue, gross margin) most likely peak in H1 2027; share prices will peak before contract prices turn negative QoQ, i.e. between Q4 2026 and Q1 2027. Implication for holders: do not wait for earnings to deteriorate before acting; watch the QoQ direction of contract prices.

Disagreement two: what is the SCA long-term contract worth — the ruling is "a 0.5–1x premium, no re-rating"

Duan Yongping's lens asks whether the business model has improved. The answer is yes: customers pay a USD 22 billion deposit upfront, sign for five years and accept a price floor — a first for the memory industry. Buffett's lens asks about coverage. The answer is only about 20% (USD 100 billion minimum revenue ÷ five years = USD 20 billion a year, against FY26 revenue of USD 129 billion).

The verdict follows coverage: contracted revenue at 20% can lift the valuation from 6.5x to 7.5–8x, but not above 10x. Getting above 10x requires two pieces of evidence: coverage above 50%, and a price floor actually enforced through a downturn. The first could appear as early as 2027; the second must wait for the next downturn.

This verdict directly shapes positioning: if the September 30 earnings disclose SCA coverage of 30%–40%, the valuation premium can be raised and the neutral target price lifted from 1,120 to around 1,300; if coverage holds at 20%, the original call stands.

Disagreement 3: whether to sell now — verdict: tier by cost basis

The odds arithmetic in the risk assessment is correct: bear case 400, base case 1,120, bull case 1,620, weighted 25/50/25 = 1,065, versus the current price of 1,082.28, an expected return of -1.6%. From the perspective of "new money," this trade holds no appeal.

But the arithmetic for "old money" is different. If the cost basis is USD 400–600, selling triggers capital gains tax (US long-term 20% plus state tax) and forfeits the free option of "SCA coverage crossing 50% → valuation re-rating." With odds near zero, tax and option value are enough to make "don't sell" the rational choice.

Verdict: Holders stay put and do not add; positions with a cost above USD 1,000 trim to half; those with no position stay out. Three price levels serve as discipline lines: a break below USD 800 (about 4.8x 2027E) is a level to consider building a position; a move above USD 1,300 (about 7.8x 2027E) trims one-third; a break below USD 700 with contract prices turning negative month on month means exit entirely.

Unified conclusion and four things to watch over the next 30 days

Taken together, the four lenses paint this picture: a good company that made the right decisions at the right time, priced by the market on peak profit, and the peak is two to three quarters away. Holding is reasonable; adding is not.

  • September 30 FY26Q4 results: whether revenue exceeds the consensus of RMB 51.4 billion, whether gross margin holds at 86%, and whether FY27Q1 EPS guidance is above USD 37
  • SCA coverage: whether management gives a specific figure for its share of DRAM sales volume, and whether it rises from 20%
  • FY27 capex detail: within guidance above RMB 40 billion, how much is HBM and how much is conventional DRAM—the latter determines the 2028 supply shock
  • October–November DRAM contract prices: whether the sequential change is still above +10%; once growth hits zero, the valuation framework will switch immediately

Conclusion

Team Lead ruling: Hold, do not add (consistent with the working papers). The four reports agree on "company quality" and diverge on "cycle position" but the views are reconcilable — fundamentals stay strong through the first half of 2027, while the share price may peak 3–6 months earlier. The SCA long-term contract warrants a 0.5–1x valuation premium, not a re-rating. On execution, tier by cost basis: hold low-cost positions, halve high-cost positions, stay out if flat. Discipline lines: build at USD 800, trim at USD 1,300, trim as soon as the contract price turns negative quarter on quarter.
