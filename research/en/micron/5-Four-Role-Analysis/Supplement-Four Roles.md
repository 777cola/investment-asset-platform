## How to read these five reports

Reports 1–4 were prepared independently by four roles, each covering only its own layer: Duan Yongping tests whether the business itself is sustainable, Buffett examines cash and capital intensity, Munger assesses industry structure and the conditions for reversal, and Li Lu works through the risk checklist and survivability. Report 5 is the Team Lead's synthesis: it lists the disagreements across the four reports, adjudicates them one by one and sets out operating discipline.

None of the four reports changed the numbers or ratings in the underlying draft; disagreements are recorded on the summary page. For conclusions only, read Report 5. To verify them, read in the order business → financials → industry → risk — the natural progression from "business" to "price".
