## Why H1 was judged falsified

Management has argued since 2025 that "scale effects will bring unit costs down and turn free cash flow positive." Three quarters on, revenue has nearly doubled while the free cash flow gap has widened from -RMB 4.71 billion to -RMB 7.47 billion. Scale arrived; cash flow did not follow, because every additional RMB 1 of revenue requires RMB 2.58 of capex.

This is a wrong directional assumption, not a delayed realisation. For it to hold, capex intensity must fall from 258% to below 80% while revenue keeps growing, which means the company must stop buying market share and shift to monetising the installed base—yet in neocloud competition, stopping the land grab means ceding share. A dilemma.

## H3 Why it is the most lethal

H3 is the only assumption that cannot be remedied once broken. If H1 breaks, you can wait it out; if H2 breaks, you can switch customers; if H3 breaks, the RMB 13.4 billion of debt maturing in 2027 must be repaid with equity, and the equity is already at USD 39.51.

A 15% refinancing cost implies annual interest of RMB 1.5 billion on every RMB 10 billion borrowed—4x its single-quarter adjusted operating profit. This is not "a bit more expensive"; it is "borrowing still kills you." So credit metrics matter more than the financials: bond yields are the leading indicator, the financials the lagging one.

## The flip side of the falsification condition: what would change my mind

Tracking the thesis is not just about confirming the bear case; it must also spell out what would break it. Three items: ① The free cash flow gap narrows for two consecutive quarters and management indicates the quarter it turns positive; ② The company successfully issues new debt with a maturity of more than 5 years and a coupon below 10%; ③ A major customer converts its contract to take-or-pay and prepays more.

If any two of the three appear simultaneously, I will upgrade from "Avoid" to "Watch." Only if all three appear simultaneously will I consider valuation. Note: a 30% share price decline is not among the triggers—a cheaper price does not change the thesis, it only changes the odds, and the other side of those odds is -70%.
