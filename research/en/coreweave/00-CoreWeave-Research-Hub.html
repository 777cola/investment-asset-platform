📝 Conclusion: Avoid

Avoid. CoreWeave's Q3 report was impressive operationally: revenue USD 3.661 billion, +168% YoY, adjusted operating profit of USD 379 million above the upper end of guidance, and backlog of USD 132.5 billion. But the report also exposed the ceiling of the model — quarterly free cash flow of negative USD 7.466 billion, net interest of USD 846 million already exceeding adjusted operating profit, total debt of USD 46.87 billion at an average cost of 7.9%, with USD 13.4 billion maturing over the next two years. On November 20 the stock fell 12% to USD 39.51, breaking below its USD 40 IPO price and down 71.4% from its 52-week high, on the same day the Nasdaq fell 5.28% and Nvidia fell 14%. A 71% decline is not a reason to buy: equity value depends on whether the credit markets keep opening, not on whether AI demand keeps growing. Reassess once the path to positive free cash flow is clear, or the debt maturity profile is successfully extended.
