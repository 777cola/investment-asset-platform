Financial analysis (Buffett's perspective) — SK hynix

The profit is real, but owner earnings need a haircut
Research date 2026/07/27  ·  Price ₩1,816,000 (+3.24%)  ·  Market cap Approximately KRW 1,291 trillion (approximately USD 920 billion)  ·  PE (TTM, as of 2026Q1) Approximately 17.2x

Core view

2026Q1 net profit was RMB 40.35 trillion and free cash flow RMB 18.67 trillion — impressive numbers. But peak earnings must be adjusted for two things: the scheduled depreciation of expansionary capex, and non-operating equity revaluation gains.

Income statement: all of the growth came from price; costs barely moved

Revenue rose 60% QoQ, DRAM shipments were flat QoQ, and NAND shipments actually fell 10%. That means the extra RMB 2 trillion of revenue came almost entirely from ASP. What about costs? Depreciation, power, materials, labor — none of these change in a single quarter because prices went up. So operating margin jumped from 58.4% to 71.5%, up 13 percentage points — this is operating leverage, and the same multiple works on the way down.

Buffett would note one detail: gross margin 79%, operating margin 71.5%, leaving only 7.5 percentage points of expenses in between. R&D, sales and G&A combined account for just 7.5%, which tells you this is a "produce it and sell it" business — no channel costs, no brand spending. The upside is extreme elasticity; the downside is that a 30% price decline would wipe out more than 70% of operating profit.

Net profit exceeds operating profit: this discrepancy needs to be broken down

2026Q1 net profit was 40.35 trillion, 2.7 trillion higher than operating profit. The reason is not a tax benefit but the revaluation of equity investments — SK hynix holds an indirect stake in Kioxia through a consortium, and Kioxia's market capitalization rose after its listing, which under accounting rules must be recognized as a change in fair value.

This money is genuine asset appreciation, but it was not earned by the chip business and generates no cash flow. Use operating profit to judge the business; use net profit to judge value per share. The two measures differ by 7% in the same report; mixing them would overstate profitability.

Balance sheet and cash flow: this time it is real money

It swung from net debt to net cash in 2025; free cash flow in Q1 2026 alone was KRW 18.67 trillion, and the July ADR offering raised another USD 26.5 billion (about KRW 37 trillion). Roughly, net cash is around KRW 95 trillion, equal to 7% of market cap. This is the biggest difference from the 2018 cycle—the prior peak came with net debt, this peak comes with net cash, so the downside resilience is far stronger.

But the structure of that cash flow needs scrutiny: the KRW 18.67 trillion was achieved with capex of only about KRW 10 trillion, and a full-year spending pace of KRW 40 trillion means free cash flow will narrow markedly over the next three quarters. Management's choice to raise equity at this point shows they know next year's cash flow will not look this good.

Conclusion

Financial verdict: real profit, real cash, a healthy balance sheet — the cleanest memory earnings report outside Samsung. But owner earnings of roughly KRW 25 trillion per quarter (rough calculation) annualize to KRW 100 trillion, and the current price of KRW 1,291 trillion is 13x owner earnings — not expensive, and certainly not as cheap as "5x PE." That is why Buffett would not buy at this price.
