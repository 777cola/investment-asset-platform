Core view

The four perspectives agree on the facts; they diverge sharply on what those facts are worth. Three points of contention, adjudicated in turn.

The governing principle: where an assumption involves uncertainty, take the conservative value; where a multiple is applied to cyclical-peak profit, substitute normalized profit. This valuation discipline applies uniformly across the reports in this collection.
Disagreement one: how many years can HBM's moat hold

The commercial lens argues for "three years": any customer switching suppliers must re-run full-system validation, and Nvidia will not risk a supply disruption to save 10% on cost. The financial lens argues for "eighteen months": all three passed HBM4 certification on 8 June, exclusivity is over, and what remains is a yield race whose gap will narrow over time.

Verdict: price on a three-year lease term, with declining rent. Specifically, apply a 2.5x HBM premium in 2027 (currently about 3x) and 2x in 2028. On this assumption, 2027 operating profit of RMB 18 trillion is reasonable and need not be higher.

Divergence two: how tight will 2027 actually be?

The bull case treats JPMorgan's estimates as hard evidence: 2027 capex +74%, yet with equipment and cleanroom constraints limiting actual bit supply growth to 13%, below demand. The bear case's rebuttal is equally strong: 13% is a model output that assumes every capacity expansion proceeds smoothly, and historically memory oversupply has always come in "a little more than planned."

Verdict: accept "tight in 1H 2027," reject "tight for all of 2027." SK hynix's own Yongin Phase 1 cleanroom comes online in February 2027, Samsung's HBM4 capacity doubles in 2027 and CXMT's 420,000-wafer capacity lands in 2027—all three supply additions point to 2H 2027. Management's July remark that "2027 is the tightest year in history" refers to order bookings, not prices.

Divergence 3: cheap or expensive

This is the most substantive disagreement. On a commercial basis, 2026E is only just over 5x and looks cheap; on an owner-earnings basis it is 13x and does not; on a risk basis, cyclicals are always cheap at the top.

Verdict: 2027 operating profit of KRW 180 trillion × 6x + net cash of KRW 95 trillion = market cap of KRW 1,175 trillion = KRW 1.65 million per share, 9% below the current price. What this means: the current price already embeds the assumption that 2027 does not collapse, but not the option that 2027 is tighter. To buy, one must buy below KRW 1.65 million, where there is a 10% margin of safety; only below KRW 1.4 million is there a 20% margin.

Unified conclusions and execution

Four-perspective consensus: company quality 8, price reasonableness 5, timing 3. Overall rating "Watch, await the July 29 earnings," consistent with the working papers.

  • Action: Do not build a position. Existing positions may be held; do not add.
  • Trigger one: July 29 operating profit ≥ 60 trillion and management maintains the 2027 tight-supply stance → permitted to build a 1/3 position below 1.75 million.
  • Trigger two: Price returns below 1.65 million (6x 2027) → build a 1/2 position.
  • Falsification condition: DRAM contract prices fall month-on-month for two consecutive months, or Samsung's disclosed HBM share exceeds 35% → exit fully, stop discussing valuation.

Of the three points of disagreement, the most important is not how many years HBM can hold up, but that "the market is already pricing 2027." The 38% drop is not because people did not know it would earn 60 trillion in the second quarter, but because they have already started calculating 2028. This is the most counterintuitive thing about cyclical stocks: the best earnings come at the worst share price.

Conclusion

Team Lead ruling: Watch, and wait for the 29 July earnings. All three disagreements were resolved on the conservative side — HBM leases at three years with declining rent, 2027 tightness recognised only in the first half, valuation at 6x 2027. Execution sets two trigger conditions and one falsification condition; price discipline takes precedence over opinion.
