Synthesis of the four masters' views — SK hynix

Four people saw four different things in the same earnings report

Core view

Duan Yongping saw "relying on price, not skill"; Buffett saw "13x owner earnings"; Munger saw "capex as a license for self-destruction"; Li Lu saw "long-term contracts as the only reason for a re-rating." Put the four together and you have the whole picture of this company.

Consensus: high earnings quality, low sustainability

None of the four questioned the authenticity of these results. Revenue, profit, cash flow and balance sheet all tie out. No one-off gains are propping up the numbers (the RMB 270 billion equity revaluation has already been stripped out separately), no receivables anomalies, no inventory build-up.

The disagreement is entirely about what these results are worth. Duan Yongping and Munger see them as proof of the cycle's position (the top). Buffett sees them as proof that the valuation is too high (13x owner earnings). Li Lu sees them as proof of structural demand (AI memory) but not of structural profit.

The real disagreement: cyclical or structural

Distill four people's views into one question: Is this memory shortage a cyclical shortfall or a structural re-rating?

Cyclical: capacity will catch up, prices will fall, and 2027–2028 will repeat 2018. Structural: AI demand for memory is step-function, HBM certification barriers let the three oligopolists collect a premium long term, and long-term agreements narrow price swings. The former implies a 6x valuation, the latter 12x. The current price of 1,291 trillion implies 7x 2027 — the market prices it between the two, leaning toward the former.

Synthesis conclusion: it is structural only if all three conditions hold

Li Lu offered a verifiable standard that the other three would agree with: look at three conditions. First, more than 50% of 2027 capacity is locked into long-term contracts with price floors and ceilings; second, HBM share remains above 45% in 2028; third, industry capex as a share of revenue falls from 13% to below 10%. If all three hold, it is a structural re-rating; if only one holds, it is a cycle.

Where things stand: the first is unknown (a reading may emerge on July 29), the second is marginal (54%, with Samsung closing in), the third clearly does not hold (capex is rising). So pricing it as a cycle is the only honest approach right now.

Conclusion

Synthesis: the earnings are grade A, the business is grade B, the price is grade C. Until three structural conditions are met, it can only be valued as a cyclical at 6-7x. That is the full logic behind "watch, wait for 7/29".
