Core view

Distil the previous seven notes into a single actionable framework: three numbers to gauge cycle position, two prices to set entry and exit, one calendar to run the tracking.

Three numbers: sizing the cycle in one minute

First: operating margin.Currently 71.5%. The historical memory peak is around 50%, and anything above 60% is top-zone territory. That is not a sell signal but a "do not add" signal.

Second: capex/revenue.Currently about 13% (KRW 40 trillion / KRW 300 trillion). Above 15% means capacity expansion is accelerating and oversupply follows within two years; below 10% means discipline holds.

Third: DRAM contract price QoQ.Currently +58%–63% (commodity), +30% (company blended). Two consecutive quarters of negative growth marks a cycle reversal.

Of the three metrics, the first tells you how good it is now, the second how bad it gets later, and the third when it turns. None require forecasting, only reading.

Two prices: RMB 1.65 million and RMB 1.40 million

1.65 million = 6x 2027 operating profit plus net cash, a 10% margin of safety — the level at which you can start buying. 1.40 million = 5x, a 20% margin of safety — the level at which you can buy heavily.

Why not buy now? At 1.816 million the stock trades at 7x, and our neutral target price sits 9% below the current price. Why not wait for 1.00 million? Because good companies rarely trade that low, and the cost of waiting too long can exceed the cost of paying up.

The logic of scaling in: buy one-third at 1.65 million, add to half at 1.40 million; if it keeps falling, the thesis is wrong, not the price. The price can be wrong; the thesis cannot.

A calendar: five dates

July 29 (Q2 results; watch operating profit and 2027 basis); September (Q3 contract price negotiations); October (Q3 results plus 2027 capex guidance); January–February 2027 (Yongin Phase 1 cleanroom activation); April 2027 (full-year 2026 results and HBM4E pricing).

Of these five dates, October's capex guidance matters most: it is the only hard evidence of supply discipline. If 2027 guidance exceeds KRW 50 trillion, expansion is accelerating and the 2028 glut arrives sooner.

The framework's closing line: don't predict, just respond

Nothing in this framework is a forecast. Operating margin comes straight off the statements, capex is looked up, contract prices are published and spot prices are simply posted. Every judgment rests on facts that have already happened, not on things that might happen.

That is the only workable way to invest in cyclicals: don't call the inflection point; size the position to where things stand now. Where things stand now is margin at a historical extreme, the share price down 38% and two days to earnings — and the corresponding action is to watch and wait. The conclusion isn't exciting, but it is actionable.

Three reminders outside the framework

First, do not use leverage. Every assumption in this framework rests on a 12-month horizon, and leverage turns time into an enemy.

Second, do not pull the trigger early simply because "it has fallen a lot." The 1.65 million level is derived from valuation, not from the size of the decline. A move from 1.816 million to 1.70 million is not "cheaper now" — it is "not there yet."

Third, do not stack bets along the same supply chain. SK hynix, Micron, Samsung, Nvidia and optical modules are essentially the same risk factor. What is diversified is companies, not risk.

Conclusion

Decision framework: three numbers to size the position (71.5% operating margin, capex/revenue 13%, contract price up 30% QoQ); two prices to set entry and exit (build 1/3 at 1.65 million, add to 1/2 at 1.40 million); one calendar to manage the tracking (7/29, September, October, January–February 2027, April). Don't predict the turning point; just respond to the position.
