## Position in the value chain: stuck at the physical bottleneck, the most valuable position in 2026

Over the past two years the AI compute bottleneck has moved from process node to packaging, and then to memory bandwidth. However fast the GPU, it is scrap metal if it cannot be fed data—and HBM is the pipe that feeds it. SK hynix sits at this chokepoint, which is why it can charge 3x the price and still have customers lining up.

How valuable is that position? A rough calculation: in an AI server with 8 GPUs, HBM accounts for 15%–20% of the total BOM. Nvidia's gross margin is 70%, while SK hynix's HBM gross margin exceeds 45% on broker estimates (general-purpose DRAM is higher). In this chain SK hynix ranks third—behind Nvidia's architecture-definition power and ASML's equipment monopoly.

## The second leg: customer switching costs, more durable than technology

Technical leadership decays; switching costs do not. HBM is no plug-and-play component: it must be tuned to the GPU's packaging substrate, power delivery, thermal design and firmware, and any customer switching suppliers must re-run full-system validation, a three-to-six-month cycle. Nvidia will not risk supply disruption to save 5% on cost.

Switching costs have a ceiling, however: they guarantee only that a customer will not easily replace you, not that it will not add a second supplier. Samsung taking about 20% of Nvidia's initial HBM4 allocation in 2026 and 28% for the full year is the second-supplier mechanism at work. Switching costs protect existing share, not pricing power.

## Samsung is the most dangerous rival — not on technology, but on capacity

Samsung is the only player capable of doubling HBM supply within a year. Its DRAM capacity is 1.3x SK hynix's, it overtook SK hynix on HBM4 qualification from February 2026, passed Nvidia certification in June, and lifted its share from 20% to 28%. Should it push share to 35% in 2027, the HBM premium would fall from 3x to 2x and SK hynix's operating margin would drop from 71.5% back to 45%–50%.

The second threat is CXMT, which is not chasing HBM but attacking the price floor of commodity DRAM. Once commodity DRAM is in oversupply, the HBM premium cannot hold either: production lines are interchangeable, so when commodity DRAM loses money manufacturers push capacity toward HBM, which then also becomes oversupplied. That is the transmission mechanism across the supply chain and the biggest hidden risk for 2027.

## The third leg: the compounding of scale and capex

The moat has another, less conspicuous source: capex efficiency from scale. SK hynix will invest KRW 38–40 trillion in 2026, and Samsung's semiconductor capex is larger, yet SK hynix generates more output per unit of investment—because it allocates more than 80% of new capacity to HBM and high-density server modules, two categories whose revenue per wafer is 2–3x that of commodity DRAM.

In numbers: for the same KRW 1 trillion of capacity spend, SK hynix can generate 30%–50% more revenue than Samsung (rough estimate). This is not a technology advantage but a product-mix selection advantage—and one reason SK hynix can sustain a 71.5% operating margin at this scale.

That advantage disappears, however, as HBM supply and demand balance: once HBM commoditises, output per unit of investment reverts to the industry average, and the only scale advantage left is R&D dilution.

## Three indicators of a moat

First, HBM share. On Counterpoint quarterly data, a drop below 50% would signal the moat has been breached; at 54%, the safety buffer is just 4 percentage points.

Second, the HBM premium multiple. At about 3x today (GB price versus commodity DRAM), brokers generally expect it to fall to 2.5x in 2027 and 2x in 2028. A drop below 2x would signal that "custom parts" have reverted to "standard parts," forcing the valuation to be set in line with Micron.

Third, the timing gap in 12-layer/16-layer mass production. SK hynix now leads Samsung by about six months; if that gap narrows to within three months, the technology moat is essentially gone. The 16-layer HBM4E sample submission timing is the key milestone in 2027.
