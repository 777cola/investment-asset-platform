Quarterly profit of KRW 37.6 trillion, stock down 38%: the two faces of the memory king

📝 Conclusion: Watch; await the July 29 earnings report

Watch; wait for the July 29 earnings. Q1 revenue of KRW 52.58 trillion and a 71.5% operating margin made it the strongest quarter in memory history, yet the stock fell from KRW 2.919 million to 1.816 million, a 37.8% drawdown. The divergence reflects the market trading 2027 in advance rather than mispricing: DRAM price increases narrowing from 60% to 30%, Samsung's HBM4 certification progressing, and a sharp rise in capex all point to loosening supply. At 17x TTM the stock looks cheap, but the denominator is peak-cycle profit. Buy conditions: second-quarter contract price increases hold at 30% and management provides evidence that supply stays tight in 2027; on price, only below KRW 1.65 million is there a margin of safety.
