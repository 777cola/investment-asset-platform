## Seven line items reconcile, confirming the working papers rest on solid financial foundations

Revenue, operating profit, net profit and full-year revenue all tie exactly to company disclosures; discrepancies in media reprints are within 0.2%. The market cap line is a useful check: the June 22 closing price of RMB 2.919 million multiplied by 711 million shares gives RMB 2,075 trillion, 0.14% above the RMB 2,072 trillion reported that day—confirming the share count basis is correct, and all subsequent per-share calculations rest on this figure.

The 5.4% operating profit beat also merits attention: first-quarter consensus was RMB 35.7 trillion against an actual RMB 37.61 trillion. Revenue, however, came in slightly below the RMB 53.6 trillion consensus at RMB 52.58 trillion. A revenue miss alongside a profit beat implies costs and product mix were better than the market expected.

## The one big divergence: how much did prices actually rise in Q2

KIS's July 13 report put Q2 blended ASP up about +30% QoQ; TrendForce's April survey had Q2 commodity DRAM contract prices up +58%–63% QoQ and NAND +70%–75%. The 28-percentage-point gap is not an error on either side.

The difference is structural. SK hynix has a high HBM revenue share, and most HBM moves under long-term agreements (LTAs) with prices locked in advance and fixed increases, whereas commodity DRAM and NAND are far more elastic in the spot market. Industry contract prices therefore rise 60% while the company's blended ASP rises only 30% — the cost of a high HBM mix. KIS applies exactly this logic in cutting Q2 operating profit to KRW 60.4 trillion and trimming its 2026/2027 forecasts by 9%/11%.

How to handle it: show both side by side in the table and use the conservative 30% for valuation. If TrendForce is right and blended ASP actually rises more than 45%, Q2 operating profit lands at KRW 65–70 trillion and the current price is clearly cheap. That is the first thing to verify on July 29.

## The finally adopted basis

Financials: All figures are on the company's K-IFRS consolidated basis, in KRW trillion to two decimal places. FX: USD 1 ≈ KRW 1,390 (mid-July 2026), used only to convert USD figures into KRW for order-of-magnitude comparison, not for precise conversion.

Valuation: 2027 operating profit multiplied by a multiple, plus net cash. We do not use P/E (TTM) or net profit: 2026Q1 net profit of KRW 40.35 trillion exceeded operating profit of KRW 37.61 trillion, the difference stemming from non-operating items such as revaluation of equity investments, so this basis cannot gauge the profitability of the chip business.

Share price: Korea Exchange closing price of KRW 1,816,000 on 2026/07/27 (+3.24%). The 52-week high of KRW 2,919,000 was the 2026/06/22 close; the intraday high was KRW 2,987,000 (June 24). This report uses closing prices throughout.

## Two things we could not verify

First, the HBM long-term agreements' pricing mechanism. Management would say only "multi-year, volume-locked, partially price-locked", disclosing neither the pricing formula nor the floor and ceiling, so we cannot judge whether the company captures upside if HBM prices rise in 2027. Second, the final 2026 capex figure: in 1Q the company said only "a substantial increase", and the KRW 38-40trn is a brokerage estimate; the 29 July briefing should provide a clear basis.

Neither gap affects our conclusion, but both affect precision. Wherever this report touches on these two points, it is flagged as "brokerage estimate" or "rough calculation".
