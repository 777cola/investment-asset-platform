Business Essence Analysis — Duan Yongping's "Right Business"

Core definition

ChangXin Technology is a Chinese manufacturer of DRAM memory chips, producing DDR4/DDR5/LPDDR series memory chips using independently developed process technology for China's domestic data center, PC, smartphone and automotive markets. It positions itself as "China's Samsung Memory"—achieving domestic substitution in DRAM, a market worth hundreds of billions of dollars.

Business characteristics

CharacteristicsJudgment
Nature of demandStructural demand — DRAM is essential to computing devices and correlates positively with computing performance
SubstitutabilityHard to replace in the short term—only 4 DRAM suppliers globally (Samsung/SK hynix/Micron/CXMT), and chip-industry certification cycles are long
Pricing modelGlobal commodity pricing (commoditization), highly cyclical, with limited pricing power for any single manufacturer
Customer stickinessMedium-high — long customer validation cycles (6-18 months); once designed in, rarely replaced
Scale effectsExtremely strong — semiconductor manufacturing is a textbook case of economies of scale: the larger the capacity, the lower the cost
Capital intensityVery high—a single 12-inch fab costs over RMB 10 billion, a heavy depreciation burden

Duan Yongping-style questioning

Q:If you had to describe what makes this business good in just one sentence?

A:Only four DRAM suppliers exist worldwide. China accounts for 30-40% of global consumption but its self-sufficiency rate is just 7.67%, leaving vast room for domestic substitution. Yet this is a grueling business: highly cyclical, asset-heavy, and locked in a technology race that never stops.

Eight

Investment thesis summary

Bull case

  • Huge room for domestic substitution: China consumes 30-40% of global DRAM, with a self-sufficiency rate of only 7.67%
  • Financial inflection point has emerged: 2025 turnaround plus 2026H1 earnings breakout; profit trend established
  • Policy underpinning: National Big Fund Phase II plus Hefei state capital are deeply tied in, an irreplaceable strategic position
  • AI demand drives growthHBM and data-center DRAM demand keeps growing; a beneficiary of the AI wave
  • Oligopolistic industry structureDRAM industry entry barriers are extremely high; over the long cycle, the supply-side landscape remains stable
  • Management commitment: Chairman locked up for 10 years, interests highly aligned

🔴 Bear case

  • The technology gap persists: 1-2 generations behind the Big Three; EUV ban may widen the gap further
  • Highly cyclical: DRAM prices swing sharply; 2026 may mark the cycle peak
  • Risk of sanctions escalationCMC list to Entity List: the "gray rhino" is imminent
  • High-valuation pressure: Even at an issue price of 308x PE, it could go higher after the open
  • HBM lags: Missing out on the core memory upside of this AI cycle
  • Samsung/SK hynix countermeasures: Price wars, patent litigation, capacity dumping and other competitive tactics

📋 Conclusion

Stay on the sidelines and wait for a reasonable price range.ChangXin Technology is an excellent strategic asset, but the DRAM cycle position (expected to peak in 2025-2026) and the high valuation (the IPO price already implies extremely high expectations) mean the best time to buy may not be now, but after a cyclical downturn is confirmed and sanction risk is fully priced in.
