IV. Worst-case scenario analysis (Munger-style inversion)

Scenario 1: A technology island under full sanctions

Conditions:Added to the BIS Entity List, DUV lithography supply cut off, maintenance of existing equipment restricted

Results:Capacity cannot expand, process iteration stalls, and in 3-5 years the technology gap widens to 3+ generations

Valuation impact:Loss of growth + cyclical downturn = fair market cap may be < RMB 300 billion

Scenario 2: Profit collapse under a cyclical reversal

Conditions:DRAM prices fall 50% in 2027, mirroring the 2018-2019 cycle

Result:CXMT net profit plunges from a peak of RMB 150–200 billion to a loss or break-even

Valuation impact:Shift from a PE of several tens of times back to PB-based pricing, potentially falling to 2-3x PB
