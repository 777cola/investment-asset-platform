📝 Preliminary conclusion

ChangXin Technology is China's only DRAM manufacturer, with irreplaceable strategic value. Benefiting from the DRAM super-cycle in 2025-2026, its earnings are growing explosively. But the 308x IPO P/E has fully priced in optimistic expectations, and combined with risks such as the technology gap (1-2 generations behind), the EUV ban, potential escalation of sanctions and strong cyclicality, we recommendWait and see, waiting for a reasonable price range after a cyclical pullback.
