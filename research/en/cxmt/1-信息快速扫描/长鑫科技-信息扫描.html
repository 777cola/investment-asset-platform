Key financial analysis

  1. Profitability recovering fast: From a loss of RMB 19.2 billion in 2023 to a profit of RMB 7.1 billion in 2025, with consolidated gross margin surging from -1.93% to 40.99%
  2. Unit cost decline: In 2025, DDR product unit price +61%, unit cost -26.26%; LPDDR product unit price +24.46%, unit cost -22.85%

6. Position in the industry chain

Customer base

  • Servers/data centers: Alibaba Cloud, Tencent
  • Personal computers: Each OEM
  • Mobile terminals: Xiaomi, domestic smartphone brands
  • Smart EV: NIO
  • Consumer electronics/IoT: Various IoT devices

Upstream supply chain dependence

  • Lithography machines: No access to ASML EUV; relies on DUV (export-controlled)
  • Etching/Thin-film deposition: AMEC, NAURA (partial domestic substitution)
  • Silicon wafers: Shin-Etsu Chemical and SUMCO dominate
  • Photoresist: Japan's JSR and TOK dominate

Source: CXMT official website; 36Kr strategic placement list

7. Geopolitical risk

Sanctions/restrictions already in place

TimeMeasures
2022US NDAA bars the US government from buying or using CXMT chips
March 2024US Commerce Department BIS considers sanctions on CXMT
June 2026The US Department of Defense added CXMT to its "Chinese Military Companies" (CMC list)

Potential risks

  1. Equipment export controls: No access to EUV lithography machines; advanced process nodes constrained
  2. Entity list risk: If added to the BIS Entity List, US equipment/software supply would be disrupted
  3. Patent/IP disputes: In 2023, 10 former Samsung employees were arrested on charges of stealing DRAM technology
  4. Technology generation gap: Process-node gap with Samsung/SK hynix may persist

Sources: Wikipedia; Reuters; Bloomberg

8. Industry outlook

Global DRAM market size

  • 2024: about USD 80 billion
  • 2025: about USD 100-110 billion (AI-driven)
  • 2027-2028 forecast: USD 120-150 billion

China's self-sufficiency rate

  • China accounts for roughly30-40%(estimated value)
  • CXMT's global market share is only7.67%
  • Semianalysis forecasts market share could reach by 202817%

AI-driven demand

  • HBM is currently the tightest DRAM category
  • US AI chip export controls on China have instead spurred domestic substitution of memory devices used alongside Chinese AI chips

Sources: 36Kr; SemiAnalysis
