II. Patent analysis

Patent portfolio characteristics

CXMT's patent information is not fully public, but based on disclosed information and standard industry practice:

  • Active patent applications: DRAM-related patents keep growing (the prospectus notes sustained increases in R&D investment)
  • Disputed technology origin: Samsung accused 10 former employees of stealing DRAM technology (2023), hinting at part of the source of its technology
  • Cross-risk with US patents: Micron and others hold extensive fundamental DRAM patents, creating infringement risk

Technology route selection

CXMT chose not to follow the three giants' EUV route, instead using DUV multi-patterning to catch up—more expensive on cost, but it avoids the fatal problem of being choked off on EUV.
