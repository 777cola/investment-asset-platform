III. Identifying chokepoint segments

Segment Degree of bottleneck Impact on China's DRAM industry
EUV lithography machines 🔴🔴🔴 Fatal Inability to obtain ASML EUV → advanced-node (≤1α) constraints → long-term technology gap
High-end photoresist 🔴🔴🔴 Fatal Japan dominates; domestic substitution progresses slowly → purity/consistency below standard
DRAM manufacturing 🟡 Moderate China has only CXMT — capacity and technology gaps remain wide
HBM advanced packaging 🟡 Moderate CXMT packaging line under construction, 2-3 years behind SK hynix
IC substrate/ABF 🟡 Moderate High-end packaging substrates still depend on Japan/Taiwan
The biggest bottleneck = EUV lithography machines.CXMT currently uses DUV (deep ultraviolet) lithography tools to produce 17nm/19nm-class DRAM, while the three major players have moved to 1α/1β nm (roughly equivalent to 12-15nm). DUV can approach more advanced nodes through multi-patterning, but this sharply raises costs and lowers yields.
