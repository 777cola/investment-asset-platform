II. Physical breakdown of the DRAM supply chain

Layer 0 (end devices): AI servers / PCs / smartphones / automobiles / data centers Layer 1 (core): DRAM chips (DDR4/DDR5/LPDDR/HBM) Layer 2 (key scan area): ├─ Silicon wafers (300mm polished wafers/epitaxial wafers) ├─ Photoresist (ArF/KrF) ├─ Specialty gases (high-purity NF3/WF6/Xe) ├─ CMP slurry/pads ├─ Targets (high-purity metals) ├─ Photomasks/masks └─ Advanced packaging substrates Layer 3 (upstream equipment): ├─ EUV lithography machines ←Biggest bottleneck├─ DUV lithography (ArFi) ├─ Etch equipment (dielectric/conductor) ├─ Thin-film deposition (ALD/CVD/PVD) ├─ Ion implantation ├─ Inspection/metrology equipment (KLA critical) └─ CMP equipment Layer 4 (infrastructure): ├─ Cleanroom construction ├─ Power supply (fabs are extremely power-hungry) ├─ Ultrapure water systems └─ Technical talent (DRAM R&D engineers extremely scarce)

IV. Analysis of China's DRAM domestic substitution path

Path A (normal path, current state)

DUV ArFi + multiple patterning → closing in on ~1α nm (expected 2027-2028)

✅No EUV needed
❌High process complexity, low yield, high cost
❌Investment per 10,000 wafers of capacity is 30-50% higher than the EUV approach

Path B (sanctions escalation path)

If added to the Entity List, DUV supply would also be cut off:

❌DUV is also restricted → no capacity expansion at the 17nm node
❌Existing fabs are difficult to maintain (spare parts supply cut off)
⚠️It can only rely on second-hand equipment, reverse engineering and domestic substitute equipment; technological iteration has essentially stalled.

Path C (breakthrough path)

Domestic EUV achieves a breakthrough (expected after 2030):

✅The technology gap can be closed
⚠️The time window is too long (4+ years); by then the three giants will have moved to more advanced nodes

V. Linkage with Demingli (001309.SZ)

Key findings:Although Demingli focuses on NAND flash modules, it still:
  1. DRAM module business: If CXMT's DRAM capacity continues to expand, Demingli can purchase CXMT DRAM dies as a downstream module maker
  2. Complementary supply chains: ChangXin Memory Technologies supplies DRAM dies (upstream); Demingli turns them into memory modules (midstream)
  3. Domestic substitution chain: CXMT DRAM + Demingli modules = a complete domestic storage solution
  4. Competitive dynamics: Demingli's competitors Longsys and Biwin are also CXMT customers; CXMT does not take sides
But note:Demingli's current business is mainly NAND (flash memory), with a limited share of DRAM modules. The direct pull-through effect of ChangXin Memory Technologies' capacity expansion on Demingli may be overstated.
