2.2 WeChat's irreplaceability

WeChat's three major moats:
1️⃣ Strong network effects— 1.35 billion MAU creates an absolute monopoly on social relationship chains
2️⃣ Infrastructureization— WeChat Pay, Mini Programs, Official Accounts and Channels form a complete ecosystem
3️⃣ B2B binding— WeCom connects more than 15 million enterprises
Key challenges:
• Douyin erodes user time (Douyin ~120 min/day per user vs WeChat ~85 min/day)
• Channels DAU reached 500 million, monetization still lags Douyin
• Vertical communities such as Xiaohongshu and Bilibili siphon off interest-based social traffic
