Revenue and profit trends

Total revenue (RMB billion)

Gross profit vs net profit (RMB billion)

Insight:Revenue grew from RMB 560.1 billion in FY2021 to RMB 751.8 billion in FY2025 (+34%). Gross profit grew much faster (+87% to RMB 422.9 billion), directly reflecting the structural improvement in gross margin. Net profit was volatile—FY2021 included large investment gains and FY2022-23 were hit by declines in investment fair value—but FY2024-25 returned to healthy operating growth.

Margin trajectory

Gross margin and net margin

Operating margin and EBITDA margin

Insight:Gross margin rose nearly 13pp over five years (43.4%→56.3%), the biggest structural change in Tencent's fundamentals. Net margin swung widely on investment gains, but the 29.9% net margin in FY2025 is already near its historical high (FY2021's 40.1% included large non-recurring gains and is not comparable).

Cash flow trends

Free cash flow (RMB billion)

FCF/net profit ratio

Insight:FCF grew from ~RMB 148 billion in FY2021 to RMB 215.6 billion in FY2025 (+46%). The FCF/net profit ratio has held at an extremely high ~90-100%, indicating Tencent's earnings are of very high quality. It dipped slightly to 95.9% in FY2025, mainly due to a deliberate increase in AI-related CapEx.

Profitability (ROE/ROIC)

ROE and ROIC trends

Insight:After ROE fell from ~23% in FY2021 (including substantial investment gains) to ~11% in FY2023, it rebounded strongly to 20.51% in FY2024-5, above the 15% excellence threshold for internet companies generally. ROIC reached 17.57%, far above the cost of funding (~3-4%), with each RMB 1 of capital deployed generating ~RMB 0.18 of return.

Business segment structure

Revenue segment breakdown (estimated, RMB billion)

Insight:Value-added services (VAS) remain the largest revenue source but their share is gradually declining (~55% to ~48%); online advertising's share rose from ~15% to ~18% and is the fastest-growing segment; enterprise services rose from ~6% to ~9%, becoming the third growth engine. This mix shift is the core driver of gross margin expansion.
