1. Business characteristics (business model)

Tencent's business model boils down to three words:Connect, Play, Pay：

  • Even: WeChat + QQ, social infrastructure with 1.3 billion DAU
  • Play: The world's largest gaming company
  • Pay: WeChat Pay + cloud services

The three business layers function as a flywheel, creating a positive feedback loop.

Buffett:"The best business is one that consistently generates high returns without requiring heavy capital investment." Asset-light with high returns (FCF/revenue 28-30%), pricing power, strong predictability. Concerns: technology iteration and regulatory risk.

Munger:"Invert, always invert." WeChat's network effects are extremely strong; replacing it would be almost as hard as reinventing a society. But its business scope is too broad.

Duan Yongping:"Do the right things, and do things right." The toll-road model: once the road is built, whoever uses it has to leave something behind. Corporate culture of benfen.

Li Lu:"China is undergoing the largest modernization process in human history." Tencent is the digital cornerstone of China's modernization, with the capacity to evolve. Gross margin rose from 43% to 56%.

2. Moat depth

Four moats combined:

  1. Network effects (10/10): WeChat DAU of 1.3 billion
  2. Switching costs (9.5/10): Social + payments + ecosystem
  3. Scale advantage (8/10): RMB 80 billion annual R&D
  4. Ecosystem synergy (9/10): Mini programs + investment portfolio

Buffett:"A truly great business must have an enduring moat that protects excellent returns on invested capital." Network effects are the ultimate moat. The Douyin threat exists, but WeChat's acquaintance-based social networking is an indispensable necessity that cannot be replaced.

Munger:"Persistence of a moat matters more than its width." Building a trust moat takes a decade. Regulatory barriers have in practice protected Tencent.

Duan Yongping:"A good moat means others cannot break in even if they try, and you yourself will not actively undermine it." Beware of self-destruction. Channels proved the ability to innovate within the core moat.

Li Lu:"The real moat is the ability to keep evolving." From messaging to payments to mini programs to Channels, it has never stopped evolving.

3. Management quality

Ma Huateng (CEO, 7.91% stake), Martin Lau (President, Goldman Sachs background), John Lo (CFO), Ren Yuxin (COO).

Buffett:"We want to invest in management teams who wake up every morning figuring out how to create value for shareholders." Rationality 5/5: sold down ~HKD 270 billion of JD.com and Meituan at highs, bought back over HKD 150 billion at lows. Candor 4/5. Resistance to inertia 5/5.

Munger:"If you aren't willing to own a stock for ten years, don't even think about owning it for ten minutes." Incentives are properly aligned. It embraces compliance proactively in the face of regulation.

Duan Yongping:"Integrity means doing the right thing, then doing it right." Do not harm users during an industry downturn. Pony Ma still personally takes part in product decisions.

Li Lu:"The most important qualities in management are honesty and competence." Learning organization. The combination of share reduction, buyback and dividends was excellent.

4. Financial health

Revenue grew from RMB 560.1 billion to RMB 751.8 billion over five years (CAGR 6.1%), gross margin rose from 43.9% to 56.4%, and FCF/revenue held steady at 28-30%. Net cash of USD 13.2 billion, D/E 0.33.

Buffett:"In the final analysis, what we focus on is free cash flow." FCF/revenue of 28-30% is Buffett's favorite type. Net cash + low leverage + high interest coverage.

Munger:"The safest investments are those where you make money without needing to predict the future." A deliberate cash buffer of USD 59.7 billion. The portfolio is undervalued (actually about USD 150-200 billion).

Duan Yongping:"Companies that borrow money are off-limits." Net cash plus continuously rising gross margins equals a shift from a bad business to a good business.

Li Lu:"The best way to understand a company's finances is to look at how it performs at the trough." At the toughest point in 2022, FCF still came in at RMB 123.4 billion and ROE stayed above 20%. In 2023, FCF surged to RMB 201 billion (+63%).

5. Growth headroom and certainty

Five growth engines:

  1. Advertising (Video Accounts): Load rate 3%->10%, incremental RMB 50-80 billion over 3 years
  2. Gaming: Valorant + Honor of Kings International, CAGR 5-10%
  3. Fintech: payment volume up 10-15%
  4. Enterprise services/cloud: China cloud CAGR 20%+
  5. AI: Hunyuan large model in the first tier

Buffett:"Certainty matters more than growth." WeChat Channels' rising volume and price follow the same logic as Coca-Cola. A CAGR of 8-12% over the next 5 years is foreseeable.

Munger:"The best way to predict the future is to understand which factors won't change." What stays the same a decade from now: social connection, digital payments, entertainment demand. Tencent dominates all three.

Duan Yongping:"A good company at a good price matters more than high growth." 10-15% annual growth with strong certainty, and buybacks adding 3-5% to per-share value each year.

Li Lu:"China's modernization drive is the best growth story." Upgrading consumption among 400 million-plus middle class. Attractive growth from a 10-year compounding perspective.

6. Risk identification

Six major risks:

  1. Regulation (Medium-High/Medium): Gaming licences, fintech regulation
  2. Competition (medium/medium-high): Douyin, Alibaba Cloud/Huawei Cloud
  3. Geopolitics (medium/high): US-China tech decoupling
  4. Technological disruption (low/high): AI reshapes social networking
  5. Portfolio (Neutral/Neutral): Does not affect core FCF
  6. Corporate culture dilution (Medium/Medium): 100,000+ employees

Buffett:"Risk comes from not knowing what you are doing." Investors who understand Tencent face controllable risk. A 15x PE is the best risk hedge.

Munger:"Avoid the obvious stupid things." Beware of overconfidence.

Duan Yongping:"What you don't do matters more than what you do." Look at users, not competitors. Don't borrow. Don't invest in what you don't understand.

Li Lu:"The core of risk management is understanding the dangers and opportunities of the company you invest in." Investing in Tencent = investing in China. Holding for the long term is itself risk management.

7. Valuation and margin of safety

PE (TTM) 15.19x (~15th historical percentile), forward PE 12.87x, FCF yield 6.0%, PB 2.95x.

Buffett:"Price is what you pay; value is what you get." ROE 20.51%. DCF base case ~HK$730, margin of safety +57%.

Munger:"Value is not a number; it is a judgment." An FCF yield of 6.0% versus 2.5% on government bonds is attractive.

Duan Yongping:"A good business model, a good corporate culture, a good price." RMB 240 billion in annual FCF + USD 13.2 billion net cash + USD 150 billion investment portfolio = payback in 10-12 years.

Li Lu:"Buy securities priced below intrinsic value." Net profit of about RMB 550 billion in 10 years, payback in about 7 years. Buybacks add 3-5% to value each year.

Conclusion: Would the four masters invest in Tencent?

Buffett: Most likely yes (80% probability)

Asset-light with high FCF, a wide moat (network effects plus switching costs), rational management (share sales plus buybacks), and a margin of safety (15x PE). His purchase of Apple shows he accepts the tech consumer-goods concept. Tencent's social nature is essentially the same as Apple's user lock-in.

Munger: Most likely yes (85% probability)

WeChat's irreplaceability, its trust moat, and the wisdom of knowing what not to do. The core social-plus-payments business is a good enough business on its own. Concerns remain about over-expansion of scope, but the flaws do not outweigh the strengths.

Duan Yongping: highly likely to have bought (90% probability)

Has publicly stated a holding in Tencent. The three elements—good business model, good corporate culture and good price—are largely satisfied. 15x PE is consistent with the principle of not overpaying.

Li Lu: Most likely yes (90% probability)

The biggest proponent of the China modernization theme. As China's digital infrastructure, Tencent fits perfectly with the investment framework of long-term compounding, evolutionary capability and social value.
