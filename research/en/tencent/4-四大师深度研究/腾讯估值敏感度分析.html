Important risk management note
Every historical drawdown was eventually recovered, but recovery time ranged from 3 months to 3 years. Tencent's share price once fell to HK$188 (forward-adjusted) in 2022, equivalent to a PE of about 10x. In an extreme scenario, the current PE of 15x still carries downside risk.

Recommendation:
(1) No leverage used
(2) Holding period of at least 3-5 years
(3) Build positions in tranches to lower the average cost
(4) Be mentally prepared for extreme declines (-30% or more)
