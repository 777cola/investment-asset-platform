  • Q2 2026 revenue growth returns to above 15%

VI. Conclusion and key judgments

🎯 Five core conclusions

  • Largest attribution for the 32% decline:Naspers selldown (-15%) plus US-China tensions valuation compression (-10%) together account for most of the decline
  • The essence of the current share price:HKD 463 is not a fundamental valuation but an "equilibrium price under placement overhang" — buyback provides a floor, but there is no upside trigger
  • Most likely scenario:Range-bound between HKD 420 and 500 over the next 6-12 months, awaiting the end of share reductions or an earnings inflection point
  • Core metrics to watch:Naspers stake-reduction pace, Channels ad load, game revenue growth, Q2 results
  • Valuation reference:PE (TTM) ~18-19x, near a 5-year low (historical median ~28x), long-term allocation value emerging
