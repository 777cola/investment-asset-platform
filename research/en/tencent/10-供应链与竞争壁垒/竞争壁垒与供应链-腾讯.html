Final verdict

Tencent's moat rating: A+ (extremely wide and deep)

  • ✅ Social network effects are the deepest moat in Chinese internet
  • ✅ Extremely high switching costs make it hard for users to leave the WeChat ecosystem
  • ✅ Complete content supply chain, covering the full industry chain of gaming, video and music
  • ✅ Investment ecosystem provides an extra layer of defense
  • ⚠️ In the short-video era, it faces structural challenges from ByteDance
  • ⚠️ AI disruption could reshape traffic distribution

Core holding thesis: As long as WeChat's social graph remains irreplaceable, Tencent holds the best toll-collection rights in China's internet — across any content, service or product consumed by 1.35 billion users, Tencent has a chance to take a cut.
