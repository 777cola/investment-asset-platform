I. E-commerce and Local Life

Meituan
Shareholding: ~17% | Largest shareholder
Estimated market cap: ~RMB 120 billion
China's largest local life services platform. Tencent provides WeChat Pay access and traffic support, while Meituan provides core offline consumption scenarios for WeChat Pay.
Pinduoduo
Shareholding: ~15%
Estimated market cap: ~RMB 100 billion
China's third-largest e-commerce platform, whose social-viral model is highly synergistic with the WeChat ecosystem. WeChat Mini Programs are its core customer-acquisition channel.
JD.com
Shareholding: ~17%
Estimated market cap: ~RMB 80 billion
China's second-largest e-commerce platform. A top-level WeChat entry point delivers massive traffic; JD.com completes Tencent's self-operated e-commerce and logistics capabilities.
Vipshop
Shareholding: ~9%
Estimated market cap: ~RMB 8 billion
China's leading off-price branded e-commerce platform, with deep ties to Tencent within the WeChat ecosystem.

II. Content and community

Kuaishou
Stake: ~17%
Estimated market cap: ~RMB 40 billion
China's second-largest short-video platform. A key Tencent ally against Douyin, Kuaishou has deep penetration in tier-3 and tier-4 cities.
Bilibili
Shareholding: ~12%
Estimated market cap: ~RMB 10 billion
China's largest Gen Z community. It collaborates with Tencent on game distribution and animation IP, complementing Tencent's content ecosystem for younger users.
Zhihu
Shareholding: ~20%
Estimated market cap: ~RMB 3 billion
China's largest knowledge Q&A community. Complements Tencent's presence in high-quality content.
Xiaohongshu
Stake: ~10% (rumored)
Estimated market cap: ~RMB 10 billion
China's largest lifestyle-sharing community. Tencent's investment defends against ByteDance acquisition rumors and adds a seeding e-commerce use case.

III. Gaming and entertainment

Riot Games
Shareholding: 100%
Acquisition amount: ~USD 400 million (wholly owned in 2015)
Developer of League of Legends and Valorant. One of Tencent's most successful wholly owned acquisitions, contributing steady revenue and global esports influence every year.
Supercell
Shareholding: 84%
Acquisition amount: ~USD 8.6 billion (2016)
Developer of Clash of Clans and Brawl Stars. A top Finnish mobile-game studio that generates exceptionally high profit per employee with a very small headcount.
Epic Games
Shareholding: 40%
Investment amount: ~USD 330 million
Fortnite developer + Unreal Engine owner. Tencent's 3.0 strategic investment helped Epic take on Steam while gaining access to AAA game engine technology.
Krafton
Stake: ~10%
Investment amount: ~USD 600 million
Developer of PUBG. Tencent secured the China publishing rights to PUBG, spawning the blockbuster cash cow Game for Peace.

IV. Fintech

WeBank
Shareholding: 30% (largest shareholder)
Valuation: ~RMB 200 billion
China's largest digital bank. Products such as Weilidai and WeBank Card serve hundreds of millions of users and form the core asset of Tencent FinTech.
ZhongAn Online
Shareholding: ~10%
Market cap: ~RMB 20 billion
China's first internet-only insurer. Partners with Tencent on insurance product distribution within the WeChat ecosystem.
Futu Securities
Shareholding: ~30%
Market cap: ~RMB 10 billion
China's leading online brokerage. Tencent provides it with traffic access and technical support.

V. Mobility and Logistics

DiDi Global
Shareholding: ~6%
Valuation: ~RMB 10 billion
China's largest mobility platform. WeChat Pay is Didi's core payment method, and Didi provides WeChat Pay with high-frequency usage scenarios.
NIO
Stake: ~5%
Investment amount: ~USD 100 million
A premium Chinese new-energy vehicle brand. Tencent's investment positions it in the smart-car sector, with potential future synergies in in-car WeChat and autonomous-driving AI.
Beike
Shareholding: ~12%
Market cap: ~RMB 10 billion
China's largest property transaction platform. Tencent supports the operation of the Beike Zhaofang mini-program within the WeChat ecosystem.

VI. Enterprise services and SaaS

Weimob Inc.
Shareholding: ~8%
Market cap: ~RMB 5 billion
The largest SaaS provider in the WeChat ecosystem. It offers merchants mini-program stores and smart retail solutions.
Youzan
Shareholding: ~6%
Market cap: ~RMB 3 billion
WeChat e-commerce SaaS tool provider. Helps brands build private-domain e-commerce within the WeChat ecosystem.
Kingsoft Office
Shareholding: ~4%
Market cap: ~RMB 10 billion
WPS Office developer. Tencent and WeCom collaborate to provide office collaboration solutions.

VII. Healthcare

DXY
Stake: ~10%
Valuation: ~RMB 5 billion
China's largest physician community and online consultation platform. Partners with WeChat in health-service scenarios.
WeDoctor
Shareholding: ~5%
Valuation: ~RMB 10 billion
China's leading internet healthcare platform. It provides online consultation, appointment booking and health management services.

VIII. Overseas investment layout

Ubisoft
Shareholding: ~5% (2025 stake-increase rumors)
Investment amount: ~USD 500 million
A major French AAA game studio (3A). Tencent strengthens global game publishing capabilities through equity partnerships.
Snap（Snapchat）
Stake: ~12%
Market cap: ~USD 3 billion
US social platform. Tencent studies Snap's AR technology and its social model for younger users.
Spotify
Shareholding: ~9%
Market cap: ~USD 5 billion
The world's largest music streaming platform. It has cross-shareholdings with Tencent Music and shares music copyright resources.
Several AI startups
Unequal shareholding
Investment amount: several billion US dollars
Invested in multiple AI startups spanning large models, AI applications and autonomous driving, accelerating its AI technology buildout.
