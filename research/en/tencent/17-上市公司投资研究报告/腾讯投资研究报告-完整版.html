1. Company overview

1.1 Company history

Tencent Holdings Limited was founded in November 1998 by Ma Huateng, Zhang Zhidong, Zeng Liqing, Xu Chenye and Chen Yidan. The company listed on the Main Board of the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in June 2004 (ticker: 00700.HK) and began trading in the OTC market via depositary receipts in 2019 (ticker: TCEHY.US).

2.2 Development trends

Trend 1: Traffic dividend peaks, entering an era of deep cultivation of existing users
The industry's competitive focus is shifting from "customer acquisition" to "raising per-user value (ARPU)" and building "ecosystem stickiness".
Trend 2: AI large models reshape the industry landscape
Tencent's Hunyuan large model is now fully embedded in core products including WeChat Search, Tencent Ads and WeCom, and AI's contribution to ad CTR and monetization efficiency rose to about 15% in FY2025.
Trend 3: Gaming goes global and enters the harvest period
Ranked first globally by game revenue. Overseas game revenue accounted for 31% of total in FY2025.
Trend 4: Accelerating commercialization of Channels and short video
WeChat Channels DAU topped 600 million, with an ad load of only 5%, far below Douyin (15%) and Kuaishou (12%), leaving substantial room for growth.

3.2 Business segments

Value-added services (~49%)

Gaming business:The world's largest gaming company. Core domestic IPs (Honor of Kings with annual gross billings of ~RMB 30 billion, Game for Peace, etc.) continue to generate stable cash flow. Internationally, it has built a global footprint through Supercell, Riot Games and Epic Games, with overseas revenue accounting for 31%.

Fintech and enterprise services (~32%)

WeChat Pay:Daily average transactions exceed 2 billion, with a mobile payment market share of about 40%. In FY2025, the segment's gross margin has risen to 45%+.Tencent Cloud:China's third-largest cloud computing player (about 12%), focused on high-value-added PaaS/SaaS products.

Advertising (~18%)

Video Account ads are the biggest growth driver—up over 60% year on year in FY2025. The Hunyuan large model powers the ad system, lifting CTR by about 15%-20%.

7. Risk factors

Competitive risk (medium-high)— ByteDance competes across the board in social, short video, gaming, advertising and AI. Whether Channels can match Douyin's monetization efficiency is the biggest mid-term uncertainty.
Regulatory risk (moderate)— Bull case (60%): no major new rules; base case (30%): partial tightening; bear case (10%): strong regulatory cycle.
Geopolitical risk (medium)— US-China tech decoupling, US restrictions on investment in China, Hong Kong stock market liquidity risk.
Slowing growth risk (medium-low)— Aging core game IP (Honor of Kings has been live for over 10 years) and new business monetization below the consensus of expectations.

10. Investment recommendation

★ BUYTarget price: HK$650 (12 months)

10.1 Investment thesis

Core thesis: Tencent sits in a golden allocation window of "valuation floor + improving fundamentals + dense catalysts".
  1. Valuation sits at a historically extremely low level:Forward PE of 12.9x is not only among its own lowest 5-year percentiles but also the lowest among global internet giants.
  2. Fundamentals continue to improve:Gross margin rose 13 percentage points over three consecutive years to 56.3%, and ROE recovered to above 20%.
  3. Three strong growth engines:WeChat Channels monetization (RMB 80-100 billion incremental potential), AI empowerment (monetization efficiency +15-20%), gaming going global (share exceeding 35%).
  4. Shareholder returns improved markedly:FY2025 buybacks plus dividends totaled about CNY 120 billion+, a shareholder yield of about 4.5%.
  5. Clear catalysts:FY2026 results beat expectations, higher Weixin Channels ad load, AI products launched, new games released.
