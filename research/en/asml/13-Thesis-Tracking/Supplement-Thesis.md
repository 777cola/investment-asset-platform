## Four assumptions already established: no need to revisit these

EUV supply falls short of demand, installation services are accelerating, value-based pricing has been fully negotiated, and gross margin is stepping up—the evidence for all four is in the 2026Q3 results; no more guessing is needed. What they underpin is 2027 revenue (EUR 52 billion) and gross margin (around 57%).

Note that "holding true" does not equal "will continue." EUV supply falling short of demand is a current state, not a permanent one; the +50% in installation services reflects both expansion of the installed base and high customer capacity utilization—the latter will shift with the cycle.

## Two to verify: the curve that decides the post-2027 trajectory

The first is AI capex. This is the only exogenous variable that can kill the valuation, and the verification window is January–April 2027: the four major North American cloud providers will issue 2027 capex guidance, and TSMC will give annual capex. If any one of them cuts by more than 15%, 2027 revenue has to be recalculated.

The second is High-NA. At EUR 350 million per unit, customers buy it because it "can do below 2nm," not because it is cheap. If TSMC, Samsung and Intel push back adoption by 1 year, the 2028 growth curve flattens—fatal for a 31x PE.

## Falsification conditions: three; if any one occurs, rewrite the thesis

**① Gross margin fell below 55% for two consecutive quarters.** This shows value-based pricing has not taken hold, or the product mix has deteriorated; either case has a large valuation impact.**② Orders turned negative year on year while revenue is still growing.** This shows the company is drawing down its order backlog, the most classic signal of a cyclical peak.**③ Mainland China's share fell below 10%.** This shows controls have effectively tightened, directly cutting a chunk of revenue.

Beyond these three, there is a soft indicator: management's comments on capacity expansion. If "studying further expansion" becomes "adjusting as needed," customers are delaying — and this leads the order figures by a quarter.

## Watch calendar

**Late January 2027:** Q4 and full-year results — watch whether Q4 gross margin holds at 57%, full-year order commentary, and 2027 guidance. **April 2027:** Q1 results — the first full quarter of value-based pricing. **July 2027:** Q2 results — first comparable High-NA shipment count. **October 2027:** Q3 results — validate the 2028 capacity plan.

Each checkpoint requires watching only three things: gross margin, orders, and China share. Everything else (share price, analyst ratings, target prices) stays out of the thesis.

## The payoff structure of the thesis

If all six assumptions hold, 2027 EPS is EUR 46; at 32x, the target is USD 1,708, 4% above the current price. If only the first four hold and the last two fail (AI capex cut, High-NA delayed), EPS is about EUR 38; at 24x, the target is USD 1,058, 36% below the current price.

That is the asymmetry of the payoff: **if the thesis is fully right, you make 4%; if it is half wrong, you lose 36%.** The thesis is not wrong; the price treats "fully right" as the base case. Only two paths can change this structure—the price falls, or earnings rise above expectations. The former can be waited for; the latter is uncontrollable.

## Three sentences to read in the next earnings report

First sentence, in the gross margin section: what the actual Q4 gross margin is, and whether the 2027 guidance gives a range. If it comes in above 57%, value-based pricing is confirmed. Second sentence, in the orders section: whether net bookings are disclosed, and whether they are up or down year on year. This is the leading indicator for 2027–2028 revenue, more important than revenue itself. Third sentence, in the China section: whether the full-year mix guidance has changed, and whether management's language on licensing has shifted from "uncertain" to "constrained".

Read those three sentences and ten minutes is enough to decide whether the thesis needs revising. Everything else — EPS above the consensus by a few points, analysts raising target prices — does not enter the decision.
