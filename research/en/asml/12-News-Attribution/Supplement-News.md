## The only new information: capacity is insufficient, and further expansion is needed

This is new language. ASML previously said "2026 capacity is fully booked"; it has now escalated to "we cannot meet demand in 2027 and 2028, and we are studying further expansion beyond +30%." The distinction matters: visibility extends from one year to three, and the company is willing to commit capex against demand it cannot yet see.

Customers are already discussing orders for 2029–2030. If true, the current equipment cycle runs longer than any in history — and it underpins our base case of EUR 52 billion revenue in 2027.

## Why the best earnings report fails to lift the stock

Three reasons. First, expectations were already stretched: the market looked for Q3 revenue of RMB 11–12 billion and a gross margin of 55%–57%, and the actual results landed at the top of that range — above consensus but within the frame. Second, part of the net profit beat came from a EUR 500 million revaluation gain, which institutions saw through immediately. Third, AI trades turned more volatile in October, and equipment stocks were sold off alongside them as "shadows of AI capex."

The deeper issue: the share price gains from 2025 through the first half of 2026 had already priced in "supply falling short of demand through 2028." The earnings report merely stated this more clearly; it added no new present value.

## Which are noise

Three items qualify as noise. First, the DUV licence rumour: no formal document exists, the company's stance is against further restrictions, and it remains a policy under discussion with no impact on current-period revenue. Second, the EUR 500 million Mistral revaluation: a book gain that does not enter cash flow and should be excluded from valuation. Third, the single-quarter China share jumping from 14% to 23%: delivery timing, not a demand shift.

Noise has a signature: a month later no one remembers it, yet on the day it is published it gets written into headlines. Separating noise from signal is simple—ask whether it changes 2027 cash flow.

## The ratio of signal to noise determines what to do

Of the seven items, one is a signal — capacity remains insufficient through 2028 — and six are noise or already known. That ratio tells you: there is no "surprise" to dig out of this company; its value shifts slowly and predictably. For a company like this, trading returns come not from "guessing news" but from "waiting on price."

The near-term share-price decline (-0.96%, -17.5% from the high) is therefore no reason to buy. A 17.5% drawdown on a stock at 41x P/E only squeezes out some of the overbought froth; it is still more than 20% away from "cheap."

## The three market moves on October 13

Results hit pre-market. Act one: the stock spiked at the open as revenue of RMB 11.662 billion and a 57.5% gross margin both beat guidance. Act two: it faded once the call began — management's 2027 commentary, though upbeat, contained no upside numbers, and analysts pressed on the EUR 500 million revaluation embedded in net profit. Act three: it weakened into the close and the following day, down more than 2% cumulatively over two sessions.

The pattern echoes the post-earnings reactions of 2024 and 2025 — ASML's share price has become desensitised to "beats" and responds only to the size of guidance raises. This time guidance was raised only at the low end (EUR 43 billion to EUR 44 billion), a "modest in-line raise" insufficient to move the stock.

The further 0.96% decline on October 16 extended broad AI-sector volatility and had nothing to do with the company. Reading it as "the market is bearish" is a misread; reading it as "now it's cheap" is also a misread.

## Noise identification checklist

Four criteria apply: anything meeting two or more is noise. First, it does not affect current cash flow (e.g., equity revaluation). Second, there is no formal document (e.g., licensing rumours). Third, it reflects delivery timing rather than a change in demand (e.g., quarterly swings in the China share). Fourth, it will not be mentioned a month from now (e.g., a target-price change by some investment bank).

Filtering the past three months of information through these four criteria leaves only one signal out of seven items: capacity will remain insufficient through 2028, and further expansion is under study. The rest can be ignored. That is also the benefit of holding this kind of company — little to read, long to wait.
