Decision framework: how much to buy, at what price, and when to admit you are wrong — ASML

Core view

Compress the previous seven pieces into one executable framework: three price tiers, one position cap, three falsification conditions, one watch calendar. The point of a framework is not to get you the buy right, but to tell you where you went wrong when you are wrong.

Price: three tiers

First tranche at USD 1,300 (25x 2027 PE): build 2%. Second tranche at USD 1,150 (22x): add to 4%–5%. Cap 6%. At the current price of USD 1,641.52 (31x), no action.

Why 25x? Because ASML's valuation range over the past decade has been 25–45x, and 25x marks the cycle bottom; at that level, even if the bear case plays out (EPS EUR 38, 24x), your loss is only 19%, not 36%.

Positioning: why the cap is only 6%

Two reasons. First, 23% exposure to export controls is a risk that cannot be priced; no company with more than 20% of revenue exposed to a single policy risk deserves a heavy position. Second, its 0.85 correlation with the semiconductor holdings means that if your portfolio already holds Nvidia and TSMC, adding it is equivalent to adding leverage.

A 6% cap means that even if it falls 50%, the damage to the portfolio is 3 percentage points—tolerable, and it will not distort judgment.

Admitting Error: Three Falsification Conditions

① Gross margin falls below 55% for two consecutive quarters (value-based pricing fails); ② orders turn negative year on year while revenue is still growing (cycle peaks); ③ mainland China's share falls below 10% (controls materially tighten). If any one occurs, halve the position; if two occur, exit entirely.

No price stop-loss. Price swings in such companies are normal cyclical behavior; a price stop-loss would have shaken us out in a 2022-style pullback and then missed the threefold gain of 2023–2025.

Exception clauses for adding to positions

If the January 2027 earnings report proves two things—Q4 gross margin holds at 57% and 2027 guidance comes in above EUR 55 billion—then the buy point moves up to USD 1,400. Because the neutral-scenario EPS would be revised up from 46 to above 50, the corresponding price at 25x naturally rises.

The point of this exception: the framework must change with the facts, not become dogma. But any change must be backed by earnings evidence, not the feeling that "it's worth more" just because the stock has risen.

When to sell: three scenarios

First, if the price reaches the optimistic scenario (USD 2,380, 38x 2027), trim to 2%–3%. Second, if a falsification condition triggers (gross margin, orders, China share), halve the position or exit entirely. Third, if a better opportunity emerges — if a name of "equal quality, 30% cheaper" appears in the same chain (such as KLA during a certain crisis), switch positions.

Three situations in which not to sell: the stock price falls (with no change in fundamentals), analysts downgrade the rating (not a fundamental change), the AI narrative cools (does not affect 2027 orders). All three happened in 2022, and in hindsight all were wrong sell points.

When the framework breaks down

This framework rests on two premises: first, that EUR 46 EPS in 2027 is credible; second, that 25–45x is a reasonable valuation range. If the premises change, the framework must be rewritten.

The first premise is tested in January 2027 (Q4 results + 2027 guidance); the second is tested by "what multiple the market assigns once value-based pricing takes hold" — if 40x becomes the norm, a 25x entry point will never appear, and at that point one must accept "buying at 28–30x". The purpose of a framework is discipline, not prophecy.

A specific timetable

October–December 2026: watch only, wait for order rumors ahead of the Q4 results (this phase usually brings no new information). January 2027: Q4 results plus 2027 guidance — the first decision point. If guidance comes in above 55 billion with a 57% gross margin, move the buy level up to USD 1,400; if guidance is below 50 billion, wait for a lower price.

April 2027: Q1 results, the first full quarter of value-based pricing — watch gross margin. July 2027: Q2 results, first comparable High-NA delivery unit counts. October 2027: Q3 results, validate the 2028 capacity plan. Four checkpoints, and at each one you read only three lines.

What to do in between? Nothing. For a company like this, trading frequency should match information frequency — four earnings reports a year means four decisions a year; the rest is waiting.

Conclusion

Framework in four rules: build at 1,300, add at 1,150, 6% cap, three falsification tests. In execution, ignore news, ratings and target prices; watch only three numbers: gross margin, orders and China share.
