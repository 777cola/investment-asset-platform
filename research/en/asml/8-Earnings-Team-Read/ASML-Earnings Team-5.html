Core view

Four readers of the same quarterly report reached four different conclusions. Duan Yongping: "the business has not deteriorated." Buffett: "excellent cash flow, but the cycle is unpredictable." Munger: "closer to the top than to the bottom." Li Lu: "right direction, wrong price." Taken together, they point one way only — watch.

Consensus one: this quarterly report is genuinely strong

None of the four doubted the authenticity of the financial report. Revenue of RMB 11.662 billion, cash up RMB 5.15 billion, RMB 4.1 billion of buybacks and installation services above guidance—these four figures corroborate one another and cannot be faked.

The structure matters more: services at 28% of revenue, a 57.5% gross margin and negative working capital thanks to advance receipts. This is not "a good quarter of sales"—the business model is improving.

Consensus 2: Net profit is not the focus

All four flag the EUR 500 million one-off gain. Duan Yongping says "discount it," Buffett says "it doesn't matter," Munger says "one-off," and Li Lu says "the long-term centre is 32%–35%." All four treat it the same way: strip it out and judge the business on gross margin and cash flow.

That matters in practice: extrapolating to 2027 on the 42.5% net margin would overstate profit by more than 20%.

Disagreement one: is gross margin a ceiling or a starting point

Munger calls the peak (an all-time high, after which the two sources reverse); Li Lu sees a slow variable (product mix plus service share can still hold up). That disagreement decides whether 2027 EPS is 46 or 50.

The test is specific: watch gross margin in Q1 and Q2 2027. Above 57%, and Munger is wrong; back down at 54%–55%, and Li Lu is wrong. The answer will be clear by mid-2027.

Divergence 2: Cycle position

Buffett and Munger both read the cycle as late-stage ("want a 30% discount," "closer to the top than the bottom"), whereas Duan Yongping and Li Lu focus on structure ("the business hasn't deteriorated," "by 2028").

The two camps do not actually conflict: structure determines the decade, the cycle determines the year. For long-term holders, structure matters more; for anyone buying now, the cycle position determines the next 12 months' return. Hence, on the same earnings report, the four land on two formulations—"keep watching" and "can hold for ten years"—but none says "buy now."

Synthesized conclusion

Four sentences, one point: the quarter proves the business has not deteriorated — it has improved — but it also proves the market already knew. The stock falling more than 2% rather than rising after the results is the market's version of that sentence.

On execution: hold if you already own it (all four agree the business is fine); wait on price if you do not (their disagreement does not change this conclusion). Entry point USD 1,300, equivalent to 25x 2027.

Conclusion

Four-analyst synthesis: business A+, financials A, price C. Consensus says "don't sell"; the debate is whether to buy and at what level; our verdict is "revisit below USD 1,300."
