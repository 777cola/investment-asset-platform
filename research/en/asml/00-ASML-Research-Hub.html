📝 Conclusion: Watch and wait for a pullback

Watch, wait for a pullback. ASML delivered the strongest quarterly report in its history for 2026Q3: net sales of EUR 11.662 billion, up 55.2% YoY, gross margin of 57.5%, full-year guidance raised to EUR 44–45 billion, and roughly 65 EUV units to be shipped for the full year. More important was the line on the earnings call — under current capacity plans, it cannot meet customer demand in either 2027 or 2028, and the company is studying further capacity expansion. This is not cycle-top euphoria; it is certainty under supply constraints. The business scores 10; the price scores 4. At the current price the stock trades at about 31x 2027 P/E, leaving only 4% upside in the neutral scenario; the shares fell more than 2% after the results rather than rising, showing the market has already priced in the good news. Wait for the 2027 P/E to fall back to 25x (about USD 1,300) before acting — only then do the odds work.
