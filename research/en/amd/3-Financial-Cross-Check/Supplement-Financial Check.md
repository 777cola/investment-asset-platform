## Six for six, zero deviation: the headline numbers are not in dispute

Revenue of RMB 11.536 billion, data center revenue of RMB 6.718 billion, gross margin of 56.0%, GAAP net income of RMB 2.297 billion, non-GAAP EPS of USD 1.66 and Q3 guidance of RMB 13 billion ±300 million — all six items match across the two sources, differing only in the last digit where rounding applies. Large-cap US earnings releases are synchronized: the 8-K, press release, conference call materials and IR website all go live in the same minute, and Chinese media republish the same day, so the risk of conflicting numbers is very low.

The one item worth flagging is the non-GAAP basis. The company reported non-GAAP net income of RMB 2.76 billion and EPS of USD 1.66, against GAAP net income of RMB 2.297 billion and EPS of USD 1.39 — a gap of RMB 463 million, driven mainly by stock-based compensation (about RMB 400 million) and amortization of acquired intangibles (about RMB 100 million). This report uses GAAP for all valuation; non-GAAP serves only to gauge operating trends — the 125x figure is on a GAAP basis; on a non-GAAP basis it becomes 104x, a 21x difference, enough to change the conclusion.

## Two items breached the threshold: one was rounded up, the other back-solved

Capex of RMB 808 million versus media reports of "about RMB 800 million" is a 1.0% deviation, stemming from rounding rather than a data error. The direction of that USD 8 million still matters: the market expected RMB 299 million, so the actual RMB 808 million is 2.7x expectations. A fabless company pulling capex to this level admits only two explanations—locking in dedicated capacity, or prepaying TSMC for advanced packaging. Both point to 2027 orders already scheduled, making this the single most informative number in the quarter's report; using the rounded figure would weaken that signal.

2025Q2 segment revenue is a derived figure, and the deviation cannot be quantified. The method: divide 2026Q2 segment revenue by (1 + YoY growth) to get 3,240 / 2,490 / 1,129 / 826, which sum to 7,685 and match 2025Q2 total revenue exactly, confirming the derivation is internally consistent; individual line items may still carry 1%–2% error, since the growth rates themselves are rounded to integers.

What does this deviation affect? Only the precision of growth rates such as "gaming -31%," not the conclusion that "data center contributed 90% of the increment"—the RMB 3.48 billion incremental gap far exceeds a 2% error band. So the approach is: keep the derived values in the tables with a note, and use only magnitude language such as "doubled" or "down about 30%" in the body text.

## Final basis and three estimates

Three estimates need to be spelled out. First, TTM net profit of USD 6.4 billion, the sum of GAAP net profit for the last four quarters, of which 2025Q3 and 2025Q4 are approximated from public quarterly reports with an error of ±3%; this corresponds to a P/E of 121–129x, written throughout this report as "about 125x". Second, TTM revenue of about USD 40 billion, also a four-quarter sum, corresponding to a P/S of 20x. Third, 2027 EPS: the company's target is "above USD 20"; this report assumes a neutral case of USD 19, a bear case of USD 12 and a bull case of USD 24, each calculated independently and not based on sell-side consensus.

The market cap basis also needs explaining: USD 483.36 × 1.66 billion shares = USD 802.4 billion. On diluted share capital of 1.67 billion shares the figure is USD 807.2 billion, a 0.6% difference with an impact on P/E of less than 1x. Full exercise of all OpenAI warrants (capped at 160 million shares) would raise share capital by 9.6%, requiring both market cap and EPS to be recalculated — a matter for after 2027, noted here only in the risk list.

In one sentence: the numbers in this report contain no case of "two sources that do not reconcile", only cases of "one source is an estimate", and every estimate is flagged.That matters more than chasing two decimal places.
