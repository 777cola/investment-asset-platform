> On the company's definition, "doubling in 2027" means data centre revenue doubles versus 2026. 2026 data centre revenue annualised is about RMB 26.9 billion (67.18×4), so doubling implies about RMB 54 billion. The "RMB 135 billion in 2027" figure, by contrast, rests on a 2026 base of about RMB 67.5 billion. The two are inconsistent, and the company has not specified the base; this report adopts the conservative reading of "annualised doubling." The share data are estimates, with no official basis.

## The key question: doubling from what base?

Management says "data center revenue will double in 2027." Doubling off what base—2026 actual revenue or 2026 annualized revenue? The two diverge widely: 2026 actual (Q2's 26.9 billion annualized, plus second-half growth) is roughly 30 billion, implying 60 billion on a doubling; doubling the annualized figure gives 54 billion.

We adopt the conservative reading: 2027 data center revenue of USD 55–60 billion, implying EPS of USD 19. If management means more than 120 billion (the most bullish sell-side interpretation), EPS would reach USD 26 and the stock would trade at just 18x—a different story altogether.

That ambiguity is itself the risk. Management gave no absolute figure on the call, only the word "double." The first thing investors should do is look for the absolute data center revenue guidance number in the Q3 results.

## Falsification conditions: when this thesis is invalidated

Four items, ranked by importance:

- Gross margin falls below 52% — signals the start of a price war, or that HBM price increases have eaten the premium. This is the hardest one.

- Data center revenue declines quarter on quarter — a single occurrence signals the second supplier's share is unstable and Nvidia has closed the capacity gap.

- MI450 single-quarter revenue below USD 2 billion in Q4 2026 — signals ramp failure, making a 2027 doubling out of the question.

- Cloud capex guidance cut by more than 10% — industry-level falsification; AMD's execution no longer matters.

If any one of the four appears, the rating drops from "Watch" to "Avoid." Note: a falling share price is not a falsification condition. A drop to USD 300 with fundamentals intact is an opportunity; a rise to USD 600 with gross margin below 52% is a trap.

## Watch calendar

October 2026 (Q3 results): whether data center revenue is ≥ USD 7.5 billion, whether gross margin is ≥55%, and the first revenue contribution from MI450.

November 2026 (NVIDIA FY2027Q3): the pace of Rubin's volume ramp and its pricing directly set the ceiling on AMD's share in 2027.

January 2027 (Q4 results): whether MI450 quarterly revenue is ≥ USD 3 billion, and the absolute level of full-year 2027 guidance.

April 2027 (Q1 results): whether data center revenue grows ≥80% YoY — the first quarter to validate the "doubling" thesis.

Four checkpoints, each capable of disproving or confirming one hypothesis. Before then, any position adjustment is guesswork.The five hypotheses are not parallel but sequential. The share hypothesis depends on MI450 ramping; MI450's ramp depends on HBM and CoWoS allocations; allocations depend on how TSMC and the memory makers distribute supply; and that distribution in turn depends on NVIDIA's demand.Across this entire chain, AMD controls only the first link.That is why the credibility of the "doubling" target cannot rest on the company's own words alone.

Why not use the share price as the validation metric? Because the share price embeds sentiment. In June 2026 AMD rose to 580 with no change in fundamentals; in July it fell to 430 with no change in fundamentals either.Validating a thesis with the share price is like judging the season with a thermometer.A thesis can only be validated with reported financials: revenue, gross margin, share, orders.

One more "time hypothesis" belongs in the thesis: MI450's window of advantage lasts only four to six quarters. NVIDIA's Rubin Ultra launches in the second half of 2027, at which point MI450 becomes a "previous-generation product," with both price and gross margin under pressure.So the 2027 EPS peak may be the high point for the next three years.
