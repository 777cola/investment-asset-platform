Synthesis of Views — AMD

Core thesis

Four investors read the same financial report and fixate on entirely different numbers: Duan Yongping on customer structure and business position, Buffett on margins and cash flow, Munger on the fluff in the narrative, Li Lu on the historical probability of a turnaround. All four reach the same conclusion: a good company, an honest report, an expensive price.

Consensus: three numbers that are not up for debate

Data center revenue of RMB 6.718 billion, +107%, gross margin of 56.0%, +12.8pct, and Q3 guidance of RMB 13 billion, RMB 500 million above consensus — these are the three pillars of this earnings report, and all of them line up.

Rarer still is that they corroborate each other: growth driven by low-price volume would not lift gross margin; margin expansion driven by price hikes would not come with a guidance beat; a beat built on channel stuffing would leave cash flow short of net profit. All three holding at once points to growth that is both real and high-quality.

Disagreement: how long it lasts, what it is worth

The Duan Yongping–Li Lu disagreement is over "position": Duan argues the second-supplier position is stable but not valuable, while Li argues Helios could change that position. The deciding point is 2027 — Helios' rack share.

The Buffett–Munger disagreement is over "measure": Buffett is willing to use non-GAAP to read operating trends, whereas Munger insists GAAP is what shareholders actually receive. Verdict: use GAAP for valuation (125x) and non-GAAP for trend (USD 1.66 EPS, sequential growth).

All four agree on one thing: 125x is not "a bit expensive" — it is "expensive enough to require flawless execution." Flawless execution means MI450 ramps on schedule, gross margin holds at 55%, OpenAI and Anthropic orders are not delayed, and Nvidia does not cut prices. The probability that all four hold at once is no more than half.

Panel conclusion

Company quality: A- (execution A+, position B, financials A).

Earnings quality: A- (real growth, improving gross margin structure, healthy cash flow; marked down for concentration and base effects).

Valuation: D (125x TTM, 24x the most optimistic 2027 EPS).

Overall: Watch, do not chase. This is not "bearish"; it is "not bullish at this price." The four investors' shared advice: wait for MI450 revenue to hit the statements, or wait for the price to return to USD 350.

There is another layer: "who is doing the pricing." All four agree the current price is expensive, but differ on "how expensive": Duan Yongping, using 2027 EPS, sees it as roughly fair; Buffett, using a 10-year DCF, sees it as 2x too expensive; Munger, discounting "cycle-peak profit," sees it as 3x too expensive; Li Lu, discounting "industry-average profit," sees it as 2.5x too expensive. The disagreement stems not from the multiple but from which year's profit to use as the denominator. That is also why this report sticks with 2027 neutral EPS of USD 19 — the middle value all four can accept.

Conclusion

Panel view: good company, real financials, expensive price. All three of us would buy this company — just not at USD 483. That is the entire meaning of "Watch."
