📝 Conclusion: Hold, do not chase the rally

Hold; do not chase the rally. At the August 31, 2026 close of USD 220.78 and a market cap of USD 5.33 trillion, the stock sits just 6.4% below its 52-week high. FY2027Q2, reported after the close on August 26, was a strong print: revenue of USD 96.22 billion (+106%), data center USD 89.02 billion (+117%), gross margin 75%, Q3 guidance of USD 108 billion, and the first-ever FY2028 growth guide of about 70%. The report also exposed three off-balance-sheet figures: supply commitments of USD 279 billion, a credit guarantee ceiling of USD 108.5 billion, and cloud purchase commitments of USD 36 billion. On valuation, FY2028 trades at just 15x, which looks cheap, but the denominator is the company's own guidance. Keep existing positions; new money waits for one of two signals: the stock back below USD 180, or the FY2028 guide validated by quarterly data for the first time.
