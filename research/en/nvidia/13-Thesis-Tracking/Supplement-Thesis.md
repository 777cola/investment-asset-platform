## Four validated: the thesis skeleton holds

Demand diffusion is the hardest data point to argue with. Hyperscale cloud came in at RMB 48.71 billion (+102% YoY, +13% QoQ) and ACIE at RMB 40.313 billion (+138% YoY, +25% QoQ). The fast-growing half is the half that was not on the customer list before. Sovereign AI rose 35% QoQ and more than 2x YoY, while NeoCloud installed capacity went from 3GW to 8GW — not platitudes in the earnings release, but volumes that can be verified again next quarter.

Supply bottlenecks are confirmed too. Q3 guidance of RMB 108 billion was set on the premise of "zero China data-center compute revenue" and still implies +12% QoQ. Management puts real demand at about 100% against guidance that assumes only 70%; the gap is entirely capacity — wafers, HBM, power and data-center space are all tight, with shortages extending to the end of FY2028.

Pricing power: Q2 gross margin 75.0%, operating margin 66.2%, opex ratio 8.7%. Even if Q4 falls to 71%–72%, that is still far above any peer. Ecosystem: AMD has chased for three years at the same growth rate without gaining share; its data-center revenue remains one-thirteenth the size.

## Article 5 is fracturing: this is the one to watch most closely

The assumption that "profit turns into cash" came under clear pressure this quarter: operating cash flow of RMB 24.077 billion against net profit of RMB 59.688 billion, a conversion rate of 40.3% versus 58.1% last year; free cash flow of RMB 21.341 billion, a conversion rate of 35.8% versus 50.9% last year.

Of the three drivers, two are natural consequences of a bigger business and one is a structural change: accounts receivable +RMB 22.346 billion (payment terms 45 → 60 days) is the structural change; inventory +RMB 5.784 billion (stockpiling for Rubin) is natural; prepayments +RMB 5.497 billion (locking in capacity) are natural; investment income of RMB 7.771 billion that did not flow into cash is an accounting effect.

To judge whether the thesis is "cracking" or "not cracking," watch two numbers: whether payment terms keep rising (65 days is a warning, 75 days is confirmation), and whether next quarter's OCF/net profit returns above 50%. If in Q3 payment terms hold at 60 days and the conversion rate returns to 50%, this assumption goes from "cracking" back to "validated"; if payment terms breach 70 days, the thesis needs rewriting.

## Article 6 must wait until 2027: guarantees are the tail risk in the thesis

The assumption that "customers can afford to pay" cannot be verified in 2026. The company disclosed three numbers: a guarantee ceiling of USD 108.5 billion, supply commitments of USD 279 billion and cloud service purchase commitments of USD 36 billion. It also gave the single most important line: next year, AI lab business requiring support from Nvidia's balance sheet will account for about a quarter of total business.

What does a quarter mean? On FY2028 revenue of RMB 641.3 billion, that is USD 160 billion of revenue, backed by a group of labs and NeoClouds that are not yet profitable. If these customers cannot raise money in 2027, Nvidia's problem is not "can't sell" but "can't collect."

Verification point: the first half of 2027. Watch three leading indicators — NeoCloud bond spreads, capex guidance from CoreWeave and Nebius, and whether Nvidia adds a "guarantee performance" line item in its 10-Q. Until then, this assumption can only be treated as "uncertainty," managed through position limits rather than a valuation discount.

## Falsification conditions and monitoring calendar

Falsification conditions (cut on two, exit on three):

- Revenue turns negative QoQ (data center revenue declines sequentially for two consecutive quarters).

- Receivables days exceed 75.

- Q4 gross margin below 71% and FY2028 guidance no longer references gross margin "recovery".

- First actual compensation payment on the RMB 108.5 billion guarantee.

- Any two of the four major cloud providers cut 2027 capex by more than 10%.

Watch calendar:

- 2026/10/01: Quarterly dividend of USD 0.25/share paid (record date 9/10); watch whether buybacks continue.

- 2026/10/21: GTC keynote in Berlin; watch the Rubin roadmap and LPU customer progress.

- 2026/11/17: FY2027Q3 results — the key checkpoint this period. Three numbers to watch: revenue vs the USD 108 billion guidance, whether gross margin lands at 74%, and whether receivables days hold at 65.

- 2027/02: FY2027Q4 results — the quarter gross margin bottoms; watch whether 71%–72% holds and whether FY2028 guidance reiterates 70%.

- 2027/05: FY2028Q1 results — the quarter price increases take effect; watch whether gross margin actually returns to 72%–73%.
