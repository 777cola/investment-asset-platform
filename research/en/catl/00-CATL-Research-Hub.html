📝 Conclusion: Buy (staged entry)

Buy (in tranches). CATL reported 2026H1 revenue of RMB 276.9 billion and net profit of RMB 43.284 billion, with global power battery share of 40.2% and domestic share of 46.7%; energy storage revenue rose 87.5% and overseas gross margin reached 29.97%. Not a single quarter has been weak on fundamentals. At RMB 291.99 the stock sits at a 52-week low, 36% below its high, on 14x 2026E earnings and 12x 2027E—the bottom of its five-year valuation range. The market is selling "de-CATLization" and the consumption tax; both are real but not fatal, since Li Auto and Xiaomi together account for only 13.6% of domestic installations. Keep the initial position to no more than one-third of the planned position; add only if Q3 single-quarter net profit growth holds at 25% and domestic share holds at 44%; stop out if power battery gross margin falls below 19%.
