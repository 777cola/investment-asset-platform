Core view

Every growth figure in the interim report is "growth in volume"; only unit profitability represents "a change in quality." In the first half the company earned RMB 98 per kWh sold, versus RMB 104 in Q1 and RMB 94 in Q2 — a clear downward line. That line says more about where the company stands now than revenue growth of +54.8%.

Let's start with the unit economics: how much profit does a single battery generate?

First-half volume of 440GWh and net profit of RMB 43.284 billion, or RMB 98/kWh. In industry context, second-tier makers generally earn RMB 30–60/kWh in unit net profit, so CATL makes more than twice what they do. This is the cash form of scale advantage.

The trend, however, is poor: RMB 104 in Q1, RMB 94 in Q2, down RMB 10 or nearly 10% in a single quarter. Revenue still rose over the same period (Q2 up 14.4% QoQ), which shows it is trading price for volume, and the trade is getting cheaper.

The next question Duan Yongping would ask: is that RMB 10 temporary or permanent? If it reflects higher costs from rising lithium carbonate prices, it is cyclical and will come back; if it reflects a price war driven by second-tier players bidding for orders, it is structural and will not. The interim report gives no cost breakdown, only one piece of circumstantial evidence — power battery gross margin fell 1.78 points and energy storage fell 1.56 points. Both fell at once, which points to price, not raw materials.

Is this business really a "business" or just "hard labor"?

Duan Yongping's test: a good business is "invest once, collect for years"; a grind is "reinvest every year." CATL sits between the two — a line built once lasts 5–8 years, but technology cycles force retooling every 3–5 years, so it is permanently reinvesting.

That leaves it deserving only a manufacturing multiple. Some point to the 100x P/E of 2021 and argue "14x now is cheap" — the wrong benchmark. In 2021 the market priced it as a growth stock; now it prices it as a manufacturer. The benchmark has changed: 14x is not "a 75% discount," it is "a different ruler."

On the manufacturing ruler, 15x normalized profit is fair value. 2027 profit of RMB 116.5 billion ÷ 4.627 billion shares = RMB 25.18 per share, ×15 = RMB 378. Against the current RMB 291.99, that is a 23% discount. Not a wide discount, but the direction is right.

Conclusion: a Buy, provided you accept that this is a manufacturing business

Duan Yongping would not buy a company simply because it is the "global number one"; he would buy only because "the price is below the value." If unit profit holds at RMB 98/kWh, 14x is cheap; if it falls to RMB 60 within two years, 14x is expensive. The crux is whether market share can be defended.

Conclusion

Read this interim report the right way: the volume story is good, the price story is bad. The single most important number here is RMB 98/kWh, which determines whether 14x P/E is cheap or fair. Owning the stock comes down to one judgment call: unit profit stops falling near RMB 90.
