Core view

This is the version for public release: framework jargon removed, only facts and judgments kept. Suitable for a WeChat official account or column; only images need to be added before publishing.

1. Best interim report, worst share price

On July 24, CATL reported its 2026 interim results: revenue of RMB 276.92 billion, up 54.8% year on year, and net profit attributable to shareholders of RMB 43.28 billion, up 42.0%. Energy storage revenue reached RMB 53.26 billion, up 87.5%. Its global power battery share stood at 40.2%, and its domestic passenger vehicle installation share at 46.7%. Capacity utilization was 94.86% — production lines are running full.

On September 28, the stock closed at RMB 291.99, a 52-week low and down 36.3% from RMB 458.60 in February. Combined A+H market capitalization shed about RMB 770 billion.

A set of results with nothing broken, and a share price at a new low. Both are true at once, which shows the market is not selling the earnings — it is selling the expectations.

II. What the market is selling: de-Ningdexia and consumption tax

The first is "de-CATL-ification." Li Auto's new L8 lineup switches entirely to Sunwoda; Li Auto also injected RMB 2.65 billion into Sunwoda Power for an 11.17% stake; Xiaomi's "Dragon Armor Battery" cells come from CALB and Sunwoda; AITO brought in CALB and Gotion.

But be clear: the essence of these moves is not "no CATL," it is "no CATL exclusivity." Automakers want pricing power. Li Auto and Xiaomi together account for only 13.6% of domestic installed capacity—they can squeeze CATL's gross margin, but they cannot crush CATL's share.

The second is the consumption tax. From September 1, 2026, lithium-ion batteries will again be subject to a 2% consumption tax, rising to 4% in September 2027. CATL mainly sells cells externally, so the tax burden falls directly on it; BYD's batteries are used in-house, so the tax falls at the vehicle level, a far smaller impact. Annualizing first-half battery revenue, 2% corresponds to roughly RMB 9.8 billion a year, equivalent to 10% of expected 2026 net profit. This is a real cost, but not a fatal one.

III. The only thing improving in the financial statements

Overseas revenue RMB 87.13 billion, up 42.4% year on year, with a gross margin of 29.97%, 8.8 points higher than domestic.

In plain terms: the same battery cells sold in Europe earn nearly half again more gross profit than those sold at home. This is CATL's most valuable segment and the real gap between it and second-tier makers — the second tier can fight a price war in China, but cannot get into Europe's automotive-grade supply chain.

The Hungary plant starts production in 2026, with initial 40GWh already booked; Indonesia begins mass production by year-end; the Spanish joint venture is underway. Each 10-point rise in the overseas share lifts blended gross margin by about 0.9 points. This is the only certain source of profit over the next three years.

IV. Is 14x actually expensive?

RMB 291.99 corresponds to a market cap of RMB 1.35 trillion. The 2026 consensus net profit is RMB 95.2 billion, implying a PE of 14.2x; 2027 is RMB 116.5 billion, implying 11.6x. CATL's five-year valuation center is 25–35x.

But do not use the 100x of 2021 as the reference point. The market treated it as a growth stock then; now it treats it as manufacturing. Use the manufacturing yardstick: 15x normalized profit gives RMB 378 for 2027, 29% above the current price; the bear case is RMB 190, 35% below; the bull case is RMB 562, 92% above.The odds skew positive, but patience is required.

One number to watch: in the first half, it earned RMB 98 per 1kWh sold, versus RMB 104 in Q1 and RMB 94 in Q2. The speed at which this line declines determines whether 14x is cheap or fair.

V. How to buy

Rating: Buy (scale in).

Initial position no more than one-third of planned position. Q3 results due late October; two numbers decide whether to add: whether single-quarter net profit holds 25% year-on-year growth, and whether domestic passenger-vehicle share holds 44%. If power battery gross margin falls below 19%, the price war is out of control—stop loss.

One honest note to close: this is not a "buy the dip" story. CATL will not return to 40x; it will be a 15–20x manufacturing leader.You are buying earnings growth, not valuation re-rating. Decide whether to buy only after you are clear on that.
