Core view

Public-release version: framework jargon stripped out, facts and judgements only. Suitable for a WeChat official account or column; only images need to be added before publishing.

1. Best interim report, worst share price

CATL reported 2026 interim results on July 24: revenue of RMB 276.92 billion, up 54.8% YoY, and net profit attributable to shareholders of RMB 43.28 billion, up 42.0%. Energy storage revenue reached RMB 53.26 billion, up 87.5%. Global power battery share stood at 40.2%, with domestic passenger vehicle installation share at 46.7%. Capacity utilisation was 94.86%, with production lines running full.

The stock closed at RMB 291.99 on September 28, a 52-week low and down 36.3% from RMB 458.60 in February. Combined A+H market capitalisation shed about RMB 770 billion.

Nothing is broken in these results, yet the share price sits at a new low. Both are true at once: the market is not selling the earnings, it is selling the expectations.

II. What the market is selling: de-Ningdexia and consumption tax

First, "de-CATL-ification." Li Auto's new L8 lineup has switched entirely to Sunwoda, and Li Auto has injected RMB 2.65 billion into Sunwoda Power for an 11.17% stake; Xiaomi's "Dragon Armor Battery" cells come from CALB and Sunwoda; AITO has brought in CALB and Gotion.

But the essence of these moves is not "no CATL" but "no CATL exclusivity": automakers want pricing power. Li Auto and Xiaomi together account for only 13.6% of domestic installed capacity—enough to squeeze CATL's gross margin, but not to crush its share.

Second, the consumption tax. From September 1, 2026, lithium-ion batteries will again be subject to a 2% consumption tax, rising to 4% in September 2027. CATL mainly sells cells externally, so the burden falls directly on it; BYD's batteries are used in-house, so the tax falls at the vehicle level, a far smaller impact. Annualising first-half battery revenue, 2% corresponds to roughly RMB 9.8 billion a year, equivalent to 10% of expected 2026 net profit. A real cost, but not a fatal one.

III. The only thing improving in the financial statements

Overseas revenue reached RMB 87.13bn, up 42.4% YoY, at a 29.97% gross margin — 8.8pp above domestic.

Put simply, the same cells sold in Europe earn nearly half again as much gross profit as those sold at home. This is CATL's most valuable segment and the real gap versus second-tier makers: the second tier can fight a price war in China, but cannot break into Europe's automotive-grade supply chain.

The Hungary plant starts production in 2026, with the initial 40GWh already booked; Indonesia reaches mass production by year-end; the Spanish joint venture is underway. Every 10pp increase in the overseas revenue share lifts blended gross margin by roughly 0.9pp. This is the only certain source of profit over the next three years.

IV. Is 14x actually expensive?

At RMB 291.99 the market cap is RMB 1.35 trillion. Consensus 2026 net profit of RMB 95.2 billion implies 14.2x, and 2027 net profit of RMB 116.5 billion implies 11.6x. CATL's five-year valuation centre is 25–35x.

Do not, however, anchor on the 100x of 2021. The market priced it as a growth stock then; it now prices it as a manufacturer. On the manufacturing yardstick, 15x normalised profit gives RMB 378 for 2027, 29% upside; the bear case is RMB 190, 35% downside; the bull case is RMB 562, 92% upside.The odds skew positive, but patience is required.

One number to watch: in 1H, it earned RMB 98 per 1kWh sold, versus RMB 104 in Q1 and RMB 94 in Q2. How fast this line declines determines whether 14x is cheap or fair.

V. How to buy

Rating: Buy (scale in).

Build no more than one-third of the planned position initially. Q3 results are due late October, and two numbers decide whether to add: whether single-quarter net profit holds 25% YoY growth, and whether domestic passenger-vehicle share holds 44%. A power battery gross margin below 19% would signal the price war is out of control—stop loss.

One honest note to close: this is not a "buy the dip" story. CATL will not return to 40x; it will be a 15–20x manufacturing leader.You are buying earnings growth, not a valuation re-rating. Decide whether to buy only once you are clear on that.
