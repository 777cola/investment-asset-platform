Core view

Consolidated gross profit was RMB 66.266 billion, net profit attributable to shareholders RMB 43.284 billion, and RMB 22.982 billion in between is unaccounted for. Of that sum, RMB 11.376 billion is R&D (legitimate spend), some is tax (legitimately owed), some is minority interest (legitimately shared), and part is "invisible efficiency leakage." Reading financial statements the way Buffett does means reading this RMB 23 billion.

A 65.3% conversion rate is high for manufacturing

Gross profit converts to net profit at 65.3%, meaning every RMB 100 of gross profit leaves RMB 65 in shareholders' hands. For comparison: automakers typically run 40%–50%, solar module makers 20%–30%, and home appliance makers around 50%. CATL's conversion is high because scale dilutes expenses, with government subsidies and investment income providing a backstop.

But Buffett would press further: how much of that RMB 65 is sustainable? The RMB 11.376 billion of R&D is unavoidable (fall behind without it), taxes must be paid, and of the remainder, other income (subsidies) and investment income are not operating in nature. Strip these out and the true operating conversion rate is roughly 55%–60%.

Owner Earnings: How Much Less Than Net Income

Buffett's owner earnings = net profit + depreciation and amortization − maintenance capital expenditure. The problem with CATL is the inability to distinguish "maintenance" from "expansionary" capex — the 764GWh under construction is expansion, but liquid production lines need retrofitting every 3–5 years, and that portion is in fact maintenance.

Rough calculation: if 40% of annual capex is maintenance (about RMB 30–40 billion, based on industry experience), owner earnings after deduction come to about 70%–75% of net profit.That is, a 14x PE translates into a "owner earnings multiple" of 19–20x.This is why Buffett would only pay 15x.

Where did the cash go: capacity, or more capacity

First-half net profit RMB 43.284 billion, annualized RMB 86.6 billion; 764GWh under construction implies RMB 230–310 billion of capex (rough estimate). The money earned is not enough to cover the money spent; the gap is filled by the 2025 H-share IPO. That is not a bad thing — if the return on new capacity exceeds the cost of capital, value is being created.

Judging the return: investment of RMB 300–400 million per GWh, annual revenue of RMB 600 million, net profit of about RMB 100 million (rough estimate at 98 yuan/kWh × 1 billion Wh), payback in 3–4 years, IRR above 20%. As long as the capacity can be sold, the math works. A capacity utilization rate of 94.86% shows it can be sold now.

Conclusion

Financial quality: A-. Gross profit conversion of 65.3% is strong, but owner earnings are only 70% of net profit; the return on capital expenditure works out, provided the 2028 capacity finds buyers. At 15x normalized profit, RMB 378 is a fair price.
