## The first accounting-basis issue: segment gross profit sums to RMB 5.3 billion less

Segment gross profit: power batteries 1,921.25 × 20.63% = RMB 39.64bn; energy storage 532.61 × 23.96% = RMB 12.76bn; combined RMB 52.40bn. Battery materials and recycling plus other businesses of RMB 31.531bn at a 27.04% materials gross margin adds RMB 8.53bn, giving RMB 60.93bn across the three. The blended gross margin of 23.93% on revenue of RMB 276.917bn, however, implies RMB 66.27bn — a gap of RMB 5.34bn, or 8.1%.

That gap reflects definition rather than arithmetic error. Segment "gross margins" are typically disclosed on a basis that nets out inter-segment transactions, whereas the blended gross margin captures unallocated other business revenue, the recycling business and internal elimination differences. Approach: use segment gross margins to compare structural shifts (power vs energy storage vs materials) and the blended gross margin to size the total; do not cross-use the two. Forcing segment figures to reconcile to blended gross profit understates it by RMB 5.3bn — equivalent to understating net profit by 12%.

## The second measurement issue: energy storage at 19.2% or 25%

Energy storage revenue came to RMB 53.261 billion, equal to 19.2% of operating revenue and 21.7% of battery segment revenue (power + storage, RMB 245.386 billion). The "about 25%" cited in the working papers does not reconcile with either figure; most likely it includes energy storage system integration and revenue from overseas energy storage project companies, which sit outside the battery segment. This report uses 19.2% (of total revenue) / 21.7% (of battery revenue) throughout.

The gap looks small but matters in practice: reading it as 25% would overstate energy storage's contribution to total gross profit by about 1 percentage point, and with it the speed at which "energy storage is taking over from power." The reality is "close to one-fifth, growing at twice the pace of power," not "already a quarter."

## The third scope issue: 525GWh is half-year capacity, not annual capacity

The interim report shows output of 498GWh and a capacity utilisation rate of 94.86%. 498÷0.9486 = 525GWh—so effective capacity was 525GWh in the first half (half-year basis), or roughly 1,050GWh annualised. Misreading this as annual capacity produces the absurd conclusion of "189% capacity utilisation" and also doubles the relative scale of the 764GWh under construction (it is actually 73% of existing annualised capacity, not 145%).

The 764GWh under construction, at a rough investment intensity of RMB 300–400 million/GWh, implies RMB 230–310 billion of capex. This is the key number for understanding "why free cash flow can only barely stay positive," and the second cash sink after consumption tax. Of the three basis issues, this one is the easiest to get wrong and the one that most affects the conclusion.

## Final adopted basis

All financials are as reported in the interim results: revenue RMB 276.917bn, net profit attributable to parent RMB 43.284bn, power batteries RMB 192.125bn, energy storage RMB 53.261bn, overseas RMB 87.129bn, blended gross margin 23.93%, R&D RMB 11.376bn. Quarterly figures are derived by subtracting the Q1 report from the interim report (Q2 revenue RMB 147.786bn, net profit RMB 22.546bn); YoY growth is calculated from back-solved prior-year comparatives and marked "back-solved".

Valuation is based on combined A+H shares of 4.627bn and 2026 consensus net profit of RMB 95.2bn (Huachuang Securities, 2026/07), implying a P/E of 14.2x. Market cap is RMB 1.35trn including H shares; looking only at A-share free-float market cap would understate it by about 30%. All estimated figures (unit net profit RMB 98/kWh, consumption tax RMB 9.8bn/year, capex under construction RMB 230–310bn) are marked "rough calculation" in the text.
