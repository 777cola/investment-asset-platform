## The first accounting-basis issue: segment gross profit sums to RMB 5.3 billion less

Gross profit by segment: power batteries 1,921.25 × 20.63% = RMB 39.64 billion; energy storage 532.61 × 23.96% = RMB 12.76 billion; total RMB 52.40 billion. The remaining battery materials and recycling, and other businesses, at RMB 31.531 billion, at a materials gross margin of 27.04% gives RMB 8.53 billion. The three sum to **RMB 60.93 billion**. But the blended gross margin of 23.93% times revenue of RMB 276.917 billion is **RMB 66.27 billion**. The gap is RMB 5.34 billion, or 8.1%.

The gap is not an arithmetic error; it is a definitional difference. Segment "gross margins" are typically disclosed on a basis that nets out inter-segment transactions, while the blended gross margin includes unallocated other business revenue, the recycling business and the difference from internal eliminations. **Approach: use segment gross margins to compare structural shifts (power vs energy storage vs materials), and the blended gross margin to size the total; do not cross-use the two.** Forcing segment figures to back out blended gross profit understates it by RMB 5.3 billion, equivalent to understating net profit by 12%.

## The second measurement issue: energy storage at 19.2% or 25%

Energy storage came to RMB 53.261 billion, 19.2% of operating revenue and 21.7% of battery segment revenue (power + storage, RMB 245.386 billion). The "about 25%" in the working papers does not reconcile with these two figures — most likely energy storage system integration and overseas energy storage project company revenue were included, and that portion sits outside the battery segment. **This report uses 19.2% (of total revenue) / 21.7% (of battery revenue) throughout.**

The gap looks small but matters in use: reading it as 25% would overstate energy storage's contribution to total gross profit by about 1 percentage point, and in turn overstate the speed at which "energy storage is taking over from power." **The actual picture is "close to one-fifth, growing at twice the pace of power," not "already a quarter."**

## The third scope issue: 525GWh is half-year capacity, not annual capacity

The interim report gives output of 498GWh and a capacity utilization rate of 94.86%. 498÷0.9486 = 525GWh—**so 525GWh is the effective capacity for the first half (half-year basis), or roughly 1,050GWh annualized**. Misreading it as annual capacity yields the absurd conclusion of "189% capacity utilization" and also doubles the relative scale of the 764GWh under construction (it is actually 73% of existing annualized capacity, not 145%).

The 764GWh under construction, at a rough investment intensity of RMB 300–400 million/GWh, implies RMB 230–310 billion of capital expenditure. This is the key number for understanding "why free cash flow can only barely stay positive," and the second cash sink after consumption tax. **Of the three basis issues, this one is the easiest to get wrong and the one that most affects the conclusion.**

Quarterly figures are derived by subtracting the Q1 report from the half-year report (Q2 revenue 147.786 billion, net profit 22.546 billion); year-on-year growth rates are calculated from back-solved prior-year comparatives and marked "back-solved".

Valuation uses combined A+H shares of 4.627 billion and 2026 consensus net profit of RMB 95.2 billion (Huachuang Securities, 2026/07), giving a PE of 14.2x. **Market cap is RMB 1.35 trillion, including H shares; looking only at A-share free-float market cap would understate it by about 30%.** All estimated figures (unit net profit RMB 98/kWh, consumption tax RMB 9.8 billion/year, capex under construction RMB 230–310 billion) are marked "rough calculation" in the text.
