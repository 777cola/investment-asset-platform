Core view

Analysis can be vague; execution must be specific—only specific numbers can be tested every quarter.

Buy point: buy only when all three conditions hold simultaneously

Condition one, valuation at the low end of the manufacturing range: currently 14.2x 2026E and 11.6x 2027E, against a five-year midpoint of 25–35x, already below the low end.

Condition two, fundamentals have not deteriorated: gross margin 20.63% (>19%), share 46.7% (>44%), unit net profit RMB 98 (>RMB 90), all three within the lines.

Condition three, some of the bad news has already come out: consumption tax implemented, reports of de-CATLization, share price at a 52-week low. All three have happened; the remaining bad news (4% tax rate, solid-state) lands in 2027.

All three conditions hold simultaneously — that is the definition of a buy point. Not "it feels cheap," but "three verifiable conditions met at the same time."

The review calendar is fixed: late October 2026, Q3 results (single-quarter net profit growth, gross margin, unit net profit); January 2027, annual report and full-year installation share (verify 44%); April 2027, Q1 results and Hungary ramp-up (verify 33% overseas share). Three dates, six numbers — a complete reconciliation chain.

Position sizing: why 2%, not 6%

Position size is set by "certainty," not by "expected return." CATL's certainty is "passes all six gates, 45/60," not a perfect score; it is "leading but not monopolistic," not "irreplaceable." So its cap is 6%–8%, and the initial position is 2%.

The math of 2%: a 30% drop drags the portfolio by 0.6% (bearable), and a 30% gain contributes 0.6% (not a missed opportunity). That is the algorithm of "get on board first, add later"—use a small position to buy information and time.

Adding to 6% requires two gates to open at once; adding to 8% requires extreme mispricing (below RMB 260) plus full fundamental confirmation. In most cases 6% is enough—holding a heavy position in a single manufacturing leader is itself a judgment error.

Gate: two numbers, read once a quarter

Gate one: Q3 single-quarter net profit YoY ≥25%. The pace in the first half was Q1 +48.5%, Q2 +36.5%. If Q3 falls below 25%, the decline is accelerating, and 14x becomes 18x.

Gate two: domestic passenger vehicle installations ≥44%. Currently 46.7%, with Li Auto + Xiaomi accounting for 13.6%. Share is the only hard read on "de-CATLization," more reliable than any research view.

Both gates are directional: they do not tell you whether the company is good, only whether "the thesis still holds." If it holds, add; if not, cut—no need to rewrite a report.

Three things not to do

First, do not buy simply because "it has fallen a lot." The drop from 458.60 to 291.99 is -36.3%, but the valuation center also fell from 25x to 15x—half of the decline was "deserved."

Second, do not use "long-term bullish on electrification" as a reason to buy. That reason held in 2021 but is not enough in 2026—the industry is still growing, but CATL no longer exclusively captures the growth.

Third, do not use price-based stop-losses. Battery stocks are highly volatile, and price stop-losses will repeatedly get whipsawed. Use fundamental stop-losses: a gross margin of 19% and a share of 40%—these two numbers are the foundation of the thesis.

Final point: the reconciliation period for this investment is three quarters (2026Q3, the 2026 annual report, 2027Q1), not three months. If you cannot wait three quarters, do not start.

If the call is wrong, how do we exit?

Three ways to be wrong, three ways to exit. Wrong on valuation (the multiple keeps derating after purchase): as long as the thesis holds, hold and wait for profit growth, letting time buy room. Wrong on share (domestic share falls below 40%): halve the position, regardless of price. Wrong on technology (solid-state reaches mass production early): exit fully, because this is asset impairment, not earnings volatility.

What the three have in common: exit conditions are written before buying, not after losing money. The three lines written at purchase (19% gross margin, 40% share, solid-state installation) are the exit mechanism.

One last point: do not revise the thesis while losing money. The thesis was written before buying; the loss is a signal from the market. The two cannot be compromised with each other.

Conclusion

Decision framework: three buy conditions, 2% initial position, two gates, three exit lines. The return here comes from profit growth (15%–25% a year), not from valuation re-rating. Target price RMB 378 (+29%), bear case RMB 190, bull case RMB 562. Every judgment can be tested in the quarterly reports — that is the ultimate value of this report.
