## Gate one to gate three: understandable, profitable, defensible

Gate one: 9 points. The 1 point deducted is because it has two businesses: selling batteries and selling materials. The former is manufacturing; the latter (battery materials and recycling) posted revenue of RMB 31.5 billion and a gross margin of 27.04%, higher than the core business, but it is small in scale and thinly disclosed. The rest is clear: in the first half, power batteries were RMB 192.125 billion, energy storage RMB 53.261 billion, and overseas RMB 87.129 billion — three cuts sufficient to calculate unit economics — first-half sales of 440GWh and net profit of RMB 43.284 billion, equivalent to RMB 98/kWh (rough calculation); Q1 was RMB 104 and Q2 RMB 94, a slowly declining line.

Gate two: 8 points. The battery is the most expensive single component in a vehicle, at 30%–40% of cost, and in theory should carry pricing power. But in the first half of 2026 the power battery gross margin fell to 20.63%, showing that pricing power is shrinking: it can say no to automakers, but dares not say no to the market. A truly good business is one that raises prices without losing volume; CATL is now cutting prices to hold volume. Hence 8 points, not 9.

Gate three: 8 points. Scale is the hardest factor here: first-half effective capacity of 525GWh, output of 498GWh, utilization of 94.86%, and 764GWh under construction. Unit cost is among the lowest in the field, and second-tier manufacturers cannot catch up. But the moat keeps out new entrants, not customers who want a second supplier — in the second half of 2026, Li Auto, Xiaomi and AITO are all adding second suppliers. This is a structural weakness of the moat, not a technology problem.

## Gate four: Zeng Yuqun's two early moves, and one time he fell behind

In 2016, when the whole industry was making lithium iron phosphate batteries, CATL bet on ternary lithium and won orders from BMW and Volkswagen on energy density. In 2021, with power batteries still in short supply, it shifted capacity to energy storage; in the first half of 2026, storage revenue reached RMB 53.261 billion, up 87.5% year on year, the only segment that is accelerating. Neither was luck — both were bets placed two years before industry consensus formed.

The third time it fell behind: solid-state batteries. In 2026, semi-solid-state began small-scale installation, and in 2027 all-solid-state enters small-batch trial production. CATL does not lead on the sulfide route; second-tier manufacturers and startups are racing for first launch instead. This is not fatal, but it means that in the next technology cycle, CATL is no longer the default first supplier.

Shareholder returns earn a plus: with the stock at a new low, it announced a RMB 20–40 billion buyback (1.5%–3.0% of market cap), and the interim report pays RMB 14.11 per 10 shares, about RMB 6.53 billion in total (rough calculation), a payout ratio of 15.1%. The amount is not generous, but the signal is clear — management believes 14x is undervalued and is willing to put real money behind that view.

## Gate Five: 14x is not a mispricing, but reasonably low

Start with three numbers. Market cap: 291.99 × 4.627 billion shares = RMB 1.35 trillion. The 2026 consensus net profit of RMB 95.2 billion (Huachuang's post-interim forecast) implies 14.2x; 2027's RMB 116.5 billion implies 11.6x. Over a five-year window, CATL's historical valuation center sits at 25–35x, and it has traded below 20x only twice: early 2024 and late 2025. 14x is a "manufacturing valuation," not a "growth-stock valuation."

The discount is justified, which is why this gets a 6 rather than an 8. First, RMB 95.2 billion for the full year means RMB 51.9 billion in the second half, up 20% HoH, versus only +8.7% HoH in Q2 — the consensus itself requires Q3 and Q4 single-quarter net profit above RMB 25 billion, and failing that means a downgrade. Second, the consumption tax lands at 2% on September 1 and rises to 4% in September 2027; based on first-half battery revenue of RMB 245.4 billion, the full-year tax burden is roughly RMB 9.8 billion, equal to 10% of 2026 expected net profit — 5% even if half is passed through.

Now the safety cushion: the balance sheet holds substantial cash and a low interest-bearing debt ratio. Even if net profit is cut 20% to RMB 76 billion, 14x becomes 17.8x, which does not amount to a valuation collapse. The conclusion at this gate: "not cheap enough to be a steal, but cheap enough to start buying."

## Gate six: reduce the buy case to two numbers

I do not accept the premise of "long-term bullish on electrification." This discipline requires writing assumptions as numbers that quarterly reports can prove wrong. Here are three: Q3 single-quarter net profit attributable to shareholders up ≥25% YoY (below that, 14x is not cheap); domestic passenger-car installation share ≥44% (now 46.7%; a 3-point drop signals accelerating de-CATLization); power battery gross margin ≥19% (now 20.63%; falling below means prices are out of control).

All three can be read in the Q3 report around October 20 — no need to wait a year. That is the rationale for tranching: the first tranche buys valuation, the second buys data, the third buys trend. For now, only the first tranche is warranted.
