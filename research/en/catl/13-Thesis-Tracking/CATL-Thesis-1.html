Core view

The buy thesis rests on five assumptions: share holds, unit profit stops falling, overseas volume ramps, the consumption tax is absorbable, and solid-state does not arrive early. Three are validated (unit profit still above RMB 90, overseas gross margin 29.97%, consumption tax impact 5%–10%); two remain pending (domestic share, solid-state progress).

Share is the most fragile: domestic passenger-car installations sit at 46.7%, with Li Auto + Xiaomi already at 13.6%; if second-tier players reach 25%–30% by 2027, this assumption breaks. Write falsification conditions as numbers and reconcile every quarter.

Assumption 1: share — headroom of only 2.7 points

Domestic passenger-car installation share is 46.7%, and the thesis requires holding 44%. The buffer is 2.7 percentage points, equivalent to one quarter of volatility. Li Auto's new L8 switching entirely to Sunwoda, Xiaomi's Longjia battery dual-sourcing, and AITO bringing in CALB and Gotion will show up in installation data between the second half of 2026 and the first half of 2027.

Do the math: Li Auto and Xiaomi together account for 13.6% of domestic installations. If they cut CATL's share from 100% to 60%, the impact is about 5.4 points; if second-tier suppliers pick up another 10% at other automakers, the cumulative impact is 7–8 points, and CATL's domestic share falls to around 39%. That breaks the 44% line.

So the question is not whether this assumption breaks, but how fast. Q4 2026 installation data is the first read.

Scenario 2: unit profit already RMB 94 in Q2, just shy of RMB 90

H1 unit net profit came in at about RMB 98/kWh, with Q1 at 104 and Q2 at 94. At this pace, Q3 could fall below RMB 90. This is the tightest of the five assumptions, since it directly determines whether 14x P/E is cheap or merely fair.

Sensitivity: every RMB 5 decline in unit net profit reduces full-year net profit by about RMB 2.2 billion (440GWh x 2 halves, rough calculation), equal to -2.3% of 2026E net profit. A RMB 20 decline cuts net profit by 9%, turning 14x into 15.4x — still acceptable; a RMB 40 decline cuts net profit by 18%, and the valuation is no longer cheap.

Assumption 3: Overseas — 3.5 points short, but the direction is right

Overseas accounts for 31.5% versus the 35% required in the paper. Hungary starts production in 2026 with the initial 40GWh already booked, Indonesia begins mass production by year-end and the Spain joint venture is progressing. If all three bases ramp on schedule, overseas share reaching 40% in 2027 is possible.

The risk is ramp speed: overseas plants typically run lower yields and labour efficiency than domestic ones, so initial gross margin may fall below 29.97%. The metric to watch is not "start of production" but "gross margin in the second quarter after start of production."

Assumption 4 and 5: consumption tax and solid-state — one is priced in, one is not

The 2% consumption tax took effect on September 1, with an impact of 5%–10%, and the market has already priced it in. The real variable is the 4% in September 2027—that would push the impact from 10% to 20%, and it has nothing to do with the cost gap versus second-tier players (second-tier players pay it too). That means industry-wide price increases, which may actually benefit the leader (smaller players cannot hold on).

Solid-state batteries are the only variable that has not been priced in. Semi-solid-state batteries will be installed in vehicles on a small scale in 2026, and all-solid-state batteries will enter small-batch trial production in 2027; CATL is not ahead.If a second-tier player announces mass-produced all-solid-state batteries installed in vehicles in 2027, CATL's liquid-battery capacity will be revalued. This cannot be verified in advance; we can only track installation data and customer design wins.

Watch calendar (2026/10–2027/04)

Around 20 October: Q3 results. Watch three numbers — single-quarter net profit YoY (threshold 25%), power battery gross margin (threshold 19%) and unit net profit (threshold RMB 90). January 2027: 2026 annual report and full-year installed share, to verify 44%. April 2027: Q1 report and Hungary plant ramp-up data, to verify the 35% overseas share.

Three dates, six numbers — a complete reconciliation chain. If any one breaks, cut the position. No debating the narrative.

Add one point: what does "the thesis hasn't broken but the stock is falling" mean

The commonest mistake in investing is asking "I got the thesis right, so why am I still losing money?" CATL is the case in point: the thesis — share, unit profit, overseas — still holds, but the valuation centre is drifting lower. The market has cut its fair multiple from 25x to 15x, and that adjustment has nothing to do with the thesis; it reflects the disappearance of the certainty premium.

The answer is not to revise the thesis but to revise the entry price. The thesis decides whether to buy; valuation decides at what price. Buying a manufacturing leader with an intact thesis at 14x still earns 20% a year from profit growth, even if the multiple never re-rates.

Conclusion

Thesis status: intact, but share and unit profit are the weak points. Three of the five assumptions hold and two await verification, both with thin margins of error (2.7pct of share, RMB 8 of unit net profit). Falsification conditions: domestic share below 44%, unit net profit below RMB 90, or power battery gross margin below 19% — any one triggers a halving of the position; any two together trigger a full exit.
