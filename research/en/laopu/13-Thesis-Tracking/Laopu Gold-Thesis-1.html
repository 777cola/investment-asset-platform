Core call: the thesis holds, but its preconditions have narrowed

Break the phrase "watch and wait for gold prices to stabilize" into six verifiable hypotheses and score each one: two hold (brand price increases, capital allocation), two fall short (inventory turnover, cash flow self-sufficiency), one is partly disproven (positive same-store growth), and one is in doubt (the category is not obsolete).

The thesis has not been fully overturned—the company is still profitable, the stores are still there, the brand is still there. But the premise has narrowed from "high growth is sustainable" to "a return to mid-speed growth once gold prices stabilize." That narrowing is itself a downgrade: from "growth stock worth watching" to "consumer stock that must wait for the cycle."

Two conditions for establishment: price increases and capital allocation

Pricing power is the hardest evidence in this thesis. Three rounds of price increases — +10%–15% in August 2025, +20%–30% in October, and +20%–30% in February 2026 — all landed, and gross margin still rose 3.2pct in the first half of 2026. That shows customers still accept the prices and the brand's bargaining power is intact.

Capital allocation holds up too: no M&A, no dilution, a 75% payout, and proactive store optimization. Among consumer stocks, that restraint is scarcer than growth.

But the significance of these two points is limited — they prove "the company is good," not "the stock is a buy." Good companies' stocks can still fall 60%, because the price already embeds expectations.

One partially disproven point: same-store

"Same-store sales can sustain positive growth" is the core support for this stock's 140x PE. In Q2 2026, that assumption was contradicted for the first time: net profit -34% to -56% YoY, revenue -76% to -87% QoQ. Although the company did not disclose same-store data separately, the declines in net profit and revenue already tell the story.

The more telling evidence is National Day: even price cuts failed to lift volume. If this were merely "pulling forward price increases," price cuts should have brought demand back; in reality they did not. This shows a shift in demand-side expectations—consumers no longer believe "if I don't buy now, it will be more expensive later."

We judge this as "partially falsified" rather than "fully falsified" because there is only one quarter of data, and Q3 has the Qixi and Mid-Autumn peak-season recovery.Give it one more quarter: if Q3 same-store sales are still negative, this assumption is void.

Two items not met: inventory and cash flow

Inventory: Target is within 250 days; actual 271 days, 55 days more than the 216 days at end-2025. This assumption deteriorated faster than expected — one quarter erased two years of improvement.

Cash flow: Target is to cover dividends; actual RMB 2.008 billion versus RMB 3.185 billion, a coverage ratio of 63%. Turning positive from negative RMB 6.848 billion was no small feat, but "turning positive by buying less inventory" does not count as truly meeting the target.

These two misses directly affect valuation — because "high dividend" is the stock's only support logic during the decline. If the dividend must be topped up from cash on the balance sheet and may shrink next year, that support is gone. If the March 2027 annual report announces a cut to the payout ratio, that would be a negative catalyst.

Falsification conditions and monitoring calendar

Falsification conditions for the overall thesis (any one triggered downgrades to "Avoid"): ① Same-store sales negative for two consecutive quarters with the decline widening; ② inventory turnover exceeds 320 days; ③ gross margin falls below 37%; ④ operating cash flow turns negative again and the payout ratio is cut; ⑤ Xu Gaoming departs with no clear succession plan.

Upgrade conditions (any one triggered upgrades to "Buy"): ① Gold holds flat in the USD 4,000–4,300 range for a full quarter and same-store sales turn positive; ② overseas stores increase to 3 with annualized sales per store of at least RMB 500 million; ③ the share price falls below HKD 250 without any of the above falsification conditions being triggered.

Watch calendar: January 2027 (full-year profit alert) for the revenue and net profit ranges and fourth-quarter sell-through; December 2026–January 2027 (Chinese New Year stocking and price increases) for whether price hikes land; March 2027 (annual report) for the payout ratio and full-year gross margin. Four data points, each answering only one question — do not read them together.

Conclusion

Thesis status: not broken, but the premise has narrowed. Of the six assumptions, two hold firmly, two fall short, one is partly falsified and one is in doubt. The conclusion is to keep watching, with zero position. The next decision point is the January 2027 positive profit alert — if fourth-quarter same-store sales are still in decline and full-year revenue comes in below RMB 34.0 billion, the thesis must be rewritten; if they turn positive, the base case of HKD 352 can be maintained.
