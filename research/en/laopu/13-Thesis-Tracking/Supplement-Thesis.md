## Two conditions must hold: price increases and capital allocation

Pricing power is the hardest evidence in this thesis. Three rounds of price increases — +10%–15% in August 2025, +20%–30% in October and +20%–30% in February 2026 — all stuck, and gross margin still rose 3.2pct in 1H26. Customers are still accepting the prices, and the brand's bargaining power remains intact.

Capital allocation holds up too: no M&A, no dilution, a 75% payout and proactive store optimisation. Among consumer stocks, that restraint is scarcer than growth.

But these two points only go so far — they prove "the company is good," not "the stock is a buy." Good companies' stocks can still fall 60%, because the price already embeds expectations.

## One partially disproven point: same-store

The 140x P/E rests on one core assumption: that same-store sales can sustain positive growth. In Q2 2026 that assumption was contradicted for the first time, with net profit down 34%-56% YoY and revenue down 76%-87% QoQ. The company does not disclose same-store data separately, but the net profit and revenue declines already tell the story.

National Day is more telling: even price cuts failed to lift volume. Had this merely been a matter of pulling forward price increases, price cuts should have brought demand back; they did not. The signal is a shift in demand-side expectations—consumers no longer believe "if I don't buy now, it will be more expensive later."

We judge the assumption partially falsified rather than fully falsified: there is only one quarter of data, and Q3 brings the Qixi and Mid-Autumn peak-season recovery.Give it one more quarter—if Q3 same-store sales are still negative, the assumption is void.

## Two items not met: inventory and cash flow

Inventory: Target: within 250 days. Actual: 271 days, 55 days above the 216 days at end-2025. This assumption deteriorated faster than expected — a single quarter erased two years of improvement.

Cash flow: Target: dividend cover. Actual: RMB 2.008 billion against RMB 3.185 billion, a coverage ratio of 63%. Turning positive from negative RMB 6.848 billion was no small feat, but getting there by buying less inventory does not count as truly meeting the target.

These two misses directly affect valuation — because the "high dividend" story is the stock's only support during the decline. If the dividend has to be topped up from balance-sheet cash and may shrink next year, that support is gone. A cut to the payout ratio announced in the March 2027 annual report would be a negative catalyst.

## Falsification conditions and monitoring calendar

Falsification conditions for the thesis (any one triggered downgrades to "Avoid"): ① same-store sales negative for two consecutive quarters with the decline widening; ② inventory turnover above 320 days; ③ gross margin below 37%; ④ operating cash flow negative again and the payout ratio cut; ⑤ Xu Gaoming departs with no clear succession plan.

Upgrade conditions (any one triggered upgrades to "Buy"): ① gold holds flat in the USD 4,000–4,300 range for a full quarter and same-store sales turn positive; ② overseas stores reach 3 with annualised sales per store of at least RMB 500 million; ③ the share price falls below HKD 250 without any falsification condition above being triggered.

Watch calendar: January 2027 (full-year profit alert) for revenue and net profit ranges and fourth-quarter sell-through; December 2026–January 2027 (Chinese New Year stocking and price increases) for whether price hikes land; March 2027 (annual report) for the payout ratio and full-year gross margin. Four data points, each answering only one question — do not read them together.
