📝 Conclusion: Watch and wait for gold prices to stabilize

Watch and wait for gold prices to stabilise. Laopu reported a strong 2026H1: revenue of RMB 19.808 billion, +60.3%; net profit of RMB 4.267 billion, +88.2%; a record net margin of 21.5%; and positive operating cash flow for the first time in three years. Yet the shares fell from HKD 849.5 to HKD 337 — a 60.3% drawdown — not because growth has disappeared, but because the market has finally begun to price in "peak profit": the 41.3% gross margin embeds a timing gap from low-cost inventory, the RMB 19 billion of inventory is a pro-cyclical amplifier, and the denominator of the 6.9x P/E is precisely this peak profit. High growth cannot support a high valuation when the market is paying a growth-stock price for what is a cyclical consumer business. The stock has now returned to consumer-stock territory (roughly 10x on sustainable profit), offering odds of about 2:1, but the probability skews pessimistic and it is not yet a buy — wait for a quarter of falling gold prices and see whether same-store sales hold up.
