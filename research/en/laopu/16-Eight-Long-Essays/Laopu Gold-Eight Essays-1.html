Cognitive reset: from the 25x myth to the 7.9x reality — Laopu Gold

Core view

On 28 June 2024, Laopu Gold listed at HKD 40.5 per share, with its offering 582 times oversubscribed. In July 2025, the stock touched HKD 1,065, up 25 times from the IPO price, with its price-to-earnings ratio reaching 140 times. On 9 October 2026, it stood at HKD 337, or 7.9 times. The same company, described by the market in two entirely different languages within two years. This piece sets out to dismantle the first of those languages.

I. How the 25x rally came about

In its listing year, the company posted revenue of RMB 8.506 billion and net profit of RMB 1.473 billion. The HKD 40.5 offer price implied a market cap of about HKD 7.1 billion, roughly 4.4x P/S and 25x PE—hardly cheap for a consumer stock.

But the market was not buying 2024 earnings; it was buying the imagination of the "gufa jin" (traditional-craft gold) category. From the second half of 2024 to the first half of 2025, gold prices rose from USD 2,000 to USD 3,500, and the company's revenue jumped from RMB 3.18 billion (2023) to RMB 8.506 billion (2024) and then to RMB 27.303 billion (2025)—an 8.6x increase in two years. Growth of that pace is almost unheard of in consumer, so the market naturally priced it as "China's Tiffany plus a gold bull market."

In July 2025, the stock traded at HKD 1,065, a market cap of about HKD 188 billion, roughly 35x 2025 net profit of RMB 4.868 billion and about 25x the 2026 profit the market expected at the time.The 140x PE figure dates from an earlier point—April 2025, when trailing profit had not yet caught up with the share price.

The rally logic was complete: category breakout + rising gold prices + scarce store network + high dividends. With all four factors in place at once, the stock took off. The problem is that the company controls only one of the four.

II. Three cognitive errors

Mistake one: treating gold-price beta as company alpha.Gross margin fell from 41.2% to 37.6% in 2024–2025, showing the company was actually hurt when gold prices rose fast; but revenue rose 221%, and the market saw only revenue. The truth: rising gold prices created a "buy the rally" mood, the mood drove volume, and volume masked the margin decline. The share price rose on sentiment, not capability.

Mistake two: treating one-off demand as sustained growth.Prices were raised 20%–30% in late February 2026; Q1 tax-inclusive sales reached about RMB 19 billion, twice the whole of 2024 in a single quarter. That number was read at the time as "brand power exploding", but it was demand pull-forward — existing customers bought ahead of the price increase, cashing in the next two quarters of volume early. Q2 revenue of RMB 2.3–3.95 billion is the bill.

Mistake three: treating category tailwind as a brand moat.Gufa gold caught fire in 2023 and became an industry standard by 2025, with Chow Tai Fook Heritage, Lao Feng Xiang Gufa and Chow Tai Seng filigree all following. Category tailwinds typically last three to five years, and Laopu is in year four. The market still gives it 25–35x PE on "perpetual brand".

III. After the Reset: What Exactly Is It

Strip out the three errors and the remaining picture is this: a high-gross-margin, high-dividend, strong-brand gold jewellery retailer whose earnings are driven by the gold-price cycle, with its growth ceiling set by store locations.

This picture maps to three valuation anchors. First, the comparables are Chow Tai Fook (32.3% gross margin, 14x PE), Chow Sang Sang (32.62%, about 7x) and Tiffany (about 20x before its acquisition by LVMH). Laopu has higher gross margin and faster growth, so a reasonable range is 10–15x.

Second, the cycle position is mid-way through a pullback from high gold prices, with earnings near peak; on cyclical-stock logic this warrants a low multiple, 8–10x.

Third, asset value is RMB 25–28 billion, corresponding to HKD 154–173 per share, which is the floor.

Combining the three anchors: a fair midpoint of 10x, corresponding to HKD 352; a floor of HKD 154–173; and a ceiling (successful overseas expansion plus category upgrade) of 15x, or HKD 528. The current price of HKD 337 sits right near the midpoint — the market has already completed its cognitive reset; many investors simply have not.

IV. The market has reset; people have not

On the cognitive reset, the market moves faster than people do. Laopu's share price fell from HKD 1,065 to HKD 337 in just fifteen months; yet many investors still view it through a "25x growth stock" framework today.

The evidence is in sell-side reports. Goldman Sachs cut its target price from 1,108 to 650 on July 12, cut it again to 560 on July 28, and maintained 560 with a "Buy" rating on September 1. The target price was halved and the rating unchanged—the most common sell-side compromise: acknowledging that the numbers must be revised down, but unwilling to admit the story is over.

A genuine reset should look like this: replace "leader in the traditional gold category" with "gold jewelry cyclical stock," replace 25–35x PE with 8–13x, and replace "revenue growth" with "same-store sales + turnover." After these three swaps, the current price of HKD 337 is not "cheap"—it is "fair."

Conclusion

The conclusion of the cognitive reset: it is not a growth stock, it is a cyclical consumer stock. Growth stocks are judged on growth rates; cyclicals are judged on where they sit in the cycle. View it through a growth-stock framework and 7.9x looks absurdly cheap; view it through a cyclical framework and 10x is a fair price, with a margin of safety only at 8x. One company, two frameworks, two answers. The value of this piece is to make you aware of which framework you are using.
