The debate is not about the numbers but about the time horizon

Core view

All four read the same numbers; their conclusions diverge on time horizon: Duan Yongping looks at the business model (ten years), Buffett at the cycle position (three to five years), Munger at failure paths (at any time), and Li Lu at the category life cycle (twenty years). Overlay the four horizons and the conclusion is "don't buy now, but worth waiting for."

Consensus take one: a good set of results — but not a good set of results for a good business

All four agree the statements look impressive: revenue +60.3%, net profit +88.2%, gross margin 41.3%, net margin 21.5%, cash flow turned positive and dividend payout 75%. Among Hong Kong consumer stocks, that is top-tier.

But all four also flag the same problem: the impressive numbers reflect cyclical tailwinds, not operational improvement. The 20%–30% price hikes stuck because gold prices are rising and customers are rushing to buy; gross margin returned to 41.3% because inventory was built at low prices; cash flow turned positive because the company bought less stock. All three move in the same direction as gold prices.

The right way to read these results, then, is as a sample from a cyclical peak, not the starting point of a growth stock. That judgment determines every valuation and position-sizing call that follows.

Consensus 2: the valuation anchor sits at 10x, not 7x and not 20x

The four frameworks imply different multiple ranges, but they converge at around 10x. Duan Yongping applies 5x (extremely conservative), Buffett 8–10x on asset value, Munger no multiple at all (he waits for the shakeout), and Li Lu 10–13x. The intersection puts the reasonable midpoint at 10x.

10x corresponds to 2027 net profit of RMB 5.7 billion and HKD 352, a 4% gap to the current price of HKD 337.That coincidence shows market pricing is efficient—the stock is already priced as a category leader in mid-cycle with slowing growth.

The claim that it is "cheap" therefore does not hold. 7.9x looks low because the denominator is peak profit; 10x looks reasonable because the denominator switches to normalized profit. Same company, two denominators, two conclusions.

The debate: how much longer can the category stay hot?

This is the one question the data cannot answer, and the point on which the four disagree most.

Duan Yongping is the most bearish: consumer fads typically run under five years, and gufa gold has been scaling since 2023—three years now—so little time remains. Buffett passes no judgement, saying only that "if the category can change, margins cannot be extrapolated," and demands a lower price. Munger is gloomier still: once the category dividend ends, he expects the 41% gross margin to converge toward 30% as peers deploy the same craft in a price war. Li Lu is the most bullish: cultural symbols outlive functional products, and gufa gold has a chance to become a long-term aesthetic branch alongside hanfu and guochao.

This report's verdict: assign the category a neutral 3–5 year assumption—neither assuming it persists long term nor that it vanishes immediately.That assumption maps to the neutral scenario of RMB 5.7 billion net profit in 2027. If the category fades early, the pessimistic scenario of RMB 4.5 billion applies; if the category upgrade succeeds, the optimistic scenario of RMB 7.4 billion applies.

Synthesis: three numbers to watch

Having synthesised four close reads, we think only three numbers are worth tracking each quarter.

First, same-store sales growth. Total revenue is distorted by new stores, overseas expansion and price increases, whereas same-store sales reflect true brand demand. Same-store sales already turned negative in Q2 2026; if Q3 and Q4 both post negative growth, the base case is void.

Second, inventory turnover days. 271 days is the warning line and 300 days the red line. Above 300 days implies channel stuffing, and the next step is price cuts to clear inventory, pushing gross margin below 37%.

Third, operating cash flow. If it turns negative again in H2 2026, the benefit from buying less inventory has run its course and the company is back to expanding on debt.

If two of the three deteriorate, downgrade from Watch to Avoid; if all three improve, upgrade to Buy with a target price of HKD 352.

Conclusion

Synthesis: good company, bad timing, fair price. None of the four deep reads makes the case to buy, and none makes the case to abandon the stock outright. The consensus is to wait until the data prove the business can make money without leaning on the gold price; the disagreement is over how long to wait. We put the window at two to three quarters, with two checkpoints: the January 2027 positive profit alert and the March 2027 annual results.
