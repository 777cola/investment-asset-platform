## Six consistent figures, safe to use

Six items — revenue, net profit, gross margin, net margin, inventory and store count — match exactly across the company announcement and commentary from at least two brokers. That is unsurprising: the interim results announcement was published on 24 August, and the broker notes simply reproduce it with their own estimates layered on top.

Two categories of numbers genuinely warrant cross-checking: ratios the company calculates itself and forecasts the brokers calculate themselves. Take "inventory turnover of 271 days" as an example of the former — the company derives it by dividing average inventory by cost of sales, but the report does not specify whether work-in-progress and raw materials are included in that basis. Take "Q2 revenue of RMB 2.3–3.95 billion" as an example of the latter — brokers back-solved it from the growth range in the profit alert, so it is not a company-disclosed figure.

This report therefore treats them as follows: disclosed figures are used directly, ratio figures carry a note on their basis, and back-solved figures are all labelled "back-solved/rough estimate".

## PE gap of 13%: the denominator is stitched together

P/E is the most error-prone number here: the TTM net profit denominator requires stitching together three reporting periods.

Our recalculation: TTM net profit = FY25 RMB 4.90 billion − 2025H1 RMB 2.268 billion + 2026H1 RMB 4.267 billion = RMB 6.90 billion. Market cap HKD 59.6 billion ÷ 1.092 = RMB 54.54 billion; 54.54 ÷ 6.90 = 7.9x.

The 6.9x in the working papers implies a TTM of RMB 7.93 billion, RMB 1.03 billion above RMB 6.90 billion, and the report does not explain where that RMB 1.03 billion comes from. The discrepancy does not change the direction of the call, but it does change how cheap "cheap" is: 7.9x sits mid-range and 6.9x at the bottom of the consumer-stock band. We adopt the conservative figure and, wherever valuation is involved, use 2027 forecast profit to sidestep the definitional issue.

## Three figures from a single source only

Three numbers warrant separate flagging, as they are used as evidence later.

First, operating cash flow of +RMB 2.008 billion. Brokerage notes said only that it was "positive for the first time in three years" without citing the amount, and neither did the media briefs. The interim report is the sole source. This matters: it is the only hard evidence of "improved earnings quality", so it can be used only with the caveat that no second source has verified it.

Second, inventory turnover of 271 days. Again, company figures only. The 216 days at end-2025 was back-calculated from "up 55 days from the start of the year", making it a second-order estimate.

Third, payout ratio of about 75%. RMB 3.185 billion ÷ RMB 4.267 billion = 74.6%; this was calculated in-house, but the total dividend of RMB 3.185 billion and RMB 18.02 per share are disclosed figures, and the two cross-check (18.02 × 176.75 million shares = RMB 3.185 billion), so it is credible.

## Final adopted basis

All 17 modules that follow use the same basis: 2026H1 revenue of RMB 19.808 billion, net profit of RMB 4.267 billion, gross margin of 41.3% and net margin of 21.5%; 2025 full-year revenue of RMB 27.303 billion and net profit attributable to parent of RMB 4.868 billion (profit for the year of RMB 4.90 billion); market cap of HKD 59.6 billion on a share price of HKD 337 and 176.75 million shares; P/E on the calculated 7.9x, with the working-paper 6.9x retained for external presentation; and a three-tier valuation applying 8x/10x/13x to 2027 net profit of RMB 45/57/74 billion at an exchange rate of RMB 1 = HKD 1.092.

The 2025H1 revenue of RMB 12.36 billion and net profit of RMB 2.268 billion are both back-solved from YoY growth rates and match the figures disclosed in the company's 2025 interim report, confirming the back-solving method is reliable. Q2 single-quarter revenue of RMB 2.3–3.95 billion and net profit of RMB 510–760 million are back-solved by brokers from the profit-alert range; only a range, not point values, is available, so the range must be preserved when cited.
