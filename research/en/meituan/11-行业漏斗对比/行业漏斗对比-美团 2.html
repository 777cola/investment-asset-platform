XI. Competitive landscape: summary and outlook

Landscape overview

The local life services market has moved beyond the binary competition of "Meituan vs Ele.me" and enteredMultipolar free-for-allNew stage:

  • Food delivery sector:Meituan 70% vs Ele.me 28% — the most stable competitive structure, with the duopoly entrenched
  • In-store segment:Meituan 43% vs Douyin 25% — the landscape shifts sharply as competition intensifies
  • Instant retail:Meituan Instashopping 40% vs JD Daojia 30% — rapid growth, landscape unsettled
  • Travel and hospitality sector:Meituan vs Ctrip vs Fliggy — the classic landscape, roiled by Douyin's entry

Forward-looking view

Time dimension Core expectations Impact on Meituan
Short term (1 year) Douyin local-life in-store GMV growth is slowing (base effect), but the absolute increment remains large. Meituan is responding by ramping up livestreaming and video content. Profit under pressure, but revenue steady
Medium term (2-3 years) Instant retail has become the new battleground. Competition between JD Hourly Shopping and Meituan Instashopping will determine the shape of the industry's third pole. Whether Douyin enters food delivery is the key variable. Higher investment, but greater room for growth
Long term (5 years) Unmanned delivery technology could disrupt the industry's cost structure. Meituan, with the largest capacity network and dispatch data, has the strongest first-mover advantage. Penetration in lower-tier markets remains the main growth driver. Moat deepens, profitability improves

⚠️ Biggest risk:If Douyin decides to enter the food delivery race (it has not yet done so at scale), it would deal a fundamental blow to Meituan's valuation logic. Food delivery is Meituan's "core traffic funnel"; if that core is threatened, traffic to in-store dining and travel would be eroded in a chain reaction. For now, the biggest obstacle to Douyin's entry into food delivery is the buildout cost and operational difficulty of a delivery network—which is precisely Meituan's strongest moat.

Investment implications

  1. Meituan's moat is wider than the market believes:A delivery network handling 75 million orders a day cannot be replicated overnight; Ele.me has chased for eight years and still has not closed the gap.
  2. Margin pressure in the in-store business is the biggest drag on valuation:The market's PS/PE discount on Meituan mainly reflects concerns over erosion of in-store profitability.
  3. Instant retail is the biggest incremental opportunity:The category expansion from "food delivery" to "delivering everything" could expand Meituan's TAM (total addressable market) by 3-5x.
  4. Valuation offers a high margin of safety:A PS of 1.3x and an FCF yield of 10.6% provide a sufficient margin of safety. Even if intensifying competition cuts profit by 30%, the PE would still be below 20x.
  5. Key monitoring indicators:Food delivery average daily order growth, in-store GTV growth, Douyin local life GMV, flash purchase penetration, delivery cost per order.
