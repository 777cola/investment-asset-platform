🔴 Largest market-data error — affects all valuation metrics: PS, PE, PB and FCF Yield

Calculation process：

  • Shares outstanding (reported): 4.756 billion shares
  • Closing share price (Yahoo confirmed): HKD 78.35
  • Correct market cap= HKD 78.35 × 4.756 billion =HKD 372.63 billion ≈ USD 47.76 billion
  • Reported market cap: HKD 483.79 billion ≈ USD 62.0 billion
  • Difference: HKD 111.17 billion (~USD 14.24 billion)

Possible reasons：

  1. Share price date mismatch: The report may have used a higher share price from a historical date (such as a point within the 52-week range) to calculate market capitalization
  2. Share class differences: May include different share classes (such as Class A/B shares, options, RSUs, etc., on a fully diluted basis)
  3. Data source error: The market cap data shown on Google Finance at the time was itself incorrect

Revision suggestion: Recalculate market cap using the current closing price of HKD 78.35 × the latest number of shares outstanding. Recommend labeling the data timestamp to clarify whether it is the "same-day" or "reporting-period" market cap.

7.2 Root cause analysis

Root cause 1: market cap basis mismatch (affects 3 metrics: PS/PE/FCF Yield)

The report substitutes HKD 483.8 billion (implying a share price of HKD 101.72) for the actual HKD 372.7 billion (actual close of HKD 78.35), systematically overstating all ratios that use market cap as the denominator. The deviation is 29.7%.

Root cause 2: incorrect PB calculation method (affects 1 PB item)

PB = Price / Book Value (share price/net assets), but the report substitutes total assets of USD 45 billion for net assets, which understates PB. Actual PB is about 2.0x, directionally consistent with MacroTrends ADR data (2.36x).

Validation process

  1. Extract all key-value pairs from financial-data.md (share price, revenue, net profit, FCF, ratios, etc.)
  2. For each data point, obtain comparable data from at least one independent source
  3. Calculate deviation percentage = |verified value - reported value| / verified value × 100%
  4. Flag data items with deviation >1%
  5. Root-cause analysis of the deviation term
