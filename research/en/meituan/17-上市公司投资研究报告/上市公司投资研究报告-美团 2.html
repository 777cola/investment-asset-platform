1Company overview

1.1 Company overview

Meituan (ticker: 3690.HK / MPNGY) is China's leading e-commerce platform for local life services. Wang Xing founded the company in 2010; it listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in September 2018 and joined the Hang Seng Index on 7 December 2020. As of July 2026, the company employed roughly 110,000 people, with a total market capitalization of about HKD 483.8 billion (approximately USD 62 billion).

1.2 Development history

  • 2010— Meituan founded in Beijing, starting with group-buying
  • 2015— Merged with Dianping to establish leadership in local life services
  • 2018— Listed on HKEX (IPO price HKD 69)
  • 2020— Added to the Hang Seng Index constituent stocks; market cap tops HKD 1 trillion
  • 2021–2022Tighter regulation and intensifying competition weigh on the share price
  • 2023–2024— Profitability improved sharply, with net profit jumping from -USD 993 million to USD 4.98 billion
  • Year to date 2025— Revenue growth slows to about 10%, but core business profitability continues to unlock

Meituan listed in Hong Kong using a VIE (variable interest entity) structure. 2Industry analysis

2.1 Overview of China's local life services market

China's local life services market is projected to exceed RMB 25 trillion in 2025 (including food delivery, mobility, travel and lifestyle services), with online penetration at roughly 25–30% and potential to rise above 40% over the long term. According to iResearch, the food delivery market will be worth about RMB 1.2 trillion in 2025, while the instant retail (non-food) market is growing faster, with a CAGR of 30%+.

3Business model

3.1 Two-sided network effects + flywheel model

Meituan's core moat is the three-sided network effect across "supply (merchants) — fulfillment (riders) — demand (consumers)." The flywheel runs as follows: more consumers → more orders → more riders (shorter delivery times) → better user experience → more consumers; and more orders → more merchant onboarding → wider category selection → more consumers.

3.3 Cost structure

Operating costs mainly comprise: delivery costs (about 55–60% of revenue, including rider fees), bandwidth and server costs, and payment processing fees. SG&A is about 22% of revenue, and R&D about 6%. Gross margin was 38.4% in 2024, on a sustained upward trajectory (only 23.7% in 2021), reflecting the release of scale effects and operating leverage.

3.4 Competitive moat

  • Delivery network— Coverage of 4,000+ counties and districts nationwide, millions of active riders, proprietary intelligent dispatch system
  • User mindshare— Brand recognition of "Meituan for food, drink, fun and play", with MAU exceeding 400 million
  • Merchant ecosystem— More than 10 million onboarded merchants, including a large number of small and medium-sized food and beverage businesses
  • Data and AI— AI for delivery route optimization at tens of millions of orders per day, leading the industry

6Growth drivers

6.1 Instant retail (Shangou) — the second growth curve

Meituan Instashopping has expanded from fresh groceries to categories including daily necessities, digital products, beauty, maternal and baby, and pharmaceuticals. The instant retail market is expected to reach RMB 1.5 trillion by 2027 (CAGR 30%+), and Meituan holds a natural advantage through its food-delivery network. Estimated Instashopping GTV was about RMB 250 billion in 2024 and is expected to exceed RMB 500 billion by 2027.

6.2 Lower-tier market penetration

Delivery and instant retail penetration in tier-3 cities and below remains far below tier-1 and tier-2 levels. Meituan continues to expand into lower-tier markets through Meituan Select and more flexible delivery pricing, which will be the core source of incremental growth over the next 2–3 years.

6.3 Digital upgrade of in-store business

Meituan's in-store business is upgrading from a "group-buying platform" to a "digital transformation advisor for merchants." By offering SaaS tools, marketing automation and data analytics, it raises merchant stickiness and lifts monetization rates.

6.4 Overseas expansion (KeeTa)

Meituan operates its food delivery business in Hong Kong under the KeeTa brand and has captured about 30% market share. Over the medium term it may expand into Southeast Asian markets (Indonesia, Thailand and others); although overseas expansion carries higher risk, it opens up new room for growth.

6.5 AI cost reduction and efficiency gains

Meituan has deployed AI at scale across delivery dispatch, intelligent customer service and merchant operations. Delivery AI cuts per-order delivery cost by roughly 5–10%, saving several billion RMB a year and directly boosting profit.

7Risk factors

⚠ The main risk factors covered in this report are as follows:

7.1 Risk of intensifying competition (high impact)

Douyin has entered the local life services sector through short videos and livestreaming, creating substantive competition for Meituan's in-store business. Leveraging over 600 million DAU and its content distribution advantage, Douyin has captured roughly 20–25% share of the restaurant group-buying segment. Alibaba has built ecosystem synergies through Ele.me, Amap and Koubei. JD.com has strengthened its instant retail footprint through JD Daojia. Competition may compress commission rates and drive up subsidy costs.

7.2 Macroeconomic and consumption downside risks (medium-to-high impact)

China's consumption recovery has fallen short of expectations. If the consumption downgrade persists, both average order value and order frequency could come under pressure. The sharp slowdown to 2.1% growth in Q3 2025 has already partly reflected this risk.

7.3 Regulatory policy risk (medium impact)

Regulatory policy on delivery-platform rider social insurance, commission caps and data security remains uncertain. If platforms were required to pay the full five social insurances and housing fund for all riders, annualized costs could rise by several billion RMB.

7.4 Risk of losses in new businesses (medium impact)

Meituan Select and other new community e-commerce businesses remain loss-making. If competition keeps burning cash, it could weigh on overall profit growth. In 2024, the operating loss margin of new businesses was about –15% to –20%.

7.5 Founder/management risk (low-to-medium impact)

Wang Xing is highly irreplaceable at Meituan. Founder health, decision-making missteps and succession risk warrant long-term monitoring.

7.6 Valuation risk (medium impact)

Current PS is about 1.3x and FCF yield about 10.6%; the valuation sits at a historical low, reflecting the market's pessimistic pricing of growth. If Q4 results fall below expectations and no catalyst emerges, the share price could decline further.
