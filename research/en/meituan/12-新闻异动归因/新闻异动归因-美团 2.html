I. Executive Summary

Meituan's share price has fallen from its 2025 high of HKD 136.10 to the current HKD 78.35, a decline of 42%, far exceeding the pullback in the Hang Seng Tech Index over the same period. This report systematically reviews the major events affecting Meituan's share price in 2025-2026, fromPolicy and regulation, competitive landscape, shareholder behavior, technological innovation, fundamental changesAssess each event's weighting on share price through five attribution dimensions.

Core attribution conclusion:

▸ Largest drawdown driver (~35-40%): Tencent stake reduction + subsidy war erodes profit expectations + worsening competitive landscape (Douyin/JD.com entry)

▸ Moderate decline driver (~25-30%): Regulatory fine of RMB 746 million (interpreted as a market signal), growth slowdown concerns, broad-market valuation compression

▸ Positive supports (offsetting ~15-20% of the decline):LongCat-2.0 model release, 2024 annual results profit above consensus, buyback plan

▸ Sentiment amplification (~10-15%)Expectations of a macroeconomic downturn, shrinking Hong Kong market liquidity, and broad pressure on the China concept stock sector

After losing this anchor, Meituan's positioning in the capital markets was downgraded from "core Tencent-affiliated asset" to "independent platform company," and this valuation-logic reset contributed roughly10-15%valuation contraction.

V. Analysis of key attribution transmission paths

Path A: competition → profit → valuation contraction (dominant path)

Douyin in-store penetration + JD instant retail → Meituan forced into subsidy war → SG&A expenses rise (already up 8.2% YoY in 2024) → market cuts 2025/2026 margin forecasts → PE compression → share price falls

Estimated contribution: -18% to -22% (about 40-50% of the total decline)

Path B: Major shareholder exit → valuation logic reset

Tencent stake reduction/share distribution → persistent technical selling pressure + loss of strategic shareholder endorsement → market reprices Meituan as an "independent platform" → valuation center shifts down (from "Tencent-affiliation premium" back to industry average)

Estimated contribution: -10% to -15% (about 20-25% of the total decline)

Path C: Regulatory normalization → uncertainty discount

RMB 746 million fine + rider social-security debate + data-security compliance → market expectations of a "full regulatory easing" disappointed → uncertainty discount persists → investors demand a higher risk premium

Estimated contribution: -5% to -8% (about 10-15% of the total decline)

Path D: growth deceleration → growth-stock re-rating

Revenue growth slows from 20% to ~10% → the market shifts from "buying growth" to "assessing value" → PS multiple compresses from ~2x to ~1.3x → the growth-stock premium fades

Estimated contribution: -5% to -8% (about 10-15% of the total decline)

📎 Appendix: Attribution Methodology

Attribution methodology:This report uses "Event-Driven Attribution" to identify, one by one, the major events affecting Meituan's share price in 2025-2026, and combines changes in the company's fundamentals with shifts in the industry environment to assess the contribution of each factor to the share-price decline.

Data limitations:

  • Some events (such as the exact dates and tranches of Tencent's stake reductions) require checking HKEX disclosure of interests data
  • Interaction effects among factors (e.g., worsening competition plus Tencent's stake reduction amplify each other) are handled in this paper as range estimates
  • The full 2025 annual report has not yet been disclosed; some figures are based on public quarterly reports and market estimates.
  • Attribution weights are qualitative plus semi-quantitative assessments and do not constitute a precise quantitative attribution model.

Update plan:Once the 2025 annual report is disclosed, this report will be updated quantitatively to incorporate actual financial data and revise the attribution weights.
