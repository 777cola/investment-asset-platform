📖 Stage 1 · Four masterclasses in close reading

Duan Yongping's lens: Is Meituan making money? How to read the 2024 net profit turnaround

I. Core question: is Meituan actually profitable?

Duan Yongping never judges whether a company has made money by looking at the net profit figure alone. He asks three questions:What is the source of the profit? Is it sustainable? Is this business worth that profit?

Meituan's net profit reached USD 4,981M in 2024, swinging from a loss in 2022 to nearly USD 5 billion in 2024—a "great reversal" that looks inspiring. But Duan Yongping would take it apart first—not looking at the story, but at the data.

✅ Key finding: operating profit accounts for 97%, with minimal non-recurring gains. This money was "earned" by Meituan, not "calculated."

III. What is "good" and "not good enough" about the business model

The good part:High-frequency essential demand (daily opens far exceed e-commerce apps), two-sided network effects (riders → faster delivery → more users → more orders), strong cash flow (USD 6.5 billion FCF).

The parts that fall short:Model skews asset-heavy (1.1 million riders are a heavy fixed cost), pricing power is weak (users are price-sensitive), and the subsidy war never really ended (SG&A of USD 10.4 billion is still growing).

"If revenue growth falls to 5-8% and gross margin stops expanding, can net profit still grow?" — a Duan Yongping-style follow-up question

IV. Duan Yongping's judgment

"Meituan is a good company, but not a good business like Apple. Its profitability is unlocking, cash flow is strong, and valuation is reasonable—but the business model has inherent weaknesses (high fixed costs, weak pricing power, intense competition). This is not a position for a heavy allocation, but it is worth watching."

III. Core points of contention

Core controversy 1: How low are Meituan's switching costs, really?
If switching costs really are low (users chase subsidies), Meituan would need to keep burning cash—Duan Yongping and Buffett's concerns hold.
If switching costs are underestimated (user habits, data lock-in, delivery experience), Munger and Li Lu's optimistic judgment is correct.
⏳ Test criterion: whether Meituan's DAU falls significantly after it cuts subsidies.
Second key debate: is the profit release "one-off" or "sustained"?
Can the net margin of 10.6% rise to 15%+? Can the gross margin of 38.4% break through 40%? With revenue growth slowing to 8%, can net profit still grow 20%+?
⏳ Test criteria: full-year 2025 results and 2026 guidance.
Core controversy 3: Is Meituan a "cyclical stock" or a "growth stock"?
Cyclical stocks → PE fluctuates between 10-15x; growth stocks → PE can expand to 20-25x.
⏳ Test criteria: margin trajectory and revenue growth over the next 12-24 months.

📝 Stage 3 · Edit and polish for publication

Meituan Earnings In-Depth: The 2024 Profit Reversal Through the Lens of Four Masters

Edit and polish for publishable version · approx. 2,200 words

In 2024, Meituan delivered a striking set of results: net profit swung from a loss of USD 990 million in 2022 and a profit of USD 1.96 billion in 2023,Jumped to USD 4.98 billion— Completed a profit turnaround of nearly RMB 10 billion within two years. Meanwhile, free cash flow reached USD 6.54 billion, and cash reserves piled up to USD 26.1 billion.

This set of numbers raises a central question:Is Meituan's profit real? Is it sustainable?

We examine this proposition from the perspectives of four value-investing masters—Buffett, Munger, Duan Yongping and Li Lu.

I. The Authenticity of Earnings: Unanimous Confirmation from Four Masters

Four independent analyses yielded oneHighly consistent conclusion: Meituan's "great reversal" is real.

Three tests gauge earnings quality: source, cash and sustainability.

① Source: Of Meituan's 2024 net profit of USD 4.98 billion, operating profit accounted for 97%. No one-off asset sales, no accounting adjustments, no non-recurring gains.

② Cash: Operating cash flow of USD 7.95 billion far exceeds net profit. For every USD 1 of profit, USD 1.6 of cash was actually collected—earnings of exceptionally high quality.

③ Sustainability: ROE jumped from -5.1% in 2022 to 20.6%, driven by dual improvement in net margin and asset turnover, not by leverage.

"Meituan earns real cash, not accounting profits." — Duan Yongping's lens

II. Scale effects: Munger's "textbook case"

Over the past four years,Revenue grew 69% while costs rose only 36.5%. Every extra $1 of revenue costs only $0.53 to earn.

R&D expense fell from USD 3.08 billion to USD 2.93 billion (an absolute decline), and the SG&A expense ratio dropped from 27.5% to 22.1%. Fixed costs were diluted, and the marginal cost of reusing the technology platform approached zero.

Munger would call it"A textbook case of scale effect"— the kind of "gets easier as it scales" business he likes best.

But Munger also noted: the "first-stage inflection point" of scale effects (from loss to profit) has been reached, but the "second-stage inflection point" (margin sustainably rising to 15%+) has yet to arrive. In 2024, FCF/revenue rose only 1.6pp (vs a 9.5pp rise in 2023).

III. Moat Debate: Concerns of Buffett and Duan Yongping

Buffett rates Meituan's moat as "moderately wide": network effects and economies of scale are strong (⭐⭐⭐⭐), butSwitching costs are weak (⭐⭐)。

Core question: how high are switching costs for users and merchants? Answer:Almost zero for users. Use Meituan today, Ele.me tomorrow, Douyin the day after—whichever is cheaper. Users are defined by "subsidy dependence" rather than "brand loyalty."

"A good business doesn't require you to guard against competitors every day. Apple doesn't worry about users leaving, Moutai doesn't worry about distributors defecting. Meituan needs continuous subsidies to maintain scale—that is not the hallmark of a good business." — Duan Yongping

IV. Consumption modernization: Li Lu's grand narrative

Services consumption accounts for about 43% of household consumption expenditure in China, far below 68% in the US. This gap means thatStructural growth potential of a trillion-dollar market。

Meituan plays the role of "infrastructure for service e-commerce" — its "30-minute living circle" meets urban consumers' 1-2 immediate needs per day, an order of magnitude more frequent than physical-goods e-commerce.

USD 26.1 billion in cash, USD 6.54 billion in free cash flow, net cash position — Meituan's financial base gives it the capacity to keep investing over a 10-year-plus horizon.

"This is not a cycle story—it is the narrative of an era." — Li Lu

It may not be the ultimate "hold for life" target (switching costs are its Achilles' heel), but at the current valuation it offers asymmetric risk-reward: net cash cushions the downside, while profit release and valuation repair provide twin upside.

Track key indicators:① Whether full-year 2025 revenue growth can hold at 8%; ② whether SG&A expenses begin to decline; ③ changes in Meituan's user retention rate after it cuts subsidies.
