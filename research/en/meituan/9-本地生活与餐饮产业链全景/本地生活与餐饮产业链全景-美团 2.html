📋 Report summary

As China's largest local life services platform, Meituan spans multiple industry chains including food delivery, in-store dining, hotels and travel, and instant retail. This report takes a full industry-chain perspective to systematically map Meituan's positioning, value-creation mechanisms, profit-sharing patterns and future evolution across each chain.

1️⃣ The full local life services industry chain

1.1 Industry chain overview

Demand side → Traffic gateway → Platform matching → Supply side → Fulfillment and delivery → After-sales service

Meituan's local life services industry chain can be summarized as a "six-layer model":

TierSegmentParticipantsMeituan's role
L1Demand side (consumers) C-end users (600 million+ annual active users) App/mini-program entry points, recommendation algorithm, membership system (Meituan Membership/Shen Membership)
L2Traffic gateway Meituan app, Dianping app, WeChat Pay grid, mini programs Super-app ecosystem + WeChat traffic partnership
L3Platform matching Meituan platform (algorithmic matching, intelligent dispatch, review system) Core platform: information matching + transaction guarantee + credit system
L4Supply side (merchants) Restaurants (9 million+), hotels (~1.4 million), retail stores, scenic spots, etc. SaaS systems (Meituan Butler/Quick Cat Purchasing), advertising placement, traffic allocation
L5Fulfillment and delivery Meituan food-delivery riders (~7 million registered riders), Meituan's delivery network Self-built instant delivery network (Meituan Delivery), open platform (crowdsourcing + dedicated delivery)
L6After-sales service Customer service system, refunds/complaints, review system Meituan customer service + Dianping review system

1.2 Industry chain value flow

Direction of value flow across the entire industry chain:

  • Capital flows: Consumer → platform (Meituan) → merchant (85-90%) + rider (delivery fee) → Meituan's retained share (commission + advertising)
  • Information flow: User reviews → platform algorithms → merchant operating decisions → service improvement → word-of-mouth
  • Logistics: Merchant/warehouse → distribution center/rider → consumer

2.4 Challenges facing food delivery

  • Rider social insurance complianceMulti-platform flexible employment policy is tightening; if social insurance contributions for delivery riders are made mandatory, per-order costs could rise by RMB 1-3.
  • Antitrust regulation:After "either-or" exclusivity was banned, merchants operating across multiple platforms became the norm, and competition between Ele.me and Douyin Waimai for quality merchants intensified.
  • Delivery efficiency bottleneck: Average delivery time per order has been compressed to 30-40 minutes, with limited room for further optimization

3.3 In-store business industry chain characteristics

  • High-margin cash cowIn-store business is Meituan's profit engine, contributing a share of profit far exceeding its share of revenue. A pure platform matchmaking model requires no delivery network and carries extremely low marginal costs.
  • Dianping's moat: Dianping's accumulation of hundreds of millions of genuine user reviews forms a data moat that is hard to replicate. Although Douyin has disrupted the in-store market through short videos, it will struggle to surpass the depth and credibility of Dianping's review system in the near term.
  • Douyin disruption: Douyin is disrupting the in-store market with a "content-as-advertising" model; in 2024, Douyin's in-store GMV exceeded USD 30 billion, creating direct competition for Meituan's in-store business. However, Meituan's countermeasures (livestreaming plus influencer store visits) are narrowing the gap.
  • Hotels and tourismMeituan entered via mid- to low-end hotels (tier-3 and tier-4 cities) and is gradually moving up into the mid- to high-end segment. In 2024, hotel room nights continued to grow, but average spend per booking was below Ctrip's. Within the industry chain, Meituan connects small and mid-sized hotels and homestays.

4.5 Industry-chain characteristics of Meituan Select

  • Supply chain: Direct sourcing from origin + central warehouse + grid warehouse + group leader pickup point — too many layers
  • Earnings dilemma: Community group buying has extremely low gross margins (<10%），履约成本高，多数市场参与者亏损
  • Meituan adjustments: Continually narrowing loss-making regions in 2024-2025, focusing on high-density cities, and shifting toward "quality group buying"
  • Competition: Pinduoduo (Duoduo Maicai) has better cost control in community group buying, while Meituan Select ranks second by market share
💡 Key insight:Instant retail is the core of Meituan's "boundaryless retail" strategy—extending food-delivery capacity to "everything to the door." Meituan Instashopping's GMV growth (~40%) far outpaces food delivery (~10%), making it the next growth engine. Xiaoxiang Supermarket (front warehouse) is Meituan's core weapon against Hema and Dingdong Maicai in fresh groceries.   • Localized operations: Saudi dining habits (Ramadan, restrictions on women) and differences in Brazil's logistics infrastructure require deep localization
  • Capital consumption: Overseas expansion requires heavy subsidy spending, which will weigh on the financial statements in the near term.
  • Regulatory risk: Middle East labor law, Brazil's tax system, data localization requirements, etc.
💡 Core insight:Overseas expansion is Meituan's strategic move to find a second growth curve. The local food-delivery markets in Saudi Arabia and Brazil are not yet consolidated, giving Meituan a chance to replicate the model it has perfected in China. But overseas expansion carries high uncertainty, and its revenue contribution should not be overestimated in the near term (overseas revenue share in 2025 is expected to<3%）。

7️⃣ Supply-chain trends and Meituan's influence

7.1 Five major trends reshaping the industry chain

Trend 1: From "food delivery" to "everything to the home"

Meituan is extending food delivery's instant-delivery capability to all categories (pharmaceuticals, 3C, beauty, alcohol and more). The industry-chain implications of this trend:

  • Category boundaries blur: The line between traditional e-commerce and local retail is blurring, and "30-minute delivery" has become the new standard
  • Physical warehouse network restructuringBrands need to build a nationwide "forward warehouse" network rather than relying solely on central warehouse shipping
  • Meituan's changing role: Upgrading from a food-delivery platform to "local retail infrastructure", becoming a new channel for brands to reach consumers

Trend two: AI reshapes supply-chain efficiency

  • Intelligent scheduling: AI algorithms optimize rider routes, cutting empty-running rates (10%→5%) and reducing delivery cost per order by RMB 0.5-1.
  • Smart recommendations: Personalized search/recommendation boosts conversion (expected up 10-20%)
  • Unmanned deliveryAutonomous driving + robotic delivery (piloted in selected areas in 2025), which over the long term can significantly reduce reliance on manpower
  • Smart pricing: Dynamic pricing model optimizes peak-and-off-peak order distribution and raises capacity utilization

Trend 3: Merchant ecosystem shifts from "traffic dependence" to "digital operations"

  • Meituan's shift from a traffic platform to a "merchant digitalization assistant": SaaS (Meituan Guanjia) + supply chain (Kuailv Jinhuo) + finance (Meituan Shengyidai)
  • Merchants' stickiness to Meituan extends from "customer acquisition" to "full-chain operations", deepening its moat
  • Kuailv Jinhuo (B2B food-ingredient supply chain) posted GMV of about USD 8-10 billion in 2024 and is gaining market share

Trend 4: Policy and regulation reshape profit distribution

  • Rider rights: Occupational injury protection (piloted from 2022) and the trend toward social security compliance shift costs from platforms to the supply side
  • Commission transparency: Regulators require platforms to clearly separate technology service fees from delivery fees, strengthening merchants' bargaining power
  • Antitrust enforcement becomes routineAfter the "choose one of two" practice was banned, multi-platform operation became the norm, and Meituan needs to retain merchants through service rather than forced exclusivity.

Trend 5: Lower-tier markets and the silver economy

  • Delivery penetration in tier-3 and tier-4 cities and rural areas remains low (<30%），是长期增长空间
  • Local-life demand among the silver generation (60+) remains underdeveloped: senior-friendly apps, large-print editions and community services
  • Meituan Select's "next-day pickup" model has unique value in lower-tier markets (low prices plus trust in community group leaders)

8️⃣ Strategic summary: Meituan's investment value from an industry-chain perspective

✅ Core advantages (supply-chain perspective)
  • Strong two-sided network effectsThe more consumers, the more merchants; the more merchants, the higher delivery density; the higher delivery density, the better the experience; the better the experience, the more consumers. A positive flywheel has formed.
  • Infrastructure-type platform: Meituan has become the "water, electricity and gas" infrastructure of China's local life services, with extremely high replacement costs
  • Economies of scope: Food delivery, flash shopping and Xiaoxiang share one delivery network, with marginal costs declining at an accelerating rate
  • Strong profit engine: In-store/hotel-travel/advertising form a high-margin profit pool that funds early-stage investment in food delivery and overseas expansion
⚠️ Core weaknesses (supply-chain perspective)
  • The profitability challenge of food deliveryRigid delivery costs plus competitive pressure limit per-order profit upside
  • Core business growth slows: Food-delivery penetration is nearing its ceiling in tier-1 and tier-2 cities, and 9.4% revenue growth can hardly support a high valuation
  • In-store moat under erosionDouyin's aggressive content push puts Dianping's "review moat" under pressure in the short-video era
  • New businesses still need timeInstant retail, overseas and Select all require sustained investment; the profit inflection point is uncertain
