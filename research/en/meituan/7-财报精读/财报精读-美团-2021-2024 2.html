10. Risks and Outlook

10.1 Principal risks

  1. Growth stall risk: Q3 2025 YoY growth was only +2.1%; a continued decline would trigger a valuation re-rating. The market is divided on whether the combination of "slowing growth + improving profit" can support the current valuation.
  2. Risk of intensifying competition: Douyin keeps investing in in-store services, while Alibaba and JD.com ramp up instant retail, potentially forcing Meituan to raise subsidies and squeezing its profit margins.
  3. New business loss riskMeituan Select and other community group-buying businesses remain loss-making; if loss reduction lags expectations, it will drag on overall margins.
  4. Macroeconomic riskConsumer trade-down and employment pressure could weigh on food-delivery average order value and in-store visit frequency.
  5. Regulatory risk: Social-security and other labor-protection policies for food-delivery riders may raise labor costs and squeeze delivery margins.

10.2 Positive outlook

  1. There is still room for profit release: The current 10.6% net margin remains in the lower-middle range among mature internet platforms (versus ~25% for Tencent and ~15% for Alibaba), leaving room for improvement.
  2. New businesses set to narrow losses: After multiple rounds of adjustment, Meituan Select's losses are expected to narrow sharply in 2025-2026.
  3. Shareholder return potential: USD 20.2 billion in net cash plus USD 6.5 billion in annual FCF gives ample firepower for large-scale buybacks. With the stock well below its 52-week high, buybacks are likely to ramp up.
  4. Overseas expansion opportunitiesKeeta (the Hong Kong food-delivery business) is a testing ground for overseas expansion; if the model proves out, long-term growth headroom will reopen.
🎯 Investment conclusion:Meituan is the undisputed leader in China's local life services sector, having completed a strategic transformation from "loss-making expansion" to "profitable growth" over 2021-2024. The current valuation (PS 1.3x, PE 12-15x) sits at a low level among technology platforms, with the market mainly concerned about slowing growth and competitive pressure. For investors willing to accept mid-to-low growth and who value cash flow quality and margin of safety, Meituan is attractive. Key metrics to watch: ① whether full-year 2025 revenue growth holds at 8-10% ② whether the SG&A expense ratio continues to improve ③ the margin trend in the in-store business ④ the scale of share buybacks.
