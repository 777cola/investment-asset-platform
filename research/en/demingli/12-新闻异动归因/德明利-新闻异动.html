Should NAND prices soften, the company's gross margin would fall back quickly and the logic behind its earnings surge would collapse overnight. Such "cycle peak" concerns intensified in July.

⚠️ The most fatal contradiction

Demingli's core contradiction:The company's earnings explosion depends entirely on the NAND price upcycle. During the upcycle, the company uses high leverage to stockpile inventory (borrowing to buy inventory), capturing a huge spread in gross margin (14.8% in 2025 → 57.42% in Q1 2026). But this model reverses sharply in a downcycle — high-priced inventory faces massive impairment, gross margin may turn negative, and net profit will post a huge loss.

Profit quality is extremely poor:Net profit in 2026Q1 was RMB 3.346 billion, but operating cash flow turned negative (-RMB 240 million), meaning more than 90% of profit is "paper profit" (inventory appreciation and accounts receivable). Once NAND prices stop rising or fall, this paper profit will evaporate quickly.

Timing-sensitive:The July 23 lock-up expiry and the Q2 interim report (due by end-August) fall squarely in a sensitive window where the NAND cycle may be peaking. The market's choice to flee ahead of the expiry reflects its avoidance of the dual risk of "cycle peak + lock-up selling".

Three key judgments

1️⃣ The limit-down is a "belated valuation correction", not a black swan
Demingli trades at 18x PB, up 160% in 3 months, with negative operating cash flow—these signals alone show the share price has decoupled sharply from fundamentals. The limit-down is more the start of a valuation reversion than a one-off black swan event.

2️⃣ The July 23 lock-up expiry is the key window to watch
Actual selling after the lock-up expiry will determine the near-term direction: if major shareholders commit to no reduction or sell only a minimal amount, sentiment may recover; if a large reduction is announced, the share price may remain under pressure. Track closely.

3️⃣ The interim report (due by end-August) matters more than the lock-up expiry
The 2026 Q2 interim report will provide the most critical validation data: ① Does gross margin still hold above 50%? ② Does operating cash flow turn positive? ③ Do inventories keep climbing? ④ Does revenue growth slow? If the interim report falls short of expectations, post-lockup selling pressure will compound with deteriorating fundamentals, producing a "Davis double-kill".
