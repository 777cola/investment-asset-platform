Demingli: Three fatal signals behind the RMB 3.3 billion net profit

2026-07-19 ·Demingli (001309.SZ)·About 5 minutes read
✦ ✦ ✦

In July 2026, Demingli delivered results that turned heads across the entire A-share market—Net profit of RMB 3.346 billion in the first quarter of 2026, surging 4,943% year on year. Gross margin jumped from 14.8% a year earlier to 57.4%, net margin reached 44.4%, and the forward PE is just 9x. Brokerage research reports are clustered with "Buy" ratings, and the stock ranks fourth across the entire A-share market in retail popularity.

On the surface, this is a chip company in the midst of hypergrowth: revenue grew from RMB 1.77 billion to RMB 10.79 billion in three years (the annualized Q1 figure has already surged to the RMB 30 billion level), net profit swung from a loss to RMB 3.3 billion in a single quarter, and ROE stands at 67%. Every data point points to—Demingli may be the most undervalued memory chip leader in A-shares。

But a truly good business can withstand the scrutiny of cash flow. And Demingli exposes three fatal signals on exactly this point.

1Signal 1: Paper net profit, cash flow deficit

In the first quarter of 2026, Demingli reported net profit of RMB 3.346 billion on its income statement. But open its cash flow statement and you will find a startling fact—Operating cash flow for the same period was -RMB 240.8 million。

This is not a one-quarter anomaly. For the full year 2025, the company's net profit was RMB 688 million, while operating cash flow was-22.41 billionOver a longer horizon, the company's operating cash flow per share has been negative every year since listing: RMB -7.81 in 2024, RMB -9.88 in 2025 and RMB -1.06 in Q1 2026.

The income statement looks impressive; the cash flow statement is deep in the red. Profits are not turning into cash—they are flowing into inventory.

What does this mean? Profit is a paper accounting figure, while cash flow is the true pulse of a business. A principle Buffett has stressed repeatedly: "If a company grows profit for years while operating cash flow stays persistently negative, it is likely manufacturing the illusion of profit through credit sales or inventory build-up." Demingli's profit growth does rest on genuine industry demand (the NAND price-upcycle), but the severe lag in cash collection exposes its bargaining position in the supply chain—Upstream needs cash to buy wafers, but downstream cannot take delivery for cash.。

2Signal two: RMB 12.2 billion inventory overhang, a gray rhino

Demingli's Q1 2026 report shows:Inventory balance RMB 12.19 billion, accounting for 66% of total assets of RMB 18.38 billion. In other words, of every 3 yuan of the company's assets, 2 yuan sit in the warehouse as chips and modules.

This is a staggering ratio. To grasp the weight of this figure, we can view it against several reference points:

First, Demingli's full-year 2025 revenue was only RMB 10.79 billion, while its inventory has already reached RMB 12.19 billion—Inventory exceeds a full year of revenueAt the 2026Q1 revenue run rate (RMB 7.5 billion per quarter), inventory turnover days reach 270, equivalent to a 9.0-month wait from purchase to sale.

Second, the company's total shareholders' equity in 2026Q1 was RMB 6.62 billion, while inventory stood at RMB 12.19 billion—All shareholder capital contributions combined are still not enough to cover inventory.If memory chip prices fall 30%, inventory impairment could theoretically reach RMB 3.66 billion, wiping out more than half of the company's net assets in one hit.

Core contradiction in Demingli's balance sheet

Shareholders' equity:RMB 6.62 billion

Interest-bearing debt:RMB 5.787 billion

Inventories equal 184% of shareholders' equity. A 20% fall in memory prices would wipe out a third of shareholders' equity.

Third, looking back at history—Demingli's gross margin hovered between 14% and 17% from 2023 to 2025, and only jumped to 57% in Q1 2026 as NAND Flash prices surged. That 57% gross margin,This reflects a one-off windfall from the memory chip price upcycle, not sustained competitiveness.Cyclical analysts know that NAND Flash prices swing violently, with a 30%-50% decline typically following each cycle peak. Once the price inflection point arrives, Demingli will not only face a cliff-edge collapse in gross margin but also absorb massive inventory impairment losses.

This is not hypothetical—in the first half of 2025, the company's gross margin fell to 5.03% and its net margin to -2.87%, precisely during the trough in memory prices.A surge and a crash are two sides of the same coin。

3Signal three: borrowing to stockpile inventory, a high-leverage gamble

If profits exist only on paper and inventory hangs overhead, where does the money keeping the company running come from? There is only one answer—Borrowing money。

At the end of Q1 2026, Demingli had short-term borrowings of RMB 3.742 billion and long-term borrowings of RMB 2.045 billion, for total interest-bearing debt of about RMB 5.787 billion. Its debt-to-asset ratio stood at 64%, close to the warning line.

More important is the structure of the cash flow statement: for full-year 2025, operating cash flow was -RMB 2.241 billion and investing cash flow was -RMB 546 million, whileFinancing cash flow +RMB 2.576 billionIn 2026Q1, operating cash flow remained at -RMB 241 million, while financing cash flow continued at +RMB 1.45 billion. The company's operating model is disturbingly clear—Weak cash generation from operations; survival depends entirely on external financing。

Without capital raising to inject funds, the company's cash would have run out long ago.

This is not the financial structure a "good business" should have. Truly good businesses, such as Moutai, Apple and TSMC, generate operating cash flow ample enough to cover investment needs and still pay large dividends and buy back stock.Demingli is the exact opposite—it must keep raising external capital to plug the double drain from operations and investment.。

If NAND prices keep rising, Demingli's high leverage gives shareholders enormous upside elasticity (this is also the core of the current bullish case); but conversely, if memory prices reverse, the company will face three overlapping risks at once: a collapse in gross margin, inventory write-downs, and debt-servicing pressure. This "triple kill" risk has been seen repeatedly in A-share history.

Why is no one telling the truth?

Readers who have made it this far may ask: if the risks are so obvious, why do brokerage research reports still uniformly say "Buy"? Why does the market keep chasing it?

The reason is simple—At the peak of the cycle for cyclical stocks, the income statement is always the most persuasive storytelling tool.RMB 3.3 billion net profit, 57% gross margin, 9x forward PE — impressive enough numbers to make most investors overlook the warning signs buried in the cash flow statement and balance sheet.

Storage chips are also the hottest AI theme right now, and the overlapping narratives of "enterprise SSD volume ramp," "domestic substitution" and "AI data center demand" have built a story investors find hard to resist. And brokerage analysts rarely publish "sell" ratings when a stock is surging.

📌 Conclusion: don't rush to "get on board"

Demingli is not a bad company, but its current price and financial structure do not suit risk-averse investors.

If you believe the memory-chip super-cycle has more than two years to run, if you can stomach drawdowns of more than 50%, and if you are not overly sensitive to cash-flow metrics—plenty of people in the market are willing to take that bet alongside you.

But if you believe in the basic principles of value investing:Profit must be backed by cash, assets must be realizable, and liabilities must carry a margin of safety, then Demingli's three fatal signals tell you this is not the time to buy.

Recommend waiting for two validation milestones:① 2026 Interim Report, and see whether gross margin can stay above 30% and operating cash flow turns positive;② Inflection point in the memory chip price cycle, and reassess at a lower price once inventory clears and the downside price risk has been released.
