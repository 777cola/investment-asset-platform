VI. List of key issues

1. How did the 44% net margin come about?

Several possible explanations:

  • ① NAND price-hike cycle: Memory chip prices surge in Q1 2026, Demingli profits from buying low and selling high
  • ② Inventory appreciation: Stockpiling at low prices earlier and selling at high prices this quarter released a one-off gain
  • ③ In-house controller shipments ramp up: In-house controller chips account for a larger revenue share, lifting overall gross margin
  • ④ Large contract: Single large-customer customized high-margin order
The evidence points to:Scenario ①+② is the most likely, given negative operating cash flow (profits not yet converted to cash) and gross margin jumping so sharply from 14.8% in 2025 to 57%.

2. Is this margin sustainable?

  • If driven mainly by a price-hike cycle → low probability of persisting (cycle peak)
  • If driven mainly by product-mix upgrade (higher enterprise SSD share + in-house controllers) → partly sustainable
  • Key validation point: 2026 interim results(by end-August) — watch whether gross margin stays above 30%

3. How should we interpret "net profit of RMB 3.3 billion vs operating cash flow of -RMB 240 million"?

  • Net profit is accrual-based (revenue recognized on delivery), but the cash has not come back
  • Inventories rose from RMB 7.058 billion to RMB 12.19 billion (+RMB 5.1 billion) → all the money earned turned into inventory
  • This shows the company is stockpiling heavily to bet on price increases—if the bet pays off, everyone is happy; if it goes wrong, huge impairments follow.

Nine, Final conclusion

Demingli is one of the most "polarized" names in the A-share market right now: extreme growth (revenue up 6x in 3 years, Q1 net margin 44%) coexists with extreme risk (inventory of RMB 12.2 billion, persistently negative operating cash flow, PB of 18x, static PE of 177x).

Recommendation: Hold

  • 🟢 If bullish:2026Q1 proved the business's explosive growth; if NAND price increases continue and the Q2 interim results confirm high margins, the current dynamic PE of ~9x is indeed undervalued
  • 🔴 If bearish:Negative operating cash flow + RMB 12.2 billion in inventory + 18x PB + the NAND cycle may be at its peak; a double hit to earnings and share price is possible in H2 2026
Key validation point: interim results at the end of August 2026— Does gross margin remain >30%? Has operating cash flow turned positive? Have inventories started to decline?
