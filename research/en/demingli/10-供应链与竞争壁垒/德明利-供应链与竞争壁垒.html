Overall moat assessment: narrow moat — moderately weak.
In-house controllers are a differentiation highlight, but in the NAND Flash module industry, upstream original manufacturers' pricing power and cyclical swings determine the fate of most module makers. Demingli has performed well in the industry upcycle (2025-2026), but has yet to prove itself inDownturnIt can sustain profitability above that of peers.
📊 Supply-chain SWOT analysis

💪 Strengths

  • Proprietary controller technology, higher value added across the supply chain
  • 2026Q1 gross margin 57.4%, far above pure module makers
  • Revenue of RMB 10.8 billion gives it some bargaining power in procurement at scale
  • Overseas channels account for 64% of export sales, with global footprint taking shape

⚠️ Weaknesses

  • Largest single supplier at 34.17%, overly concentrated
  • No strategic partnership with any NAND manufacturer
  • Exports at 64% face dual geopolitical and FX risks
  • Weak brand power and low customer stickiness

🚀 Opportunities

  • Possibility of domestic substitution for YMTC
  • Expanding into the enterprise/automotive-grade memory market
  • AI server memory demand surges
  • Downstream localization substitution trend

🔴 Threats

  • Downside risk at the storage chip cycle inflection (1.5 years into a 3-4 year upcycle)
  • China-US tech decoupling intensifies, overseas supply chains hit
  • Competitors Jiangbolong and Baiwei Storage are catching up
  • NAND makers push into the downstream module market

🔍 Overall conclusion

Supply chain resilience rating: ⚠️ Moderately weak

Competitive moat strength: ⚠️ Narrow moat

Core differentiating variable:Proprietary controller technology + large-scale procurement

Biggest vulnerability:Single-supplier concentration 34.17% + exports 64% + inventory RMB 12.19 billion (66% of total assets)

Testing the downcycle:Not yet disproven; module makers have historically tended to be the first to suffer in NAND price-decline cycles

Core view:Demingli's proprietary controller technology provides the most differentiated competitive moat among A-share module makers, inUpturnamplified gross-margin elasticity (57.4% in 2026Q1). However, two structural concerns linger at the supply-chain level:① Over-concentration of suppliers (single supplier at 34%) + ② massive inventory at 66% of inventory/total assetsOnce the memory cycle turns down, these two risks could erupt at the same time—a double blow of inventory write-downs and gross margin compression. Its current competitive moat is not yet strong enough to keep it insulated during a downturn.
