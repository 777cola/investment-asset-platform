When the NAND price cycle reverses, Demingli's risk path is very clear:
NAND prices fall → inventory write-downs → profit plunges → operating cash flow deteriorates further → debt-servicing pressure rises → share price slumps
During the memory downcycle of 2022-2023, gross margins at several memory module makers fell from over 30% to single digits, with inventory write-downs running into the billions.

3. The "9x dynamic PE" trap

⚠️ The current dynamic PE of only ~9x looks cheap, but it rests on the aggressive assumption that the 57% gross margin is sustainable.
If NAND prices fall 30% → gross margin reverts to the industry average of 25% → net profit could drop from an annualized RMB 13.4 billion to RMB 2-3 billion → dynamic PE instantly rises to 40-60x.
For cyclical stocks, a "low PE" is often a danger signal at the cycle top, not a value signal.
