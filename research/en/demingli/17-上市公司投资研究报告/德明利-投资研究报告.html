Company overview

Shenzhen Demingling Technology Co., Ltd. (ticker: 001309.SZ) is a leading Chinese provider of memory chip modules and solutions. Its core business spans the R&D and design, packaging and testing, and module production and sales of NAND Flash and DRAM memory chips, with products widely used in consumer electronics, data centers, industrial control, automotive electronics and other fields.

Industry analysis

Demingli operates in the memory chip industry, which has a typical dual nature of "cyclicality plus growth." In the short term, memory chip prices fluctuate cyclically, driven by supply-side dynamics; in the long term, structural growth comes from AI storage demand, domestic substitution and the data explosion.

2.3 Domestic substitution: a long-term structural dividend

China is the world's largest memory chip consumer market (~30% share), yet its self-sufficiency rate is only ~5-8%. Driven by the national strategy of "self-reliance and controllability," domestic memory module and controller chip companies face a decade-long golden window for substitution. Demingli, as one of the few domestic companies with full-process NAND Flash module capability, benefits directly from this trend.

Key takeaways
The memory chip industry is on a long-term growth path driven by two engines: structural AI demand and domestic substitution. In the near term, however, watch for the risk of memory chip prices falling from cyclical highs. If NAND Flash prices correct in 2026, Demingli's gross margin and net profit will come under significant pressure.

3.2 Synergies of the "two-wheel drive"

Distribution businessIt is the company's "cash cow" and "customer gateway" — through large-scale chip distribution (revenue of ~RMB 6 billion in 2025), the company has built partnerships with hundreds of downstream customers while accumulating procurement bargaining power with upstream original manufacturers.

Module manufacturingIt is the company's "profit center" — performing secondary processing on distributed wafers/dies to raise added value. A typical "distribution acquires customers → modules convert them" funnel model: the conversion rate from distribution customers to module customers is ~15-20%.

Customized solutionsIt is the future "growth flywheel" — enterprise SSDs and industrial-control storage carry the highest gross margins and the strongest customer stickiness, but also the highest barriers to entry (qualification cycles, firmware development capability).

Core business model risks
Demingli's profit depends heavily on "memory chip price changes" — in upcycles gross margin and revenue surge, while in downcycles inventory write-downs and collapsing gross margin quickly erode profit. The extreme swing from a 14.8% gross margin in 2025 to 57.4% in Q1 2026 is clear evidence. This reflects not operating capability but the beta of the industry cycle.

Core competitive risk
Demingli occupies a "middleman" position—squeezed above by the original manufacturers' price cycle and below by peer price wars. In the competitive landscape, module makers have limited room to differentiate: products are highly commoditized, switching costs are low, and competition ultimately comes down to supply-chain efficiency, scale and cost control. Whether the 57.4% gross margin in Q1 2026 is sustainable is the key to judging whether the company's competitiveness can change qualitatively.

Risk factors

The risk matrix Demingli currently faces centres on "inventory impairment" and "cycle reversal" — should these two risks resonate, profit could reverse sharply. The following ranks them by severity.

8.1 Inventory impairment risk (🔴 Very high)

  • Inventories RMB 12.19 billion:Exceeds full-year 2025 revenue (RMB 10.79 billion) and is 17.7 times net profit
  • Stockpiling at the cycle peak:The company stocked up heavily when memory chip prices were high; if prices turn downward, impairment losses could reach RMB 1-3 billion.
  • Inventory turnover days >200 days:Far above the 90-day level considered healthy for the industry, indicating low inventory turnover efficiency
  • Impairment sensitivity estimate:If memory chip prices fall 20%, inventory impairment would be about RMB 2.4 billion, enough to cover 3.5 times full-year 2025 net profit

8.2 Memory chip cycle reversal risk (🔴 Extremely high)

  • Cycle positioning:The memory chip upcycle is likely in its mid-to-late stage, with price correction pressure looming in 2026H2–2027
  • Original manufacturer restarts production:Samsung/SK hynix/Micron have gradually restored capacity; rising supply will weigh on prices
  • Demand-side uncertainty:Durability of consumer electronics recovery in doubt; AI memory demand strong but still a limited share
  • Historical pattern:Memory chips run in full cycles of every 3-4 years; the last trough was in 2023H1, and the upcycle has lasted more than 2 years

8.3 Cash flow depletion risk (🔴 High)

  • Operating cash flow remains negative:2025: -RMB 2.241 billion; 2026Q1: -RMB 240 million; total: -RMB 2.48 billion
  • Funding chain heavily reliant on financing:If banks tighten credit or memory chip prices fall and inventory cannot be liquidated, cash flow could break down
  • Accounts receivable and inventory tie up substantial liquidity

8.4 Single-supplier/customer dependence (🟡 Medium-high)

  • Upstream NAND Flash is highly concentrated (CR5>95%), leaving the company with little bargaining power
  • If China-US trade friction disrupts the supply chain (e.g., further sanctions on YMTC), it could affect supply stability.

8.5 Valuation risk (🟡 medium-high)

  • PB 18.37x：For a distribution plus asset-light module company, the PB valuation sits at an extremely high level
  • PE (TTM) 29.66x vs forward PE 9x:The huge gap reflects extreme market skepticism about the sustainability of 2026Q1 net profit
  • If the net margin normalizes in Q2–Q4 2026 (10-15%), full-year net profit could fall well below the annualized level

11.2 Investment thesis statement

Demingli is a high-beta A-share play in memory chip modules, benefiting from three structural trends: the AI memory demand surge, domestic substitution, and product mix upgrading. We recommend investing within a "cyclical stock framework" rather than a "growth stock framework", tracking memory chip price trends and inventory digestion progress as the core indicators.
