💡 Investment implications
1. Demingli is a "leveraged cyclical bet"— Looks like the lowest PE, but is in fact the highest risk. Profit depends on NAND price increases plus inventory stockpiling; once the cycle reverses, profit collapses faster than peers. PB of 18x shows the market has already fully priced in the "optimistic scenario".

2. Longsys offers "moderate risk plus scale advantage"— Module leader, deeply tied to SK hynix, positive operating cash flow. Weaknesses: low gross margin, limited technical barriers. Less upside leverage than Demingli in a memory bull market, but more resilient in a bear market.

3. Biwin Storage is a "high-beta small cap"— The embedded storage segment has a structural growth story (IoT/industrial control), but operating cash flow is also negative, and it is small in scale with poor liquidity.

4. Netac Technology is a defensive play— Lightest assets (inventory 35%), lowest liabilities (30%), stable cash flow, but a shrinking business and an extremely high PE—essentially a "value trap."

Conclusion:The memory module industry currently sits at a cyclical high.Demingli has the worst risk-reward ratio(The market has already priced in a perfect scenario). If forced to choose,LongsysIt is the most balanced choice among the four—but are there better memory investment targets? (e.g., holding Samsung/SK hynix directly, or waiting for the cycle bottom before entering)
