I. Definition of the Core Thesis

📌 Core assumptions (investment thesis)
The 44% net margin in 2026Q1 stemmed from the memory price upcycle; if the NAND/DRAM price cycle turns down, the thesis breaks down
As a memory module maker (Flash + DRAM), Demingli's profit does not come from an intrinsic competitive advantage but from passively benefiting from the memory chip price upcycle. Once the upcycle ends and prices fall back, gross and net margins will revert to normal (~15%/6%), and the current valuation framework will collapse.
Paper dimensionContent
🧠 Core thesis Demingli is an amplifier of the NAND/DRAM price-upcycle: stockpiling, then price hikes, then high gross margins, then an earnings explosion. But this is not a moat; it is a cyclical tailwind.
💰 Profit source breakdown Net profit in 2026Q1 was RMB 3.346 billion, of which roughly RMB 3 billion+ came from gross-margin expansion driven by memory price increases (operating cost of only RMB 3.210 billion vs. revenue of RMB 7.538 billion, a gross margin of 57.4%). Full-year 2025 gross margin was just 14.8%, with a net margin of 6.4%.
🏭 Business essence Memory module distributors/solution providers — buy NAND Flash/DRAM wafers upstream, then package, test and sell under their own brand downstream. No own fabs, no core technical moat; profit from memory chip price spreads.
🔄 Cyclical sensitivity Very high. Memory chips (NAND/DRAM) are a highly cyclical segment, historically going through a price cycle every 2-3 years. Demingli's profit elasticity far exceeds that of memory IDMs (such as Samsung, Micron and YMTC).
⚠️ Biggest risk Inventory write-downs. Inventory of RMB 12.19 billion (66% of total assets); a 10% fall in memory prices would trigger write-down losses of about RMB 1.2 billion+, enough to wipe out a single quarter's profit.

V. Analysis of the thesis breakdown path

💥 Scenario analysis of thesis breakdown

  1. Trigger event:TrendForce reports monthly NAND contract prices flat/down more than 3% month-on-month (July-August 2026), the first clear signal that the memory cycle has peaked.
  2. Market reaction:The A-share memory sector corrected broadly, with Demingli's high-beta profile magnifying its decline (a beneficiary of memory price hikes turned victim of the cyclical cooldown).
  3. Interim results shock:The August 2026 interim report shows gross margin falling below 35%, net profit down quarter on quarter from Q1 (RMB 5 billion to <RMB 3 billion), and operating cash flow still negative.
  4. Inventory panic:Self-fulfilling price-decline expectations: original manufacturers cut prices → customers wait and see → Demingli shipments slow → inventory turnover days rise → writedowns become unavoidable. A 5% writedown on RMB 12.19 billion of inventory equals a RMB 610 million loss; a 10% writedown equals a RMB 1.22 billion loss.
  5. Credit contraction:Banks/suppliers see falling profits plus inventory write-downs and cut credit lines. RMB 5.787 billion of interest-bearing debt faces refinancing risk. Receivables turnover deteriorates.
  6. Profit cliff:Gross margin fell from 57% back to 15%, with revenue declining in tandem (memory price hikes gone, volumes stuck). Annualized net profit fell from ~RMB 13.4 billion (Q1×4) back to ~RMB 500-700 million (2025 level), down 95%+.
  7. Valuation collapse:PE expanded from 9x (earnings falling faster), or from 9x to a loss with no PE. PB of 18x is unsustainable at the cycle bottom. The stock is down 80-90% from its high above RMB 500, searching for a bottom (back to reasonable valuation at the cycle trough).
🚨 Warning thresholds (harbingers of thesis breakdown)
① NAND contract prices fell 2.0% month-on-month for two consecutive months→ Cycle inflection confirmed, yellow light triggered
2) Interim report gross margin <35%→ Price-hike dividend fades sharply, triggering amber light
3) Interim report operating cash flow still negative→ Persistent profit padding triggers red flag
④ Inventory impairment in a single quarter > RMB 500 million→ Cyclical downturn accelerates, investment thesis collapses
⑤ Announcement of reduction by major shareholder after lock-up expiry→ Insider signal, conviction in the thesis wavers

AI demand keeps NAND/DRAM in persistent shortage, holding memory prices elevated through 2027. Demingli's full-year 2026 net profit ~RMB 10-12 billion, with gross margin sustained at 35-45%.

But:Even in the optimistic scenario, can operating cash flow turn positive? Will inventory keep swelling? The cycle will end one day.
Valuation:PE 10-12x, fair market cap RMB 120-150 billion; the current price already reflects most expectations.

Core assumption: the price-hike cycle peaks in H2 2026 and then gradually declines
2026H1 marks the profit peak, with gross margin beginning to decline in H2. Full-year net profit of ~RMB 6-8 billion, with Q3/Q4 single-quarter profit halving.

Key:Inventory digested at the cycle peak; 2026Q4 inventory falls below RMB 8 billion and cash flow turns positive.
Valuation:Strong first half, weak second half. After the annual report, PE re-rated from 9x to 15-20x (though profit was already declining), leaving the stock exposed to a Davis double-kill, where falling profit outweighs valuation expansion. Current PB of 18x is extremely high, with 50%+ downside risk.

Core assumption: memory prices fall rapidly by 20-30% in Q3-Q4 2026
Excess capacity release plus demand falling short of expectations led to a sharp drop in memory prices. Demingli's high-priced inventory saw large impairments, and Q3-Q4 may show losses.
