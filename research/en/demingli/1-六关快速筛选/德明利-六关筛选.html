Gate one: Can I understand this business?
★★★★★☆

Core view: the business model is clear, but watch for a qualitative shift in the business mix.

Demingli makes storage modules and controllers—it buys NAND Flash chips from SK hynix/Micron/Samsung, turns them into SSDs, memory cards and embedded storage, and sells to phone makers and data-center customers. The business model is clear: "trading plus processing" of memory chips.

⚠️ Key takeaway:2026Q1 gross margin of 57.4% far exceeds traditional module makers (typically 10-25%), indicating a qualitative shift in the business mix—possibly an integrated in-house controller chip plus module model.
