Growth investorPerspective

Core question:What growth stage is Demingli in? Can AI computing demand keep driving storage module growth? Where is the growth ceiling?

Growth drivers

  • AI server storage demand:Large-model training and inference are driving surging demand for high-bandwidth memory (HBM) and enterprise SSDs, and Demingli, as China's leading storage module maker, is a direct beneficiary.
  • Domestic substitution progress:Amid the China-US tech rivalry, domestic cloud providers and data centers are accelerating the adoption of homegrown storage, and Demingli is well positioned to capture share.
  • Product mix upgrade:Migrating from consumer-grade modules to enterprise/data-center-grade modules leaves room for gains in both unit value and gross margin.

Growth risks

  • Cyclical peak risk:The 2025-2026 memory upcycle has run for a considerable time and may enter a downcycle in the second half of 2026, at which point revenue growth will slow sharply.
  • Rising competition:Domestic memory module makers (Longsys, Biwin Storage and others) are expanding capacity aggressively, and a price war could erode gross margins.
  • Technology iteration risk:Changes in technology roadmaps such as HBM and CXL could reshape the competitive landscape.
Growth investor conclusion:Demingli sits within an AI-driven memory super-cycle, and its growth story has a consensus foundation in the market. But watch for a cyclical inflection—the single-quarter profit of RMB 3.346 billion may already be a "cyclical peak" rather than a "growth starting point." Growth investors should closely track gross margin changes each quarter; a drop below 45% should prompt consideration of trimming positions.

Industry and technology analystPerspective

Core question:Where is the memory industry cycle? How strong are Demingli's supply chain and competitive moat? How will changes in technology routes affect the company?

Judging the memory industry cycle

Historically, the memory chip (DRAM/NAND Flash) cycle typically lasts 3-4 years:

  • Upswing phase (2024H2-2026H1):AI demand surge drives strong gains for Dimingli
  • Current (2026Q2-Q3):May be in a cycleTop section, original manufacturers' capacity utilization is already near full load
  • Downside risks (2026H2-2027):Slower demand growth plus supply release could push prices into a downtrend

Supply chain analysis

Demingli's business model is "procure NAND wafers from original manufacturers → module design/processing → sell to downstream customers." The core barrier lies not in technology but in:

  1. Original manufacturer relationships:Can it secure sufficient supply of granules at reasonable prices during the price-hike cycle?
  2. Customer relationships:Can it lock in major customers (e.g., server OEMs, cloud providers)
  3. Inventory management capability:Managing inventory risk amid price volatility

RMB 12.2 billion in inventory shows Demingli aggressively adding positions at the top of the cycle—if it has called the direction right (prices keep rising), earnings will hit new highs; but if the cycle turns, it will face enormous destocking pressure.
