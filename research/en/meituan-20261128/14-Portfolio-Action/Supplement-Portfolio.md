## Why is the cap 5% rather than 10%?

Three reasons. **First, it does not control its own profit.** How far the marketing expense ratio can fall depends on Alibaba's and Douyin's budgets, not on Wang Xing. For companies where "external variables dominate" like this, the position cap should be lower than for companies that "price on their own terms" (such as Moutai, Apple).

**Second, its downside is 23%.** The bear case is HKD 52, corresponding to a scenario where 2027 net profit is only RMB 11 billion. This scenario is not low-probability — it will happen if in-store share keeps eroding and Brazil losses widen.

**Third, it is highly correlated with other China internet names.** If the portfolio already holds Alibaba, Tencent and JD.com, adding Meituan is effectively leveraged betting on a "China internet recovery," not diversification. **5% is the single-name cap and also part of sector exposure.**

## How are the price levels for adding and trimming positions derived?

**At HKD 58:** market cap HKD 358.3 billion = RMB 329.6 billion; deduct net cash of RMB 120 billion = RMB 209.6 billion for the core business; 2027E RMB 23 billion = 9.1x. **Buying a still-growing local-services leader at 9x is a "1:4 risk-reward" setup.**

**Do not chase above HKD 74:** that implies 18.4x 2027E and 13x ex-net cash, compressing upside to under 30% and cutting risk-reward to 1:1.3. **Not worth adding here, but not a sell either — 2027 net profit could beat expectations.**

**Use facts, not price, for stops.** A break below HKD 55 is not itself a sell reason (it may be market sentiment); but if Q4 operating profit falls below RMB 3.5 billion, or share drops below 48%, cut regardless of the stock price. **Price is others' vote; data is the company's answer.**

## Correlation with other holdings

A rough estimate based on 2026 daily correlations: **Meituan's correlation coefficient with Alibaba, JD.com and Pinduoduo is above 0.7; around 0.6 with Tencent; above 0.8 with the Hang Seng Tech Index.** This means buying Meituan is essentially adding exposure to "China internet ADRs/H-shares".

Correlation with the AI compute chain (Nvidia, TSMC, SK hynix) is close to zero, even slightly negative — **in 2026 capital is switching back and forth between the "AI narrative" and "cash flow", and the two lines often move in opposite directions.** If the portfolio is already overweight the AI chain, Meituan is a hedge; if already overweight China internet, Meituan is a duplicate bet.

Practical recommendation: **manage "China internet" as a single exposure, capped at 15%; Meituan takes 5% of it (one third).** This avoids damage from a wrong call on a single stock, and avoids losing meaning through over-diversification.

## Rebalancing discipline

**Quarterly rebalancing:** Recalculate the target position after each earnings report. If it rises more than 20% above target (e.g., 5% to 6%), trim back to 5%; if it falls more than 20%, top back up. **No monthly adjustments — the information density of this business is quarterly; monthly adjustments only get dragged around by noise.**

**Annual review:** If net profit comes in above RMB 23 billion in 2027, raise the target position from 5% to 6%–7% (higher certainty); if it stays below RMB 15 billion for two consecutive years, cut to 2% or exit (thesis invalidated).

**One last discipline:** Never add on "it has fallen a lot"; add only because "the data has improved" or "the price has entered the odds range." **On the HKD 61.45 day, the data was not out yet; on the HKD 67.85 day, the data was out. The former was a bet; the latter was a calculation.**
