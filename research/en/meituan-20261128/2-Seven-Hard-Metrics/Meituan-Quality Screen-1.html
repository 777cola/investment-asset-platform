Core thesis

Only two and a half criteria passed in the August table; five pass this time. All of the improvement comes on the profit side: gross margin 33.5%→34.7% (estimated), operating cash flow swinging from negative for the year to strongly positive for the quarter, and free cash flow positive for the first time (estimated). The two still failing are ROE and net margin—the first three quarters of 2026 combined still show a net loss of RMB 2.60 billion, and ROE on an annual basis remains negative.

This table is not meant to score Meituan but to judge "whether it is a good company temporarily damaged." The answer leans positive: revenue growth of +21.6% is the highest in three years, net cash of RMB 120 billion shows no financial risk, and a gross margin stable at 34%–35% shows unit economics are intact. What is broken is expenses, not the business.

Which lines never turned into passes, and why?

The most important is revenue growth. Q1 +9.2%, Q2 +14.4%, Q3 +21.6% — three consecutive quarters of acceleration, counterintuitive against a backdrop of subsidy rollback. Subsidy rollback usually means order volume declines and revenue growth drops a notch; instead revenue accelerated, and there is only one explanation: order volume did not fall, and average order value and advertising monetisation came back. This metric went from "pass" to "a hard pass," and it underpins this entire report.

The second is cash flow. Operating cash flow in 2025 was -RMB 13.82 billion, the ugliest year in this business's history; by 2026Q2, net profit of RMB 2.16 billion already corresponded to operating cash flow of RMB 9.7 billion (4.5x), and on the same basis the Q3 peak season is still estimated above 4x.Food delivery is a prepaid business — users pay first, riders are settled later, merchants settle monthly — so as long as it does not burn cash, cash flow is naturally better than profit. This is not the result of management effort; it is built into the business model.

The third is gross margin. The 33%–35% range spans both the 2024 peak and the 2025 melee, showing that unit economics have not been structurally damaged — only the expense ratio has changed.The stability of this metric is the dividing line between "temporarily damaged" and "permanently worse."

The two points that still hold up, and whether either is fatal

ROE and net margin both fail, but they stem from the same issue: the first three quarters still posted a net loss of RMB 2.60 billion. Q3's single-quarter profit of RMB 2.071 billion cannot offset the RMB -6.83 billion hole in Q1. On 2027 net profit of RMB 23 billion and equity of about RMB 230 billion, ROE is roughly 10%, still below the 15% threshold; to return to 15%, net profit must reach RMB 34.5 billion, the 2024 level of RMB 35.8 billion.

This is not fatal, for three reasons. First, there is no financial risk—net cash of RMB 120 billion and interest-bearing debt near zero mean the losses burn the company's own cash, not borrowed money. Second, the losses are a deliberate choice: of the RMB 23.355 billion loss in 2025, the vast majority is user incentives and rider subsidies, a tap that can be turned off, and Q3 already turned off part of it. Third, competitors' losses are equally brutal; this war has no winner, only whoever stops first.

There is only one fatal condition: if 2027 net profit still cannot reach RMB 15 billion, the era of 20% operating margin is completely over, and the company should be valued as a utility at 12x, corresponding to HKD 45. That is the price of the pessimistic scenario.

How to use this table

The seven hard metrics are not a scorecard but a mine-detection chart. Where things stand today: balance sheet at full marks, cash flow passing, income statement only just climbing out of the hole. Historically, that combination describes a company that has just passed the cyclical bottom — not a growth stock, and not a value trap.

The method is fixed: measure once a quarter and watch only two things — whether net margin has been positive for three consecutive quarters, and whether ROE has returned above 8%. The first can be confirmed in 2027Q1 (Q4 will most likely still be positive); the second must wait for the 2027 annual report.If both pass, seven out of seven, and that is when to build a heavy position; if both fail, this round of profit is merely deferred expenses, not a recovery.

Conclusion

Five of seven criteria pass; two fail (ROE, net margin). Neither is a structural defect — the repair is simply incomplete. A stable gross margin, RMB 120 billion of net cash and revenue accelerating for three consecutive quarters underwrite the "the company will not die" leg; the ROE and net margin failures mean "we cannot go overweight yet." Trigger: net margin positive for three consecutive quarters in Q1 2027 and ROE back above 8% on the 2027 net profit guidance — seven of seven met, rating upgraded.
