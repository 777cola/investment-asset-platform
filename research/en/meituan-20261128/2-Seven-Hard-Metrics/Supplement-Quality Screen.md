## Which lines never turned into passes, and why?

The most important one is revenue growth. Q1 +9.2%, Q2 +14.4%, Q3 +21.6% — three consecutive quarters of acceleration, which is counterintuitive against the backdrop of subsidy rollback. Subsidy rollback usually means order volume declines and revenue growth drops a notch; what actually happened was revenue acceleration, and there is only one explanation: order volume did not fall, and average order value and advertising monetization came back. This metric went from "pass" to "a hard pass," and it is the foundation of this entire report.

The second is cash flow. Operating cash flow in 2025 was -RMB 13.82 billion, the ugliest year in this business's history; by 2026Q2, net profit of RMB 2.16 billion already corresponded to operating cash flow of RMB 9.7 billion (4.5x), and on the same basis, the Q3 peak season is still estimated above 4x.Food delivery is a prepaid business — users pay first, riders are settled later, merchants settle monthly — so as long as it does not burn cash, cash flow is naturally better than profit. This is not the result of management effort; it is built into the business model.

The third is gross margin. The 33%–35% range spans both the 2024 peak and the 2025 melee, showing that unit economics have not been structurally damaged — only the expense ratio has changed.This metric's stability is the dividing line between "temporarily damaged" and "permanently worse."

## The two points that still hold up, and whether they are fatal

ROE and net margin fail, but it is essentially the same issue: the first three quarters still posted a net loss of RMB 2.60 billion. Q3's single-quarter profit of RMB 2.071 billion cannot offset the RMB -6.83 billion hole in Q1. On 2027 net profit of RMB 23 billion and equity of about RMB 230 billion, ROE is roughly 10%, still below the 15% threshold; to return to 15%, net profit must reach RMB 34.5 billion, the 2024 level of RMB 35.8 billion.

This is not fatal, for three reasons. First, the company has no financial risk—net cash of RMB 120 billion, interest-bearing debt near zero; the losses burn its own cash, not borrowed money. Second, the losses are a deliberate choice: of the RMB 23.355 billion loss in 2025, the vast majority is user incentives and rider subsidies, a tap that can be turned off, and Q3 already turned off part of it. Third, competitors' losses are equally brutal; this war has no winner, only whoever stops first.

There is only one fatal condition: if 2027 net profit still cannot reach RMB 15 billion, it means the era of 20% operating margin is completely over, and the company should be valued as a utility at 12x, corresponding to HKD 45. That is the price of the pessimistic scenario.

## How to use this table

The seven hard metrics are not a scorecard; they are a mine-detection chart. Its current state: balance sheet at full marks, cash flow passing, income statement just climbing out of the hole. Historically, this combination corresponds to "a company that has just passed the cyclical bottom" — not a growth stock, and not a value trap.

The method is fixed: measure once a quarter, and look at only two things — whether the net margin has been positive for three consecutive quarters, and whether ROE has returned above 8%. The former can be confirmed in Q1 2027 (Q4 will most likely still be positive); the latter must wait for the 2027 annual report.If both pass, seven out of seven, and that is when to build a heavy position; if both fail, this round of profit is merely deferred expenses, not a recovery.
