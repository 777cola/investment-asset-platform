## This module's structure: four close readings → synthesis → publishable draft

Six pages, one pipeline: four readers each take the same quarterly report (about 1,000 words apiece), an editor synthesises their views into a matrix, and a final polish turns it into a publishable article.

- Duan Yongping close read: users first, profit second — all three revenue lines accelerating at once, costs reined in, buyback too small.

- Buffett close read: all three statements pass — a thin income statement, good cash flow, a clean balance sheet, capital allocation C+.

- Munger close read: first ask whether this profit can repeat — the three preconditions (rivals hold prices, rider costs stay flat, in-store holds) all sit outside Meituan's control.

- Li Lu close read: take the ten-year view — category expansion, overseas replicability, regulatory risk landing; people are the biggest worry.

- View synthesis: three points of consensus, two disagreements, and three uses for holders, watchers and heavy positions.

- Editor's polished version: a publishable long read, concluding that "the inflection is real, but it is not the inflection that ends the war".

## The three synthesized conclusions

Consensus one: the inflection point is real. Revenue +21.6%, net profit RMB 2.071 billion, core local commerce profitable at the operating level for two consecutive quarters and the marketing expense ratio down from 40% to 22.9% — four people reached the same conclusion from different angles.

Consensus two: profit quality is mediocre. Operating profit was only RMB 1.71 billion, a 1.5% margin, and roughly RMB 360 million of the RMB 2.071 billion net profit was non-operating. Single-quarter profit cannot be annualised: 20.71×4 = RMB 8.28 billion implies 46x, an absurd valuation.

Consensus three: capital allocation is conservative. RMB 120 billion of net cash, buybacks of just 0.4% of market cap, no dividend, and USD 1 billion committed to Brazil.

The first disagreement — how long profit can last (Duan Yongping optimistic, Munger cautious) — was settled as a 4–6 quarter ceasefire window. The second — whether to invest overseas now (Buffett against, Li Lu in favour) — was settled by assuming no overseas profit in the neutral case and some only in the optimistic case.
