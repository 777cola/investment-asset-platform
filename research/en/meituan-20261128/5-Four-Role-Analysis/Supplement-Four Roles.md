## This module's structure: four roles plus one adjudicator

Four independent analyses draw on the same dataset (2026Q3 results announcement, 2026 interim report, 2025 annual report, 27 November daily close), apply different frameworks and cross-check each other's conclusions.

- Business analysis (Duan Yongping): why users still open the app — revenue +21.6% answers that; merchant services growing only 8% is the concern.

- Financial analysis (Buffett): of RMB 2.071 billion, RMB 1.71 billion was earned from operations; FCF is 3x net profit; net cash is 31% of market cap.

- Industry research (Munger): three structural flaws (low barriers, costly exit, homogeneous products); worth HKD 79 under the mediocre assumption of an 8% ten-year margin.

- Risk assessment (Li Lu): the urban-density dividend holds, but the endgame is undecided (winner-takes-all versus fragmentation differs 2.5x); the biggest uncertainty is management generational transition.

- Team Lead summary: three disagreements (whether profit is recovery or deferral, whether share will return, what overseas expansion is worth) adjudicated one by one and translated into position sizing.

Adjudication principle: verifiable evidence over inference, company disclosure over estimates, price conditions over forecasts. Final action: buy 30% at the current price, add 30% below HKD 58, and fill the position after 2027 Q4 data confirms.

## Comparison of conclusions across the four roles

Duan Yongping: The business is still there, the users are still there, management has made the right calls, and the price is not expensive — though not extremely cheap either. First tranche 30%.

Buffett: Thin but real profits, good cash flow, a clean balance sheet and conservative capital allocation. Intrinsic value HKD 94–102, so the current price sits at a 28%–33% discount — still 10 points short of the 40% he requires.

Munger: Industry structure dictates that steady-state margins will not be high; the 20.9% in 2024 is an outlier. The three things that could ruin the business — rider costs becoming employee costs, technology erasing density, and the entry point migrating — have not happened, but all are on the way.

Li Lu: The structural advantage holds, the endgame is undecided, overseas expansion is an option rather than the main thesis, and regulatory risk has already landed. The only thing that cannot be quantified is the management generational transition, so the position should not exceed 5% of the portfolio.

The four have no disagreement on "whether to buy" — the disagreement is on "how much to buy," and the Team Lead ultimately settled that with "opportunity cost vs discount" at 30%.
