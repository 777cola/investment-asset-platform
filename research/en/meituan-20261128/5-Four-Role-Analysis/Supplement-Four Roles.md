## This module's structure: four roles plus one adjudicator

Four independent analyses share the same dataset (2026Q3 results announcement, 2026 interim report, 2025 annual report, November 27 daily close), with different frameworks and cross-validating conclusions. · Business analysis (Duan Yongping): why users still open it — revenue +21.6% is the answer; merchant services growing only 8% is the concern.
· Financial analysis (Buffett): of RMB 2.071 billion, RMB 1.71 billion is earned from operations; FCF is 3x net profit; net cash is 31% of market cap.
· Industry research (Munger): three structural flaws (low barriers, costly exit, homogeneous product); worth HKD 79 under the mediocre assumption of an 8% ten-year profit margin.
· Risk assessment (Li Lu): the urban density dividend holds; the endgame is undecided (winner-take-all versus split share differs by 2.5x); the biggest uncertainty is management generational transition.
· Team Lead summary: three disagreements (whether profit is recovery or deferral, whether share will return, what overseas expansion is worth) adjudicated one by one and translated into position sizing.
Adjudication principle: verifiable evidence over inference, company disclosure over estimates, price conditions over forecasts. Final action: buy 30% at the current price, add 30% below HKD 58, fill out after 2027 Q4 data confirms.

## Comparison of conclusions across the four roles

Duan Yongping: The business is still there, the users are still there, management made the right choices, the price is not expensive but not extremely cheap either — initial position 30%.

Buffett: Thin but real margins, good cash flow, a clean balance sheet, conservative capital allocation; intrinsic value HKD 94–102, the current price is at a 28%–33% discount, still 10 points short of the 40% he requires.

Munger: Industry structure dictates that the steady-state margin will not be high; 20.9% in 2024 is an outlier; the three things that could ruin the business (rider costs becoming employee costs, technology erasing density, entry-point migration) have not happened but are all on the way.

Li Lu: The structural advantage holds, the endgame is undecided, overseas expansion is an option rather than the main thesis, regulatory risk has already landed; the only thing that cannot be quantified is the management generational transition — position no more than 5% of the portfolio.

The four have no disagreement on "whether to buy"; they disagree on "how much to buy" — and that disagreement was ultimately adjudicated by the Team Lead, weighing "opportunity cost vs discount", at 30%.
