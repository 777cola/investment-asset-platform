Core view

All four roles land in strikingly similar territory: buy, in tranches. The process nonetheless throws up three genuine disagreements—whether profit is recovering or merely deferred, whether share will come back, and what overseas expansion is worth. This section sets out those disagreements and gives verdicts with reasoning.

Disagreement one: is profit recovering or deferred?

Buffett's role runs the numbers carefully: gross profit up RMB 8.7 billion YoY, marketing spend down RMB 11.5 billion, R&D up RMB 880 million, operating profit improved by RMB 19.5 billion. He concludes that "the recovery is real," since gross profit improvement accounts for 44% — it is not all cost-cutting.

Li Lu's role counters: the marketing expense ratio is 22.9%, 7 points above 16% in 2024. Those 7 points came from "the rival temporarily holding fire," not from Meituan itself. Once the rival steps up spending again, those 7 points go back.

Verdict: the recovery is real, but the magnitude is overstated. Q3 operating profit of RMB 1.71 billion and core local commerce of RMB 4.52 billion (estimated) can be confirmed; whether Q4 can hold RMB 5 billion cannot. The model's neutral scenario therefore uses a 10% core operating margin (not 20.9% as in 2024), treating "the rival not fighting" as a one-off windfall, not the norm.

Divergence 2: Will market share come back?

Duan Yongping on users: revenue +21.6%, delivery revenue +20%, gross margin up 1.7pp — all three confirm users are still there, so there is no reason share should not come back.

Munger on structure: the three players have held 50/43/7 for three quarters — a stalemate equilibrium, not a reconciliation equilibrium. Any one of them wanting another 2 points of share must spend over RMB 10 billion more, and the return on that spend no longer works.

Verdict: model on a 50% midpoint, not 55% as the neutral assumption. Share is the outcome of competition, not a function of operations; until two consecutive quarters of recovery are visible, it cannot go into the base case. 55% sits only in the bull case, corresponding to 2027 net profit of RMB 33 billion.

Disagreement three: what overseas expansion is worth, and positioning

The Li Lu role treats overseas expansion as an option: 22 months of profitability in Saudi Arabia proves the model is replicable; if Brazil succeeds, Meituan goes from a "Chinese company" to a "global company," and the valuation can re-rate from 19x to 25x.

The Buffett role counts only cash: USD 1 billion spent on buybacks at the current price would retire 2.6% of shares—a certain return; investing in Brazil is uncertain.

Verdict: the base case ascribes no value to overseas profit; only the bull case does; if Brazil's annual loss exceeds RMB 5 billion, downgrade one notch. Rationale: the option has value, but you cannot pay full price for an option. This is also why the base-case target price is HKD 98 (excluding overseas) and the bull case is HKD 149 (including overseas breaking even).

Position sizing: the Duan Yongping role advocates buying 30% at the current price, the Buffett role advocates waiting for HKD 58–62. The verdict adopts the former—because Q4 data will not be out until March 2027, and the opportunity cost of waiting a quarter matters more than an 8% extra discount. But the second tranche must wait for price: add another 30% below HKD 58.

Final conclusion: buy 30%, leave the rest to price and data

The four roles agree on buying; they differ only on size — hence an initial 30%. Three reasons: the business is intact (revenue accelerating, gross margin stable, cash flow positive), the price is not expensive (16.8x 2027E, 11.5x after net cash), and the payoff is asymmetric (23% downside, 44% upside, odds of 1 : 1.9).

Three reasons not to size up: share has stopped falling at 50% but has not recovered, the marketing expense ratio is still 7 points too high, and the RMB 23 billion net profit for 2027 is an assumption. Disprove any one of the three and the valuation gets marked down a notch.

The watch calendar: March 2027 Q4 and full-year results (core local commerce operating profit ≥ RMB 5 billion), May 2027 Q1 (net margin positive for three consecutive quarters), August 2027 interim results (Brazil losses and in-store share). Three points in time, three verifications, adding to the position in three tranches. No forecasts, no all-in, follow the data.

Conclusion

Verdict: Buy. Initiate with 30% at the current price of HKD 67.85, add 30% below HKD 58, and add the remaining 40% once Q4 2027 data confirms. All three debates are settled on "verifiable evidence first": we treat the recovery as real, the share at 50%, and overseas as option value with a discount applied.Falsification conditions are hard-coded: if Q4 core local commerce operating profit falls below RMB 3.5 billion, or share falls below 48%, cut the position unconditionally.
