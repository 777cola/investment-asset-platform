## Stage one and two: revenue is back, but profit comes from cost-cutting

Start with the revenue mix. Total revenue in 2026Q3 was RMB 115.56 billion, of which core local commerce was about RMB 80.02 billion (estimated, 69.3%) and new initiatives about RMB 35.54 billion (30.7%). Core local commerce splits into three parts: delivery service revenue, merchant service revenue (commission plus advertising), and product sales (self-operated pharmaceuticals and liquor). **You only need to watch three lines in this report: delivery service revenue growth, merchant service revenue growth, and core local commerce operating profit.** The first line tells us order volume and average order value, the second tells us whether merchants are still willing to pay, and the third tells us whether subsidies have pulled back.

This section loses 1 point, for "can read revenue, cannot read profit." Revenue can be modeled: order volume × average order value, merchant count × commission rate, ad load — all three variables have historical ranges. Profit cannot be modeled: it equals the difference in how much each of the three parties is willing to burn. Revenue in 2025 was RMB 364.85 billion, up only 8.1%, yet operating profit swung from +RMB 45.1 billion in 2024 to -RMB 17 billion — **an 8-point gap in revenue, a RMB 62.1 billion gap in profit. That is what this business looks like now.**

The second section looks at the extremes on both ends. At the good end: core local commerce operating margin was 20.9% in 2024 and group operating profit was RMB 45.1 billion, with almost no capex and cash collected before it is paid out — one of the best business models in Chinese internet. At the bad end: a single-quarter net loss of RMB 18.632 billion in 2025Q3. Now it is in between: Q3 gross profit of RMB 40.06 billion, gross margin 34.7% (estimated), operating margin 1.5%. **A score of 7 means this: at steady state it is a good business, but half of its income statement is always written into someone else's budget.**

## Stage three and four: share stabilizes, management delivers

Moat scores 7, down another point from 8 in August — not because it deteriorated, but because the evidence hardened. Three things rivals cannot build short-term: about 6 million riders with orders, about 8 million active merchants, and minute-level dispatch across 2,800 counties and districts.**But the 2025 war proved this moat stops new entrants, not giants willing to lose money.** Order-volume share fell from about 65% in early 2025 to about 51% in February 2026 and about 50% this quarter — it stopped falling but did not recover. The halt is good: the marginal effect of subsidies is exhausted. The lack of recovery is also fact: rivals have not exited, they have just stopped bidding up.

Management scores 8, the only one of the six gates revised up. The reason is simple: **in August 2026 it said "third-quarter per-order delivery cost will be higher than the second quarter and profit will be a bit worse," and the numbers it delivered on November 27 matched — core local commerce operating profit fell from RMB 5.67 billion to about RMB 4.5 billion quarter on quarter, but did not break down.** Management daring to give bad guidance on the earnings call and delivering on it is worth more than good guidance. On capital allocation: AI spending is restrained (R&D RMB 8.65 billion, 7.5% of revenue, growth of +11.3% below revenue growth), the USD 2 billion buyback authorization was executed in the HKD 66.5–69 range, and Wang Xing explicitly said the company will not build a "Token factory."

The 2 points deducted are about boundaries: Brazil has signed a five-year USD 1 billion investment agreement, the only battlefield where "how much you lose is your call, how much you earn is your rival's call." New initiatives' third-quarter loss widened from RMB 1.74 billion to about RMB 2.16 billion quarter on quarter — that is where it comes from.

## Gate five and six: the denominator is cheap; the assumptions are expensive

HKD 67.85 × 6.175 billion shares = HKD 419 billion, or RMB 385.5 billion at an exchange rate of 0.92. Net cash on the balance sheet is about RMB 120 billion (a media estimate, not a disclosed figure); netting that out leaves a core-business market cap of RMB 265.5 billion. On 2027 net profit attributable to shareholders of RMB 23 billion: **16.8x including net cash, 11.5x excluding it.** Against the RMB 35.8 billion this business once earned in a single year in 2024, that multiple is not expensive.

But 7 points rather than 9, because the RMB 23 billion is calculated, not observed. It requires three conditions to hold at once: core local commerce operating margin back to 10% (now 5.6%), new-business losses narrowed to within RMB 1 billion, and the marketing expense ratio back below 18%. **If any one of the three fails, 2027 net profit drops below RMB 15 billion, implying 25x.** The three scenarios: bear case HKD 52 (-23%), base case HKD 98 (+44%), bull case HKD 149 (+119%), a payoff ratio of 1 : 1.9.

Gate six scores 7, because whether this purchase is right or wrong does not take three years to find out. When Q4 and full-year results are published in March 2027, three numbers will appear at once: whether Q4 core local commerce operating profit holds at RMB 5 billion, whether order-volume share has recovered to 51%, and whether the marketing expense ratio has fallen below 22%. **If two of the three miss, the investment is wrong: trim the position, no need to agonize.**

## Veto items and items to be verified

**Veto items: none.** No single clause is enough to kill this investment. But one hard constraint goes on record here: **if core local commerce operating profit falls below RMB 3.5 billion in Q4 2026, or unit-share falls below 48%, the rating drops immediately from "Buy" to "Watch."** If either happens, it means Q3 profit was not a recovery but seasonality.

**Three items to verify.** First, the direction of subsidy levels — Q3 marketing expense was RMB 26.5 billion, a expense ratio of 22.9% (estimated), 7 points above the 16% of the same period in 2024; management itself says this is "still above normal levels," and this number is the source of all the elasticity. Second, the operating margin of the in-store business — this is the fattest profit pool, Douyin's attack has not stopped, and management has already flagged that it will sacrifice margin for share. Third, Brazil — USD 1 billion over 5 years is only a committed ceiling; the actual pace becomes visible in the first half of 2027.

One more item does not belong to the company but determines the share price: **overall Hong Kong market liquidity.** The Hang Seng Tech Index is down more than 25% year-to-date in 2026, and Meituan still has 36% of upside to its 52-week high; half of that gap is sector multiple compression, not company fundamentals. This does not constitute a veto, but it dictates "scaling in" rather than "all at once."
