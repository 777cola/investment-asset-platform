📝 Conclusion: Buy; add in tranches

Buy, accumulate in tranches. At the November 27, 2026 close of HKD 67.85 and a market cap of HKD 419 billion, the stock rose 8.21% in a single day after earnings but remains 36% below its 52-week high. The November 27 evening Q3 report nailed down the single most important point: revenue of RMB 115.56 billion, up 21.6% year on year and 10.4% quarter on quarter, with net profit of RMB 2.07 billion versus a loss of RMB 18.63 billion a year earlier—the worst quarter of the subsidy war is over, and core local commerce has posted operating profit for two consecutive quarters. But adjusted net profit of RMB 1.85 billion came in slightly below expectations, showing the profit was saved, not earned. On 2027 net profit of RMB 23 billion, that is 11.5x ex-net-cash and 16.8x including net cash, for a risk-reward of 1:1.9. Worth buying, in three tranches; do not chase above HKD 74.
