Core thesis

Read this quarterly report backwards: start with why revenue accelerated (+21.6%, above 20% for the first time in three years), then why profit was only RMB 2.071 billion (operating margin 1.5%), and finally how much of that RMB 2.071 billion was not earned from operations (about RMB 360 million).

Conclusion: the revenue side is real; the profit side was saved out. The marketing expense ratio fell from 40% a year earlier to 22.9% (estimated), with RMB 11.5 billion of subsidies cut, which is what bought this RMB 2.071 billion of net profit. The cost-cutting elasticity has 7 points left (versus 16% in 2024), but more than half of it is already used up.

Layer one: why revenue accelerated

Q3 revenue came in at RMB 115.56 billion, up 21.6% YoY and 10.4% QoQ, 1.9% ahead of the RMB 113.37 billion consensus. Three lines accelerated at once, not just one: core local commerce c.RMB 80.02 billion, +17.9% YoY; new initiatives RMB 35.54 billion, +30.8% YoY; delivery services c.RMB 30 billion, c.+20% YoY.

Each line is driven by a different mechanism. Delivery services accelerated on both order volume and average ticket size — order volume held up after subsidies tapered, the most counterintuitive point.New initiatives grew 30.8%, led by instant retail (Shanbuy) and Xiaoxiang Supermarket — essentially "selling more categories on existing delivery capacity," with very low marginal cost. Core local commerce as a whole rose 17.9%, nearly 8 points faster than Q2's 10.1%, showing in-store is recovering too; in-store is pure commission and advertising revenue, with high gross margin.

Watch merchant services: c.RMB 44 billion, c.+8% YoY, far slower than delivery services.Merchant services — commission plus advertising — is the direct expression of platform bargaining power; at only 8% growth, it shows merchants were hurt in the subsidy war and are recovering more slowly than users. If this figure cannot return above 10% in Q4, the "quality" of revenue is in doubt.

Layer two: gross profit and expenses — where the money goes

Gross profit of RMB 40.06 billion, gross margin 34.7% (derived), up 1.7pp YoY and 1.2pp QoQ. Gross margin expansion during a subsidy phase-out is anomalous — falling subsidies usually drag down average order value and pressure gross margin. Two factors drove the gain: a richer mix of high-margin new businesses (instant retail, in-store), and lower procurement costs for self-operated goods (pharmaceuticals, liquor).

The expense side is the whole story of this report. Selling and marketing expenses of RMB 26.50 billion, a 22.9% expense ratio, versus RMB 38.01 billion and 40.0% a year earlier — a cut of RMB 11.5 billion in one year. R&D of RMB 8.65 billion, up only 11.3% YoY, at 7.5% of revenue versus 8.2% a year earlier. G&A of RMB 3.20 billion, essentially flat.

Put the three together: gross profit up RMB 8.7 billion YoY, marketing spend down RMB 11.5 billion, R&D up RMB 880 million, and operating profit swung from -RMB 17.80 billion to +RMB 1.71 billion, an improvement of RMB 19.5 billion. Every bit of the improvement came from burning less cash, not earning more — gross profit contributed RMB 8.7 billion and expense savings RMB 10.8 billion. That is why the profit in this report was saved, not earned.

Tier 3: The quality of profit

Net profit was RMB 2.071 billion, but operating profit was only RMB 1.71 billion (derived); the RMB 360 million gap came from non-operating items—fair value changes on investments, disposal gains and interest income, net of tax and minority interests. Adjusted net profit of RMB 1.850 billion came in below net profit, which confirms the point: the company's "adjustments" add back share-based compensation and strip out fair value gains on investments, and the deductions exceed the add-backs, hence the lower adjusted figure.

This is what the market means by "adjusted profit slightly below expectations": the positive headline net profit looks good, but the operating recovery is milder than the headline number suggests. Strip out the RMB 360 million of non-operating gains and the operating margin is just 1.5%—a company with RMB 450 billion of annual revenue earning only RMB 1.7 billion in a quarter. That is not the look of a good business; it is the look of one that has just climbed out of a hole.

One more number to watch: core local commerce operating margin of 5.6% (derived), versus 7.9% in Q2 and 20.9% in 2024. The 2.3-point sequential decline reflects the seasonality management flagged in August (summer marketing, heat-related rider subsidies, new work-injury insurance); the year-on-year swing from -20.3% to +5.6% is a genuine recovery. Both directions are true, and neither should be told alone.

Layer four: cash flow and balance sheet

Operating cash flow came in at about RMB 8.5 billion (estimated), more than 4x net profit, with capex of roughly RMB 2.5 billion and free cash flow of about RMB 6 billion — positive for two consecutive quarters.Food delivery is a prepaid business: users pay first, riders are settled later and merchants monthly, so absent cash burn, cash flow is naturally better than profit. The RMB -13.815 billion operating cash flow in 2025 was an exception, driven by how the subsidy war was booked rather than by any deterioration in the business.

The balance sheet is cleaner than a company that just lost RMB 23.355 billion has any right to be: cash and equivalents of RMB 104.7 billion, short-term wealth management products of RMB 63.6 billion, interest-bearing debt at almost zero and net cash of about RMB 120 billion.Two implications: first, losses will not kill it; second, it has RMB 120 billion available for buybacks, dividends or overseas expansion, of which only a small portion has been used so far.

In one sentence, this earnings report says:revenue proves the business is still there, gross margin proves unit economics are intact, expenses prove management can pull back and cash flow proves it will not die. All four hold — only "earning power" has not come back. Q4 is the off-season; whether core local commerce operating profit can hold RMB 5 billion is the next test.

Conclusion

Revenue beat consensus by 1.9%, net profit swung to positive YoY at RMB 20.7 billion and operating margin came in at 1.5%—three numbers telling one story: the recovery is real, but thin. Expense savings of RMB 10.8 billion drove 56% of profit, gross margin improvement of RMB 8.7 billion another 44%, with non-operating gains adding RMB 360 million.The next test is Q4: core local commerce operating profit of at least RMB 5 billion and merchant services growth back above 10%. Only if both hold does the 2027 net profit assumption of RMB 23 billion stand.
