## Two verified: revenue and expenses

Revenue is the hardest evidence. Three consecutive quarters of acceleration (+9.2% → +14.4% → +21.6%), achieved even as subsidies were phased out. The hypothesis holds, which overturns the bear case that "subsidies are what retain users."

Expenses are half-validated. The marketing expense ratio fell from 40% last year to 22.9% — the right direction and a large move, but 2024 was 16%, so it is still 7 points short. Those 7 points hinge not on management's willingness but on competitors'. I therefore mark this "partially validated."

Together, the two show the business's foundation is intact. An intact foundation means this investment "won't lose big"; whether it makes money depends on the three points below.

## Two points to verify: margins and new business

A return of core local commerce operating margin to 10% underpins this investment. The margin now stands at 5.6% (Q3 estimate), 7.9% (Q2) and 20.9% (2024). A return to 10% lifts quarterly operating profit from RMB 4.5 billion to RMB 8 billion, adding RMB 14 billion a year and implying 2027 net profit of RMB 23 billion.

The path is clear: the marketing expense ratio falls from 22.9% to 18% (4.9 points saved, or about RMB 22 billion annualized), while delivery cost drops 1–2 points through density optimization. Neither requires price increases; both require only that competitors hold prices.

New businesses breaking even in 2027 is the harder call. The Q3 loss was RMB 2.16 billion, a 6.1% loss ratio, widening QoQ on Brazil expansion. If Brazil loses RMB 5 billion in 2027 and Saudi Arabia and Hong Kong contribute RMB 2 billion of profit, new businesses still lose RMB 3 billion overall. Break-even must wait until 2028.

## One segment that weakened: in-store

In-store is Meituan's fattest profit pool at around 30% operating margin, and it is also the only assumption that has now weakened for two consecutive quarters. Douyin's in-store GTV exceeded RMB 800 billion in 2025, growing nearly 60% and squeezing Meituan's in-store share from above 80% to around 60%.

That erosion will not show up immediately in group profit—in-store still makes money, just less of it. But it caps long-term valuation: if in-store share falls to 50%, Meituan's profit structure shifts from "high-margin in-store subsidising low-margin fulfillment" to "both segments low-margin," and the valuation framework has to be rebuilt.

Metrics to watch: in-store GTV share (semi-annual), in-store operating margin (quarterly), Douyin local life GMV growth (quarterly). If two of the three deteriorate, this assumption is void and our neutral target price drops from HKD 98 to HKD 80.

## Falsification conditions and monitoring calendar

Three falsification conditions; cut the position if any one holds:

- 2026Q4 core local commerce operating profit < RMB 3.5 billion (would suggest Q3 was a peak-season illusion)

- Order volume share falls below 48% (would suggest rivals have re-engaged on price, and that it is working)

- Marketing expense ratio rebounds above 26% (would signal the truce is over)

Watch calendar: March 2027 Q4 and full-year results (core local commerce operating profit, Brazil losses); May 2027 Q1 (net margin positive for three consecutive quarters); August 2027 interim results (in-store share, new business loss ratio).

Upgrade conditions: Q4 core local commerce operating profit ≥ RMB 5 billion, share back to 51%, marketing expense ratio ≤22%; if two of the three are met, upgrade the rating and add a third tranche.
