## Two verified: revenue and expenses

**Revenue is the hardest evidence.** Three consecutive quarters of acceleration (+9.2% → +14.4% → +21.6%), and this happened as subsidies were being phased out. **This hypothesis is validated, which means the bear case that "users are retained by subsidies" is overturned.**

**Expenses are half-validated.** Marketing expense ratio fell from 40% last year to 22.9% — right direction, large magnitude; but 2024 was 16%, still 7 points short. **Those 7 points are not a matter of management's willingness, but of competitors' willingness.** So I mark it "partially validated."

Together, these two show the business's foundation is intact. **An intact foundation guarantees this investment "won't lose big"; whether it makes money depends on the three points below.**

## Two points to verify: margins and new business

**Core local commerce operating margin returning to 10% is the core assumption behind this investment.** It now stands at 5.6% (Q3 estimate), 7.9% (Q2), 20.9% (2024). **A return to 10% means quarterly operating profit rises from RMB 4.5 billion to RMB 8 billion, adding RMB 14 billion a year, corresponding to 2027 net profit of RMB 23 billion.**

The path is clear: marketing expense ratio falls from 22.9% to 18% (4.9 points saved = about RMB 22 billion annualized), and delivery cost drops 1–2 points through density optimization. **Neither requires price increases, only that competitors do not raise prices.**

**New businesses breaking even in 2027** is the harder call. Q3 loss was RMB 2.16 billion, a 6.1% loss ratio, widening quarter on quarter on Brazil expansion. **If Brazil loses RMB 5 billion in 2027 and Saudi Arabia and Hong Kong contribute RMB 2 billion of profit, new businesses overall still lose RMB 3 billion.** Break-even must wait until 2028.

## One segment that weakened: in-store

In-store is Meituan's fattest profit pool (operating margin around 30%), and also the only assumption that has "weakened for two consecutive quarters." **Douyin's in-store GTV exceeded RMB 800 billion in 2025, growing nearly 60%, squeezing Meituan's in-store share from above 80% to around 60%.**

This weakening will not show up immediately in group profit—in-store still makes money, just less of it. **But it is the ceiling on long-term valuation: if in-store share falls to 50%, Meituan's profit structure shifts from "high-margin in-store subsidizing low-margin fulfillment" to "both segments low-margin," and the valuation framework must be rebuilt.**

Metrics to watch: **in-store GTV share (semi-annual), in-store operating margin (quarterly), Douyin local life GMV growth (quarterly).** If two of the three deteriorate, this assumption is void and the neutral target price drops from HKD 98 to HKD 80.

## Falsification conditions and monitoring calendar

**Three falsification conditions; cut the position if any one holds:**

- 2026Q4 core local commerce operating profit &lt; RMB 3.5 billion (signals Q3 was a peak-season illusion)
- Order volume share falls below 48% (signals rivals re-engaged on price and it is working)
- Marketing expense ratio rebounds above 26% (signals the truce is over)

**Watch calendar:** March 2027 Q4 and full-year results (core local commerce operating profit, Brazil losses); May 2027 Q1 (net margin positive for three consecutive quarters); August 2027 interim results (in-store share, new business loss ratio).

**Upgrade conditions:** Q4 core local commerce operating profit ≥ RMB 5 billion, share back to 51%, marketing expense ratio ≤22%; if two of the three are met, upgrade the rating and add a third tranche.
