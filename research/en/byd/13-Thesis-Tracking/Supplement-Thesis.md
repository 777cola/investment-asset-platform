## Two things that hold: overseas and gross margin

Overseas is the strongest part. Exports 792,000 units, +67.8% YoY, 43.8% of sales; overseas auto revenue RMB 129.5 billion, +55.8% YoY. **And it is not volume pushed by low prices — revenue growth of 55.8% against volume growth of 67.8% means unit prices fell only slightly, rare in a year of price wars.**

Gross margin holds up too. Q2 auto gross margin 23.0%, +4.3 pts YoY; group gross margin 18.85%, +0.84 pts YoY. **Rising against an industry-wide decline shows the vertical-integration cost advantage is real.** But watch its sustainability — 23% is most likely the peak of this cycle.

## Four scenarios that did not materialize: three need time, one needs luck

**Domestic recovery is the most critical and the slowest.** August sales rebounded month on month but remained negative year on year, showing the "stabilization" is not yet complete. This requires two consecutive months of positive year-on-year growth to confirm, and at the earliest that means Q4 2026.

**R&D recovery depends on management's choices, not the market.** If the Q3 report still shows an R&D expense ratio below 6.8%, it means "protecting profit" is the full-year strategy, and the 2027 product cycle should be discounted.

**FX is inherently uncontrollable.** With overseas revenue approaching half of the total, currency swings are the norm. All we can do is gauge their magnitude—H1 saw a RMB 8.3 billion swing.

**Valuation depends on the share price, not the company.** RMB 70–75 corresponds to 23x neutral net profit, and only then does the risk-reward work.

## Falsification conditions: when this thesis is invalidated

The thesis is invalidated not by a falling share price, but by any one of the following fundamentals:

- **Auto gross margin below 18% for two consecutive quarters.** That means the cost advantage has been eaten by the price war, and vertical integration is no longer a moat.
- **R&D expense ratio falls below 5.5%.** That means management has shifted entirely to the short term, with no new models to compete with in 2027–2028.
- **Export growth drops below 20%.** That means the overseas window closes early, and the base-case net profit of RMB 34 billion does not hold.
- **Domestic sales decline by double digits for six consecutive months.** That means share loss is irreversible, and the company becomes a business "kept alive by overseas markets."

If any one of the four appears, the rating drops straight from "Watch" to "Avoid," with no waiting for price. **Because a cheap price cannot make up for flawed logic.**

## Watch calendar: five time points

**1st of every month:** Sales announcement. Watch domestic YoY only; two consecutive months of positive growth = initial position signal.

**Late October:** Q3 report. Watch automotive gross margin (can it hold 20%) and R&D expense ratio (can it recover to 7%). **This is the most important earnings report of the year.**

**Early January 2027:** December sales + full-year target. Watch the company's 2027 guidance; conservative guidance means management itself is not optimistic.

**Q1 2027:** Hungary plant starts production. Watch European registrations; this is the validation point for whether the "tariff bypass" holds.

**March 2027:** Annual report. Watch whether full-year free cash flow turns positive and whether the dividend payout ratio rises. **If FCF is negative for two consecutive years, the valuation denominator must be recalculated.**
