Core call: the thesis has not been disproven, but neither has it been validated

Break the buy case into six verifiable assumptions. Two currently hold (rapid overseas growth, automotive gross margin above 20%); four do not (domestic turnaround, R&D recovery, FX stability, reasonable valuation).

The thesis has not been falsified—there is no hard damage such as a "gross margin collapse" or "export stall"—but it has not been validated either. The status is therefore "under review," not "holding." That is also why a 0 position is right now: position size should follow validation, not gut feel.

Two things that hold: overseas and gross margin

Overseas is the strongest part of the story: exports of 792,000 units, +67.8% YoY, 43.8% of sales, with overseas auto revenue of RMB 129.5 billion, +55.8% YoY. Nor is this volume pushed by low prices — revenue growth of 55.8% against volume growth of 67.8% implies only a slight decline in unit prices, rare in a year of price wars.

Gross margin holds up too: Q2 auto gross margin 23.0%, +4.3 pts YoY, and group gross margin 18.85%, +0.84 pts YoY. Rising against an industry-wide decline shows the vertical-integration cost advantage is real. Watch sustainability, though — 23% is most likely the peak of this cycle.

Four scenarios that did not materialise: three need time, one needs luck

Domestic recovery is the most critical driver and the slowest to materialise. August sales rebounded MoM but remained negative YoY, so the stabilisation is not yet complete. Confirmation requires two consecutive months of positive YoY growth, which at the earliest means 2026Q4.

R&D recovery hinges on management's choices, not the market. If the 2026Q3 report still shows an R&D expense ratio below 6.8%, "protecting profit" is the full-year strategy and the 2027 product cycle should be discounted.

FX is inherently uncontrollable. With overseas revenue approaching half of the total, currency swings are the norm; all we can do is gauge their magnitude—1H26 saw a RMB 8.3 billion swing.

Valuation depends on the share price, not the company. RMB 70–75 corresponds to 23x neutral net profit, and only at that level does the risk-reward work.

Falsification conditions: when this thesis is invalidated

The thesis breaks not on a falling share price but on any one of the following fundamentals:

  • Auto gross margin below 18% for two consecutive quarters. The price war has eroded the cost advantage and vertical integration is no longer a moat.
  • R&D expense ratio below 5.5%. Management has pivoted entirely to the short term, leaving no new models to compete with in 2027–2028.
  • Export growth below 20%. The overseas window closes early and the base-case net profit of RMB 34 billion no longer holds.
  • Domestic sales down double digits for six consecutive months. Share loss is irreversible and the company becomes a business "kept alive by overseas markets."

If any one of the four appears, the rating drops straight from "Watch" to "Avoid," with no waiting for price. Because a cheap price cannot make up for flawed logic.

Watch calendar: five time points

1st of every month: Sales announcement. Watch domestic YoY only; two consecutive months of positive growth would be an initial position signal.

Late October: Q3 report. Watch automotive gross margin (can it hold 20%) and R&D expense ratio (can it recover to 7%). This is the most important earnings report of the year.

Early January 2027: December sales plus full-year target. Watch the company's 2027 guidance; conservative guidance would signal management itself is not optimistic.

Q1 2027: Hungary plant starts production. Watch European registrations; this is the validation point for whether the "tariff bypass" holds.

March 2027: Annual report. Watch whether full-year free cash flow turns positive and whether the dividend payout ratio rises. If FCF is negative for two consecutive years, the valuation denominator must be recalculated.

Conclusion

Tracking conclusion: two of six hypotheses hold; the thesis is valid but unproven. Overseas high growth and automotive gross margin are real; a domestic return to positive, R&D recovery, FX and valuation have yet to fall into place. None of the four falsification conditions has triggered, so we do not downgrade; but none of the verification conditions is fully met either, so we do not add to the position. The next decision point is the Q3 report in late October.
