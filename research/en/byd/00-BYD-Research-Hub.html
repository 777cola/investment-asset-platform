📝 Conclusion: Watch and wait for an inflection point in domestic sales

Watch, wait for an inflection in domestic volume. The stock closed at RMB 87.4 on 2026/09/04, implying a market cap of RMB 796.8 billion and a 21% discount to its 52-week high. Interim results were mixed: H1 revenue of RMB 344.82 billion (-7.1%) and net profit of RMB 12.33 billion (-20.5%), with domestic volume down 15.7% against an industry up 7.3%, confirming share loss. Q2 standalone net profit, however, was RMB 8.24 billion, +29.6% YoY, gross margin reached 18.9%, a near-one-year high, and overseas revenue accounted for 52.6% of the total, surpassing the domestic business for the first time. The issue is valuation: at 27x TTM, our neutral case implies just 3% upside and the bear case -37%, so the risk/reward does not hold. Two buy triggers: domestic monthly volume turning positive YoY, or the stock returning to just above RMB 70.
