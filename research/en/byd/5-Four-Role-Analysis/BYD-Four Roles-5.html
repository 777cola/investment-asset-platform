Four seats' points of divergence, rulings and execution plan

Core view

Four seats ran independently and converged on the same call: first-class company, genuine improvement in the second quarter, but share is being lost and the price has not been discounted. Not one seat argued for buying now. The only real disagreement concerns the quality of that 29.6% growth in the second quarter.

The four-seat conclusion is this report's own projection; the divergence reflects differences in analytical frameworks rather than factual disputes.
Consensus: three points beyond dispute

First, the business quality is first-rate. No other Chinese automaker globally matches its vertical integration, cost control and speed of overseas plant construction. None of the four seats questioned its capabilities.

Second, the second quarter did improve. Net profit was RMB 8.24 billion, up 29.6% YoY and 102% QoQ; automotive gross margin was 23.0%, up 4.3 points YoY; net profit per vehicle was RMB 0.72 million. These numbers are not manufactured — they reflect volumes recovering from 0.70 million to 1.11 million units plus the overseas mix.

Third, the current price does not constitute a buying point. At 27x TTM, the neutral scenario is RMB 90 (+3%) and the bear case RMB 55 (-37%), unanimous across the four seats.

Disagreement: how much of that 29.6% came from cost savings

The commercial view is about operations: volumes are recovering, overseas mix is above half and automotive gross margin stands at 23% — genuine improvements. The financial view is about structure: R&D expense fell by RMB 6.3 billion while net profit fell by only RMB 3.2 billion — without the R&D cuts, profit would have shrunk 40% rather than grown.

The verdict is "half and half." Digging deeper separates the two: the 4.3-point gross margin gain in the second quarter came from overseas mix and scale effects, and that part is real; the R&D expense ratio falling from 7.97% to 6.76% is the part that was "saved." The problem is that the "saved" half is unsustainable — R&D cannot be cut 20% year after year.

So the 2027 earnings forecast needs a discount. The base case of RMB 34 billion already assumes R&D recovers (expense ratio back to around 7%); cut it further and profit could reach RMB 38 billion, but the 2028 product cycle would look far worse. That trade is not worth it.

Watch calendar: three points in time, three types of action

1st of every month: sales. Watch one number only — domestic YoY. Two consecutive months of positive growth and we open the first position. Late October: Q3 report. Watch two numbers — whether automotive gross margin holds 20% and whether the R&D expense ratio returns to 7%. Q1 2027: Hungary plant starts production. Watch European registrations — the key to whether the overseas story can run another three years.

None of these three points requires forecasting, only recording. If Q3 automotive gross margin falls below 18% while the R&D expense ratio drops below 5.5%, there is no need to wait for sales — the thesis is dead; remove it from the watchlist.

Verdict: maintain "Watch," with conditions fixed

Rating unchanged: Watch. We need to see an inflection in domestic volume. We will not change our rating on the back of a single quarter's improvement in Q2, since half of that improvement came from one-off items. Only one data point can change our conclusion: domestic monthly sales turning positive YoY. That print is released on the 1st of each month, making it more timely than any quarterly report.

Execution plan: currently 0 position. Build the first one-third of the position (about 1.5% portfolio weight) if either condition is met: ① domestic monthly sales positive YoY for two consecutive months; ② the share price returns to the RMB 70–75 range (corresponding to 23x neutral net profit). If both conditions are met, add to 3%.

Stop-loss and exit: if automotive gross margin falls below 18% for two consecutive quarters, or the R&D expense ratio drops below 5.5%, the thesis is void. Not because the numbers look ugly, but because that would signal "protecting profit" has become "giving up the future" — and the story changes.

Conclusion

Four-seat verdict: don't buy, watch closely. The franchise is first-rate and did improve in Q2, but half of that improvement came from cost savings, it is still losing share, and a 27x valuation leaves no margin for error. Execution: 0 position, two triggers (domestic monthly sales turn positive / share price RMB 70–75), one hard stop (auto gross margin below 18% for two consecutive quarters). Top of the watchlist, but not a cent allocated to it.
