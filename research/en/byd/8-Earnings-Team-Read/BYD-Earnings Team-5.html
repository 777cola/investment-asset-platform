Core view

Four close reads in, the striking point is not that the conclusions agree but that each reader pulled different numbers from the same financial report: Duan Yongping on gross margin and R&D, Buffett on cash flow and capex, Munger on three deteriorating line items, Li Lu on overseas capacity. Stack the four risk points together and you have the full picture of this company.

These multiples mark the midpoint of the reasonable P/E range each perspective derives under its own framework; they are not target prices. All four perspectives draw on the 2026 interim results and public information.
Consensus 1: The second-quarter improvement is real, but do not extrapolate it

All four flag the strong second quarter: net profit of RMB 8.24 billion, up 29.6% YoY and 102% QoQ, automotive gross margin of 23.0%, up 4.3pp YoY, and net profit per vehicle of RMB 0.72 million. But all four make the same point: this is a recovery from a severe first-quarter collapse. Q1 2026 net profit was RMB 4.09 billion, against RMB 9.15 billion a year earlier, down about 55% YoY.

That makes "inflection" a word to treat with caution. Going from -55% to +29.6% is a return to normal, not outperformance. First-half absolute profit of RMB 12.33 billion remains below the RMB 15.51 billion of the year-earlier period.

Consensus 2: Valuation leaves no room for a bad case

27x TTM, on TTM net profit of RMB 29.44 billion. All four analysts' fair multiples cluster around 20x, implying a market cap of RMB 590 billion and a share price of about RMB 65. In other words, on their numbers the stock is roughly 25% overvalued at the current price.

This is a directional call, not a precise calculation, but it makes one point: the current price leaves no room for continued domestic decline. If domestic sales keep falling at a double-digit pace through Q3 and Q4, the market will reprice, and RMB 65 will not look alarmist.

Divergence: how many years of overseas premium can be sustained?

Duan Yongping gives it 3 years; Munger gives no time frame, asking only whether the business will disappear; Li Lu gives 3–4 years; Buffett gives none, as he does not pay for a "window period". The disagreement really turns on whether you will pay a premium for certainty.

Our treatment: assume 3 years of high growth, then deceleration to industry growth. That yields neutral-case 2027 net profit of RMB 34 billion, a figure that already embeds overseas growth slowing from 67.8% to below 50%. A more optimistic view would assume overseas earnings stay this profitable forever, which is unreasonable.

Final call: Watch, two conditions

The synthesized conclusion matches all four deep reads: top-tier company quality, genuine current-period improvement, and a valuation that leaves no margin of safety. Rating stays at "Watch, wait for the domestic volume inflection."

Two triggers: (1) domestic monthly sales turn positive YoY for two consecutive months; (2) the stock returns to just above RMB 70 (about 23x neutral net profit). The first is a fundamental signal, the second a price signal; either one alone justifies starting a position, and both together justify building to full weight.

Conclusion

Synthesis: Four readers, four angles on the same financial statements, one conclusion — the company's capabilities have not changed, its circumstances have, and its price has not. The capabilities are worth 25x, the circumstances 18x, and the price on offer is 27x. That gap is the risk.
