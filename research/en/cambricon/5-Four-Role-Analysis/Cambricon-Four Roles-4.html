Core view

The risk is not "will it fall," but "which variables, once they move, break the thesis." Cambricon has six: foundry, HBM, customers, inventory, valuation, governance.

Geopolitics: the Entity List is an identity, not an event

Being placed on the Entity List on 2022/12/15 means it can never obtain the most advanced US technology and equipment, nor can it ever access TSMC's advanced process nodes. This is not a one-off shock; it is a permanent constraint. The upside is that competitors are equally constrained; the downside is that its technological ceiling has been capped.

More subtly, if US-China relations ease and controls are relaxed, Nvidia's specially designed cards will flood back in, and the urgency of domestic substitution will decline — for Cambricon, "sanctions relief" is a negative, not a positive. This is a counterintuitive but real risk.

Supply chain: it staked RMB 8.2 billion upstream

Of the RMB 8.248 billion in inventory, raw materials account for RMB 5.749 billion and consigned processing materials RMB 2.537 billion; prepayments total RMB 2.914 billion. This money is riding on two things: SMIC wafers and HBM dies. Any delay in either link hits both revenue and asset quality at once.

HBM is the tighter link. Global HBM is dominated by SK hynix, Samsung and Micron, with domestic CXMT just starting to ramp; in 2026 HBM will be in short supply and prices will rise. Cambricon must compete with Huawei for the same HBM allocation, and Huawei's purchase volume is several times its own. This is the cost of being a "second supplier" on the production side.

Customer: 88.66% concentration is a structural risk

In 2025, the top five customers accounted for RMB 5.76 billion, or 88.66%; in Q1 2025, a single large customer at one point accounted for 96.48%. Under this structure, one customer's procurement cadence is a quarter's earnings report. Q2 grew only 7.84% QoQ, likely because acceptance by a major customer was delayed.

Li Lu would ask a more fundamental question: why do these customers buy from it? If the answer is "because they can't get Ascend," the customer relationship is fragile; if the answer is "because its software stack is easier to use," that is stickiness. From public information, the former weighs more.

Valuation and governance: two amplifiers

A 218x P/E means any risk that materializes will be amplified in the share price. From June 30 to August 17, fundamentals did not change, yet the stock fell 27.6% and the P/E compressed from 369x to 218x—that is the amplifier effect of valuation.

Governance risk is a single point: Chen Tianshi holds 28.35% and serves as both chairman and general manager, with no counterbalancing force on the board. This is not a question of "he will do bad things," but of "when he misjudges, no one can stop him." For a company whose survival rests on bets on technology roadmaps, this is a substantive risk.

Conclusion

Risk conclusion: Four of the six variables carry a "high" weight and are highly correlated—foundry, HBM, customers, valuation; deterioration in any one drags down the other three. This is not a "diversified risk" portfolio but "four faces of the same risk." This is the core basis for the avoid rating.
