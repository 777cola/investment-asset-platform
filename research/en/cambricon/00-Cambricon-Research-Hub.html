Profit Doubles, Cash of Just RMB 300mn: What Makes Trillion-Yuan Cambricon So Expensive

📝 Conclusion: Avoid; wait for valuation to pull back

Avoid; wait for the valuation to come down. Cambricon reported H1 2026 revenue of RMB 5.996 billion, +108% YoY, and net profit of RMB 2.311 billion, +123% YoY, with a 55.25% gross margin and 38.5% net margin—real results, not a story. Q2 revenue, however, grew just 7.84% QoQ, so growth is shifting gears; inventory of RMB 8.248 billion equals 61% of net assets, and profit has yet to convert into cash. At RMB 1,156 the stock trades on 218x TTM P/E and about 40x P/S, against a neutral-scenario target of RMB 830. The scarcity deserves respect, but this price already discounts the optimistic assumptions of 2028. Wait for revenue to catch up with the valuation, or for the price to fall below RMB 800.
