⑧ Decision framework: three numbers, two lines, one thing — Cambricon

Core view

The previous seven pieces covered "what it is"; this one covers "what to do". The conclusion is short: stay away and wait for valuations to fall; judge using only three numbers, two lines and one thing.

Three numbers: 218, 7.84%, 0.135
218x — TTM P/E, which determines "expensive or not." Against 368.97x on June 30, it has compressed 40.9%, but is still three times the industry average (74.86x). 7.84% — Q2 revenue QoQ, which determines "fast or not." From +159.56% YoY in Q1 to +75.83% in Q2, QoQ growth is down to single digits. 0.135 — cash content of net profit, which determines "real or not." Net profit of RMB 2.311 billion recovered only RMB 311 million in cash.

Together the three numbers yield one judgment: expensive, decelerating, and cash not coming back. Any one alone is not fatal; all three together leave no room for debate.

Two thresholds: RMB 800 and 15%
Price line RMB 800: equal to 77x the neutral 2027 net profit of RMB 6.5 billion; the neutral scenario implies 4% upside, the optimistic 81% and the pessimistic −50%, improving the payoff ratio from 1:2.6 to about 1:1. Data line: Q3 up 15% QoQ: the minimum required to meet the full-year assessment line of RMB 13.5 billion; hitting it shows capacity is in place and the shift is a matter of pace.

If either line triggers, reopen the discussion; if neither does, keep waiting. Waiting needs no reason; buying does.

One thing: be clear about what you are being paid for

Buying Cambricon at this level does not earn you "money from company growth" (that would require RMB 59.5 billion in 2028 revenue), nor "money from dividends" (30% payout ratio, dividend yield below 0.1%). What you earn is "money from the next buyer willing to pay a higher multiple." This is a game, not an investment.

If you admit you are playing a game, then accept the rules of the game: set stop-losses, follow the trend, do not overstay. If you say you are investing, then accept the rules of investing: wait for the price, calculate cash flow, hold with conviction. The most dangerous thing is doing game-playing in the language of investing—that is self-deception.

Finally: this company deserves respect

From a single office at the Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2016 to the first STAR Market stock to reach a RMB 1 trillion market cap in 2026; from being sanctioned and losing money for three straight years to earning RMB 2.059 billion in a year and RMB 2.311 billion in half a year; from an IP supplier to others to the second source of domestic computing power. This is a company that has truly delivered.

But "a company worth respecting" and "a stock worth buying" are two separate judgments. The first asks what problems it has solved; the second asks what price you paid. Cambricon has solved the "whether China has it" question for domestic computing power; it has not yet solved the shareholder's "is it worth it" question. Wait until it solves the second before buying.

If a buy price must be given: the intersection of four methods

Method one, P/E approach: 2027 neutral net profit of RMB 6.5 billion × 70x (lower bound for growth stocks) = RMB 455 billion = RMB 724.Method two, P/S approach: 2027 revenue of RMB 17.5 billion × 25x PS (A-share semiconductor design sector midpoint) = RMB 437.5 billion = RMB 696.

Method three, two-way PEG validation: 2027 revenue growth of 35%, at 1.5x PEG implies 52x = RMB 338 billion = RMB 538; at 2x PEG implies 70x = RMB 455 billion = RMB 724.Method four, sum-of-the-parts: the peer 218x is too high, and Hygon's 250x dynamic P/E is also unreasonable, so fall back to "PEG ≤ 1.5" (2027 growth of 40% × 1.5 = 60x), giving RMB 400 billion = RMB 636.

The four methods yield a range of RMB 538–724, with a median of about RMB 650; adding a scarcity premium, RMB 700 is the "genuinely cheap" line.So the RMB 800 line is "on the expensive side but worth starting to look at," while RMB 700 is "genuinely cheap." This also explains why in m14 the position is only raised to 5% below RMB 700 — that is not conservatism, it is calculated.

If already holding: three exit rules

Assume you built a position below RMB 1,000. What now? Rule one: do not add. A 218x P/E is no place to add. Rule two: set a fundamental stop. If Q3 QoQ growth is <5% and inventory keeps rising, halve the position; if single-quarter gross margin is <50%, exit entirely.

Rule three: take profits on price, not stop-losses on price. Halve at RMB 1,450 (bull case target); exit entirely above RMB 1,600. The logic: let fundamentals decide whether to leave, let price decide how much you earn.

One-sentence version

If you remember only one line, remember this: Cambricon is a good company worth waiting for; RMB 1,156 is a price not worth waiting at. Wait until it falls below RMB 800, or until Q3 proves capacity has genuinely come on stream, then come back.

Conclusion

Decision framework: three numbers (218/7.84%/0.135) for qualitative characterization, two lines (RMB 800/15%) for quantification, one question (whose money are you making) for qualitative judgment. Conclusion: avoid; wait for valuation to pull back. Rating consistent with the working paper.
