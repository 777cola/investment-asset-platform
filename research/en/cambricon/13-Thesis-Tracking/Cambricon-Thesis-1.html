Core thesis

Break the "bullish on Cambricon" case into six testable assumptions: the domestic compute gap persists (holds), capacity keeps pace (to be verified), gross margin holds at 53% (holds), cash conversion returns to 0.5 (does not hold), customer concentration falls below 80% (does not hold), valuation offers a margin of safety (does not hold). Only two and a half of the six stand up.

No surprise—two years past breakeven, the assumptions were always going to be half true, half false. What matters is knowing which one gets falsified first: the earliest test is the late-October Q3 report, and three numbers settle it: Q3 revenue QoQ, quarterly gross margin, operating cash flow. With no position on, this tracking sheet is not for "managing a holding" but for "preparing to enter."

The gap is real, and so is the gross profit

The first point (the gap persists) needs little argument. As long as US controls remain in place and Ascend capacity is prioritised for internal use, customers must retain a non-Huawei path. Contract liabilities rose from RMB 610,000 to RMB 188 million in 1H—customers voted with prepayments—and Q2 was still +75.83% YoY, so demand has not collapsed.

The third point (gross margin) is the more valuable one. Gross margin reached 55.25%, and 56.10% in Q2 alone, up another 1.77pp QoQ—gross margin was still improving in the same quarter that growth shifted gears—which rules out "price cuts for volume." Had the company been buying growth with price cuts, gross margin would have fallen 5 points long ago.

The most critical question remains unanswered: can deliveries keep pace?

The second point is the crux of the entire tracking table. The company has committed RMB 2.914 billion in prepayments and RMB 8.248 billion in inventory to capacity, with plans to deliver about 500,000 units in 2026 (media citation; the company has not issued formal guidance). If that capacity comes online, Q3 revenue should grow more than 15% QoQ; if it does not, Q2's 7.84% becomes the new normal.

Verification is simple: watch Q3 single-quarter revenue. RMB 3.111 billion × 1.15 = RMB 3.58 billion is the passing line; below RMB 3.3 billion (up 5% QoQ) is a fail. This number will be available in late October; no need to wait a year.

Three that do not hold: cash, concentration, valuation

Criterion four (cash) is the hardest to fix. OCF/net profit of 0.135 needs to return to 0.5, which implies second-half operating cash flow above RMB 2 billion—close to impossible while inventory build continues. This criterion will most likely improve only in 2027, when capacity stops expanding.

Criterion five (customer concentration) must wait for the annual report. The 88.66% for 2025 is historical data; the 2026 interim report did not disclose it, so strictly speaking it is "unknown." We treat it as "not met" absent evidence of improvement. Criterion six (valuation) changes daily; at 218x it is still more than half way from offering a margin of safety.

Falsification conditions: three red lines

① Q3 single-quarter revenue down more than 5% QoQ (below RMB 3.27 billion) with inventory still rising — the shift reflects a demand problem, not a timing one; walk away and stop waiting for RMB 800. ② Single-quarter gross margin falls below 50% — the company has begun cutting prices to move volume, the moat is leaking, and the valuation framework needs rebuilding. ③ Top five customers rise above 90% of revenue, or public information emerges of a major customer shifting orders to Ascend — the second-supplier thesis is broken.

If any one of the three red lines triggers, move the company from the "watch list" to "no longer covered." Falsification conditions are valuable not for forecasting but for deciding in advance "under what circumstances I admit I am wrong."

Watch calendar for next steps
  • Late October 2026: Q3 report — QoQ revenue, quarterly gross margin and operating cash flow at a glance; the shortest validation window.
  • December 2026: industry tenders and cloud vendors' 2027 capex guidance — determines whether the second assumption (a persistent gap) still holds next year.
  • January 2027: annual earnings pre-announcement — whether the RMB 13.5 billion assessment line is met directly determines whether incentives unlock.
  • March 2027: annual report — top five customer concentration updated for the first time; the fifth assumption is settled.
  • April 2027: Q1 report — whether Siyuan 690 volume ramp materializes determines the credibility of the RMB 27 billion assessment line for 2027.

Of the five dates, only the first requires preparation now. Leave the rest until they arrive; no need to worry ahead of time.

Conclusion

Tracking conclusion: two of six hypotheses hold, one awaits verification, three do not; overall rating "Watch." The gap and gross margin are genuine supports; cash and valuation are clear drags. The Q3 report in late October is the first gate: Q3 revenue growth of ≥15% QoQ would warrant an upgrade to "Watch"; growth below 5% with inventory still rising would mean removal from the list. No position currently; the tracking table is for preparing entry, not managing holdings.
