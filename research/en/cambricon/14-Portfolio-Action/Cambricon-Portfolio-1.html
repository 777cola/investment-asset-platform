Core thesis

The conclusion is blunt: 0 position now, no watch position. The reason is the payoff skew—neutral target RMB 830 (−28%), bear case RMB 400 (−65%), bull case RMB 1,450 (+25%), giving a probability-weighted expected return of −24% at 25/50/25. Building a "watch position" at this payoff is paying real money for the psychological comfort of "I'm afraid of missing out."

The price discipline, stated plainly: open a first tranche of 3% at RMB 800—900; add to 5% below RMB 700; add nothing above RMB 1,400, and halve any existing holding. A stop-loss does not apply to a zero position; once established, the stop sits on the fundamental trigger of "Q3 quarter-on-quarter <5% with inventories still rising," not on price—because this company's volatility will knock out a price stop first and return to fundamentals afterwards.

Why 0% and not "try 1%"

Many will argue that with a company this good, you buy 1% just to keep it on the radar. I don't accept that. A 1% position makes no money; it only makes you stare at the stock price every day. It also plants a psychological anchor: once you own it, you start hunting for bullish reasons—position bias.

The more practical objection is the payoff. An expected return of −24% means every purchase at this level loses money over the long run. A 24% loss on a 1% position hits the portfolio by only 0.24%, yet it consumes attention and judgment. At 218x P/E, attention is more expensive than money.

One more piece of portfolio arithmetic: assume a portfolio of RMB 1 million and a 5% cap (RMB 50,000). The neutral scenario (RMB 830) gives a 4% gain; the optimistic scenario (RMB 1,450), a 25% gain; the pessimistic scenario (RMB 400), a 65% loss—an expected contribution of −1.2% from a 5% position. In other words, even if this position is right, it contributes only 1 point; if wrong, it loses 3 points. With risk-reward this asymmetric, the only correct action is not to do it.

How was the RMB 800 threshold set?

This is not a guess. 2027 neutral net profit of RMB 6.5 billion × 80x = RMB 520 billion market cap ÷ 628.29 million shares = RMB 827; setting the entry at 800 leaves a 3% buffer. At this price, the neutral case offers 4% upside, the optimistic case 81% upside and the pessimistic case −50%, shifting the risk-reward from 1:2.6 to about 1:1.

Why not wait for RMB 700? Because waiting for RMB 700 implies you believe even the neutral case will not materialise—that is the pricing of the "pessimistic case." If the stock does reach RMB 700, one thing must first be confirmed: has the valuation fallen, or has the thesis broken. If the latter, do not buy even at RMB 700.

Triggers for adding to and trimming positions
Three conditions to add to the position (3% → 5%), all of which must be met simultaneously: ① Q3 and Q4 revenue up more than 15% QoQ (full-year RMB 13.5 billion achieved); ② operating cash flow/net profit back above 0.5; ③ gross margin no lower than 53%. Three conditions to cut the position (5% → 0), any one of which triggers: ① single-quarter gross margin falls below 50%; ② top-five customer concentration rises above 90% or a major customer shifts orders; ③ share price returns above RMB 1,400 (valuation overextended).

Note there is no "stop-loss price" here. For a stock that swings 5% a day on average, a price stop-loss will only shake you out at the bottom. Use fundamentals for stop-loss, use price for take-profit—this is the only workable discipline for high-volatility growth stocks.

Correlation with other holdings: not a diversification tool

Cambricon's earnings driver is domestic AI compute capex, which is highly correlated with the following assets: Hygon Information (same track, rough correlation coefficient 0.7–0.8), SMIC (upstream capacity, 0.6–0.7), and memory and optical modules (AI compute chain, 0.5–0.7). If your portfolio already holds AI compute chain positions, adding Cambricon amounts to adding leverage, not diversification.

Its correlation with consumer, dividend and utility assets is low or even negative, so in theory it can hedge — but that is hedging low-volatility assets with high-volatility assets, putting the cart before the horse. The right approach: cap the AI compute chain as a whole at 10%–15% of the portfolio, with Cambricon only a small slice of that.

Correlation coefficients are rough estimates based on industry logic and historical price action, without rigorous regression; they serve only to judge position diversification and are not a quantitative basis.

Conclusion

Portfolio conclusion: 0 position, no watchlist position. At 218x P/E and -24% expected return, any position is negative EV. Price discipline: open 3% at RMB 800–900, 5% below RMB 700, halve above RMB 1,400; fundamental stop-loss (exit if Q3 QoQ growth <5% and inventory rises). Highly correlated with Hygon, SMIC and memory/optical module names; it cannot serve as a diversification tool.
